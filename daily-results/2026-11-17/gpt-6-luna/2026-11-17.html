🌐 TOP STORIES

AMD Completes World Labs Acquisition, Bringing Fei-Fei Li to Its AI Team

AMD announced completion of its World Labs acquisition on Nov. 16. The company says Fei-Fei Li joins as executive vice president and chief scientist, and the research team will continue foundational model work while collaborating with AMD engineers on future AI infrastructure. World Labs develops models for interactive 3D environments and simulation.

OpenAI Expands Free Frontier-Model Access to 200,000 Researchers

OpenAI said it is expanding its ChatGPT for Academic Researchers program to offer 200,000 researchers worldwide free access to its frontier models. The announcement concerns research access and program scale, not a new model release. The organization describes the program as support for scientific research and collaboration.

Moonshot Redirects Kimi Toward Biology, Chemistry and Drug Research

Moonshot AI is steering its Kimi models toward biology, chemistry and pharmaceutical research as it sees diminishing returns from coding benchmarks, according to remarks by international director Giacomo Ficari at Web Summit. One cited application integrates Kimi K3.1 Pro into DP Technology’s BioMaster assistant. The report cautions that fully independent AI scientific breakthroughs remain distant.

CMS Roadmap Proposes State Sandboxes and New Clinical-AI Policies

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services published a clinical AI roadmap proposing federal-state sandbox work, improved health-data sharing, post-market surveillance, liability clarification and outcome-linked payment models. The roadmap frames the proposals as a way to expand clinical AI adoption. Reporting notes unresolved questions about FDA oversight of state pilots, including Utah’s.

Anthropic Shortens Vulnerability Disclosure Timelines as AI Discovery Accelerates

Anthropic says it will shorten its coordinated vulnerability disclosure timelines beginning Jan. 1, 2027: 30 days for high- and critical-severity issues and 45 days for other issues. The company cites faster AI-assisted vulnerability discovery and rediscovery. Its seven-day disclosure period for vulnerabilities under active exploitation remains unchanged.
