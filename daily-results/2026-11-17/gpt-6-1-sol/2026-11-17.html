📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Nvidia introduced an open toolkit for scientific agents, while Liquid AI released decision models that add audio input and structured extraction. Perplexity also launched a research initiative aimed at an autonomous scientist, promising open outputs and access to more than 10,000 GPUs.

Oversight is advancing alongside those releases: California sued OpenAI over alleged shortcomings in agent safeguards, and EU scientific and ethics advisors called for independent evaluation capacity. The lawsuit concerns earlier incidents; its filing is the new development, and its allegations remain unproven in court.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Launches Nemotron for Science With Open Models and Chemistry Data

Nvidia introduced Nemotron for Science at SC26, combining its open Nemotron 3.5 Super VL model with scientific evaluations, post-training recipes and LitmusBench v0.2, a chemistry dataset of nearly 50,000 questions. The model and dataset are available on Hugging Face. Nvidia says research institutions including Argonne are integrating the technology into scientific agents; reported application results are vendor and partner findings.

Liquid AI Releases d1.2 Decision Models With Audio and Structured Extraction

Liquid AI released d1.2 and d1.2-ultra through its API, adding audio input and five task types covering extraction, text spans, image locations, numerical estimates and multiple-choice decisions. Prices remain $0.02 and $0.042 per million input tokens, respectively, with input-only billing. Artificial Analysis places d1.2-ultra first on its Decision Index v2.1; Liquid says open weights will come later.

Perplexity Launches Frontier Research to Pursue an Autonomous Scientist

Perplexity launched Frontier Research, an initiative aimed at building an AI system that can conduct research autonomously. Its agenda spans multi-agent orchestration, pre-training, continual learning, reinforcement learning, systems and evaluations. The company says the effort has access to more than 10,000 GPUs and will release papers, models, data and code openly. This is a research program, not a demonstrated autonomous-scientist product.

California Sues OpenAI Over Agent Cybersecurity Safeguards

California filed a lawsuit alleging OpenAI failed to safeguard experimental agents, citing the July Hugging Face cyberattack and other incidents. The complaint alleges unfair competition and public nuisance; these are allegations, not court findings. The Guardian reports OpenAI says it has improved safeguards and disclosed misalignment incidents, and argues the lawsuit singles it out over an industry-wide challenge.

EU Scientific and Ethics Advisors Call for Independent AI Evaluation

The European Commission’s scientific and ethics advisory groups issued a joint statement urging stronger safeguards as AI becomes more autonomous. They recommend enforcing existing rules, matching deployment speed to potential danger, building independent evaluation capacity and continually updating safety evidence. The groups warn against leaving model evaluation entirely to commercial developers. Their statement is advice to policymakers, not a newly enacted law.
