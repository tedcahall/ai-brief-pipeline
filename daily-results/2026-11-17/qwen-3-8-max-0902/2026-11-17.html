📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

AI for science had a breakout day. OpenAI said it will expand free frontier model access to up to 200,000 scientists, NVIDIA launched Nemotron for Science at SC26 with open models and chemistry datasets, and Perplexity committed more than 10,000 GPUs to a new Frontier Research lab aimed at building an automated researcher.

In model news, Liquid AI released its d1.2 decision models that top the Artificial Analysis Decision Index, and Noeri, founded by Stanford professors Yejin Choi and Carlos Guestrin, exited stealth with a $110M seed led by Menlo Ventures.

Infrastructure also moved, with OpenRouter now integrated directly into Hugging Face, while SemiAnalysis argued that more than 1.5 GW of restricted chips are already inside China, a flow it says could reshape open source AI if it unwinds.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Expands Free Frontier Model Access to 200,000 Scientists

OpenAI announced it will expand free access to its frontier models for up to 200,000 scientists worldwide through its academic researchers program. Applications for the next round open November 23, with 50,000 new grants awarded first come, first served. The company said program data is not used for model training by default and reported that participants in a study of more than 1,800 researchers nearly doubled their productivity.

Liquid AI Releases d1.2 Omnimodal Decision Models

Liquid AI released d1.2 and d1.2-ultra, decision models that process text, images and audio and return calibrated answers across five new task types including value extraction, span finding and object localization. The company said d1.2-ultra ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1 with a score of 75. Pricing is $0.02 per million input tokens for d1.2 and $0.042 for d1.2-ultra.

Perplexity Launches Frontier Research to Build an Automated Scientist

Perplexity announced Frontier Research, a lab aimed at building systems that perform autonomous research, supported by more than 10,000 GPUs. Work will focus on multi-agent orchestration, reinforcement learning, continual learning and pre-training, with algorithms, models, evaluations and benchmarks released openly. Crypto Briefing reported Andrew Gordon Wilson leads the team, and Perplexity is hiring researchers in four cities.

NVIDIA Introduces Nemotron for Science at SC26

At SC26 in Chicago, NVIDIA introduced Nemotron for Science, combining its Nemotron 3.5 Super VL open model, a new open chemistry dataset and post-training recipes so researchers can build specialized scientific discovery agents. NVIDIA said institutions including the Department of Energy's Argonne National Laboratory are already using Nemotron to build agents that plan tasks, run simulations and check results.

Noeri Exits Stealth With $110M Seed Led by Menlo Ventures

Noeri, a Palo Alto startup co-founded by Stanford professors Yejin Choi and Carlos Guestrin, launched November 16 with a $110M seed round led by Menlo Ventures. Nvidia, Snowflake and Cisco joined as strategic backers with Madrona, Kindred, Wing and NEA, per Bloomberg. Noeri builds specialized AI systems that learn from a company's own data and propose changes to instructions, tools and model routing, each explained for human approval.
