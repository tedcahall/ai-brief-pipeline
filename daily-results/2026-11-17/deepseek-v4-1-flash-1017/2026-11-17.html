🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Puts Computer-Use Agents Inside Hospital Software With 20+ Healthcare Plugins

OpenAI said on November 16 that ChatGPT for Healthcare now includes computer-use agents that read screens, click and type across existing hospital software, alongside improved trusted clinical search and more than 20 new plugins. Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare and CVS Health are named as deployments. OpenAI says content shared with the product is not used to train models, and organizations must use services covered by their BAA.

Liquid AI Releases Omnimodal Decision Models That Lead a Benchmark Index

Liquid AI released d1.2 and d1.2-ultra on November 16, decision models that accept text, images and audio and return calibrated answers rather than prose. The company says d1.2-ultra scores 75 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1, two points above the previous best, and prices the models at $0.02 and $0.042 per million input tokens. These are vendor-reported results without independent replication.

Perplexity Launches Frontier Research to Build an Automated Researcher

Perplexity announced Frontier Research on November 16, a team whose stated goal is a system that can perform autonomous research. The company committed at least 10,000 GPUs and said the work will cover multi-agent orchestration, reinforcement learning, continual learning, systems and pre-training, with papers, models, data and benchmarks published openly. The unit is hiring in San Francisco, Palo Alto, New York and London.

NVIDIA Introduces Nemotron for Science With an Open Chemistry Dataset

NVIDIA introduced Nemotron for Science at SC26 on November 16, bundling its Nemotron 3.5 Super VL open model with LitmusBench v0.2, an open dataset of nearly 50,000 chemistry reasoning questions, plus post-training recipes. Argonne National Laboratory and Jülich Supercomputing Centre are named as integrators, and life-sciences companies are customizing the model. NVIDIA is the source for all capability claims.

China's Top AI Labs Ship Models With Reduced Offensive Cyber Capability

Bloomberg reported on November 17 that Alibaba, Tencent and Moonshot shipped recent models with reduced offensive cyber capability and stronger defensive behavior. Alibaba released its latest Qwen flagship without weights, Tencent cut cyber functions from Hy4, and Moonshot did similar work on Kimi K3.1 Pro. The report ties the pattern to a Chinese government proposal to restrict exports of attack-capable tools.
