NOV 17, 2026

📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

OpenAI is bringing four outside organizations into scoped assessments of its model alignment and safeguards, while Liquid AI has released new decision models that accept audio as well as text and images. Perplexity has launched a research unit with the stated goal of building an autonomous research scientist.

OpenAI is also expanding its research-access program, with a goal of reaching 200,000 researchers, and Cohere has put video search into private preview. These are announcements and plans, not proof of future performance.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI brings four outside assessors into its safety work

OpenAI signed agreements with Apollo Research, METR, Transluce and Goodfire for embedded assessments of model alignment and safeguards. The company says assessors will receive access tailored to each project, potentially including model checkpoints, training data and monitoring systems. The assessments are scoped pilots, not findings that the systems are safe.

Liquid AI releases d1.2 decision models with audio input

Liquid AI released d1.2 and d1.2-ultra, adding audio to text and image inputs and five new task types. The models return structured decisions rather than ordinary generated prose. Liquid says the models are available through its API; its evaluations of the new tasks use internal test sets built from public datasets.

Perplexity launches research unit focused on autonomous science

Perplexity announced Frontier Research, a unit aiming to build an AI system capable of conducting research autonomously. It plans work across multi-agent orchestration, pre-training, continual learning, reinforcement learning, systems and evaluations. The company says it will publish research outputs and support the effort with more than 10,000 GPUs. The autonomous scientist remains an objective, not an established capability.

OpenAI expands free research access toward 200,000 scientists

OpenAI says its academic researcher program aims to reach up to 200,000 people worldwide. Applications for 50,000 new grants open November 23, with each grant covering the recipient and up to two collaborators on ChatGPT Plus. OpenAI reports that 20,000 researchers have joined since the pilot began; the larger number is a goal, not current enrollment.

Cohere previews searchable video in Compass

Cohere introduced Video Intelligence in private preview for design partners. The feature indexes spoken and visual material and returns relevant video segments with timestamps, transcripts and visual descriptions. It combines video and document retrieval for enterprise search and agents. General availability and pricing have not been announced.
