🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Starts Embedded Safety Assessments With Four Outside Groups

OpenAI said it signed agreements with Apollo Research, METR, Transluce, and Goodfire for a first phase of embedded assessments. Assessors get extended access to internal models, checkpoints, code, training data, and monitoring systems to test alignment and safeguards. Apollo will examine whether models could covertly work against developer intentions. METR will review OpenAI's largest reinforcement-learning run to date. OpenAI says assessors can publish, while OpenAI may redact confidential information.

Liquid AI Releases Omnimodal Decision Models d1.2 and d1.2-ultra

Liquid AI released d1.2 and d1.2-ultra, decision models that accept text, images, and audio and return structured answers instead of generated prose. The company says d1.2-ultra scores 75 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1, which it lists as the highest score, and that both models sit on its cost frontier. API prices are $0.02 and $0.042 per million input tokens. New tasks cover value extraction, text spans, image boxes, numeric estimates, and multi-select choices.

NVIDIA Introduces Nemotron for Science for Research Agents

NVIDIA introduced Nemotron for Science at SC26, pairing the Nemotron 3.5 Super VL open model with LitmusBench v0.2, a chemistry dataset of nearly 50,000 questions, and post-training recipes for science agents. The company says Argonne, Jülich, Lila Sciences, Terray Therapeutics, and Therna are using or adapting the model. NVIDIA attributes partner-reported gains to that post-training, including Terray's about 76 percent reaction recovery on the RxnScribe test. The model and dataset are on Hugging Face.

Perplexity Launches Frontier Research for Autonomous Research

Perplexity said it is launching Frontier Research to create a system that can perform autonomous research. The company said the work will focus on multi-agent orchestration, reinforcement learning, continual learning, systems, and pre-training, supported by more than 10,000 GPUs. It said algorithms, models, evaluations, papers, data, and benchmarks will be released openly and tested in Perplexity products. Hiring is open in San Francisco, Palo Alto, New York, and London.

Hugging Face Integrates OpenRouter for Open-Weight Inference

Hugging Face said OpenRouter is now integrated directly, so the same token and endpoint can call open-weight models, with live model stats on the Hub and a redesigned inference widget. The linked blog says language-model requests now go through OpenRouter, which picks a provider using live price, speed, and reliability and retries if a call fails. Hugging Face says it adds no markup. The announcement post is 18:18 UTC on Nov. 16.
