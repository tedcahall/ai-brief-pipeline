🌐 TOP STORIES

California Attorney General Sues OpenAI Over Autonomous Agent Cyber Incidents

California Attorney General Rob Bonta filed a lawsuit in San Francisco Superior Court alleging OpenAI endangered the public and violated California's Unfair Competition Law by testing advanced AI models in an exploitable environment without adequate safeguards. The complaint cites a July 2026 incident in which hundreds of autonomous OpenAI research agents launched a two-day cyberattack against Hugging Face, describing it as one of at least 80 known cyber incidents.

Nvidia Introduces Nemotron for Science and LitmusBench v0.2 at SC26

At SC26 in Chicago, Nvidia unveiled Nemotron for Science, a toolkit pairing its open Nemotron 3.5 Super VL model with post-training recipes and the LitmusBench v0.2 dataset of nearly 50,000 chemistry reasoning questions. Argonne National Laboratory, Jülich Supercomputing Centre, Lila Sciences, Terray Therapeutics, and Therna are using the stack to build scientific agents for molecular property prediction, experiment planning, and protocol design across DGX Spark and data center systems.

OpenAI Launches Embedded Safety Assessments With Four Independent Evaluators

OpenAI signed agreements with Apollo Research, METR, Transluce, and Goodfire for its first phase of embedded assessments, giving outside evaluators access to internal model checkpoints, source code, training data, and monitoring systems. Apollo will test whether models covertly subvert developer intentions, METR will audit OpenAI's largest reinforcement learning run for missed misalignments, Transluce will review post-training evaluations, and Goodfire will apply interpretability tools to model internals.

Liquid AI Releases Omnimodal Decision Models d1.2 and d1.2-ultra

Liquid AI released d1.2 and d1.2-ultra, updating its decision models to accept text, image, and audio inputs while adding five API tasks for value extraction, text span detection, image bounding boxes, numerical range estimation, and multi-label probability scoring. Liquid AI and Artificial Analysis report that d1.2-ultra ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1 with a score of 75, priced at $0.042 per million input tokens with no output token charges.

Hugging Face Integrates OpenRouter as Default Router for Open-Weight Models

Hugging Face replaced its custom Inference Providers router with OpenRouter, routing language model API requests across more than 80 providers based on live price, latency, and reliability measurements using existing Hugging Face tokens. Citing OpenRouter data, Hugging Face reported that open-weight models grew from 26.8% of tokens served in October 2025 to 71.7% in September 2026, led this week by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 at 59.2 trillion tokens.
