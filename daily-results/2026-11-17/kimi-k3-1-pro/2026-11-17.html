🌐 TOP STORIES

Liquid AI Releases d1.2 and d1.2-Ultra Omnimodal Decision Models

Liquid AI released d1.2 and d1.2-ultra, adding audio input and five structured decision tasks spanning value and span extraction, object localization, numerical estimates and multi-label choices. Both are available through the Liquid API and bill input tokens only. Artificial Analysis ranks d1.2-ultra first on its Decision Index v2.1 with 75 points, two ahead of the previous leader. Liquid's internal task results remain vendor-reported.

OpenAI Gives Four Independent Assessors Deep Access to Internal Models and Training

OpenAI signed Apollo Research, METR, Transluce and Goodfire for its first embedded assessment phase. The assessors will examine alignment and safeguards using selected internal checkpoints, code, data, monitoring systems and documentation. METR plans to investigate misaligned behavior in OpenAI's largest reinforcement learning run, while Apollo will test subversion claims. OpenAI retains confidential redaction and responsible-disclosure rights, and its Safety and Security Committee receives unredacted reports.

Perplexity Launches Frontier Research With More Than 10,000 GPUs

Perplexity created Frontier Research to pursue autonomous research across multi-agent orchestration, reinforcement learning, continual learning, systems and pre-training. The company says more than 10,000 GPUs will support the work, with algorithms, models, evaluations, papers, data and benchmarks released openly and tested in Perplexity products. The unit is hiring in four cities. No performance results or release schedule accompanied the announcement.

NVIDIA Introduces Nemotron for Science and Open LitmusBench Chemistry Data

NVIDIA introduced Nemotron for Science at SC26, combining the open Nemotron 3.5 Super VL model, scientific evaluations, post-training recipes and the LitmusBench v0.2 dataset. LitmusBench contains nearly 50,000 chemistry-reasoning questions drawn from molecular-property and experimental drug-discovery data. The model and dataset are available on Hugging Face. NVIDIA said Argonne, Lila Sciences, Terray Therapeutics and other research organizations are already applying the tools.

Moonshot AI Steers Kimi Toward Biology, Chemistry and Pharma Research

Moonshot AI is redirecting its Kimi models toward biology, chemistry and pharmaceutical research as coding-benchmark gains flatten, international director Giacomo Ficari said at Web Summit in Lisbon, The Decoder reports. DP Technology is integrating Kimi K3.1 Pro into its BioMaster research assistant. Fully independent breakthroughs remain distant. In a June Citi survey of 50 US research leaders cited by the article, 24 percent expected AI to noticeably improve drug-development success rates.
