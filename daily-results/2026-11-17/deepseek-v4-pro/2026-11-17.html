🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Opens Embedded Assessments With Four Independent AI Labs

OpenAI said it is working with Apollo Research, METR, Transluce and Goodfire AI on embedded assessments that give outside experts deep access to its internal models and systems to investigate alignment and test safeguards. The program adds independent scrutiny to how OpenAI identifies and manages risks, including a planned review of its largest reinforcement learning run to date.

Liquid AI Releases d1.2 and d1.2-ultra Omnimodal Decision Models

Liquid AI released d1.2 and d1.2-ultra, omnimodal decision models that accept text, image and audio input and add five new output tasks such as reading values and locating objects. The company reports d1.2-ultra ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1 at 75, with d1.2 at 69, at the same price as the prior generation.

NVIDIA Unveils Nemotron for Science Open Models at SC26

NVIDIA introduced Nemotron for Science at the SC26 conference, a collection of open models, datasets, tools and post-training recipes for building AI agents that plan tasks, run simulations and check results. Announced by Ian Buck, the collection is already being used by institutions including Argonne National Laboratory to accelerate scientific discovery.

SemiAnalysis Says Over 1.5 GW of Banned Nvidia Chips Reached China

SemiAnalysis estimated that more than 1.5 gigawatts of export-restricted Nvidia AI chips have reached China despite US controls, a figure analysts equate to roughly 1.5 million GPUs. The firm warns that unwinding that web could reshape the open-source AI ecosystem, and the finding has been linked to scrutiny around Nvidia's recent CEO transition.

Hugging Face Integrates OpenRouter Directly Into the Hub

Hugging Face integrated OpenRouter directly into the Hub, letting developers use one token and endpoint across both platforms alongside live model statistics and a redesigned inference widget. The companies frame the move as accelerating open-source AI by bringing open models into one place.
