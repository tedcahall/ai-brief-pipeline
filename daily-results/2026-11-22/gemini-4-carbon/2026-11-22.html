🌐 TOP STORIES

Lean and Mathlib Communities Certify Claude-Assisted Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

New developments: The Lean and Mathlib communities, endorsed by the Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative, certified on Saturday that both the statement and Levent Alpöge's 17-million-line Claude-assisted Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis are correct. MIT mathematician Andrew Sutherland independently compiled all 175,187 modules in a sandbox, confirmed reliance on only three standard axioms, and verified the exported proof across independent checkers including nanoda, con-leche, and con-ron.

Baseten Releases Open-Weights Bison-1 and Groundhog-1 Decision Models on Gemma 4

Baseten released bison-1, a 31B dense model, and groundhog-1, a 26B mixture-of-experts model with about 4B active parameters, post-trained on Google's Gemma 4 backbone. Both accept text and image inputs across a 128k-token context window, ship with open weights on Hugging Face, and cost $0.10 and $0.035 per million API tokens. Baseten reports bison-1 leads the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1 at 76 with a 0.14 Brier calibration score.

Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus Settles Open Quantum Optics Problem in Lean

Researchers from the University of Tübingen, Google DeepMind, NYU Abu Dhabi, and the University of Jena used Google DeepMind's agentic formal proof system, AlphaProof Nexus, to settle a quantum optics problem open since 2017. Their machine-checked Lean proof establishes an unconditional no-go theorem showing standard linear optics and probabilistic pair sources cannot construct N-photon, D-dimensional entangled Greenberger-Horne-Zeilinger states for even N of at least 4 when D is at least N.

UBS Projects Global AI Spending Will Jump 40% to $1.4 Trillion in 2027

UBS projected global AI investment will climb from $1 trillion in 2026 to $1.4 trillion in 2027 and $1.5 trillion in 2028, with roughly 80% of 2027 spending coming from Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and SpaceX. The bank warned that financial returns remain uncertain, citing risks from power and data-center bottlenecks, higher financing costs, slowing cloud growth, and falling AI prices compressing provider margins.

GAO Warns FDA Has Authorized Zero Mental Health AI Chatbots Amid Youth Use

In a Science and Tech Spotlight report, the U.S. Government Accountability Office noted that nearly one-fifth of Americans aged 12 to 21 used an AI chatbot for mental health advice in 2025, even though the FDA has authorized zero chatbots for mental health care as of November 2026. The agency warned that sycophantic model behavior and ineffective guardrails can reinforce delusions, negative feelings, or self-harm.
