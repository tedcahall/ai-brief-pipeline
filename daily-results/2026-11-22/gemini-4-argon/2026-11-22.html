🌐 TOP STORIES

Lean and Mathlib Communities Certify Formal Verification of Riemann Hypothesis Proof

New developments: Following Friday's claim by Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge of a Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, the Lean and Mathlib communities issued a joint statement endorsed by the Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative certifying that both the statement and the 17-million-line Lean proof are correct. Two comparison tools confirmed the theorem matches Mathlib's RiemannHypothesis definition, and independent checkers including nanoda and con-leche verified the exported proof.

Google Introduces Multi-Token Prediction Speculative Decoding Drafters for Gemma 4

Google researchers published an optimized Multi-Token Prediction (G-MTP) speculative decoding architecture and released checkpoints for the Gemma 4 model family. Using a shared 4-layer drafting head with target activation injection, zero-copy KV-cache cross-attention, and clustered vocabulary projection, the authors report speedups up to 3.1x on large server setups, 2.3x on H100 GPUs, and 2.2x on smartphone GPUs, while domain-shift fine-tuning requires up to 1.83x fewer target forward passes.

AlphaProof Nexus Discovers Formal Quantum Optics No-Go Theorem in Lean

Researchers from the University of Tübingen and Google DeepMind used AI-assisted formal proof search via AlphaProof Nexus in Lean to resolve an open quantum optics question posed in 2017. The machine-checked proof establishes a tensor-algebraic no-go theorem showing that no high-dimensional photonic Greenberger-Horne-Zeilinger (GHZ) state can be generated using only linear optics and probabilistic pair sources for any even photon count of four or more when dimensions equal or exceed photon count.

Google Pro2 Agent Reaches 76.0% Medal Rate on Complete MLE-Bench

Google researchers and academic collaborators introduced Pro2 Agent, an autonomous AI research system that replaces direct codebase iteration with an explicit Problem State tracking suspected bottlenecks and candidate fixes. Through controlled hypothesis-testing experiments, the agent matches the highest previously reported 70.7% medal rate on the complete MLE-Bench under a 24-hour budget and reaches 76.0% with a 48-hour budget, while generating transparent research notes for human review.

U.S. Data Centers Running American AI Chips Serve Chinese Open Models Globally

Although the U.S. Commerce Department issued a rule closing a loophole on remote Chinese access to overseas AI chips, a Fortune analysis notes that U.S.-owned data centers and inference providers including Together AI, Baseten, Fireworks, and Amazon Bedrock still serve Chinese open-weight models on American GPUs. Fortune reports that DeepSeek's latest model is offered by at least 16 non-Chinese inference providers, expanding global adoption and API revenue for Chinese labs.
