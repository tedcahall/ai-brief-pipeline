🌐 TOP STORIES

Lean and Mathlib Communities Certify Claude-Assisted Riemann Hypothesis Proof

New developments: Following Levent Alpöge's November 20 release of a 17-million-line Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, the Lean and Mathlib communities issued a joint statement endorsed by the Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative certifying that both the statement and proof are correct. Independent compilation and verification across Lean's kernel and third-party checkers con-leche, con-ron, and nanoda confirmed all five headline theorems rely solely on standard axioms.

Google DeepMind and University of Tübingen Settle Open Quantum Optics Problem With AlphaProof Nexus

Researchers from the University of Tübingen and Google DeepMind published an arXiv paper showing that AI-assisted formal proof search in Lean using AlphaProof Nexus resolved a quantum optics conjecture open since 2017. The machine-checked proof establishes a tensor-algebraic no-go theorem showing no complex-weighted monochromatic quantum graph can generate high-dimensional photonic GHZ states for any even photon count of four or more when dimension equals or exceeds photon count.

Data Centers Running U.S. Chips Serve Chinese Open-Weight AI Models Globally

Fortune reports that even after the U.S. Commerce Department issued a November 19 rule closing remote-access export loopholes, data centers running U.S.-designed chips continue to accelerate global adoption of Chinese open-weight models. U.S. inference providers including Together AI, Baseten, Fireworks, and Amazon Bedrock host models such as DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and Moonshot's Kimi K3, expanding international usage and generating licensing revenue for Chinese AI labs.

UBS Projects Global AI Investment Will Reach $1.4 Trillion in 2027

In a new report covered by ANI, UBS projects worldwide AI-related investment will rise 40% from $1 trillion in 2026 to $1.4 trillion in 2027 and $1.5 trillion in 2028. UBS estimates roughly 80% of 2027 spending will come from Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and SpaceX, while warning that power transmission bottlenecks, data center shortages, higher financing costs, and unproven financial returns remain key risks.

U.S. GAO Warns No AI Chatbot Has FDA Authorization for Mental Health Care

A U.S. Government Accountability Office Science and Tech Spotlight reports that nearly one-fifth of Americans aged 12 to 21 used an AI chatbot for mental health advice in 2025, yet as of November 2026 the FDA has not authorized any chatbot for mental health care. The GAO warned that sycophancy bias can reinforce delusions or self-harm and cautioned that existing developer guardrails may prove ineffective.
