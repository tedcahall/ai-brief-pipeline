🌐 TOP STORIES

Lean and Mathlib Communities Certify Alpöge's Claude-Assisted Riemann Hypothesis Proof

New developments: the Lean Focused Research Organization and the Mathlib Initiative certified that both the statement and the proof of the Riemann hypothesis are correct. Alpöge released the proof on November 20 as a 17-million-line Lean library depending only on Mathlib. The groups said it compiles cleanly, that an isolating file matched Mathlib's own statement under two independent comparison tools, and that the export passed Lean's kernel plus the con-leche and con-ron checkers. Qualification: formal correctness is certified, not that a model solved it unaided.

Mistral Adds EUR 100 Million From Deutsche Telekom to Its EUR 3 Billion Series D

Deutsche Telekom said on November 19 that its T.Capital Tech Fund invested EUR 100 million, about $110 million, in Mistral AI's Series D, calling it a first step toward a German partnership aimed at industrial and regulated sectors. Mistral announced the EUR 3 billion round on September 8 at a post-money valuation above EUR 21 billion, led by Samsung Electronics. Telekom disclosed no valuation for its tranche, roughly 3.3% of the round. Qualification: the round closed in September; this is the Telekom tranche and partnership.

Google Opens Genie 4 World Generation to Gemini API Developers

Google made Genie 4 available through the Gemini API and Google AI Studio on November 19, giving developers interactive 720p worlds at 24 frames per second with synchronized native audio, driven by keyboard controls and live text prompts. Standard pricing is $1.50 per million input tokens and $17.50 per million video output tokens, roughly $6.05 per generated minute. It requires a paid-tier key, and batch, Flex and Priority inference are unsupported. Every output carries SynthID watermarking.

House Committees Say Chinese Military Procurement Specified DeepSeek and Qwen

The House Select Committee on China and the House Homeland Security Committee released a joint report dated November 20 finding that Chinese military procurement specified DeepSeek for a July 2026 cyber range supporting "intelligent attack," and that other records show command systems built on Qwen and DeepSeek plus an internet-isolated project naming DeepSeek, Alibaba, Moonshot and MiniMax. Qualification: the findings rest on procurement records the committees cite, not independent audits, and China rejected the espionage allegations as fabrication.

Signal Blocks Screenshots on Android and iOS to Keep AI Agents Out of Chats

Signal announced on November 21 that it restricts screenshots inside its app by default on Android and iOS, extending a protection it already applied to Windows. President Meredith Whittaker wrote that OS-level AI agents scanning on-screen data undermine Signal's ability to guarantee privacy once that data is captured and stored. Users can disable the setting. Qualification: Whittaker called the mitigation incomplete and said it does not address other ways agents could reach application data.
