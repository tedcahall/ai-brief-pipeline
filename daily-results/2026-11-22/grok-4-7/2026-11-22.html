🌐 TOP STORIES

METR Finds Frontier AI Time Horizons Doubling Roughly Every 3.6 Months

METR's November 20 update evaluates public models through Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra on tasks relevant to AI research and development. On its combined TH1.1 and TH2 suites, METR estimates that frontier time horizons are doubling roughly every 3.6 months. The group says public evidence is still not enough to rule out drastic AI R&D acceleration in the next couple of years, and that more realistic tasks are harder to measure.

House Panels Say PLA Sought DeepSeek and Qwen for Cyber Drills

The House Select Committee on China and the Homeland Security Committee released a joint report on November 20. The chairs say Chinese open-weight models are already used inside American companies because they are inexpensive and competitive. The report cites PLA records that required DeepSeek for a cyber-training range and sought DeepSeek, Qwen, Moonshot, and MiniMax for military agents on an offline network.

AlphaProof Nexus Finds a Machine-Checked No-Go for Photonic GHZ States

A preprint submitted on November 19 by Mario Krenn reports a machine-checked proof that a class of high-dimensional photonic GHZ states cannot be built from probabilistic pair sources and linear optics. The abstract says the proof was discovered with AlphaProof Nexus and then given a physical interpretation. It is a no-go theorem for even photon counts of at least 4 when the dimension is at least that count.

Gemma 4 Researchers Report Drafting Speedups of Up to 3.1x

A November 19 preprint describes G-MTP, a multi-token prediction drafter tested on the Gemma 4 family. The authors report server-side speedups of up to 3.1 times, up to 2.3 times on an H100 for smaller models, and up to 2.2 times on a smartphone GPU. They also describe fine-tuning recipes that use up to 1.83 times fewer target forward passes than tuning the backbone alone. These figures are the authors' measurements.

Musk Says Tesla AI4 Generates Real-Time Video on Half an Accelerator

Elon Musk wrote on November 21 that Tesla AI4 was producing real-time AI video, with no game engine, using half of one TRIP accelerator. The post quotes a Tesla AI clip and does not include an independent measurement or a paper. It is a company demonstration claim from the observed post, not a verified benchmark, and it falls inside the saved collection window.
