🌐 TOP STORIES

Lean and Mathlib Communities Certify Alpöge's Riemann Hypothesis Proof

New developments: the Lean and Mathlib communities, endorsed by the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative, issued a joint statement on November 21 certifying that both the statement and the proof in Levent Alpöge's 17-million-line Lean library are correct. Dozens of people reproduced the compilation, two independent tools confirmed the theorem matches Mathlib's Riemann Hypothesis statement, and several proof checkers verified the exported proof. The statement did not describe Claude's role.

METR Finds AI R&D Capabilities Improving Rapidly and Harder to Measure

METR published a capability update covering public models through Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra. It reports that models usually match or outperform skilled humans on its coding and optimization tasks, perform worse on tasks that are costlier to turn into RL training environments, and show worse judgment than human experts where clear empirical feedback is absent. METR says current public evidence cannot rule out drastic AI R&D acceleration within the next couple of years.

OpenRouter Warns MiniMax Prompts May Have Been Read by a Third Party

OpenRouter emailed customers that prompts sent to MiniMax models between July and October may have been read, after a researcher's planted test URLs were accessed 40 to 130 hours later, according to Ars Technica and a Hacker News post quoting the email. Ars reported that Dragos researcher HD Moore seeded nearly two million prompts with canary secrets, finding three compromised hosting providers and resellers logging prompts. OpenRouter removed a Zero Data Retention label from MiniMax.

Fortune: Data Centers Full of U.S. Chips Are Serving Chinese AI Models

Fortune reported that U.S.-owned data centers filled with U.S. chips rent capacity to inference providers such as Together AI, Baseten and Fireworks, which serve Chinese open-weight models to global users, with DeepSeek's latest model offered by at least 16 providers headquartered outside China. The piece follows a Commerce Department rule published November 19 restricting remote access to advanced chips, and notes Moonshot seeks revenue sharing from large Kimi K3 deployments.

Nasdaq Posts Worst Week Since 2020 as AI Trade Sells Off

New developments: NPR reported that the Nasdaq fell 10% for the week, its worst performance since March 2020, while the S&P 500 and Dow entered corrections at Friday's close. Anthropic, which gained 17% in its Thursday debut, slumped more than 8% on Friday to close near its IPO price. NPR cited higher interest rates, Democratic pledges of tougher AI regulation after the midterms, and leadership turmoil at Nvidia following Jensen Huang's resignation.
