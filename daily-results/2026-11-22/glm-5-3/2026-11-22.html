🌐 TOP STORIES

Elon Musk Warns AI Industry Faces Severe Cash Shortage in 2027

Musk said Nov. 21 that delays between paying for AI compute and earning revenue from it could require hundreds of billions of dollars in working capital in 2027, calling the coming cash shortage severe. He added that getting massive compute turned on, cooled and running reliably is extremely hard. Multiple outlets echoed the warning as debt markets tighten for providers without real revenue.

Fortune: U.S. Data Centers Full of American Chips Are Serving Chinese AI Models

Fortune reported that U.S.-based inference providers including Together AI, Baseten and Fireworks serve Chinese AI models on infrastructure powered by American chips. The report says the same infrastructure that secures a U.S. advantage in chips and cloud gives Chinese models a distribution platform, complicating export-control policy goals.

OpenAI Pushes GPT-Rosalind Agents for Neurodegenerative Disease Research

OpenAI, PerturbAI and Scripps Research ran nearly 2,000 GPT-Rosalind agents in parallel over a 7.7-million-cell mouse brain CRISPR atlas, completing the computational pass in 32 minutes. OpenAI says the system surfaced a new lead: restoring cholesterol transport may protect neurons from degeneration, tied to DCTN1 and Perry syndrome.

New developments: SenseTime Ships Official Open-Weights SenseNova Loop-9B

New developments: SenseTime moved Loop-9B from October's preview to an official open-weights release. The 9B-parameter multimodal model reuses layers for deeper computation and reports benchmark gains over the Preview, with NL2Repo up from 19.91 to 41.36 and GAIA from 37.86 to 69.90. Day-0 4-bit AWQ weights need about 15 GiB of GPU memory at 256K context.

NYT: AI Will Bring More Tech Disruptions Beyond Current Outages

The New York Times argues that recent tech outages are only the beginning of AI-driven disruptions. It cites the founder of PWN AI, who used AI to meticulously probe a widespread bug, as evidence that AI tools now let small actors find and potentially exploit flaws at scale, raising systemic reliability and security concerns.
