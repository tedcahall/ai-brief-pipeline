Lean and Mathlib Communities Certify Claude-Assisted Riemann Hypothesis Proof

New developments: Following Levent Alpöge's November 20 release of a 17-million-line Lean library, the Lean and Mathlib communities, endorsed by the Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative, certified that both the theorem statement and proof are valid. Dozens of contributors compiled the codebase, verified Mathlib statement equivalence with comparator and con-leche, and ran independent proof checkers.

U.S. Inference Providers Serve Chinese Open-Weight AI Models on American Chips

A Fortune analysis reports that U.S. cloud and inference providers including Together AI, Baseten, Fireworks, and Amazon Bedrock are serving Chinese open-weight models on American-designed chips. Despite a November 19 Commerce Department rule closing remote-access loopholes, at least 16 non-Chinese providers host DeepSeek's latest model, while Moonshot AI seeks 30% revenue-sharing from U.S. clouds for Kimi K3.

Elon Musk Warns AI Infrastructure Delays Could Trigger Billions in Cash Crunch

Elon Musk warned on X that turning on, cooling, and stabilizing massive AI compute clusters could trigger a major working-capital crunch next year. Responding to researcher François Chollet, who cited tight HBM supply through 2029 and 70% to 85% inference margins, Musk said even a few months of deployment delays would require hundreds of billions in extra capital.

U.S. Lawmakers Push for Tougher AI Chip Export Rules After Commerce Loophole Crackdown

Al Jazeera reports that U.S. lawmakers are pressing for stricter semiconductor safeguards after the Commerce Department moved to restrict remote access to AI chips in countries including Singapore, Malaysia, and Brazil. Representative Pramila Jayapal reiterated her federal AI charter proposal, while House Judiciary members opened a probe into whether Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick blocked earlier smuggling curbs.

U.S. GAO Reports No AI Chatbot Has FDA Authorization for Mental Health Care

A November U.S. Government Accountability Office spotlight reports that nearly one-fifth of Americans aged 12 to 21 used an AI chatbot for mental health advice in 2025, even though the FDA has not authorized any chatbot for mental health care. The GAO warns that sycophancy bias and ineffective guardrails risk reinforcing delusions or self-harm.
