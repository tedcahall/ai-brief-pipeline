🌐 TOP STORIES

Lean and Mathlib Communities Verify Claude-Assisted Riemann Hypothesis Proof

The Lean and Mathlib communities certified Levent Alpöge’s Claude-assisted Riemann Hypothesis proof after independent compilation, comparator checks and multiple proof checkers. Their joint statement confirms both the formal statement and proof, marking substantive verification beyond the initial claim while preserving Alpöge’s central human role.

Google Researchers Report Up to 3.1x Faster Gemma 4 Inference

Google researchers introduced G-MTP, a shared drafting head using target activations, zero-copy KV-cache cross-attention and clustered vocabulary projection. They report up to 3.1x faster Gemma 4 inference on servers and 2.2x on a smartphone GPU, with domain-adaptation recipes requiring fewer target forward passes.

Pro2 Agent Reaches 76% Medal Rate on Full MLE-Bench

Pro2 Agent represents blocking problems and candidate fixes in an explicit Problem State rather than treating the codebase as its only state. The paper reports a 70.7% medal rate at 24 hours and 76.0% at 48 hours on the complete MLE-Bench, alongside reusable research notes.

GPT-6.2 Astra Improves Coding but Falls Back on Chat in Agent Arena

Arena ranked GPT-6.2 Astra sixth overall and third in coding, reporting a 50-point coding gain and 54-point chat decline versus GPT-6 Astra. Its writing and translation success rate also fell from 91.2% to 85.9%, showing mixed real-world trade-offs despite coding gains.

GAO Finds No FDA-Authorized AI Chatbots for Mental Health

The US Government Accountability Office reported that no AI chatbot had FDA marketing authorization for mental-health care as of November 2026. It flagged uncertain efficacy, sycophancy, privacy, guardrail and regulatory gaps, while acknowledging that purpose-built tools could complement professionals and improve access.
