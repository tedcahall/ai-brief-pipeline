🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic files S-1 with the SEC for proposed Nasdaq IPO

Anthropic, PBC filed a Form S-1 registration statement with the SEC on October 26 for a proposed IPO of its Class A common stock, intending to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol "ANT". Share count and price range are undetermined, the registration is not yet effective, and the filing states proceeds may fund acquisitions and investments. It is a filing, not a completed offering.

OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Terra with cheaper, faster computer use

GPT-6.1 Terra is available to all Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and through the API as gpt-6.1-terra. OpenAI says it substantially outperforms GPT-5.6 Terra on Agents' Last Exam, matches GPT-6 Astra at one-tenth the price per token, finishes tasks over 3x faster than Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol, and cuts standard API prices 50%. Sol and Astra remain its deepest-reasoning models.

OpenAI announces three AI-assisted scientific discoveries for expert review

OpenAI announced three new discoveries in mathematics, computer science and physics, which third-party reporting ties to a claimed disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis, a proof related to the Kakeya conjectures, and an existence proof for four-dimensional quantum field theories including QCD. OpenAI says it is submitting the results to academic communities for judgment; the claims are lab-announced, not independently validated.

Cloudflare and LMSYS disclose cross-request KV cache exposure in SGLang deployments

Cloudflare notified LMSYS on September 9 of cross-request KV cache exposure in its GLM-5.3 deployment on Workers AI, traced to SGLang's request-rejection path in disaggregated serving, where prefill and decode admission queues checked whether a request was finished rather than rejected. The October 26 coordinated disclosure advises disaggregated SGLang operators to update to v0.5.22 or later; only deployments with KV pools smaller than model context are affected.

MiniMax M3.1 Flash runs locally through Unsloth 2-bit Dynamic GGUFs

MiniMax says Unsloth's Dynamic GGUFs bring its open-weights 428B-parameter M3.1 Flash model to a 143.9GB 2-bit footprint, with 91.5% top-1 token agreement in Unsloth's evaluation across seven domains, low enough for self-hosting on 150GB of RAM through Unsloth Desktop and llama.cpp, with an OpenAI-compatible API. The quantization figures are community-evaluated, not independently verified.
