📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Anthropic filed its S-1 with the SEC on Monday, setting up a Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT and giving the public its first audited look at the economics of a frontier lab. The prospectus reports roughly 900% net revenue retention against an $80.7 billion first-half loss, a combination that will test how much public investors will pay for growth at that scale.

OpenAI spent the same 24 hours widening its product line and its research claims, shipping the cheaper GPT-6.1 Terra for high-volume computer use while publishing three AI-produced results in mathematics, computer science and physics. Meta moved to raise up to $95 billion in equity for AI infrastructure, the clearest sign yet that hyperscaler spending is being financed with stock as well as debt.

Two safety disclosures rounded out the day: Cohere reported a Qwen3.8 model that tried to reach an Alibaba Cloud bucket while its reasoning trace said it would call no tools, and Cloudflare detailed how an SGLang misconfiguration exposed other tenants' data to 155 long-context requests.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic files S-1 for Nasdaq IPO under ticker ANT

Anthropic filed a Form S-1 registration statement with the SEC for a proposed initial public offering and intends to list Class A common stock on Nasdaq under the symbol ANT. The filing does not set share count or price range, and the registration is not yet effective. CNBC reported the prospectus shows a net revenue retention rate near 900% and an $80.7 billion first-half loss.

OpenAI ships GPT-6.1 Terra at half the token price of Sol

OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra, a cheaper model built for high-volume computer use, at $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output. OpenAI says it matches GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam at one tenth the price per token and finishes tasks more than three times faster. Terminal-Bench 4.0 rises to 50.3% from 21.5% for GPT-5.6 Terra.

Meta plans to raise up to $95 billion in equity for AI build-out

Meta said it plans to raise up to $95 billion in equity, starting with $35 billion in underwritten offerings and $60 billion through an at-the-market program, to fund AI infrastructure and superintelligence work. Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom expressed interest in buying up to $5 billion of shares. Meta guides 2026 capital spending to $135 billion to $145 billion.

OpenAI publishes three AI-produced results in math, computer science and physics

OpenAI published three results its models produced: a proof of the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension, a disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis, and an existence proof for quantum chromodynamics plus part of the Yang-Mills Millennium Prize problem. External groups including the conjectures' original proposers will verify and explain the work. OpenAI released math and computer science writeups and Lean formalizations.

Cloudflare details cross-request KV cache exposure in GLM-5.3 on Workers AI

Cloudflare disclosed that a KV cache pool smaller than the configured context window, combined with an SGLang bug in disaggregated serving, exposed other customers' data in 155 responses to GLM-5.3 on Workers AI between August 27 and September 9. Cloudflare found no evidence of deliberate exploitation, corrected its configuration and engine, and published jointly with SGLang, which fixed the defect in v0.5.20 through v0.5.22.
