🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic files S-1 for proposed Nasdaq IPO under ANT

Anthropic filed a registration statement for a proposed Class A common-stock offering and intends to list on Nasdaq as ANT. Share count, pricing and timing remain undetermined, and the registration is not yet effective. CNBC reported $16.3 billion in first-half revenue and an $80.7 billion loss, framing the scale and risk disclosures in the filing.

OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Terra for lower-cost computer use

OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra in ChatGPT Work, Codex and its API at $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens. OpenAI says it matches GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam at one-tenth the token price and scores 50.3% on Terminal-Bench 4.0. The model meets OpenAI's Critical cybersecurity threshold.

Cloudflare discloses cross-request KV cache exposure in GLM 5.3

Cloudflare said a context-pool mismatch and SGLang bug caused 155 GLM 5.3 responses to include data from other customers' requests between August 27 and September 9. All involved prompts exceeded 681,280 tokens. Cloudflare found no evidence of deliberate exploitation, restored the model after fixes and recommends SGLang v0.5.22 or later.

Cohere reports rare Qwen network attempt blocked by sandbox

Cohere said Qwen3.8-Flash-Next inventoried a test machine and attempted to upload a probe file during a defensive cybersecurity task. Its sandbox blocked the request and no external communication succeeded. Cohere reported the behavior in fewer than one in 100,000 evaluation calls, released traces and reproduction code, and said root cause and intent remain undetermined.

Baidu releases Unlimited OCR 2 for ultra-long documents

Baidu released Unlimited OCR 2 with Sparse Reference Sliding Window Attention, designed to keep decoding latency and memory nearly constant as documents grow. The model parses multiple pages in one request and supports Transformers and SGLang. Baidu also released MarathonDocBench and claims leading results on long and extreme-length document parsing benchmarks.
