🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic files for proposed IPO under Nasdaq symbol ANT

Anthropic filed an S-1 registration statement for a proposed initial public offering of Class A shares and intends to list on Nasdaq as ANT. The company says the share count and price range remain undetermined, and the registration statement is not yet effective. This is a filing, not a completed IPO or an offer to sell shares.

OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Terra with lower-cost computer use

OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API, with standard prices of $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens. OpenAI reports Astra-level performance on Agents’ Last Exam at one-tenth the token price, with tasks finishing over three times faster. Those comparisons are benchmark-specific vendor results, not a claim of universal parity.

OpenAI opens scientific claims to academic scrutiny

OpenAI disclosed AI-generated results in harmonic analysis, computational complexity and quantum field theory, describing the manuscripts as raw research data. It released mathematical and computer-science artifacts while external groups examine the work; physics materials remain with an expert collaboration. The company reports Lean formalizations, but independent interpretation and verification are ongoing. These announcements should not be read as settled scientific consensus.

Meta outlines initial $95 billion equity plan for AI expansion

Meta’s initial announcement proposed $35 billion in underwritten equity offerings and a subsequent $60 billion at-the-market program to fund AI infrastructure and personal superintelligence. Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom expressed interest in buying up to $5 billion collectively. These figures describe the initial financing plan, not completed proceeds; the later program depends on future issuance of non-voting Class C shares.

Cloudflare discloses resolved cross-request AI cache exposure

Cloudflare disclosed that a configuration mismatch and SGLang bug exposed other customers’ request data in 155 GLM-5.3 responses involving very long prompts. The exposure ran from August 27 until remediation on September 9; the coordinated report is new. Cloudflare says no other Workers AI model was affected and it found no deliberate exploitation. Operators are urged to update SGLang to version 0.5.22 or later.
