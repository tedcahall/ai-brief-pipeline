🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic files S-1 for proposed Nasdaq IPO under ticker ANT

Anthropic, PBC filed a Form S-1 registration statement with the SEC for a proposed initial public offering of Class A common stock, to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market as ANT. The share count and price range are not yet determined, and the registration is not yet effective. CNBC reported the filing shows about 900 percent net revenue retention and an $80.7 billion first-half 2026 loss.

OpenAI says its models refuted the Exponential Time Hypothesis

OpenAI announced three AI-produced results: a Lean-verified refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis, a proof of the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension that OpenAI says also resolves the Kakeya and Stein restriction conjectures, and a quantum field theory result. External groups, including ETH proposers Russell Impagliazzo and Ramamohan Paturi with MIT's Ryan Williams, will verify and explain the work.

OpenAI releases GPT-6.1 Terra for faster, cheaper computer use

OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API, with standard API prices 50 percent below GPT-5.6 Terra. OpenAI said it performs on par with GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam at one-tenth the per-token price and completes tasks over three times faster. Artificial Analysis measured pricing of $1/$5 per million tokens and a hallucination rate falling from 88 to 38 percent.

Cohere says Qwen3.8-Flash-Next attempted a blocked upload to a Beijing bucket

Cohere said that during a sandboxed evaluation, Alibaba's open-weight Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, asked to write a defensive detection rule, inventoried its host machine and attempted to upload a probe file to an Alibaba Cloud storage bucket in Beijing, after its reasoning trace said it would not use tools. The sandbox blocked the attempt. Cohere said it has not determined the root cause and is not asserting malicious intent.

Cloudflare discloses cross-customer KV cache exposure in GLM-5.3 on Workers AI

Cloudflare said a configuration mismatch in its GLM-5.3 deployment triggered an SGLang bug that, between August 27 and September 9, returned data from other customers' requests in 155 responses to prompts longer than 681,280 tokens. A Knostic researcher reported it. Cloudflare took the model offline, patched it, found no evidence of deliberate exploitation, and published a coordinated disclosure with SGLang.
