🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Enters Final IPO Week Seeking Up to $100 Billion

JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon interviewed Dario Amodei on Friday with less than a week before Anthropic plans to list, The New York Times reported. Anthropic has disclosed it aims to raise up to $100 billion at a valuation of up to $1.4 trillion. Twenty-two banks led by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are running the roadshow, and seven tech companies have committed $26 billion after listing.

Thinking Machines Details 4-Bit Training Behind Open-Weight Inkling 2 Small

Thinking Machines published its end-to-end 4-bit training recipe, which uses Nvidia's NVFP4 format from the start of pretraining through the final reinforcement learning rollout on GB300 hardware. The lab says Inkling 2 Small was pretrained in 68 hours and that the recipe delivers an 8% pretraining speedup and a 20% RL speedup over full MXFP8 training. The model's weights are now on Hugging Face.

OpenAI Disbands Its Standalone Robotics Team

OpenAI confirmed to Fortune that it has dissolved its standalone robotics team. Robotics researchers now report to Chief Research Officer Mark Chen, while applied robotics and hardware staff moved to the Applied organization under President Greg Brockman. A spokesperson said robotics remains core to OpenAI's long-term vision, and Fortune reports researchers have seen surprising results connecting GPT-6 Astra directly to robots.

Claude Helps Decode 663 Theorems in Kurt Gödel's Notebooks

Helsinki logician Jan von Plato used Claude to digest 1,009 pages of Kurt Gödel's 1940s shorthand workbooks, work that had progressed about a page a week, The Guardian reported. Claude extracted 663 theorems Gödel had proved, sketched or outlined. In the Coq proof assistant, about 400 were verified as stated, about 200 needed repair and about 50 were false.

Cloudflare Warns AI Pentesting Orchestrators Are Proliferating

Cloudflare said more than 50 AI-orchestrated penetration-testing framework families attempted to scan its customers over three days, from thousands of IP addresses. Since ARTEX's creator took the open-source tool down on October 8, more than 12,000 related repositories have appeared, and daily requests from such tools have grown 30x since October 1. Cloudflare recommends a six-pillar defense it calls the Modern Security Model.
