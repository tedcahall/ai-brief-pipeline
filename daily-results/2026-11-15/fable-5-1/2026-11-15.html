🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Disbands Its Standalone Robotics Team as General Models Start Running Robots

OpenAI confirmed to Fortune that it has disbanded its standalone robotics team, moving robotics researchers under Chief Research Officer Mark Chen and the applied robotics and hardware teams into the Applied organization under President Greg Brockman. A spokesperson said general-purpose frontier models, including GPT-6 Astra, have proven directly applicable to robotics, making a dedicated unit unnecessary. OpenAI says it still plans its own humanoid robot, with a first public demonstration targeted for 2027.

Research Claims China's Top Open-Weight Models Carry Export-Banned Blackwell Training Fingerprints

Will DePue of the nonprofit Unemployment Labs analyzed open-weight checkpoints from DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax and Ant Group and reported training fingerprints consistent with Nvidia Blackwell GPUs, which are barred from export to China, TechCrunch reported. The analysis does not establish where the chips sat or that export rules were broken; offshore data centers in Malaysia are one possibility. Bloomberg separately reported banned chips at a Shanghai data center linked to Megaspeed's Alice Huang.

Amodei Pitches Investors on Safety and Culture Ahead of Anthropic's Thursday Debut

New developments: with shares expected to begin trading Thursday under the ticker ANT, Dario Amodei told JPMorgan Chase chief Jamie Dimon on Friday that Anthropic is "very paranoid" about AI safety and chose enterprise customers because that focus is synergistic with safety, AFP reported. The $70 to $78 range would raise up to $64.4 billion and value the company at about $1.4 trillion. Amodei said every hire passes a culture interview.

Cloudflare Reports Thirtyfold Surge in Traffic From Autonomous AI Pentest Frameworks

Cloudflare says daily requests from AI penetration-testing and exploitation tools have grown 30x since Oct. 1, with more than 50 framework families scanning its customers in the past three days and thousands of IP addresses running them. The open-source ARTEX framework was taken down by its creator on Oct. 8, but Cloudflare counts over 12,000 repositories and several hundred forks carrying its lineage, and it recommends a six-pillar defensive model.

Goldman Sachs Tallies $687 Billion of AI-Linked Debt Issuance This Year

A Goldman Sachs report estimates AI-related debt issuance across global syndicated credit markets at $687 billion year to date as of Nov. 3, with five hyperscalers accounting for 43 percent, The Economic Times reported. Goldman also counts $1.7 trillion in hyperscaler lease commitments, about three quarters not yet started, plus $1.9 trillion in other unconditional purchase commitments, and calls market saturation the most binding constraint on further AI borrowing.
