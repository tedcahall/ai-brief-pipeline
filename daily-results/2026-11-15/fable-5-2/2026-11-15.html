🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Disbands Stand-Alone Robotics Team as General-Purpose Models Start Running Robots

OpenAI confirmed to Fortune that it has disbanded its stand-alone robotics team. Robotics researchers now sit in the main research organization under Chief Research Officer Mark Chen, while applied robotics and hardware staff moved to the Applied organization under President Greg Brockman. A spokesperson said general-purpose frontier models including GPT-6 Astra have shown they can be applied directly to robotics, and that robotics remains core to OpenAI's long-term vision.

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Small Weights for Download

New developments: Thinking Machines Lab updated its Inkling 2 Small announcement on Nov 12 to say the model's weights are now downloadable on Hugging Face, following an initial closed-weight launch on its Tinker platform. The company describes a mixture-of-experts model with 594B total and 17B active parameters that scores 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Hosted APIs are listed at NVIDIA, Fireworks, Together, Baseten, Nebius, Modal and Cloudflare.

Weight Forensics Ties Leading Chinese Open Models to Export-Banned Nvidia Blackwell Chips

Will DePue, a former OpenAI researcher who leads the nonprofit Unemployment Labs, analyzed open weights from DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax and Ant Group and reported evidence that models including Kimi K3 and DeepSeek V4 were trained on Nvidia Blackwell GPUs, which are barred from export to China, TechCrunch reports. The article notes this does not prove export laws were violated, since the chips could sit in offshore data centers.

Anthropic Takes IPO Pitch to Wall Street With 22 Banks Ahead of Pricing

New developments: JPMorgan chief Jamie Dimon interviewed CEO Dario Amodei on Friday during Anthropic's IPO roadshow, less than a week before the planned share sale, the New York Times reports. Anthropic has lined up 22 banks led by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley and aims to raise up to $100 billion at a valuation of up to $1.4 trillion. Seven tech companies including Broadcom, Nvidia and Microsoft agreed to invest $26 billion after listing.

Nvidia's Role as AI Financier Faces Scrutiny After Huang's Exit

New developments: Goldman Sachs analyst James Schneider wrote that Nvidia may find it harder to negotiate new multi-party ecosystem investments at its previous scale after Jensen Huang's departure, CNBC reports. Nvidia had $36 billion in off-balance-sheet AI cloud backstops for neoclouds as of its July quarterly filing, and CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. CNBC said the exit has not produced a broad shock to the credit market.
