🌐 TOP STORIES

Goldman Sachs Estimates AI-Related Debt Hits $687 Billion as Hyperscaler Commitments Swell

Goldman Sachs reported that global AI-related syndicated debt issuance reached $687 billion year-to-date through November 3, with five hyperscalers accounting for 43 percent, or $296 billion, above its $250 billion full-year forecast. The bank also tracked $1.7 trillion in hyperscaler lease obligations and $1.9 trillion in unconditional purchase commitments, warning alongside S&P that market saturation and 2028 monetization milestones will pressure credit.

Weight Forensics Link Six Chinese Frontier Models to Nvidia Blackwell GPUs

Unemployment Labs, led by former OpenAI researcher Will DePue, analyzed published model weights and found numerical fingerprints matching export-restricted Nvidia Blackwell GPUs in six Chinese models released since April 2026, including DeepSeek-V4-Flash, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 and K3.1-Pro, MiniMax-M3, and Ant Group's Ling-3.1-flash. The researchers noted the method indicates Blackwell hardware access without establishing chip location or legal violations.

Fed Vice Chair Philip Jefferson Warns AI Infrastructure Boom Adds to Inflation Pressures

Speaking at UC San Diego on November 13, Federal Reserve Vice Chair Philip Jefferson warned that massive data center and AI infrastructure spending is outstripping memory and logic chip supply, pushing up prices for technology components and consumer goods while core PCE inflation stood at 3.1 percent in September. Jefferson said AI-driven productivity gains should eventually ease price pressures, though timing remains uncertain.

Perplexity Releases Open-Weight Decider v1.3 Models With Text-Span Extraction

Perplexity released open-weight Decider v1.3 (27B) and distilled Decider Flash v1.3 under an Apache license on Hugging Face, OpenRouter, and its Decisions API. Designed for classification, routing, and scoring in a single forward pass, the v1.3 models add bidirectional attention and a locate feature for exact character-span extraction, scoring 70 and 64 on Artificial Analysis's Decision Index v2.1.

Former OpenAI Board Member Helen Toner Says AI Safety Cannot Rely on Trusting CEOs

In a November 14 Decoder interview with The Verge, Georgetown CSET executive director and former OpenAI board member Helen Toner argued that voluntary accords and board-level governance cannot withstand commercial pressures at frontier labs, citing recent testing-environment breakouts and Anthropic's upcoming IPO. Toner added that bipartisan voter and policymaker concern over AI risk has outlasted heavy industry political spending.
