🌐 TOP STORIES

Perplexity Releases Open-Weight Decider v1.3 and Flash v1.3 Decision Models

Perplexity released Decider v1.3 (27B, built on a Qwen3.8-27B backbone) and Decider Flash v1.3 (4B) under Apache 2.0. Rather than generating conversational prose, the multimodal 256K-context models output calibrated probabilities for choice, boolean, and rubric-scoring tasks, plus a Locate mode for verbatim span extraction with character offsets. On Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1, the models scored 70 (#6) and 64 (#10) at $0.02 and $0.01 per million input tokens.

Weight Forensics Link Six Chinese Frontier Models to Nvidia Blackwell GPUs

A checkpoint-forensics study by former OpenAI researcher Will DePue at Unemployment Labs finds that six open-weight models released since April 2026 by DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax, and Ant Group contain initial-weight numerical patterns matching export-restricted Nvidia Blackwell GPUs. DeepSeek-V4-Flash and MiniMax-M3 match 148-SM B200/B300 chips, while DeepSeek-V4-Pro, Kimi K3, Kimi K3.1-Pro, and Ling-3.1-flash match 152-SM GB200/GB300 hardware. The study notes these fingerprints do not establish chip location or prove export violations.

Artificial Analysis Upgrades Coding Agent Index to v2.1 as Claude Models Tie at 58

Artificial Analysis launched Coding Agent Index v2.1, replacing FrontierCode v1.1 with the 132-task FrontierCode-AA 2.0 benchmark and upgrading Terminal-Bench to v4.1 alongside SWE-Atlas-QnA-AA. Across tested agent harnesses, scores fell by an average of 5 points on the harder evaluation suite. Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 (high and xhigh) and Claude Opus 5.5 (max) shared first place at 58, while per-task costs ranged from $1.51 for GPT-6.1 Sol to $44.22 for Fable 5.2 max.

Cloudflare Warns Over 50 AI Penetration-Testing Framework Families Are Scanning Networks

Cloudflare reported a sharp escalation in autonomous offensive security scans driven by LLM-orchestrated penetration-testing frameworks. Although the creator of the open-source ARTEX agentic framework took down the repository on October 8 citing malicious misuse, more than 12,000 derivative repositories have emerged, and Cloudflare observed over 50 distinct framework families scanning customer networks from thousands of IP addresses over three days. Cloudflare detailed adaptive WAF and behavioral detections to counter the traffic.

AI-Linked Syndicated Debt Reaches $687 Billion Amid $3.6 Trillion in Hyperscaler Commitments

Global AI-related debt issuance across syndicated credit markets reached an estimated $687 billion year-to-date as of November 3, according to a Goldman Sachs report cited by The Economic Times. Only 43% of that borrowing was issued directly by the five largest cloud hyperscalers, which also carry $1.7 trillion in data center lease commitments and $1.9 trillion in unconditional purchase obligations for cloud capacity, servers, networking, and power, including $685 billion due by late 2027.
