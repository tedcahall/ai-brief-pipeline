🌐 TOP STORIES

Thinking Machines Pretrains Inkling 2 Small in 68 Hours With 4-Bit

Thinking Machines Lab says Inkling 2 Small was pretrained in 68 hours on NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 racks, and calls it the largest model it is aware of that was trained end to end with a native 4-bit forward pass. The lab reports an 8 percent pretraining speedup and a 20 percent reinforcement-learning speedup versus full MXFP8 in its largest jobs. NVIDIA and the model card describe a 594 billion parameter model with 17 billion active.

OpenAI Disbands Its Standalone Robotics Team

Fortune reported on November 13 that OpenAI confirmed it had disbanded its standalone robotics team. Robotics researchers now report to chief research officer Mark Chen, and applied robotics and hardware staff report to president Greg Brockman. A spokesperson said robotics remains part of the company's long-term vision, and that general-purpose models, including GPT-6 Astra, can be applied directly to robots.

Perplexity Releases Open-Weight Decider v1.3 Models

Perplexity's November 12 post says it released Decider v1.3 and the smaller Decider Flash v1.3 under an Apache license, with a locate feature that returns matching text and character spans. On 63 classification tasks whose references were relabeled by a frontier model, Perplexity reports 91.6 percent mean accuracy for v1.3, 6.54 points above its production classifiers. That figure is the company's own evaluation, not an independent result.

Claude Helps Surface Theorems in Gödel's Notebooks

The Guardian reported on November 13 that logician Jan von Plato used Claude to digest 1,009 pages of Kurt Gödel's 1940s shorthand notebooks. Von Plato said Claude identified 663 theorems, sketches, or proof ideas; about 400 checked in Coq as stated, about 200 needed repairs, and about 50 were false. He said a reconstructed axiom-of-choice argument passed Coq. A logician who had not seen it said resemblance is not a completed proof.

Cloudflare Says AI Pentest Orchestrators Are Scanning at Scale

Cloudflare reported on November 13 that AI penetration-testing orchestrators are scanning its customers at growing scale. It said more than 50 framework families tried scans in the prior three days, that source addresses had reached the thousands, and that daily requests were 30 times the October 1 level. Cloudflare traced much of the spread to forks of ARTEX after its creator took the project down on October 8.
