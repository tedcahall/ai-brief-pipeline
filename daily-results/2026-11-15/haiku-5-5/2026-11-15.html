🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Seeks Up to $100 Billion Raise at Up to $1.4 Trillion Valuation Before Listing

The New York Times reports Anthropic disclosed plans to raise up to $100 billion at a valuation of up to $1.4 trillion, down from about $2 trillion floated this summer. Individual investors are expected to take about 20 percent of the offering. Broadcom, Nvidia and Microsoft have agreed to invest a combined $26 billion immediately after listing. The $100 billion figure is a target raise, not final pricing.

Nvidia's AI Financing Role Under Scrutiny After Huang Departure

CNBC reports Jensen Huang stepped down as Nvidia CEO on Thursday amid a scandal it describes as espionage and adultery, and CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. Goldman Sachs analyst James Schneider wrote that Nvidia may find it harder to negotiate new multi-party AI investments at its previous scale. Nvidia had $36 billion in off-balance-sheet AI cloud backstops for neoclouds, according to its July filing. The Nasdaq 100 fell 1.2% on Friday.

Researchers Claim Leading Chinese Open-Weight Models Show Blackwell Training Fingerprints

TechCrunch reports that Will DePue of Unemployment Labs analyzed open weights from DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax and Ant Group, finding evidence he says is consistent with training on Blackwell GPUs, which U.S. rules bar from export to China. The article names Kimi K3 and DeepSeek V4 as the most-watched examples. TechCrunch notes this does not prove full training on Nvidia chips or a violation of export law, since chips may have run offshore.

OpenAI Launches Daybreak Red Team and Defense Factory Agents on GPT-6 Cyber

AI Weekly reports OpenAI introduced Daybreak Red Team, now in early access, which deploys agent fleets to pen-test web applications and retest patches, and Daybreak Defense Factory, which scans codebases and prepares merge-ready pull requests on GPT-6 Cyber. AI Weekly says OpenAI's write-up names no design-partner customers and publishes no benchmarks. A separate report citing The Register gives head-to-head figures against Anthropic that this edition could not verify.

Thinking Machines Releases 594B-Parameter Inkling 2 Small Under Apache 2.0

The Hugging Face model card describes Inkling 2 Small as a general-purpose multimodal model that accepts text and image input, released with open weights. AlphaSignal reports a 594 billion parameter mixture-of-experts model with 17 billion active parameters, pretrained natively in NVFP4 on GB300 NVL72 racks in 68 hours over 8.4 trillion tokens. Sources give conflicting release dates, so this edition states no single date.
