🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet So Its Dots Agents Can Pay for Purchases

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, which lets its dots agents complete purchases on a user's behalf using payment tokens issued by American Express, Mastercard and Visa. Users approve each purchase with a biometric check such as Face ID, and neither the agent nor the merchant receives the underlying card number. It starts with US ChatGPT Pro subscribers on eligible US cards, alongside a commerce partner playbook on agent access and risk systems.

Anthropic's IPO Draws About Six Times the Shares on Offer Ahead of Nasdaq Debut

Anthropic's initial public offering drew orders for about six times the shares on offer, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter. The company and its co-founders are seeking up to $64.35 billion at $70 to $78 a share plus a $26 billion private placement anchored by Google, Nvidia and AMD, according to filings. Pricing is expected Nov. 18 with trading on Nasdaq under ANT expected Nov. 19, per CNBC.

Google Opens Antigravity Multi-Agent Tools After Agents Help Prove Open Problems

Google Research made Antigravity's /teamwork multi-agent workflows available to external researchers and developers, with applications for 10,000 academics to get six months of top-tier access. The post reports a full Lean 4 verified proof of the 2013 Courtade-Kumar conjecture with CUHK, USC and CMU researchers, an autonomous end-to-end formalization of the Weil conjectures across 28,800 subagent invocations, and a 30-agent NMR structure workflow reaching 95% top-1 exact match.

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Small Weights Under Apache 2.0

Thinking Machines Lab published Inkling 2 Small's weights on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0 after external safety testing and red-teaming, following an earlier closed Tinker release. The 594B-parameter mixture-of-experts model, with 17B active parameters and a 1M-token context window, was trained in NVFP4 on Nvidia GB300 NVL72 racks in 68 hours, and Thinking Machines reports a score of 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Inference recipes ship for vLLM, SGLang, Unsloth and TokenSpeed.

Forensic Study Finds Blackwell Fingerprints in Six Chinese Frontier Models

Unemployment Labs, a consultancy founded by former OpenAI engineer Will DePue, published a forensic analysis of published model weights concluding that six models released since April 2026 by DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax and Ant Group were initialized on export-controlled Nvidia Blackwell hardware, while earlier models from the same labs match Hopper. The report says the fingerprint supports but does not prove Blackwell also trained them, and does not establish chip location or control violations.
