Jensen Huang Resigns from Nvidia After Spy Scandal — $500B AI Financing Imperiled

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO on November 12 after revelations he had affairs with Alice Huang, a woman U.S. officials believe was spying for China, and senior Nvidia executive Kimberly Powell. The board launched an independent investigation, and Huang agreed to appear before Congress. Democratic lawmakers are investigating the Trump administration's approval of Nvidia chip exports to Chinese-linked companies. Nvidia's $500 billion AI financing initiative — mobilized through MOUs with six Wall Street firms — is faltering, with at least one partner weighing withdrawal.

Nvidia's $500B AI Financing Plan Falters After Huang Exit, AI Debt Hits $687B

Nvidia's $500 billion AI financing plan is faltering after Huang's departure, with at least one of six Wall Street partners weighing withdrawal, Seoul Economic Daily and CNBC reported. Goldman Sachs estimates global AI-related debt reached $687 billion year-to-date as of November 3, with major tech companies carrying trillions in additional lease and purchase commitments. Only 43% of the debt was issued directly by the five largest hyperscalers, indicating the ecosystem-wide scale of AI infrastructure borrowing.

Six of Nvidia's Biggest Customers Are Building Their Own Chips

Six of Nvidia's seven largest customers — Google, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, Anthropic, and OpenAI — are developing their own AI chips, SemiAnalysis reported. Only SpaceX among the top seven has not pursued custom silicon. Google deploys TPUs, Amazon runs Trainium and Inferentia, Microsoft has Maia accelerators, Meta produces MTIA, and OpenAI signed a custom deal with Broadcom. The trend has gained urgency after Huang's resignation raised questions about Nvidia's future supply chain stability.

LangChain Raises $300M Series C at $3B Valuation

LangChain raised a $300 million Series C at a $3 billion valuation led by CapitalG, with participation from Singapore's GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP, and Sapphire Ventures. The four-year-old San Francisco company now has more than 400 employees and 7,000-plus organizations using LangSmith, its platform for managing AI agents. LangChain was pre-revenue and pre-commercial product in summer 2023, underscoring the rapid maturation of the AI agent infrastructure market.

Geoffrey Hinton Warns EU Leaders AI Could Outsmart Humans in 18 Months

Nobel laureate Geoffrey Hinton briefed all 27 EU member state leaders at a Dublin summit on November 13, warning that AI could surpass human intelligence within 18 months. Hinton cited Paul Christiano's timeline — Christiano is a new OpenAI board member. Hinton had previously estimated a 10-year timeline but was convinced by Christiano that timelines might be much shorter. His warning was characterized as 'terrifying' and drew comparisons to his earlier, more conservative estimates.
