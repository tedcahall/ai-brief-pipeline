🌐 TOP STORIES

Forensic Analysis Finds Blackwell GPU Fingerprints in Top Chinese Open-Weight Models

Unemployment Labs, a nonprofit led by former OpenAI researcher Will DePue, analyzed published weights from DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax and Ant Group. It reports that six models released since April 2026 carry numerical patterns matching Nvidia Blackwell GPUs, while earlier models match Hopper. The report says it does not establish where the chips were located, how access was obtained, or whether export controls were violated.

Claude Surfaces 663 Theorems in Kurt Gödel's Shorthand Notebooks

Helsinki logician Jan von Plato, who has studied Gödel's 1,009 pages of notebooks since 2017, enlisted Claude in September and received a digest in days. Claude listed 663 theorems Gödel proved, sketched or outlined. Von Plato told the Guardian that in Coq checks about 400 were verified as stated, about 200 were repaired and about 50 were false.

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Small Weights, a 594B Model Pretrained in NVFP4

Thinking Machines Lab says Inkling 2 Small is a Mixture-of-Experts model with 594B total and 17B active parameters, image input and variable effort, scoring 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Its weights became downloadable on Hugging Face on November 12 after the lab planned an Apache 2.0 release following external safety testing. NVIDIA says the model was pretrained in 68 hours using NVFP4 on GB300 NVL72 racks.

Artificial Analysis Upgrades Coding Agent Index as Claude Fable 5.2 and Opus 5.5 Tie at 58

Artificial Analysis released Coding Agent Index v2.1, replacing FrontierCode 1.1 with FrontierCode-AA 2.0 and Terminal-Bench 4.0 with 4.1. It reports Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Opus 5.5 sharing the lead at 58, with Claude Sonnet 5.5 at 56 and Gemini 4 Carbon at 55. Scores fall about 5 points on average versus v1, and cost per task varies about 30x among configurations scoring 48 or higher.

NYT Reports Anthropic's Rivals Have Turned on Amodei Ahead of Expected IPO

The New York Times, citing more than 20 industry sources, many anonymous, reports that Dario Amodei's public warnings about AI risk have strained relations with peers. It describes Amodei refusing to sign a July letter, drafted by Satya Nadella and Jensen Huang, supporting open-weight models; 200 other companies signed. Anthropic is moving toward what the Times calls likely one of the largest IPOs ever, later this month.
