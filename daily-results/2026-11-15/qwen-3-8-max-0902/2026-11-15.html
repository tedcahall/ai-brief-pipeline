🌐 TOP STORIES

Perplexity Open-Sources Decider v1.3 and Decider Flash Decision Models

Perplexity released Decider v1.3 and Decider Flash v1.3, decision models that return calibrated probabilities instead of generated text, and published open weights under Apache 2.0 on Hugging Face on November 13. The 27B and 4B models add a locate capability for evidence extraction, support 256K-token context and image input, and score 70 and 64 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1.

Nvidia's $500 Billion AI Financing Plan Falters After Huang Exit

New developments: after Jensen Huang's November 12 resignation, Nvidia's $500 billion-plus AI financing platform, announced in August with six Wall Street firms, is faltering. Seoul Economic Daily reported at least one partner is weighing withdrawal, and Business Insider reported the plan had already faced senior-level internal resistance. No partner has publicly confirmed an exit.

Claude Helps Unlock Gödel's Lost Notebooks, Surfacing 663 Theorems

Helsinki logician Jan von Plato used Anthropic's Claude on transcriptions of 1,009 pages of Kurt Gödel's 1940s shorthand notebooks, a corpus a research team had decoded at roughly one page a week over nine years. Claude extracted 663 theorem and proof ideas; about 400 verified as stated in the Coq proof checker, about 200 needed repairs and about 50 were false.

Inkling 2 Small NVFP4 Checkpoint Lands on Hugging Face

New developments: after the November 5 launch covered with weights still pending, the NVFP4 checkpoint of Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Small is now downloadable on Hugging Face. The 594B-parameter multimodal mixture-of-experts model, with 17B active parameters, was pretrained end-to-end in native 4-bit NVFP4 on NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 racks over 8.4 trillion tokens in 68 hours.

HubSpot Refounds Around AI Agents as Brian Halligan Returns as CEO

HubSpot's board reappointed co-founder Brian Halligan as CEO effective November 12, replacing Yamini Rangan, and named Karen Burgess as next CFO, calling the move a refounding of the company. CTO Dharmesh Shah said HubSpot is shifting significant resources toward a headless strategy in which AI agents and harnesses such as Claude and ChatGPT consume its capabilities through MCP connectors.
