🌐 TOP STORIES

Perplexity Releases Open-Weight Decider v1.3 Models

Perplexity has released Decider v1.3 and a smaller Flash variant under an Apache license. The decision models classify and score inputs instead of generating free-form answers, and add a locate feature that extracts relevant text spans. The company says both support text and vision tasks and are available through its API and Hugging Face.

Researchers Find Blackwell Fingerprints in Chinese AI Model Weights

Unemployment Labs says numerical patterns in six open models from DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax and Ant Group match NVIDIA Blackwell hardware. The analysis identifies evidence about how weights were initialized; it does not establish where chips were located or whether export controls were violated. TechCrunch independently reported the findings and their limitations.

Coding Agent Index Refresh Changes Its Tests

Artificial Analysis updated its Coding Agent Index with FrontierCode 2.0 and Terminal-Bench 4.1. Its own measurements put Claude Fable 5.2 and Opus 5.5 in a tie at 58 on the revised index. The results evaluate model-and-agent combinations, and scores on changed tests are not directly comparable with those on the previous version.

SatBench Tracks How Quickly AI Benchmarks Saturate

Ethan Mollick introduced SatBench to examine how quickly AI benchmarks reach saturation. He says 63% of 173 benchmarks cited by labs since 2023 have reached his saturation threshold. A separate report notes the analysis distinguishes laboratory-reported results from independent evaluations, a qualification that matters when interpreting the aggregate.

Nvidia Leadership Change Raises Questions About AI Financing

New developments: After Jensen Huang’s departure, CNBC reports that analysts are examining whether NVIDIA will change its approach to financing AI infrastructure. Goldman Sachs expects existing announced commitments to be honored but sees potential difficulty arranging new deals at the same scale. The company’s eventual strategy remains uncertain, according to a CreditSights analyst.
