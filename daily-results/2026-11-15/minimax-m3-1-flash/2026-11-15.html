🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet So Its Agents Can Pay Merchants

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, letting its dots agents complete purchases for users without exposing real card details and requiring a security check on every payment. Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, it opens first to US ChatGPT Pro subscribers. OpenAI says 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week and published a Commerce Partner Playbook for merchants.

Nvidia's Role as AI Financier Comes Into Question After Jensen Huang's Exit

CNBC reports that Nvidia's role as a financier of AI infrastructure is under scrutiny after Jensen Huang stepped down. Goldman Sachs analyst James Schneider said new multi-party ecosystem investments may be harder to negotiate at the same scale, and CreditSights analyst Andy Li expects creative deal-making to moderate. Nvidia had $36 billion in off-balance-sheet AI cloud backstops in its July filing.

Checkpoint Forensics Finds Export-Banned Blackwell GPUs Behind Chinese Frontier Models

Unemployment Labs, led by Will DePue, analyzed the weights of open models from DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax and Ant Group and found fingerprints of Blackwell GPUs in widely watched releases including Kimi K3 and DeepSeek V4. TechCrunch notes Epoch AI estimates about two million advanced chips reached China in 2024 and 2025 through concealment schemes, while offshore datacenters remain a legal grey area.

Claude Extracted 663 Theorems From Godel's Notebooks, About 400 Formally Verified

Jan von Plato of the University of Helsinki says Claude read 1,009 pages of Godel's 1940s shorthand workbooks in days and extracted 663 theorems, about 400 of which held as stated in Coq checks. Human teams had managed roughly one page a week since 2017. Von Plato says the workbooks contain results not known until the 1970s and 1980s and 14 unexploited themes in logic.

Artificial Analysis Refreshes Its Coding Agent Index as Claude Models Tie at the Top

Artificial Analysis released version 2.1 of its Coding Agent Index, upgrading FrontierCode to 2.0 and Terminal-Bench to 4.1. Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Opus 5.5 share the lead at 58, and Kimi Code CLI with Kimi K3.1 Pro leads Terminal-Bench 4.1 at 68 percent. Every configuration tested on both task sets scored lower on the new FrontierCode.
