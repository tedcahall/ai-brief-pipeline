🌐 TOP STORIES

Perplexity Updates Decider Models for Low-Cost AI Decisions

Artificial Analysis reports that Perplexity has released Decider v1.3 and Decider Flash v1.3, scoring 70 and 64 on its Decision Index. Flash improves four points over its predecessor. The evaluator lists text and image input, a 256K context window and Apache 2.0 licensing, with input pricing of $0.02 and $0.01 per million tokens through Perplexity’s API.

Artificial Analysis Refreshes Its Coding Agent Benchmarks

The Coding Agent Index now incorporates FrontierCode-AA 2.0 and Terminal-Bench 4.1, changing both tasks and verifiers. Artificial Analysis reports a shared lead of 58 for selected Claude Fable 5.2 and Opus 5.5 configurations. Scores fall five points on average in the revised index; that reflects a changed measurement, not evidence that the underlying models became worse.

Model-Weight Forensics Finds Blackwell Fingerprints in Chinese AI Models

New developments: TechCrunch’s reporting brings attention to a technical analysis identifying Blackwell initialization fingerprints in six models from DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Ant Group. Unemployment Labs says those traces support, but do not prove, training on the same hardware. The method does not establish chip location, how access was obtained or whether export controls were violated, and its GPU-count estimates remain uncertain.

Cloudflare Reports Rapid Growth in AI-Orchestrated Attacks

Cloudflare says daily requests from tracked AI penetration-testing and exploitation tools have grown thirtyfold since October 1. Its incident responders describe stolen CI/CD tokens being used for lateral movement and automated reconnaissance. These are the vendor’s network observations, not a measured industry-wide attack rate; its recommended defenses include identity-gated access, schema validation, rate limits and tighter credential controls.

HubSpot Plans Simpler Pricing and More Agent-Based Access

In his second public refounding update, HubSpot cofounder Dharmesh Shah says the company is working to simplify pricing and strengthen Breeze and its Claude/ChatGPT MCP connectors. He describes conversational access as an increasingly important gateway to HubSpot’s value. This is stated product direction and work in progress, not a completed pricing rollout or proof of measured customer productivity gains.
