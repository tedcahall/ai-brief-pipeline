🌐 TOP STORIES

Introducing ChatGPT Wallet

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, a payment feature for agents designed to let them pay without access to a user’s actual card details and require a security check for each purchase. The announcement positions the product as infrastructure for agentic commerce, while merchant support and rollout constraints remain practical limits.

Early progress on accelerating drug development with Claude

Anthropic describes research partnerships with six organizations and early applications of Claude across drug development. One Generate Biomedicines design was about 70% shorter than a standard sequence and showed improved measures in company-reported laboratory tests; these are preliminary results, not evidence of clinical benefit.

Export-banned Nvidia chips are behind China’s most powerful AI models, researchers say

TechCrunch reports that analysis by Will DePue of Unemployment Labs found model-weight evidence consistent with leading Chinese AI models having trained on Nvidia Blackwell GPUs restricted from export to China. The evidence does not show that all training used those chips or establish a legal violation; offshore compute is a possible route.

AI-related debt exposure touches USD 687 billion as financing commitments mount: Goldman Sachs

A Nov. 14 ANI report carried by The Tribune attributes an estimate of $687 billion in AI-linked syndicated-credit exposure, as of Nov. 3, to Goldman Sachs. This is reported debt exposure, not total AI investment; lease and purchase commitments are distinct. The underlying Goldman note was not independently located, so the figure remains a secondary-source estimate.

NVIDIA spotlights AI and robotics applications in recycling

NVIDIA said vision AI and robotics are being used to sort clothing, bottles and mixed recyclables, recover reusable materials and divert material from landfills. The company’s Nov. 14 post describes applications through startups but does not provide independent measurement for the aggregate impact claim.
