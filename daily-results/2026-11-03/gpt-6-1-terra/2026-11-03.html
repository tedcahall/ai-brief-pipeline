🌐 TOP STORIES

MIT researchers use GPT-6 Astra to repair Qwen model weights

MIT researchers report that GPT-6 Astra identified a repetition problem in Qwen3.5-0.8B and edited 0.016% of its weights. Under their evaluation settings, MATH-500 accuracy rose from 7.9% to 48.1%. The result is a targeted repair, not proof of recursive self-improvement or a general cost advantage over training.

Oracle files for $20 billion in stock and debt offerings

Oracle filed a preliminary prospectus for $10 billion in common stock alongside a separate $10 billion senior-notes offering. The company cautioned that the concurrent notes offering may not close as described or at all. CNBC reports the fundraising supports an AI and cloud infrastructure buildout; proceeds are for general corporate purposes.

RoboArchitect improves robot policies by rewriting their harness

Researchers report that RoboArchitect uses a coding model to diagnose robot rollouts and revise control code around a frozen policy. In their tests, average success on held-out scenes rose from 9.7% to 43.3%, without changing model weights. The preprint describes research results, not a guarantee of performance in every deployment.

OpenAI introduces LifeVal for everyday AI assistance

OpenAI unveiled LifeVal, an evaluation for practical tasks such as childcare searches, budgets and government paperwork. The company says it used time-use datasets and a survey of 3,995 people to identify tasks where assistance is valued. Its reported examples are company measurements, not an independent comparison of all available assistants.

OpenAI plans security sprint with Cathedral and raises bounties

OpenAI says Cathedral researchers will join its next internal security sprint and use OpenAI models to test the company’s defenses. It is also increasing rewards for critical bug reports by more than fourfold. This is a planned defensive exercise, not an announcement that a new breach has occurred.
