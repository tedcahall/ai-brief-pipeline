🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Releases FlashProve for Provable Model Inference

Anthropic released FlashProve on November 2, an Apache 2.0 research prototype that uses zkSNARK proofs to show a response came from committed model weights without revealing those weights. On Llama 4 Maverick, the lab reports that one H100 generates proofs at about 110 tokens per second. Anthropic frames the release as progress toward verifiable inference for customers, evaluators, and auditors, and as an open prototype rather than a completed system for every frontier model.

MIT Researchers Edit a Small Qwen Model's Weights Directly

MIT CSAIL researchers reported on November 2 that GPT-6 Astra edited Qwen3.5-0.8B directly, changing about 0.016% of its weights without a training loop. In their evaluation, with sampling held fixed, MATH-500 rose from 7.9% to 48.1%, and instruction-following on IFEval rose about threefold. The authors say the edit targeted reasoning loops already flagged on the model card. VentureBeat notes this is a repair of repetition, not proof that new mathematical knowledge was inserted.

OpenAI Introduces LifeVal for Everyday Assistant Tasks

OpenAI published LifeVal, an evaluation of practical assistant work such as childcare, government services, household administration, and personal finances. The task list comes from U.S. and multinational time-use studies plus a survey of 3,995 people. On the public page, current models score 90.4% to 100% on five example workflows, including kindergarten enrollment, flood volunteers, a housing search, remote game-watching, and a diabetes meal plan. OpenAI says the benchmark tracks agentic help, not academic knowledge tests.

Oracle Files a $10 Billion Stock Offer and Matching Notes

Oracle filed a preliminary prospectus dated November 2 to offer $10 billion of common stock and, concurrently, $10 billion of senior notes. The filing says neither deal depends on the other, and proceeds may include capital spending, debt repayment, and investments. CNBC reported the raise follows an investor-day cap of $20 billion in additional capital-markets funding through fiscal 2030, and that shares fell 8.8% on Monday. The prospectus is marked subject to completion.

Microsoft Opens Consultation on a Humanist AI Code of Conduct

Microsoft AI published a draft Humanist AI Code of Conduct for public comment and called it a training manual for MAI models. It says models should not resist shutdown, widen their goals, or hide reasoning from auditors, and it bars weapons of mass harm and child-safety violations. Mustafa Suleyman announced the consultation on November 2. The page says six weeks of feedback; his post said three, so the length is not consistent.
