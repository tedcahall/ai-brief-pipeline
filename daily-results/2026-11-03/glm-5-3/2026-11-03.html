🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI's Pando Cuts Push Latency 80% and Serves Nearly a Petabyte of Git Data a Day

OpenAI detailed Pando, a Git backend built for its monorepo in about 10 weeks after a reported 2,442% surge in commits from engineers and Codex agents. Pando now powers all development in the monorepo, cut push latency by roughly 80%, and serves nearly a petabyte of Git data a day without changing how engineers use Git. Greg Brockman said OpenAI is considering offering it to others.

OpenAI Introduces LifeVal, a Personal-Assistant Benchmark Built From ChatGPT User Feedback

OpenAI introduced LifeVal, a benchmark measuring how well models handle everyday personal tasks such as finding childcare, accessing government services and managing personal finances, shaped by feedback from thousands of ChatGPT users. The company said it has used LifeVal over the past year to measure and improve its models, and AlphaSignal reports it drew on roughly 4,000 user surveys and 100 detailed task authors.

Palantir's US Commercial Revenue Tops Government for the First Time, Up 148% to $984M

Palantir reported Q3 revenue of $2.31 billion, up 96% year over year, with US commercial revenue up 148% to $984 million, surpassing US government revenue for the first time. GAAP net income reached $1.128 billion, up 137%, and the company raised full-year revenue guidance to 91% growth and US commercial revenue guidance to at least 143%.

Oracle Files $20 Billion Stock-and-Bond Raise to Fund Its AI Buildout

Oracle filed to sell $10 billion in common stock and $10 billion in senior notes to help pay for its AI and cloud data center buildout, CNBC reported. Shares fell more than 6% after the filing. Oracle said it does not plan to raise more than $20 billion in additional AI financing through fiscal 2030, targeting revenue above $225 billion by that year.

Anthropic Opens Applications for Paid Residency Research Program

Anthropic opened applications for the Residency, a paid, fixed-term research program for academics, technical founders and staff-level engineers, with no ML background required. Placements last six weeks to six months, and Superpower Daily reports compensation equivalent to $350,000 to $500,000 per year. Applications for this round close November 22, 2026.
