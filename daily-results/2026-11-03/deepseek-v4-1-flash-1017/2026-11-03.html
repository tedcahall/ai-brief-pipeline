🌐 TOP STORIES

Oracle Files $10 Billion Stock and $10 Billion Note Offering to Fund AI Buildout

Oracle filed with the SEC to sell $10 billion in common stock and $10 billion in senior notes to fund its AI and cloud data-center buildout, CNBC reported. Shares fell 8.8% Monday. The company carries $125.34 billion in debt as of August 31 and holds a BBB- S&P rating. Oracle says it expects positive free cash flow in the second half of 2028.

China's NDRC Moves to Regulate AI Across Its Full Lifecycle

China's National Development and Reform Commission is accelerating comprehensive AI legislation covering the full model lifecycle, Bloomberg reported. Wang Ruomeng said the rules would extend from data to computing power. The framework would require developers to disclose safety risks and report incidents, with transition periods and regulatory sandboxes. It would also address job displacement, over-reliance, deepfakes, algorithmic discrimination and privacy leaks.

OpenAI Expands Security Sprint With Cathedral and Quadruples Bug Bounties

OpenAI said it is planning another internal security sprint and will partner with Cathedral Systems, whose researchers will use OpenAI models alongside internal teams to probe defenses. The company is more than quadrupling bug bounty rewards for critical findings, with exceptional reports eligible for up to $172,000 including bonuses. OpenAI cited recent industry incidents and a $1 billion commitment to subsidized access for frontline cyber defenders.

Palantir's US Commercial Revenue Jumps 148% as AI Demand Tops Government

Palantir beat third-quarter estimates with revenue of $2.31 billion, nearly double a year earlier, and net income of $1.13 billion. US commercial revenue rose 148% to $984 million, surpassing US government revenue of $953 million for the first time. The company raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to about $8.53 billion and expects US commercial revenue above $3.56 billion.

OpenAI Built Pando, a New Git Backend for Its Agent-Scale Monorepo

OpenAI detailed Pando, a new Git backend that now powers all development in its monorepo while keeping the standard Git protocol and client. Pando cut median push latency by nearly 5x and serves close to a petabyte of Git data per day. OpenAI attributes the load to Codex-driven parallel work and says Pando scales writes across branches and reuses packfiles for reads.
