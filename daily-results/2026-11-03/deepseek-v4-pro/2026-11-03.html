🌐 TOP STORIES

MIT CSAIL Uses GPT-6 Astra to Edit Qwen Weights, Lifting MATH-500 From 7.9% to 48.1%

MIT CSAIL researchers used GPT-6 Astra to directly edit the open weights of Qwen3.5-0.8B, a technique they call weight-space programming, tracing looping failure directions to two layers and changing about 0.016% of parameters while sampling was held fixed. The intervention lifted the model's MATH-500 score from 7.9% to 48.1%, and GSM8K from 13.8% to 67.1%, without gradient descent. VentureBeat describes the approach as gene editing for neural networks.

OpenAI Partners With Cathedral Systems and More Than Quadruples Critical Bug-Bounty Rewards

OpenAI announced its next internal security sprint in partnership with Cathedral Systems, whose researchers will use OpenAI models alongside OpenAI teams to challenge its defenses, and raised its Security Bug Bounty so exceptional critical findings can earn up to $172,000 including bonuses, more than quadrupling critical rewards. Cathedral Systems is a startup previously credited with breaching Anthropic.

OpenAI Details Pando, a Git Backend That Cuts Push Latency ~80% and Serves a Petabyte Daily

OpenAI Developers detailed Pando, a Git-compatible backend built for its monorepo that cut push latency by about 80% and now serves nearly a petabyte of Git data a day, letting the company grow Git capacity alongside agent usage without changing how engineers and agents use Git. The team also contributed fixes back to Git.

Mistral Large 4 Writes a Quantum-Chemistry Solver From Scratch

Mistral AI demonstrated Mistral Large 4 writing a quantum-chemistry self-consistent field solver from scratch from a short prompt, validating its output and building an interactive 3D view, then independently choosing how to break a molecule and which orbitals to show without further input. The model also understood the chemistry enough to decide the demonstration itself.

Crusoe Starts Building Its Own Medium-Voltage Switchgear, Cutting Lead Times 40%

Crusoe, the builder of the Stargate site in Abilene, Texas, is now manufacturing its own medium-voltage switchgear in-house through Crusoe Industries, which it says cuts its lead time by roughly 40% on power gear that previously carried 12 to 18 month lead times on the AI datacenter critical path. SemiAnalysis frames owning the gear as a way neoclouds reach power faster.
