🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Moves Its Messages API From Python to Rust, With Claude Agents Fixing Differences

Anthropic published an engineering post on migrating its Messages API, the route used by customers and by Claude Code and Cowork, from Python to Rust. It turned the production API into a verifier: shadowed traffic produced records of differences, and coordinating Claude agents grouped and fixed them in sandboxes. Anthropic reports about 70% less CPU per request and about 20% lower median time to first token, with no downtime.

SemiAnalysis Adds Google TPU and Amazon Trainium to InferenceX, Reporting 1.2x to 2.5x Performance per Dollar

SemiAnalysis said Amazon's Trainium and Google's TPU now appear on its InferenceX and AgentX inference benchmarks, and reported 1.2x to 2.5x the performance per dollar of Nvidia GPUs. A day earlier it posted Amazon's first Trainium3 UltraServer results, claiming 2.28x better performance per dollar than GB300 NVL72, B300 and MI355. These are the benchmark operator's own figures and depend on its chosen workloads.

House Members Introduce RSI Safety Act to Track AI Models That Help Build Their Successors

Representatives Josh Gottheimer (D-N.J.) and Nathaniel Moran (R-Texas) introduced the RSI Safety Act on November 24, according to Crypto Briefing. Developers above a compute threshold that spent at least $100 million on training in the prior year would file quarterly reports with a new Commerce Department office on AI's role in data, algorithm design, experimentation and safety evaluation. False reports could draw penalties up to $10 million.

OpenAI Begins Testing Private Inference With a Small Group of Customers

Sam Altman said OpenAI is testing Private Inference, part of its Private Intelligence offering, with a small group of customers. Firstpost reports it uses confidential computing and strict controls to protect prompts and responses while a model processes them, separate from Zero Data Retention, and is listed as an alpha feature with an API preview. Altman added that ZDR with Private Safety Processing allows automated safety reviews without OpenAI personnel seeing the content.

SemiAnalysis GDPx Benchmark Puts OpenAI's Astra Ahead of Claude Fable 5.2

SemiAnalysis published GDPx results for recent models: GPT-6.2 Astra ranks first at 19.3%, Claude Fable 5.2 second at 16.4%, Gemini 4 Carbon fifth at 10.9% and Gemini 4 Neon sixth at 10.1%. It calls Qwen4-Max the highest-scoring Chinese model at 7.44%. SemiAnalysis notes that safety classifiers lower some scores and that this is its own benchmark of 206 tasks across 11 domains.
