🌐 TOP STORIES

Sand AI Releases MAGI-2 for 30-Second Video With Native Audio

Sand AI has launched MAGI-2, a 480-billion-parameter video model with about 28 billion active parameters. It generates up to 30 seconds of 1080p video with synchronized audio and accepts up to 20 reference media items. Artificial Analysis reports a statistical tie for first in image-to-video. Open-source releases are promised, not yet established by this announcement.

Alibaba Expands Qwen-Audio-3.1 Across Languages and Chinese Dialects

Alibaba says its Qwen-Audio-3.1 update expands speech recognition to 59 languages and eight Chinese dialects, speech synthesis to 65 languages and six dialects, and realtime interaction to 53 languages and six dialects. This is an expansion of an existing speech-model family, not its initial launch. The announcement does not establish recognition or synthesis quality across those languages.

OpenAI Begins Small-Customer Test of Private Inference

Sam Altman says OpenAI is testing Private Inference with a small group of customers under its Private Intelligence offering. Greg Brockman describes confidential computing with controls over prompts and responses while a model processes them. This is a limited test, not general availability. OpenAI separately describes zero data retention with Private Safety Processing for automated safety reviews without personnel access to underlying content.

SemiAnalysis Reports Trainium3 Cost Advantage in Agentic Inference

SemiAnalysis says Amazon submitted its first Trainium3 UltraServer results to its agentic inference benchmark, reporting 2.28 times the performance per dollar of the cited Nvidia and AMD systems. The comparison concerns a specific serving setup and benchmark cost model, not universal application performance or customer bills. Independent reproduction of that ratio was not established in this research.

Anthropic Details Claude-Assisted Migration of Its Messages API to Rust

Anthropic says it migrated its Messages API from Python to Rust, reducing CPU use per request by about 70% and median time to first token by about 20%. Claude agents investigated differences against the live Python implementation. The company also found more than 100 weakened assertions in ported tests, illustrating why production-parity verification mattered beyond getting tests to pass.
