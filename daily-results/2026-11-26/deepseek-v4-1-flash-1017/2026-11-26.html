🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Ported Its Messages API to Rust, Cutting CPU Use About 70%

Anthropic said it migrated its Messages API, the endpoint that serves Claude Code, Cowork and external customers, from Python to Rust, reporting roughly 70% less CPU per request and about 20% lower median time to first token without taking the service offline. The company said API traffic grew about 1,800 times from early 2024 to fall 2026. Claude wrote a first version of the core request path in about a week, then used the live Python service as a verifier, with fixes entering the outer loop only after review.

SemiAnalysis Benchmark Puts Amazon Trainium3 at 2.28x GB300 Performance per Dollar

SemiAnalysis said Amazon submitted its first Trainium3 UltraServer results to AgentX, its agentic inference benchmark, showing up to 2.28 times better performance per dollar than Nvidia's GB300 NVL72, B300 and AMD's MI355, which it attributed to a wide expert-parallel design for external customers. The figures come from Amazon-submitted results replayed by a third-party harness, not an independent procurement test. SemiAnalysis published its full Trainium3 deep dive on November 27.

Sand AI Releases 480B-Parameter MAGI-2 Video Model

Beijing-based Sand AI released MAGI-2, a 480-billion-parameter model with about 28 billion active parameters that generates up to 30 seconds of 1080p, 25 fps video with native audio and accepts up to 20 reference images, videos or audio clips. Sand AI reported a 151.8-point Elo gain over its August preview on Artificial Analysis Video Arena v2.1. Artificial Analysis ranked it first in image to video, statistically tied with ByteDance's Seedance 2.5, at $0.08 per second.

Bipartisan RSI Safety Act Would Require Quarterly Reports on AI-Built AI

Representatives Josh Gottheimer and Nathaniel Moran introduced the RSI Safety Act, which would require the largest AI developers to report quarterly on how much of their research is performed by AI systems, covering training inputs, algorithmic and architectural design, experimentation and safety evaluation, and to show whether people can still meaningfully oversee the work. The bill sets disclosure requirements rather than banning automated research, and its sponsors framed it as transparency rather than a brake on innovation.

OpenAI Begins Alpha Testing Private Inference for Business Customers

OpenAI is testing Private Inference with a small group of customers as part of its Private Intelligence offering, Sam Altman said. OpenAI describes the feature as confidential computing plus strict controls designed to protect prompts and responses while a model processes them, aimed at sensitive enterprise work. The company pairs it with Zero Data Retention and Private Safety Processing, which it says enables automated safety review without staff access to underlying content.
