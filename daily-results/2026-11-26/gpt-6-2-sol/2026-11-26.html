🌐 TOP STORIES

Sand AI’s MAGI-2 Ties Seedance 2.5 in Image-to-Video at About One-Seventh the Price

Artificial Analysis put Sand AI’s MAGI-2 in a statistical tie with ByteDance’s Dreamina Seedance 2.5 on its image-to-video benchmark. It listed MAGI-2 at $4.80 per minute of 1080p video with audio, versus $34.12 for Seedance. The Chinese lab released the model on November 24 as a proprietary API. MAGI-2 ranked seventh on the separate text-to-video benchmark.

FCC Authorizes SpaceX to Deploy Up to One Million Orbital Compute Satellites

The FCC authorized SpaceX’s Starmind constellation on November 25, allowing up to one million satellites designed to process data in orbit for use on Earth. The order allows radio links to back up the system’s optical network and support emergency communications. It sets a regulatory ceiling, not a deployment schedule or a commitment to build anywhere near that many satellites.

OpenAI Begins Private Inference Testing With a Small Group of Customers

Sam Altman said OpenAI has begun testing Private Inference with a small group of customers. The previously announced service uses confidential computing to protect prompts and responses while the model processes them, a different protection from limiting what gets retained afterward. OpenAI has not announced general availability for the service.

Anthropic Used Claude Agents to Help Move Its Messages API From Python to Rust

Anthropic says its Rust implementation of the Messages API uses about 70% less CPU per request and cut median time to first token by about 20%. The engineering team used the live Python service as a reference, comparing the two versions on production traffic and dispatching Claude agents to investigate and fix differences. The company says it shipped without taking the API offline.

U.S. House Bill Would Require Frontier Labs to Report How They Automate AI Research

Representatives Josh Gottheimer and Nathaniel Moran introduced the RSI Safety Act on November 24. The proposal would require covered frontier developers to file quarterly disclosures on AI’s role in building and evaluating models, describe how humans can intervene, and flag material changes in automation. The measure was referred to the House Science, Space, and Technology Committee; it is not law.
