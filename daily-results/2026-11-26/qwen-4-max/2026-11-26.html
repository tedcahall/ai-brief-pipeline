🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Tests Private Inference to Keep Prompts Confidential During Processing

OpenAI is testing Private Inference with a small group of customers, Sam Altman said November 25. The offering, part of OpenAI Private Intelligence, combines confidential computing with strict controls designed to protect prompts and responses while a model processes them. OpenAI's business page lists it alongside zero-data-retention protections for eligible workflows, and Greg Brockman confirmed the testing phase.

Amazon Trainium3 UltraServer Claims 2.28x Perf-per-Dollar Over Nvidia GB300 in Agentic Benchmark

Amazon submitted its first Trainium3 UltraServer results to SemiAnalysis's AgentX agentic inference benchmark, showing 2.28x better performance per dollar than Nvidia's GB300 NVL72 and B300 and AMD's MI355, SemiAnalysis reported November 25. The firm credited a wide expert-parallelism setup and noted Anthropic already runs Trainium at scale. Nvidia's newest Vera Rubin systems were not included in the test.

Anthropic Migrated Its Messages API to Rust With Claude Agents as Verifiers

Anthropic says it moved the Messages API, the main path for Claude Code, Cowork and API customers, from Python to Rust without taking the service offline. Traffic grew roughly 1,800x from early 2024 to fall 2026. Using the production API itself as a verifier and dispatching Claude agents to fix differences, the Rust service uses about 70% less CPU per request and cuts median time to first token about 20%.

Sand AI's MAGI-2 Tops Image-to-Video Quality at One-Seventh the Price

Beijing startup Sand AI's 480-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts video model MAGI-2 debuted at number one on Artificial Analysis's image-to-video leaderboard, statistically tied with ByteDance's Dreamina Seedance 2.5, at $0.08 per second of 1080p output, roughly one-seventh the cost. Released November 24, it generates 4-to-30-second clips with synchronized audio in a single pass and accepts up to 20 multimodal references.

Bipartisan RSI Safety Act Would Track How Much AI Research Is Automated

Reps. Josh Gottheimer, D-N.J., and Nathaniel Moran, R-Texas, introduced the RSI Safety Act November 24. It would require the largest AI developers to report quarterly on AI involvement and human oversight across data, algorithmic design, experimentation and safety evaluation, with early warnings for material shifts toward automated research. The bill follows reporting on internal lab doubts about releasing increasingly capable models.
