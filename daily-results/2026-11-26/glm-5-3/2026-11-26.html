🌐 TOP STORIES

Amazon's Trainium3 Leads SemiAnalysis' New AgentX Benchmark on Performance per Dollar

SemiAnalysis reported that Amazon's first Trainium3 UltraServer submission to its new agentic inference benchmark AgentX delivered 2.28x better performance per dollar than NVIDIA's GB300 NVL72 and B300 and AMD's MI355X, using a wide expert-parallelism approach. AgentX is the first industry-standard inference benchmark to span Trainium, TPU, NVIDIA and AMD GPUs and OpenAI's custom Jalapeño ASIC, and is backed by buyers including Microsoft Azure, Amazon, Google, Oracle and Meta.

OpenAI Begins Testing Private Inference Under Its Private Intelligence Offering

Sam Altman said OpenAI is now testing Private Inference, the latest offering under OpenAI Private Intelligence, with a small group of customers. It combines confidential computing with strict, verifiable controls designed to protect prompts and responses while a model processes them, and Zero Data Retention with Private Safety Processing enables automated safety reviews without OpenAI personnel accessing the underlying content.

METR Sees OpenAI's Pretraining Loss Falling About 7x Faster Since Early 2026

New developments: METR's analysis of OpenAI's newly published pretraining loss data finds log-perplexity falling about seven times faster since early 2026 than over 2022 to 2025. METR notes it does not yet see a comparable acceleration in OpenAI benchmark scores such as ECI, says the interpretation is still debated internally, and encourages other developers to publish the same data.

Anthropic Moved Its Messages API From Python to Rust With No Downtime

Anthropic detailed its migration of the Messages API, the endpoint behind Claude API, Claude Code and Cowork traffic, from Python to Rust, with much of the code written by Claude and no Rust developers initially on the team. It reports about 70% less CPU and over 80% less memory per request, a 20% faster median time to first token, and public traffic moved over in September with no downtime, verified by shadowing production traffic to within 0.4% matching differences.

Sand AI's 480B-Parameter MAGI-2 Debuts at No. 1 in Image-to-Video

Chinese lab Sand AI released MAGI-2, a 480B-parameter (28B active) multi-head latent MoE video model generating up to 30 seconds of 1080p, 25 fps audio-video with omni-reference across up to 20 media items. Artificial Analysis scores it number one in image-to-video on AA-Video-I2V v2.1, statistically tied with Dreamina Seedance 2.5 at $4.80 per minute versus $34.12, and it ranks seventh in text-to-video.
