🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Begins Testing Private Inference for Confidential Computing

OpenAI is testing Private Inference, which uses confidential computing to protect business prompts and responses while its models process them, with a small group of customers. CEO Sam Altman said the offering, part of OpenAI Private Intelligence, will make frontier AI useful for more sensitive enterprise work.

SemiAnalysis: Trainium and TPU Beat Nvidia GPUs on Performance per Dollar

SemiAnalysis added AWS Trainium and Google TPU to its InferenceX and AgentX benchmark, reporting the accelerators deliver 1.2x to 2.5x the performance per dollar of Nvidia GPUs. The firm framed the result as a rebuke to a Jensen Huang claim seven months ago that TPU and Trainium would never appear on InferenceX.

Sand AI's 480B-Parameter MAGI-2 Sets a New Video Quality-Price Frontier

Sand AI's MAGI-2, a 480B-parameter multi-head latent Mixture-of-Experts video model, debuted at number one in Artificial Analysis image-to-video rankings, statistically tied with Dreamina Seedance 2.5. At $0.08 per second it undercuts Seedance 2.5 by roughly 7x on cost for 1080p video with audio.

Alibaba Expands Qwen-Audio-3.1 to 65 Languages With Deeper Chinese Dialect Support

Alibaba updated its Qwen-Audio-3.1 speech foundation models with broader multilingual coverage, reaching 59 languages for ASR, 65 for TTS and 53 for realtime speech, alongside enhanced recognition and synthesis for Chinese dialects. The models are available now across the three voice APIs.

Claude Fable 5.2 Leads New FrontierPhysics Research Benchmark

Artificial Analysis launched a FrontierPhysics leaderboard of 54 private, physicist-authored research tasks that require literature review, simulation and a manuscript. Claude Fable 5.2 led at 32 percent, six points ahead of GPT-6.2 Astra, with Gemini 4 Neon passing numeric checks 54 percent of the time but review only 28 percent.
