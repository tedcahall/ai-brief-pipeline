🌐 TOP STORIES

Google's Cogentic Agent Posts Six Autonomous Math Papers Including Claimed Integer Factoring Bound

Google Research added six mathematics papers to its public GitHub repository on November 25 produced by Cogentic, a multi-agent discovery harness running on Gemini 4. According to the repository READMEs, each paper and a companion Lean 4 formalization were generated with no human interaction beyond the initial prompt. The lead preprint claims a randomized algorithm factoring any integer N in proven expected time L_N[1/3, 105], though the Lean files are unpublished and unreviewed.

Anthropic Uses Claude Agents to Migrate Live Messages API From Python to Rust

Anthropic announced that it migrated its core Messages API from Python to Rust after API traffic expanded roughly 1,800-fold between early 2024 and fall 2026. Claude wrote the initial Rust core request path in about a week, while engineers used the production Python service as a live verifier to detect behavioral differences and automatically dispatch Claude agents to fix edge cases. Anthropic reported 70% lower CPU usage per request and 20% faster median time-to-first-token.

OpenAI Pilots Private Inference on Isolated Hardware for Enterprise Customers

OpenAI began testing Private Inference with a small group of enterprise customers under its OpenAI Private Intelligence offering, CEO Sam Altman announced on November 25. Queries run on isolated confidential computing hardware whose security can be independently verified so OpenAI personnel cannot view customer prompts or responses during processing. The pilot pairs with Zero Data Retention and Private Safety Processing, which encrypts flagged misuse records inside the customer's own cloud.

Sand AI Launches 480B-Parameter MAGI-2 Flagship Video and Audio Generation Model

Sand AI released MAGI-2, scaling its Multi-Head Latent Mixture-of-Experts video architecture more than fourfold from its August preview to 480 billion total parameters and roughly 28 billion active parameters trained on 4 trillion tokens. The model generates up to 30 seconds of 1080p, 25 fps video with synchronized native audio and omni-reference across up to 20 media items. Artificial Analysis reported that MAGI-2 debuted tied for first on its Image-to-Video leaderboard at $4.80 per minute.

House Lawmakers Introduce Bipartisan RSI Safety Act to Track AI-Assisted Model Development

U.S. Representatives Josh Gottheimer and Nathaniel Moran introduced the bipartisan RSI Safety Act to establish federal oversight of recursive self-improvement in frontier AI development. The bill would require developers exceeding defined compute thresholds and spending at least $100 million annually on training to submit quarterly reports to a new Commerce Department office detailing AI involvement and human oversight across data preparation, algorithmic design, experimentation, and safety evaluations, with civil penalties up to $10 million.
