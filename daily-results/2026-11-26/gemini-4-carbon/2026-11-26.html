🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Research's Cogentic Agent Posts Six Math Papers, Led by a Claimed Factoring Bound

Google Research added six mathematics papers to its public GitHub repository on November 25, all produced by Cogentic, a multi-agent system built with Google DeepMind on Gemini 4 using no human input past the initial prompt. The lead paper claims an unconditional L_N[1/3, 105] integer factoring bound, improving on a 1992 rigorous bound. None is peer reviewed, Lean 4 proofs remain unreleased, and the large constant leaves RSA unaffected.

US Chip Curbs and Anti-Distillation Defenses Reportedly Slow Chinese AI Labs

Restrictions on remote access to advanced Nvidia chips in overseas data centers and anti-distillation defenses from OpenAI and Anthropic are delaying projects at Chinese AI labs, The Information reported via The Decoder. DeepSeek researchers internally estimated last month that their gap behind leading U.S. models could widen from roughly three to six months to nine months without distillation, while Moonshot AI canceled a $150 million Nvidia Blackwell deal in Indonesia.

OpenAI Pilots Private Inference on Isolated Hardware for Enterprise Data

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman announced that the company is testing Private Inference with a small group of business customers under OpenAI Private Intelligence. Queries run on isolated, independently verifiable hardware so OpenAI cannot access underlying prompts or responses, paired with Zero Data Retention and Private Safety Processing for automated abuse screening. Separately, OpenAI began rolling out a single shared ChatGPT usage meter merging Chat and Work for select Pro subscribers.

FCC Conditionally Clears SpaceX Plan for One Million Orbital AI Satellites

The Federal Communications Commission granted SpaceX conditional authorization on November 25 to deploy up to 1 million Starmind satellites designed to operate as solar-powered orbital AI data centers linked to Starlink by laser, Ars Technica reported. The order sets deployment milestones through 2040 and declines a full environmental review, while withholding SpaceX's requested waiver for non-reentry satellite disposal above 600 kilometers pending further compliance data.

House Lawmakers Propose Bipartisan RSI Safety Act to Track AI-Built Models

Reps. Josh Gottheimer (D-N.J.) and Nathaniel Moran (R-Texas) introduced the bipartisan RSI Safety Act to monitor recursive self-improvement at frontier AI labs. Developers meeting compute thresholds and spending at least $100 million annually on training would file quarterly reports with a new Commerce Department office detailing AI involvement and human oversight across data, algorithms, experiments, and safety evaluations, backed by whistleblower protections and civil penalties up to $10 million.
