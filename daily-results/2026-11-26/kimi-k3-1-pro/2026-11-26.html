Sand AI's 480B-parameter MAGI-2 leads today's edition, extending frontier-scale mixture-of-experts training to long-form 1080p audio-video generation. 🌐 TOP STORIES

Sand AI Launches 480B-Parameter MAGI-2 Video Model

Sand AI has launched MAGI-2, a 480B-total-parameter mixture-of-experts video model with about 28B active parameters. The company says it generates as much as 30 seconds of 1080p, 25 fps video with synchronized audio and accepts up to 20 reference media items. Artificial Analysis placed it first in image-to-video, statistically tied with Dreamina Seedance 2.5; Sand AI says open-source releases and a technical report will follow.

OpenAI Begins Limited Customer Tests of Private Inference

OpenAI is testing Private Inference with a small group of business customers, Sam Altman announced. The alpha feature uses confidential computing and verifiable controls to protect prompts and responses during model processing. It sits within OpenAI Private Intelligence alongside zero data retention and Private Safety Processing. OpenAI has not announced a general-availability date; the limited trial extends its business privacy offering rather than establishing broad availability.

Qwen-Audio-3.1 Expands Multilingual Speech Coverage

Alibaba's Qwen team expanded Qwen-Audio-3.1 across three hosted endpoints. ASR now lists 59 languages and eight Chinese dialects, TTS 65 languages and six dialects, and Realtime 53 languages and six dialects. The company highlighted improved dialect recognition and synthesis. No new accuracy benchmarks accompanied the announcement, so the expanded coverage counts do not establish performance on the added languages.

Anthropic Uses Claude Agents to Migrate Messages API to Rust

Anthropic migrated its Messages API from Python to Rust, using the existing production service as a verifier and teams of Claude agents to isolate and fix behavioral differences. The company reports about 70% lower CPU use per request and roughly 20% lower median time to first token, with no outage or feature freeze. Human engineers reviewed fixes before deployment, including rejecting agent shortcuts that passed local checks.

Bipartisan RSI Safety Act Would Require AI R&D Disclosures

Representatives Josh Gottheimer and Nathaniel Moran introduced H.R. 11157, the RSI Safety Act of 2026, on November 24. The bipartisan bill would require major frontier AI developers to make quarterly disclosures about AI involvement in research, human oversight and material shifts in automation. It would also require Commerce to report findings and legislative recommendations to Congress. The bill was referred to the House Science, Space, and Technology Committee.
