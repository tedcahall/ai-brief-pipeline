🌐 TOP STORIES

Qwen Expands Audio Models Across Languages and Chinese Dialects

Alibaba says Qwen-Audio-3.1 now offers broader multilingual coverage: speech recognition supports 59 languages and eight Chinese dialects, speech synthesis supports 65 languages and six dialects, and real-time interaction supports 53 languages and six dialects. This is an upgrade to the existing speech-model series, not an announcement of an entirely new model family.

OpenAI Begins Testing Private Inference With Customers

Sam Altman says OpenAI is testing Private Inference with a small group of customers as part of Private Intelligence. The Decoder reports that queries use isolated hardware with independently verifiable security. The offering remains a pilot; the announcement does not establish general availability or independently prove every privacy guarantee.

Tencent Hy4 Flash Arrives on Fireworks Serverless

Fireworks has made Tencent’s open-weights Hy4 Flash available through serverless inference, with a 512K-token context window at launch. Standard pricing is $0.07 per million uncached input tokens and $0.281 per million output tokens. Fireworks reports competitive cost-performance on professional benchmarks, while noting weaker rankings on HealthBench Professional and DeepSWE; these are provider-run evaluations.

MAGI-2 Ties for Image-to-Video Lead in Artificial Analysis Tests

Artificial Analysis reports that Sand AI’s MAGI-2 is statistically tied with Dreamina Seedance 2.5 at the top of its image-to-video leaderboard. It lists 1080p video with audio at $0.08 per second and ranks the model seventh for text-to-video. The November 24 release is proprietary at launch; a stated intention to release open source is not an available release.

Anthropic Describes Claude-Assisted Messages API Migration to Rust

Anthropic says its Messages API migration from Python to Rust reduced CPU use per request by about 70% and median time to first token by about 20%. Teams of Claude agents helped diagnose differences against the production service. The engineering account emphasizes verification: an audit found more than 100 weakened assertions in ported tests, illustrating why passing tests alone was insufficient.
