🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Tests Private Inference for Business Customers

OpenAI has started testing Private Inference with a small group of business customers, an alpha feature that uses confidential computing to keep prompts and replies private while models process them. Sam Altman announced the trial on X on Wednesday. The company says it is lining up more computing power for a much larger rollout but gave no date. Private Inference is the second part of OpenAI's privacy package, after Private Safety Processing.

Anthropic Moves Messages API From Python to Rust

Anthropic says it migrated its Messages API, the gateway that customers and its own Claude Code and Cowork apps use to reach Claude, from Python to Rust. The company describes using the production Python service as a verifier, with teams of Claude agents finding and fixing differences between the two versions. The post says the Rust version uses about 70% less CPU per request.

Amazon Trainium3 Posts 2.28x Performance per Dollar in SemiAnalysis AgentX Benchmark

SemiAnalysis reports that Amazon submitted its first Trainium3 UltraServer results to its Agentic Inference Benchmark and shows 2.28x better performance per dollar than GB300 NVL72, B300 and MI355X. The figure is a result from SemiAnalysis's benchmark for a single Amazon submission, so it reflects one published test rather than an independent audit.

RSI Safety Act Introduced in House

Representatives Josh Gottheimer (D-N.J.) and Nathaniel Moran (R-Texas) introduced the RSI Safety Act on November 24. It would require the largest AI developers to file quarterly reports with a new Commerce Department office on how much of their development work is handled by AI. Civil penalties for false or incomplete reporting could reach $10 million. The bill has been introduced only and has not advanced to a vote.

Artificial Analysis Launches AA-Robotics-Sim v0.1 Humanoid Test

Artificial Analysis launched AA-Robotics-Sim v0.1, a research preview in which language models in native harnesses control a simulated Unitree G1 humanoid across 10 tasks. GPT-6.2 Astra leads with 71.0, 13.5 points ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 at 57.5. The firm reports that every model reached the goal in navigation episodes, but only five dishes reached the dishwasher rack across 90 episodes.
