🌐 TOP STORIES

Google's Cogentic Agent Posts Six Math Papers, Including a Proven Factoring Bound

Google Research posted six papers to its GitHub repository on November 25, each credited to Cogentic, a multi-agent mathematical discovery system built on Gemini 4, with Lean 4 proofs and, according to Google, no human input beyond the initial prompt. The lead paper claims the first unconditional factoring algorithm in the L[1/3] class; its constant of 105 makes it a theory result, not a practical threat to encryption.

OpenAI Begins Testing Private Inference With a Small Group of Customers

Sam Altman said OpenAI is testing Private Inference, part of its Private Intelligence offering for businesses, with a small group of customers. The feature uses confidential computing and strict controls to protect prompts and responses while a model processes them, complementing Zero Data Retention. Firstpost reports OpenAI's product page lists it as an alpha with an API preview, alongside Private Safety Processing for automated safety reviews.

Anthropic Moves Its Messages API From Python to Rust, Verified by Claude Agents

Anthropic said it migrated its Messages API, which serves API customers and Claude Code, from Python to Rust after traffic grew roughly 1,800 times from early 2024 to fall 2026. Claude wrote a first version of the core request path in about a week, and agents fixed differences found on production traffic. The new service uses about 70% less CPU per request and cuts median time to first token by about 20%.

House Bill Would Require Frontier Labs to Report AI's Role in Building Their Models

Representatives Josh Gottheimer (D-N.J.) and Nathaniel Moran (R-Texas) introduced the RSI Safety Act on November 24. It would require developers above a compute threshold that spent at least $100 million on training in the prior year to report quarterly to a new Commerce Department office on AI's role and human oversight in data, design, experimentation and safety evaluation, with civil penalties up to $10 million for false reports.

Amazon's Trainium3 Posts 2.28x Nvidia GB300 Performance per Dollar on SemiAnalysis Benchmark

SemiAnalysis said Amazon's first Trainium3 UltraServer submission to its AgentX agentic inference benchmark delivered up to 2.28 times the performance per dollar of Nvidia's GB300 NVL72 and B300 and AMD's MI355X, crediting a wide expert-parallel serving approach. The firm later said Google TPUs and Trainium now both appear on InferenceX and AgentX, at 1.2 to 2.5 times the performance per dollar of Nvidia GPUs.
