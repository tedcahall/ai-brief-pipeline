🌐 TOP STORIES

Liquid AI Releases d1.3-ultra Decision Model, Tying for #1 at 60% Lower Cost

Liquid AI launched d1.3-ultra, a 64K-context text-and-image decision model that outputs calibrated option probabilities in a single pass. On the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1, d1.3-ultra ties Base Labs' Bison 31B for first place with a score of 76.4 while costing $133 per million tasks ($0.042 per million input tokens, free output), roughly 60% less than Bison 31B's $345. The update adds top-k option rankings, image and audio number/date extraction, and audio timestamp spans.

SemiAnalysis Benchmarks Show AWS Trainium3 and Google TPUv7 Outperforming Nvidia on Inference Cost

SemiAnalysis added AWS Trainium3 and Google TPUv7 to its open-source InferenceX and UBenchX benchmark suites, reporting that both custom accelerators demonstrate 1.5x to 2.5x better performance per dollar at release than equivalent Nvidia GPUs and beat or match Nvidia's GB300 in token generation TCO. Using Descriptor Generation Engines and a tile-native architecture designed at Annapurna Labs, Trainium3 reaches peak HBM bandwidth utilization at small message sizes where conventional GPUs fall short.

Anthropic CEO Calls for Federal Authority to Block Unsafe Models and Pledges $200M for Impact Research

In an interview with ABC News and an accompanying policy essay, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei warned that AI safety guardrails are falling behind exponential capability gains and proposed giving the U.S. government narrow power to block model deployments deemed unsafe by third-party assessments. Anthropic paired the proposal with a $200 million research commitment and an Economic Policy Framework outlining policy responses across 5%, 10%, and severe AI-driven unemployment scenarios.

Poland Signs Five-Year, $300 Million Agreement to Deploy Palantir's Maven AI Defense Platform

Poland's Ministry of National Defence signed a five-year contract estimated at $300 million (1.2 billion zloty) with Palantir Technologies to deploy the AI-powered Maven Smart System across all branches of the Polish armed forces. Defence Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz said the battlefield analysis and command-and-control platform will operate under sole Polish ownership of the servers, cryptographic keys, and data, following military testing that began in December 2023.

Los Angeles Unified School District Blocks Google's AI Overviews on Half a Million Student Devices

The Los Angeles Unified School District disabled Google's AI Overviews on school-issued devices for its roughly 500,000 students, enforcing the one-year generative AI moratorium the district enacted in September. The block follows Google's refusal to unbundle AI-generated summaries from Google Search for K-12 schools. LAUSD said it suspended the feature to keep student experiences safe and age-appropriate, and officials are evaluating alternative search providers if Google does not offer a permanent opt-out.
