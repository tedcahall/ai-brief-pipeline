🌐 TOP STORIES

Liquid AI Releases d1.3-ultra, Tying for First on Artificial Analysis Decision Index at Lower Cost

Liquid AI launched d1.3-ultra on November 27, a single-pass decision model tying Bison 31B for first on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1 at 76.4. Liquid AI says it matches that score at roughly 60% lower cost per million tasks ($0.042 per million input tokens, no output charge), while adding top-k rankings, image and audio data extraction, and audio time-span detection via its API.

AWS Trainium3 and Google TPU Outperform Nvidia GB300 on InferenceX Cost-Performance

SemiAnalysis published an InferenceX and UBenchX deep dive showing AWS Trainium3 and Google TPU live on InferenceX, delivering 1.5x to 2.5x better performance per dollar at release than equivalent Nvidia GPUs. On end-to-end AgentX coding-agent workloads, Trainium3 with vLLM trailed only Nvidia Vera Rubin on cost-performance and reached a 73.5% modeled profit margin at 135 tokens per second per user versus 53% for GB300 with SGLang.

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei Calls for Narrow Federal Power to Block Unsafe AI Models

In an exclusive ABC News interview following his November 25 policy essay and Anthropic's $200 million societal-impact research pledge, CEO Dario Amodei called for stronger AI regulation giving the government narrow power to block unsafe deployments after third-party assessments. Citing Anthropic's unreleased Claude Mythos model, Amodei floated wage reinsurance and higher AI or corporate taxes, and said any global pause requires verifiable participation from all major actors including China.

Poland Signs Five-Year, $300 Million Deal With Palantir for Maven Battlefield AI

Poland's defense ministry signed a five-year, $300 million (1.2 billion zloty) contract with Palantir Technologies on November 27 to deploy the Maven Smart System across all branches of the Polish armed forces for battlefield data analysis, targeting, and command-and-control. Defense Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz said all servers and data remain under Polish military control and noted NATO's existing Maven integration strengthens alliance interoperability.

Tencent Open-Sources GGUF Weights for 102-Language Hy-MT3-30B-A3B Translation Model

Tencent Hunyuan released local llama.cpp GGUF quantizations (IQ2, Q4_K_M, and Q8_0) for Hy-MT3-30B-A3B, a 30-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts translation model with 3 billion active parameters covering 102 languages, up from 38 in Hy-MT2. In Tencent's reported benchmarks, the non-thinking model outperforms larger models including Qwen3.8-Max, Kimi-K3.1-Pro, and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash while requiring under 1 TFLOP per sample, and ships alongside the open-source TransRubric evaluation suite.
