🌐 TOP STORIES

Experts Warn AI Labs Are Sitting on Undisclosed Encryption Breakthroughs

New developments: beyond Google's factoring papers covered November 27, The Guardian reports computer scientist Scott Aaronson is virtually certain, from multiple reliable sources, that AI labs are now sitting on one or more undisclosed breakthroughs in the mathematics behind encryption, with potential disruption to banking, diplomatic and email security within months. Aaronson said anyone informed is assuming such results exist, but no lab has confirmed any announcement, and experts urge migration to post-quantum cryptography.

Dario Amodei Calls for Stronger AI Regulation in ABC News Interview

In an exclusive ABC News interview, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei said government should have the narrow power to block or deter deployment of models that third-party assessment finds present unacceptable risks, pairing a Wednesday letter with a $200 million investment in research on AI's impact on society. He argued a pause would require agreement from all major AI players and said he does not trust China to act responsibly.

Liquid AI Releases d1.3-ultra, Top Decision Model on Artificial Analysis Index

Liquid AI released d1.3-ultra on November 27, a decision model recursively improved from d1-ultra that scores 76.4 on Artificial Analysis' Decision Index v2.1, a half point ahead of Baseten's Bison 31B and first of 27 evaluated models. The model returns calibrated decisions rather than generated tokens, adds rankings, extraction of numbers and dates from pictures and audio, and time spans in audio, at roughly 60 percent lower cost per task than the model it tied.

Poland Signs $300 Million Deal for Palantir's Maven Battlefield AI System

Poland's defense ministry signed a five-year agreement valued at about $300 million for Palantir's Maven Smart System, the AI battlefield analysis platform already used by Ukraine, NATO and the US military. Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz told reporters it would be stupid and wrong not to use it, crediting the system with helping Ukraine withstand more than four years of fighting Russia.

Los Angeles Schools Block Google AI Overviews on District Devices

Los Angeles school officials disabled AI-generated summaries in Google searches on school-issued devices, a milestone in enforcing the district's AI moratorium and a direct rebuff of a new Google policy, which had threatened to cut schools' search access. Parents' videos confirmed AI Overview was no longer an option on district devices, and officials are considering dropping Google products entirely.
