Experts Warn AI Labs May Hold Undisclosed Breakthroughs That Could Break Encryption

New developments: a Guardian exclusive reports experts fear AI labs are withholding encryption-related results. Scott Aaronson said he is virtually certain labs hold cryptographic "bombshells", while Bank of England governor Andrew Bailey urged caution releasing models. No lab has confirmed an encryption breakthrough, and Aaronson stressed human verification of AI-produced proofs is still required.

Liquid AI Releases d1.3-ultra, Tying Bison 31B atop Decision Index at Lower Cost

Liquid AI released d1.3-ultra on November 27, a single-pass decision model that Artificial Analysis scores 76.4 on its Decision Index v2.1, tied with Bison 31B at roughly 60% lower cost per million tasks. It adds ranking, image and audio extraction, and audio time spans, with a 64K context window and $0.042 per million input tokens.

Poland Signs Five-Year, $300 Million Deal for Palantir's Maven AI Battlefield System

Poland's defence ministry signed a five-year, $300 million contract for Palantir's Maven Smart System on November 27, joining the US military and NATO as users. Defence minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz said all servers and data remain in Polish military possession, and CEO Alex Karp attended the signing. The system supports command-and-control and target identification.

Los Angeles Unified Blocks Google AI Overviews on School Devices

Los Angeles Unified, the largest US district to sideline Google's AI, quietly disabled AI Overview on school-issued devices, enforcing its K-12 generative-AI moratorium. Google says search and AI cannot be separated and warned schools could lose search access. The half-million-student district's CIO said it can change its default search engine "literally overnight".

Tencent's Hy-MT3 Translation Models Reach Local PCs With 102-Language Coverage

Tencent released GGUF versions of its Hy-MT3 translation family on November 27, enabling local use via llama.cpp. The 30B-A3B MoE activates 3B parameters and covers 102 languages; Tencent claims it beats every non-thinking model it tested, including Qwen3.8-Max and Kimi-K3.1-Pro, while approaching closed thinking models. The base models were open-sourced November 24.
