🌐 TOP STORIES

Liquid AI Releases d1.3-ultra Decision Model, Tying Top Benchmark Score at 61% Lower Task Cost

Liquid AI released d1.3-ultra on November 27, tying Base Labs' Bison 31B for first place on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1 at 76 (76.4 reported by Liquid AI) while costing $133 versus $345 per million index tasks, about 61% less. Priced at $0.042 per million input tokens with no output charge, the 64K-context model adds option ranking, image-and-audio number and date extraction, and audio timestamp spans.

SemiAnalysis Finds AWS Trainium3 Beats Nvidia GB300 on Inference Cost per Token

SemiAnalysis added AWS Trainium3 and Google TPUs to its open-source InferenceX benchmark on November 27, reporting that Trainium3 running vLLM outperforms Nvidia's GB300 across the total-cost-of-ownership versus interactivity curve, trailing only Nvidia's Vera Rubin. At 135 tokens per second per user on Z.ai's GLM-5.3, SemiAnalysis modeled a 73.5% gross margin for Trainium3 versus 53% for GB300 with SGLang, though GB300 led on compute-bound prefill latency.

Computer Scientists Warn Frontier AI Labs May Hold Undisclosed Cryptography Breakthroughs

Theoretical computer scientist Scott Aaronson wrote that he has specific information that frontier AI labs will soon publish a major theoretical cryptanalysis advance that poses no immediate danger to deployed encryption, while separately noting unverified rumors of breaks affecting systems such as RSA. Following recent AI-assisted mathematical proofs, The Independent and The Guardian reported rising concern among cryptographers and British lawmakers, though neither OpenAI nor Anthropic has confirmed any cryptography breakthrough.

Situational Awareness Estimates Anthropic Spends $420 Million a Month Running Claude Internally

In its third-quarter investor letter, Leopold Aschenbrenner's hedge fund Situational Awareness estimated that Anthropic spent about $420 million a month in Q3 2026 running Claude internally, alongside $961 million serving external users and $1.91 billion on training and experiments. That figure represents roughly 30% of Claude's serving compute, within an estimated range of $165 million to $890 million monthly. Anthropic's IPO filing previously noted over 250-fold internal usage growth.

Physicists Dispute Quantum Gravity Preprint Built With 400 Million OpenAI Astra Tokens

Stanford and OpenAI physicist Venkatesa Chandrasekaran posted an unrefereed arXiv preprint proposing an algebraic quantization of gravity and black-hole entropy calculations developed over months using roughly 400 million tokens of OpenAI-supplied GPT-6 Astra compute. Reviewing the paper, University College London physicist Jonathan Oppenheim argued that it leaves core canonical-quantization and diffeomorphism-invariance obstacles unresolved, warning that theoretical physics lacks formal verification tools like Lean to filter AI-assisted claims.
