AI Labs Sitting on 'Bombshell' Encryption Revelations, Experts Warn

The Guardian reported November 28 that computer scientists suspect AI companies have advanced the mathematics behind encryption, potentially exposing banking transactions, diplomatic cables and private emails within months. Scott Aaronson said he is virtually certain, from multiple reliable sources, that labs hold one or more cryptography bombshells. Steven Murdoch said the circa-2032 post-quantum migration target will probably be missed. Experts emphasize that claimed proofs still require human verification.

Poland Signs Five-Year, $300 Million Palantir Maven Deal

Poland's defense ministry signed a five-year contract worth an estimated $300 million for Palantir's Maven Smart System, the battlefield data analysis software already used by NATO and the US military. Defense minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz said Poland will own the keys, servers and information. The deal runs against the European trend, with France and Germany pursuing a sovereign rival, and follows domestic protests and privacy questions raised with Poland's human rights ombudsman.

Liquid AI Releases d1.3-ultra, Tying Bison 31B Atop the Decision Index

Liquid AI released d1.3-ultra on November 27, updating its d1.2-ultra flagship decision model with what it calls recursive improvements from d1-ultra. Artificial Analysis independently scored it 76 on Decision Index v2.1, tied with Bison 31B for first at 60% lower cost per million tasks, and equal-top with Gemini Decision 1 on AA-Omniscience-Decision. The text and image model has a 64K context, costs $0.042 per million input tokens and is served through Liquid AI's API.

Los Angeles Schools Block Google AI Features as Districts Weigh Dropping Google

Los Angeles Unified, enrolling half a million students, quietly disabled AI Overview in Google search on school devices, becoming the largest US district to sideline Google's AI features, the Associated Press reported. Google says search and AI cannot be separated, and district CIO Jack Kelanic said LAUSD can change its default search engine overnight. Hundreds of Google employees petitioned for a permanent school workaround, and Fairfax County built plug-ins to strip AI summaries.

Aschenbrenner Q3 Letter Sees 2027 AI Acceleration Across Five Bets

Leopold Aschenbrenner's Q3 2026 letter for Situational Awareness LP, dated November 24, argues the market underestimates AI's pace and that 2027 could compress three years of progress across foundation models, storage chips, GPU rentals, data-center power and robotics. It names ten companies of interest, including Anthropic, SK hynix and SpaceX. A widely shared excerpt estimates Anthropic's internal Claude use at about one-third of worldwide Claude usage.
