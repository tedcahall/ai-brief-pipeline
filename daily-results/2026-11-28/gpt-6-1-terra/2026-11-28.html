🌐 TOP STORIES

Liquid AI Releases d1.3-ultra Decision Model

Liquid AI has released d1.3-ultra, a hosted model designed for decision tasks such as classification and extraction. The company says it adds ranking and improves handling of documents, images and audio. Artificial Analysis places it at the top of its decision index on rounded scores. The index measures a defined set of tasks, not general intelligence.

Experts Warn of Possible AI Cryptography Breakthroughs, Without Public Proof

New developments: The Guardian reports that computer scientist Scott Aaronson has heard credible but undisclosed reports of AI-related cryptography discoveries. Other experts caution that the specifics and consequences are unknown. No lab has publicly established that widely used encryption has been broken, and mathematical claims still require verification.

Poland Signs $300 Million Palantir Military AI Agreement

Poland signed a five-year, $300 million agreement for Palantir’s Maven Smart System, an AI-powered platform for battlefield analysis and decision support, according to Notes from Poland. The defense minister said Polish forces will retain possession of the servers and data. Palantir also confirmed the adoption in its own public statement.

Kimi Scores Diverge Across Public and Private Benchmarks

SemiAnalysis reports that Moonshot’s Kimi K3.1 ranks first on Terminal-Bench 4.1 but 16th of 26 on its private GDPx benchmark. The evaluator argues the gap reflects optimization for public tests. That is its interpretation, not proof of the training process; different benchmarks measure different tasks and conditions.

Photonic Chip Startup Tests AI Coprocessors in Data Centers

Q.ANT is piloting photonic processors at IONOS facilities as potential coprocessors for AI workloads, WIRED reports. Its CEO says simulations suggest energy savings, but acknowledges that the projected four- to fivefold reduction has not yet been demonstrated on real hardware. The company expects models to need adaptation for the processors.
