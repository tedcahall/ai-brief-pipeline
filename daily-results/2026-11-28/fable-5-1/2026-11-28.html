🌐 TOP STORIES

Experts Say AI Labs May Be Sitting on Encryption-Breaking Math Results

Scott Aaronson told the Guardian there are "well-attested rumours" that frontier models have broken ground on decades-old maths problems underpinning encryption, and wrote this week he is "virtually certain" labs hold cryptography bombshells. UCL's Steven Murdoch and Surrey's Alan Woodward said banks and governments must be ready to move quickly. Bank of England governor Andrew Bailey urged OpenAI and Anthropic to be cautious releasing models; neither lab has announced anything.

Dario Amodei Tells ABC Governments Should Be Able to Block Unsafe AI Deployments

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei told ABC News he is proposing regulation that gives government a narrow ability to block deployment of unsafe AI, echoing a letter ABC dates to Wednesday. Anthropic paired the essay with a $200 million commitment to research on AI's societal impact and an economic policy framework for job displacement. Amodei said a development pause would need verifiable agreement from all players, adding "I don't trust China at all."

Musk Says Google Wants to Rent More SpaceX Compute as GPU Block Prices Rise

Elon Musk posted on X on Saturday that Google has expressed interest in renting more computing capacity from SpaceX, Reuters reported. Under a deal announced in June, Google pays SpaceX $920 million a month through June 2029 for about 110,000 Nvidia GPUs, and SpaceX filings put its Anthropic and Google compute agreements above $70 billion combined. Musk also said rental prices for large coherent training blocks are rising while smaller allocations are getting cheaper.

Liquid AI Releases d1.3-ultra Decision Model, Tying for First on Artificial Analysis Index

Liquid AI released d1.3-ultra on November 27, recursively improved from d1-ultra, adding ranking outputs, number and date extraction from images and audio, and audio time spans. Artificial Analysis independently scored it 76 on its Decision Index v2.1, tying Bison 31B at about 60 percent lower cost per million tasks, at $0.042 per million input tokens with no output charge. It is live in the Liquid console; OpenRouter and Vercel are listed as coming soon.

Poland Signs $300 Million Five-Year Palantir Deal for Maven Battlefield AI

Poland's defence ministry signed a $300 million (1.2 billion zloty) five-year contract on Friday for Palantir's Maven Smart System, an AI platform for battlefield data analysis already used by the US military and NATO Allied Command Operations, with CEO Alex Karp attending. Defence minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz said all servers and data will remain with Polish forces and that no European provider offered equivalent capabilities, while acknowledging criticism of Palantir's government data work.
