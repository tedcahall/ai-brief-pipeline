🌐 TOP STORIES

Liquid AI’s d1.3-ultra Ties Bison 31B on Decision Tasks

Liquid AI released d1.3-ultra in its console with support for ranking options and extracting dates or amounts from images. Artificial Analysis reports a score of 76 on its decision index, one point above the previous model and level with Bison 31B. That comparison measures decision tasks, not general language-model performance.

Tencent Packages Its 102-Language Translation Model for Local Use

Tencent released GGUF weights for its Hy-MT3-30B-A3B translation model, giving llama.cpp users a choice of three quantization levels. The mixture-of-experts model activates three billion parameters at a time and supports 102 languages, up from 38 in the previous generation. Its accuracy comparisons come from Tencent’s own evaluations.

Cryptographers Weigh Reports of Unpublished AI Advances

Computer scientist Scott Aaronson told the Guardian he considers reports of unpublished advances in cryptography credible. His account matters because banks and governments rely on the difficulty of particular mathematical problems to protect communications. The report does not establish that a lab can decrypt those communications or that deployed encryption has been broken.

IONOS Pilots Photonic Chips for AI Workloads

WIRED reports that German chipmaker Q.ANT has placed photonic processors in IONOS facilities for a pilot. The idea is to run some calculations with light alongside electronic GPUs, rather than replace those GPUs. Q.ANT says more than 30 engineering teams are developing models for photonic computing; the pilot is not evidence of fleet-wide energy savings.

Unsloth Connects Local Models to Three Creative Tools

Unsloth says it used a local Inkling 2 Mini model to create assets in Unreal Engine, Unity and Blender without human intervention. Its setup guide explains what makes this possible: each application exposes tools that a model running on the same machine can call. The demonstration concerns asset creation, not autonomous production of a finished game.
