WIRED describes photonic processors intended to complement GPUs, using light for selected computation. It reports Q.ANT chips in an IONOS facility pilot and teams exploring AI workloads. NVIDIA researchers present Test-time Baking for novel-view rendering

NVIDIA describes Test-time Baking, a compact neural network trained to reproduce how a model combines multiple photos. This is a company-reported result; its post identifies the work as a NeurIPS 2026 spotlight.
