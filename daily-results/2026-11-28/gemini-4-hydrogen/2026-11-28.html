🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei Calls for Government Power to Block Unsafe AI Deployment

In an ABC News interview and accompanying letter, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei urged stronger regulation giving governments narrow authority to block deployment of AI models deemed unacceptably risky by third-party assessments. Amodei announced a $200 million investment into societal impact research, cited internal testing of Claude Mythos on software vulnerabilities, and argued any AI development pause would require verifiable global participation.

Experts Warn Frontier AI Labs May Hold Cryptography-Breaking Math Results

New developments: Computer scientist Scott Aaronson told The Guardian he has heard credible but unverified rumors that frontier AI labs hold breakthroughs on decades-old math problems underpinning RSA and elliptic-curve encryption. Bank of England Governor Andrew Bailey warned AI progress has pushed quantum timelines into the background, while Ethereum Foundation researchers and Vitalik Buterin outlined defensive wallet migration plans pending human verification of any claimed proofs.

Liquid AI Releases d1.3-ultra, Tying for First on Artificial Analysis Decision Index

Liquid AI launched d1.3-ultra, a single-pass multimodal decision model scoring 76.4 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1 to tie Bison 31B for first place. Priced at $0.042 per million input tokens with no output token charge and a 64K context window, the release adds top-k ranking, number and date extraction from images and audio, and uncertainty refusals.

Poland Signs $300 Million Five-Year Deal With Palantir for Maven Battlefield AI

Poland's defense ministry signed a five-year, $300 million contract with Palantir for its Maven Smart System to support military command, control, and battlefield data analysis. Defense Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz said all servers and data will remain in Polish military possession and noted NATO already integrates Maven into alliance exercises, while Palantir CEO Alex Karp attended the signing.

Los Angeles Schools Block Google AI Overviews as Districts Weigh Dropping Google Search

The Los Angeles Unified School District quietly disabled Google AI Overviews on school-issued devices for its half-million students while enforcing a one-year generative AI moratorium. Google has told districts that Search and AI cannot be separated for education accounts, prompting LAUSD and other districts to consider alternative search providers as hundreds of Google employees petitioned leadership for an opt-out.
