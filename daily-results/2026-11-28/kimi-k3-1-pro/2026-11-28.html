🌐 TOP STORIES

Scott Aaronson Warns AI Labs May Be Sitting on Cryptography Breakthroughs

The Guardian reports that Scott Aaronson and other computer scientists see well-attested rumors of AI-assisted advances in mathematics related to cryptography. No lab has announced a concrete encryption breakthrough, and the reported discovery could either weaken or strengthen security. Aaronson said the claimed proofs still require human verification, while Surrey professor Alan Woodward cautioned that any finding would not undo encryption immediately.

Liquid AI Launches d1.3-ultra Decision Model With Multimodal Extraction

Liquid AI released d1.3-ultra on November 27, adding top-k rankings and extraction of numbers and dates from text, images and audio. Artificial Analysis reported a tie with Bison 31B at 76.4 on its Decision Index v2.1, with roughly 60% lower cost per million tasks. The 64K-context model is available through Liquid AI’s console and API; OpenRouter and Vercel access is planned.

Los Angeles Schools Disable Google AI Overviews on Student Devices

Los Angeles Unified disabled Google’s AI Overview summaries on school-issued devices, making it the largest district to sideline the feature, according to AP. The district called the suspension temporary and cited age-appropriate, safe student experiences. Google says AI cannot be separated from search for schools, prompting districts to consider alternative providers while Google develops opt-in controls and a guided-learning tool.

Trainium3 and Google TPUs Post InferenceX Cost Gains Over NVIDIA GPUs

SemiAnalysis reported Google TPU and Amazon Trainium results on InferenceX and AgentX, estimating 1.2x to 2.5x better performance per dollar than NVIDIA GPUs. Trainium3’s peak 2.28x result is an official preview still undergoing validation and depends on SemiAnalysis’s ownership-cost assumptions. The tests used Z.ai’s GLM-5.2/GLM-5.3 on vLLM and measured long-context, multi-turn agent workloads.

Anthropic CEO Proposes Deployment Blocks and Commits $350 Million

Dario Amodei told ABC News that governments should be able to block deployment of models judged to present unacceptable risks after third-party assessments. Anthropic’s accompanying economic framework proposes wage insurance, modernized unemployment support and possible new tax revenue sources. The company separately committed $200 million to economic research and $150 million to a national fellowship program.
