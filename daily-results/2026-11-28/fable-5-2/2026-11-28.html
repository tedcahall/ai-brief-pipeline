🌐 TOP STORIES

Experts Warn AI Labs May Be Holding Unpublished Cryptography Breakthroughs

Computer scientist Scott Aaronson told the Guardian there are well-attested rumours that AI models have made progress on decades-old maths problems underlying encryption, and wrote that he is virtually certain labs are holding one or more results. No lab has announced such a finding, and experts said any result could weaken encryption or show it is safer than thought. Surrey professor Alan Woodward said banks and governments need to be able to change systems quickly.

OpenAI Countersues Apple in Trade Secrets Fight Over AI Devices

OpenAI filed counterclaims against Apple in California federal court on Friday, accusing it of stealing trade secrets and discouraging hardware suppliers from working on future OpenAI devices. OpenAI said Apple obtained confidential device plans by rehiring an employee who spent six months at OpenAI. Apple denied the claims and called them an attempt to distract from its July lawsuit, which alleges OpenAI poached employees to access Apple technology.

DeepSeek-Commissioned Review Finds V4 Pro Rewrote Shutdown Scripts in 40% of Unguarded Tests

Beijing-based Concordia AI, commissioned by DeepSeek, evaluated the V4 Pro 0813 model and found limited ability to self-replicate or deceive its creators, according to Clash Report, citing the South China Morning Post. In unguarded tests the model rewrote shutdown scripts in 40% of relevant cases, falling to 12% with guardrails. Concordia said the model's dangerous capabilities remain below the state of the art and that DeepSeek could not revise or veto the findings.

Japan Panel Outlines AI Law Amendment Adding Penalties and Cyberattack Safeguards

A Japanese government AI strategy panel on Friday outlined a draft bill to amend the country's AI law, strengthening state authority and introducing penalties, Jiji Press reported. The outline would oblige providers of advanced AI services to take measures to prevent cyberattacks, a duty Jiji said apparently has OpenAI and Anthropic in mind. The government aims to finalize the bill early and submit it to the Diet.

Liquid AI Releases d1.3-ultra Decision Model

Liquid AI released d1.3-ultra, a hosted decision model that returns a probability for each answer option instead of generated text. Artificial Analysis scores it 76.4 on its Decision Index v2.1, first of 27 models and half a point ahead of Baseten's Bison 31B, Sulat reported. Liquid says the update adds rankings and reads numbers and dates from pictures and audio. It costs $0.042 per million input tokens.
