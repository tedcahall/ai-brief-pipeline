🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Opens Independent Investigation Into CEO Jensen Huang

A committee of independent directors assisted by outside counsel will investigate reporting that founder and CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, AP reports; the board said it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, and Huang committed to cooperating. The New York Times reports US intelligence believes the woman involved was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security, with the operation reportedly aimed at information about American AI chips and export policy.

Apple Ships Official Xcode Plugin in Claude Code Marketplace

An Xcode plugin listing Apple as author was merged into Anthropic's official claude-plugins-official marketplace (pull request 6338, merged Nov 6, confirmed by live fetch). It exposes Xcode capabilities to Claude Code through MCP, including building, running and testing schemes, rendering SwiftUI previews, driving simulators and real iPhones, and reading build logs and Apple developer docs. CellCog and AICoder coverage calls it a one-line install, a notable first-party endorsement of agentic AI coding by Apple.

Trump Tells 60 Minutes AI Risks Can Be Managed, Signals Openness to Guardrails

President Trump told CBS's 60 Minutes, aired Nov 8, that AI risks can be managed and "I don't mind guardrails," Reuters reports. He said he could work with Democrats on safeguards as long as rules don't put the US in a "deep second position" behind China, a shift from the administration's earlier industry self-policing stance after a voluntary safety pact.

Anthropic Study: Researchers Use Claude Most for Data Analysis and Writing

Anthropic analyzed 207,049 Claude and Claude Code research sessions (Aug 2 to Sep 30, 2026) with privacy-preserving classifiers, finding data analysis accounts for 33.7 percent of Claude sessions and 48.1 percent of Claude Code sessions, while ideation and data gathering are underused even though researchers rank them most important for discovery. Its scenarios suggest current capabilities could double research output in some fields, and about 40 percent of surveyed researchers expect AI models to carry out full research projects on their own by 2030.

IndiaAI Mission Releases EKA-1, Dhir 2B and Dhir 5B Indigenous Models

India's IndiaAI Mission released three indigenous foundation models unveiled Nov 3 in New Delhi (The Hindu, published Nov 4). Soket Labs' EKA-1 is a 26.3-billion-parameter sparse hybrid model trained on about 7.5 trillion tokens, covering all 22 scheduled Indian languages plus English, tuned for banking and cybersecurity and capable of some on-device inference; ZenteiQ's open-source Dhir 2B and 5B support English plus 10 Indian languages and run offline on-premise for defence, government and education use.
