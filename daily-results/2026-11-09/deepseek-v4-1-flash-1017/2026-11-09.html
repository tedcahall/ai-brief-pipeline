🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Opens Independent Probe Into Whether CEO Jensen Huang Was Targeted by Chinese Intelligence

Nvidia's board said Sunday it opened an independent investigation, with outside counsel, after reports that founder and chief executive Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, CNBC reported. The board said it found no reason to believe Nvidia secrets or national security were compromised and that Huang remains chief executive. Huang acknowledged an affair with an executive at Nvidia customer Megaspeed, and China's Foreign Ministry on Monday called the spying allegations unsubstantiated.

Anthropic Commits $3.4 Billion to Gas-Fired Generators for Its Data Centers

Anthropic struck a deal worth about $3.4 billion for gas-fired power generators for its data centers, the Financial Times reported Sunday. The company bought commitments for 1.7 gigawatts of heavy-duty generators in late September from San Antonio-based OnePWR Solutions, including engineering and construction support to install them, CNBC reported. Anthropic has said it expects to spend more than $500 billion over ten years on AI infrastructure.

Baidu Opens ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base Weights Under a Restrictive License

Baidu released the weights of ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base, a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 280 billion total parameters and 14 billion active per token, pretrained on 33.5 trillion tokens with a 1M-token context for fine-tuning and inference. The checkpoint is the end of pretraining, before mid- or post-training. Coverage noted the custom license excludes the EU, UK and South Korea.

DeepSeek Says Harness Agent App Reached 1.54 Million Daily Users

DeepSeek said its open-source agent application, Harness, reached 1.54 million daily active users by November 4, less than three months after its August 13 debut, Bloomberg reported. Daily users were about 271,000 at the end of September. DeepSeek is planning a plugin marketplace and a support programme with token grants and technical assistance for plugin creators.

India's IndiaAI Mission Releases EKA-1 and the Open-Source Dhīr Models

The IndiaAI Mission released three indigenous foundation models: EKA-1, a 26.3-billion-parameter model from Soket Labs trained on about 7.5 trillion tokens across 22 scheduled Indian languages and English, and the open-source Dhīr 2B and Dhīr 5B from ZenteiQ for on-premise, offline deployment. The Hindu reported the models were unveiled at an event attended by IT Secretary S. Krishnan. Dhīr supports English and 10 Indian languages.
