🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Opens Independent Probe After Jensen Huang Admits Affair With Suspected Chinese Spy

Nvidia's board said a committee of independent directors, assisted by outside counsel, will investigate reports that CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, after Huang admitted an affair with Alice Huang, a businesswoman U.S. agencies reportedly identified as a Chinese spy. The board said it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised; China called the allegation unsubstantiated, NBC News reports.

LG AI Research Opens K-EXAONE 2.5 API Free of Charge

LG AI Research made the API for K-EXAONE 2.5, an additionally post-trained version of K-EXAONE 2.0, free to developers, startups and researchers from Nov. 9, announcing it at Nvidia AI Day Seoul. LG says the model improves more than 70 percent across 24 combined metrics versus its August release and reports in-house scores of 74.6 on AutomationBench and 59.2 on SWE-Bench Pro, Digital Today reports.

Trump Tells 60 Minutes AI Risks Are Manageable and He Does Not Mind Guardrails

President Donald Trump told CBS's 60 Minutes that AI risks can be managed and that he does not mind guardrails, signaling openness to working with Democrats on safeguards as long as rules do not leave the US in a deep second position behind China. Separately, he called Nvidia's Jensen Huang an honorable guy but said he had not spoken to him about the allegations, The Indian Express reports.

Musk Promises Grok Bot Fixes After Usage-Limit Complaints

Elon Musk said Grok Bot fixes for improved token efficiency and smarter behavior are underway after a subscriber said a $200 plan that lasted seven days the prior week now ran out in 14 hours, figures TeslaNorth notes are unverified. Musk gave no details on weekly limits or prices; xAI lists Premium, Super and Ultra plans at $30, $100 and $200 a month.

Artificial Analysis Launches Decision Index v2 With Private Benchmarks and Calibration Scoring

Artificial Analysis released Decision Index v2, raising the share built on private datasets to 45 percent from 25 percent and adopting a headline score that rewards calibrated confidence. Drex Max v1.1 and GLiDE v1.2 lead dedicated decision models at 73, while Claude Haiku 5.5 (Max) scores 77 and GPT-6 Luna (Max) 76, though Luna costs about 15 times more per million tasks than Drex Max.
