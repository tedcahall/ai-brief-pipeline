🌐 TOP STORIES

NVIDIA Board Opens Independent Investigation Into Jensen Huang

The NVIDIA board said it is opening an independent investigation into reporting that founder and CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, overseen by a committee of independent directors with outside counsel. The board said it has no reason to believe national security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised, and that Huang supports the review.

NVIDIA Brings Korea's Leading Open Models Into Production With Dynamo

NVIDIA published Dynamo-optimized deployment recipes for four Korean open models: A.X K2 from SK Telecom, K-EXAONE 2.0 from LG AI Research, Solar Open 2 from Upstage and Motif-3 from Motif Technologies. NVIDIA says the recipes, tuned for B200 GPUs, deliver up to twice the inference performance out of the box when a model no longer fits on a single GPU.

DeepSeek Harness Reaches 1.54 Million Daily Active Users

DeepSeek said its open-source Harness agent app reached 1.54 million daily active users as of November 4, with roughly 92 trillion tokens consumed per day and about 35,000 third-party plugins, around 60 percent of active users having built or installed one. The company is planning an official plugin marketplace and a creator support program with token incentives.

Artificial Analysis Ships Decision Index v2 With a Calibration Metric

Artificial Analysis released version 2 of its Decision Index, which scores decision models on correctness and calibrated confidence instead of accuracy alone. Private datasets now make up 45 percent of the index, up from 25 percent, and a new 1,500-document AA-Paperclip benchmark was added. Drex Max v1.1 from Nace AI leads the decision models with 73 points.

xAI Says Grok Bot Token Efficiency and Behavior Fixes Are Underway

xAI said improvements to token efficiency and behavior in Grok Bot are underway, after users of the agent posted a long thread of complaints about usage limits and token consumption. xAI product lead matt palmer asked for feedback on November 8, and Elon Musk said a round table with the Grok Bot engineering team had set overnight fixes.
