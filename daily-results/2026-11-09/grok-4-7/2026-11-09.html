🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Opens Inquiry After Report Jensen Huang Was Targeted by Foreign Intelligence

CNBC reported that Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation after U.S. intelligence agencies raised concerns that a businesswoman affiliated with customer Megaspeed had passed information to Beijing. Jensen Huang told staff on Sunday that he had an affair and that the board was investigating. The board said it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised. A spokesperson said he would continue as CEO.

Artificial Analysis Releases Decision Index v2 With a Calibration Score

Artificial Analysis published Decision Index v2 on November 8, moving 45% of the index onto private datasets, up from 25%. The new Decision Score rewards correct answers and calibrated confidence. The publisher says Drex Max v1.1 and GLiDE v1.2 both score 73, with Mammoth 27B at 72. Claude Haiku 5.5 (Max) scores 77 and GPT-6 Luna (Max) scores 76, at much higher cost and latency. Those figures are Artificial Analysis measurements, not an independent replication.

NVIDIA Publishes Dynamo Recipes for Four Korean Open Models

NVIDIA said it is releasing Dynamo deployment recipes for SK Telecom's A.X K2, LG AI Research's K-EXAONE 2.0, Upstage's Solar Open 2, and Motif Technologies' Motif-3. The company says the B200-optimized recipes deliver up to 2x inference performance out of the box. That speed figure is Nvidia's claim. Dynamo is described as an open-source framework for multi-node inference serving. The post was published November 9, before the 09:59 UTC collection cutoff.

Anthropic Reports Claude Leads 26% of Its AI Research Work

Anthropic published prototype measurements of how its models are built. It says Claude leads 26% of its AI research work, and that more than 90% of that work sits at or above an AI-collaborates level. As of August 2026, about 30,000 agents were doing research and engineering on its most-used internal platform. In one July week, about 6% of AI R&D compute went to safety. These are company figures, not a third-party audit.

DeepSeek Says Harness Reached 1.54 Million Daily Users

TechNode, citing IT Home, reported that DeepSeek plans an official plugin marketplace for Harness, plus creator support and work on plugin API stability. Harness had 1.54 million daily active users as of November 4, counted through DeepSeek's official API. About 35,000 third-party plugins were listed, and about 60% of active users had created or installed at least one. The user count is the company's reported figure, not an independent audit.
