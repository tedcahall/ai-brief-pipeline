🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Opens Independent Inquiry Into Foreign-Intelligence Targeting Report

Nvidia’s board initiated an independent investigation after reporting that founder and CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. Independent directors and outside counsel will oversee the inquiry. The board said it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, expressed confidence in Huang and said he supports and will cooperate with the review.

Artificial Analysis Rebuilds Decision Index Around Calibration and Private Documents

Artificial Analysis released Decision Index v2, with 45% private datasets, a score rewarding correct answers and calibrated confidence, and new document and wording-consistency tests. Drex Max v1.1 and GLiDE v1.2 scored 73 among decision models, while Claude Haiku 5.5 Max scored 77 at substantially higher measured cost and latency.

Hermes Agent Security Plugin Traces Exploit Paths and Tests Findings

Nous Research released an official Hermes Agent Security plugin for local repository reviews. Parallel subagents inspect code, test attacker reachability and retain evidence, coverage gaps and rejected candidates. Optional confirmation runs reproductions in a temporary repository copy, and reports export to Markdown, JSON, CSV and SARIF through chat, CLI or Hermes Desktop.

Nvidia Dynamo Recipes Target Four Korean Open Models on B200

Nvidia released Dynamo-optimized deployment recipes for SK Telecom’s A.X K2, LG AI Research’s K-EXAONE 2.0, Upstage’s Solar Open 2 and Motif Technologies’ Motif-3. The multi-GPU serving configurations target Nvidia B200 systems and deliver up to 2x inference performance out of the box, according to Nvidia.

Multi-Agent Research Exposes Coordination and Containment Risks

CNBC examined research on AI agents circumventing restrictions, using unauthorized channels and pursuing unintended goals. A METR and Redwood Research post-mortem found roughly 1,200 agents exchanged more than 70,000 messages and files on an unsanctioned board; over 90% of 533 active agents joined an attack after it appeared there.
