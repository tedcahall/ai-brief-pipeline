🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Opens Independent Investigation After Reports CEO Jensen Huang Was Targeted by Chinese Intelligence

Nvidia's board said Sunday that independent directors and outside counsel will investigate reports that CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, TechCrunch reports. Huang told staff he had an affair; a memo circulated among US policymakers claimed the woman, a former Megaspeed executive, was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security. The board said it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised.

Anthropic Strikes a $3.4 Billion Deal for Gas Generators to Power Data Centers, FT Reports

Anthropic agreed in late September to pay about $3.4 billion for commitments covering 1.7 gigawatts of heavy-duty gas generators from San Antonio supplier OnePWR Solutions, the Financial Times reported Sunday, according to a Reuters account carried by CNBC. The deal includes engineering and construction capabilities but not OnePWR itself or its staff. Reuters could not immediately verify the report, and neither company responded to requests for comment.

Tencent's Chief AI Scientist Warns Models Will Soon Reach a Biology Harm Threshold as Hy4 Weights Near Release

Speaking at a Hong Kong science event on Saturday, Tencent chief AI scientist Yao Shunyu said the cyber abilities of AI models, and soon their biology abilities, will reach a threshold where harm is possible, The Next Web reports, citing the South China Morning Post. He urged Chinese open-model developers to take safety seriously. Tencent plans to release Hy4's weights and a safety-testing report in the coming days, per Reuters.

Trump Says AI Risks Can Be Managed and He Does Not Mind Guardrails

President Donald Trump told CBS's 60 Minutes that the risks posed by AI can be managed and expressed openness to working with Democrats on safeguards, Reuters reports. "I don't mind guardrails," he said, adding that he did not want regulation to put the United States in "a deep second position" behind China. Reuters noted it remains unclear what level of safeguards he would support.

DeepSeek Says Its Harness Agent Platform Reached 1.54 Million Daily Active Users

DeepSeek said its Harness agent platform reached 1.54 million daily active users as of November 4, counted by users of its official API, with daily consumption of about 92 trillion tokens, 36Kr reports. The figures, disclosed on November 5, are self-reported. Third-party plugins have grown to about 35,000 since the v0.1 release on August 13, and around 60 percent of active users have created or installed at least one.
