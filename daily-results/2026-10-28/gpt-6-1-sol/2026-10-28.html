🌐 TOP STORIES

Google DeepMind previews Genie 4 with native audio and live world steering

Google DeepMind introduced Genie 4, a world model that generates interactive environments with native sound and dialogue, accepts live scene edits, and uses Gemini models to guide multi-step events. It is a research preview for trusted testers, with broader consumer and developer access still forthcoming. Google notes that object permanence, physical interactions and long-term consistency remain limitations, rather than presenting the simulations as reliable substitutes for reality.

Liquid AI releases six open-weight LFM3 models for specialized on-device AI

Liquid AI released LFM3, six roughly 2B-parameter models covering text, vision, audio, combined modalities and structured decisions. They share a backbone mixing gated short convolutions, sliding-window attention and global attention. The text model supports a 256K-token context window, and the family is designed for local deployment and single-GPU fine-tuning. Weights are available now; the performance comparisons in the announcement are Liquid's own evaluations.

OpenAI opens AI-generated science claims to independent examination

OpenAI announced AI-generated results concerning local smoothing and Kakeya, the Exponential Time Hypothesis, and quantum field theory. It published mathematics and computer-science write-ups and Lean formalizations, while a separate physics group will decide how to release its materials. Researchers including ETH's original proposers are organizing independent examination. Their participation is not certification of the claims, and the announcement does not establish immediate practical algorithms or completed community verification.

Anthropic expands drug-development research and acquires Mithril Medicine

Anthropic described research partnerships with six medicine-development organizations and disclosed its acquisition of Mithril Medicine, a one-person company developing treatments for rare muscular dystrophies. Case studies span molecule design, laboratory analysis, manufacturing documentation and trial planning. Anthropic reports promising mouse results from Mithril and says human trials could begin as soon as late 2028 if candidates hold up; most of the disclosed research remains preclinical.

Oracle plans Google TPU access on OCI beginning in 2027

Oracle and Google announced plans to offer Google TPUs through Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, with availability beginning in calendar 2027 and ramping over multiple years. Oracle will supply power, cooling and datacenter networking. The companies describe OCI as the first hyperscale cloud outside Google to offer the accelerators, expanding training and inference choices; this is a future deployment announcement, not general availability today.
