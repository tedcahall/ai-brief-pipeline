🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases AI-Generated Proofs Across Math, Computer Science and Physics

OpenAI released model-generated results covering local smoothing and Kakeya, a refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis, and existence proofs for quantum field theories including QCD and pure Yang-Mills. The ETH result and natural-language proof were formalized in Lean. External academic groups will examine the work, with different release approaches for each result and further scrutiny still required.

Google DeepMind Introduces Genie 4 With Native Audio and Real-Time Steering

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, an interactive world model that generates environments, ambient sound and dialogue in real time. Gemini models help steer multi-step events and maintain coherence. The research preview is initially available to trusted testers, with broader access planned for Google AI Ultra subscribers and early API partners. Google cautions that object permanence, long-term consistency and text rendering remain limitations.

Liquid AI Launches Six Open-Weight LFM3 Models for On-Device Agents

Liquid AI released six open-weight LFM3 models spanning text, vision, audio, omni and decision tasks, all derived from a shared 1.7B backbone. The family uses a tri-hybrid architecture and supports fine-tuning on one GPU. Its text model offers a 256K-token context window. Liquid says the models are available through Hugging Face, its API and playground for local and specialized deployments.

Artificial Analysis Overhauls Intelligence Index With Mostly Private Evaluations

Artificial Analysis launched Intelligence Index v5, adding FrontierCode 2.0 and Terminal-Bench-Science while upgrading three evaluations. Private test sets or held-out answers now cover 60% of the index. Claude Opus 5.5 leads at 58, ahead of GPT-6 Astra at 57. The reported open-weights gap widened to 12 points, though changed tests mean the result is not a like-for-like model comparison.

Anthropic Expands Claude Research Into Drug Discovery and Life Sciences

Anthropic described research partnerships with six organizations developing medicines and said it has begun drug-discovery efforts of its own. The work examines how Claude can support stages of drug development, alongside early results from the collaborations. The announcement expands Anthropic’s life-sciences activity beyond general-purpose model access, while the company presents the findings as early work rather than validated clinical outcomes.
