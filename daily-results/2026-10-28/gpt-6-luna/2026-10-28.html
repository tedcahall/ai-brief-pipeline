🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Publishes AI-Generated Work on Major Math and Computing Conjectures

OpenAI published AI-produced work in mathematics, computer science and physics. The company says its models proved the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension, formalized the result in Lean, and produced a result refuting the Exponential Time Hypothesis that a research group is now examining. OpenAI describes the findings as early research output, not settled expert consensus.

Google DeepMind introduces Genie 4 for real-time interactive world simulation

Google DeepMind introduced Genie 4, a world model that generates interactive environments with real-time steering, physics and native audio, including responsive character dialogue. The company says Gemini models help keep multi-step simulations coherent. Access is a research preview for trusted testers, with broader access planned later through Project Genie and early-access programs.

Liquid AI releases six-model LFM3 family for multimodal and on-device use

Liquid AI announced six open-weight LFM3 models built around a shared 1.7-billion-parameter base, covering text, vision, audio, omni and decision tasks. The company says its new tri-hybrid architecture was designed for efficient long-context inference and local fine-tuning. Weights and model access are available through its listed repositories and services; performance claims remain company-reported.

Anthropic reports early drug-development work and begins in-house discovery

Anthropic described research partnerships with six biotechnology and pharmaceutical organizations and said it has begun its own drug-discovery programs, including work on rare childhood muscular dystrophy. The company reported early partner results involving antibody designs and CAR T-cell research, while emphasizing that the programs remain exploratory. It says Claude currently supports scientific and operational tasks across development workflows.

Oracle to offer Google TPUs through OCI, expanding hyperscale AI compute choice

Oracle and Google announced that Google Tensor Processing Units will be offered through Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, the first planned availability of Google TPUs on a hyperscale cloud outside Google. OCI will supply datacenter power, cooling and networking. The companies say the service will cover training and inference workloads, with availability expected in calendar 2027 and a multiyear ramp.
