🌐 TOP STORIES

Google DeepMind unveils Genie 4 interactive world model

Google DeepMind introduced Genie 4, an experimental world model that generates interactive environments with native audio and permits text-guided changes as a scene unfolds. Gemini models help steer multistep simulations. Access is initially limited to trusted testers; the lab says visual consistency, physical interactions and long-running coherence remain research challenges.

Liquid AI releases LFM3 small multimodal model family

Liquid AI released six open-weight LFM3 models built on a shared 1.7-billion-parameter backbone, with text, vision, audio, combined modalities and decision variants. The text model supports a 256,000-token context. The company targets local deployment and fine-tuning; its model card cautions against using the small text model for complex agentic coding.

Ant releases Ling 3.1 Flash 1019 model weights

Ant’s InclusionAI released weights for Ling 3.1 Flash 1019 and a separate base checkpoint after reporting the model’s benchmark score earlier. Artificial Analysis measured 44 on its previous index, versus 41 for the predecessor. The released checkpoint is large, and an independent analysis notes a discrepancy between advertised and configured context lengths.

Baidu releases Unlimited OCR 2 for long-document parsing

Baidu released Unlimited OCR 2, an open model designed to parse lengthy documents across pages. Its model card describes a sparse-reference attention approach and provides inference code and weights. The team also introduced a long-document benchmark; claims about performance and near-constant decoding costs are the developers’ results, not independent validation.

Oracle plans to offer Google TPUs in its cloud

Oracle and Google announced plans to make Google’s AI accelerators available through Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in 2027. The companies say the arrangement will extend TPU access beyond Google’s own hyperscale cloud, with Oracle supplying data-center power, cooling and networking. The announcement is a future availability commitment, not a product already in service.
