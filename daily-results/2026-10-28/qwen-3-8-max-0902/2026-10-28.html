🌐 TOP STORIES

Google DeepMind launches Genie 4, a real-time interactive world model

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, its most realistic and interactive world model yet, generating dynamic environments in real time with native audio, advanced physics, and Gemini-based reasoning that lets users explore and steer scenes live. The company said Genie 4 is built on Gemini Omni for photorealistic detail and has begun rolling it out to trusted testers, with wider access and a public waitlist opening soon.

OpenAI announces three scientific discoveries in math, computer science and physics

OpenAI announced three major scientific discoveries produced by its models with academic partners, one each in mathematics (harmonic analysis), computer science (complexity theory), and physics (quantum field theory). President Greg Brockman said the results show AI starting to help improve our understanding of the universe. Community researchers noted the two mathematicians who proposed the Exponential Time Hypothesis are among those engaging with the complexity result.

Artificial Analysis launches Intelligence Index v5 with mostly private benchmarks

Artificial Analysis released Intelligence Index v5, its biggest upgrade since January, with 60% of the composite now built on private test sets or held-out answers, adding Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0 and Terminal-Bench-Science. Claude Opus 5.5 (max) leads at 58, and the firm reports the open-weights gap widened from 3 points to 12, with Kimi K3.1 Pro the top open-weights model at 46.

Anthropic announces drug-discovery partnerships with six medicine developers

Anthropic said its scientists have engaged in deep research partnerships with six organizations developing new medicines and that it has begun drug-discovery efforts of its own. In an October 27 research post, published the day after its S-1 registration statement became public, the company shared early results on how Claude can support multiple stages of drug development and described its wider life-sciences ambitions.

Liquid AI releases LFM3, an open family of on-device multimodal models

Liquid AI introduced LFM3, a new generation of Liquid Foundation Models built on a tri-hybrid architecture with new multimodal encoders, spanning six model instances across text, audio, vision, and omni modalities plus decision models. The company says LFM3-VL-2B performs competitively with models roughly six times larger, fine-tunes on a single GPU, and ships with open weights and day-zero partner support.
