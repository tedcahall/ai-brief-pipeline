OCT 28, 2026

📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Frontier work split in two directions: Google DeepMind's Genie 4 pushes real-time world simulation with native audio, while Liquid AI's open-weight LFM3 family targets on-device agents. Microsoft's MAI-Image-2.7-Preview reached the top of independent image editing leaderboards.

Measurement also got harder to game: Artificial Analysis moved 60 percent of Intelligence Index v5 behind private test sets and reported the open-weights gap widening from three points to twelve. Chinese labs stayed active, with inclusionAI open-sourcing Ling-3.1-flash-1019 and SparkLLM's compact Spark-X2.6-1.7B picking up day-0 serving support.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Google DeepMind releases Genie 4 world model with native audio and real-time steering

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, a world model that generates interactive environments in real time with native audio, ambient sound and interactive dialogue. It is built on Gemini Omni, with Gemini 3.8 Flash acting as a guiding agent that reviews frames and plans multi-step events. It is rolling out as a research preview to trusted testers, then to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and Gemini API early access partners.

Liquid AI ships LFM3 open-weight family for on-device multimodal agents

Liquid AI introduced LFM3, six open-weight models around 2B parameters covering text, vision, audio, omni and decision tasks on one hybrid backbone pre-trained on 22T tokens. The company says LFM3-1.7B matches or beats larger open models on agentic benchmarks and runs on-device with a 256K context window. Weights are published on Hugging Face, with API and playground access available.

Microsoft's MAI-Image-2.7-Preview lands at number two on image editing leaderboards

Microsoft AI released MAI-Image-2.7-Preview, an image editing model that Microsoft says improves on MAI-Image-2.6 across all 10 evaluated use cases and all 7 editing actions measured by Artificial Analysis. Independent leaderboards place it at number three on Arena Edit and number two on Artificial Analysis image editing, making Microsoft AI the second-ranked lab. It is editing-only for now, with text-to-image described as coming soon.

InclusionAI open-sources Ling-3.1-flash-1019 with 1M token context

InclusionAI open-sourced Ling-3.1-flash-1019, an update to Ling-3.1-flash with 560B total parameters, 25B active and a 1M token context window. The lab reports AA-Briefcase rising from about 1400 to 1524 Elo and AutomationBench-AA from 62 to 68 percent, with CritPt at 26 percent. Weights for Ling-3.1-flash-1019 and a Ling-3.1-flash-base training foundation are published on Hugging Face and ModelScope.

Artificial Analysis puts 60 percent of Intelligence Index v5 behind private test sets

Artificial Analysis released Intelligence Index v5, its largest revision since January 2026, putting 60 percent of the composite score behind private test sets or held-out answers. The release adds Cognition's private FrontierCode 2.0 coding benchmark and a 70-task Terminal-Bench-Science suite, and replaces Humanity's Last Exam with HLE-Diamond. Claude Opus 5.5 leads at 58, and the gap to the best open-weights model widened from three points to twelve.
