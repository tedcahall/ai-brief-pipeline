🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI announces three major scientific discoveries in math, computer science and physics

OpenAI said its models, working with academic communities, produced three discoveries in harmonic analysis, complexity theory, and quantum field theory, including resolving two mathematical conjectures and a result bearing on the Exponential Time Hypothesis. The announcement drew both interest and skepticism from researchers; OpenAI's Greg Brockman separately posted that "p still probably doesn't equal np".

Google DeepMind launches Genie 4 interactive world model

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, a world model built on Gemini Omni that generates photorealistic, interactive simulations with advanced physics, native audio, and Gemini reasoning. It rolls out first to trusted testers, then to Google AI Ultra subscribers, the API, and Google AI Studio, with the company framing it as a step toward virtual environments for testing agents and robots.

Liquid AI open-sources LFM3, six 2B-class foundation models on one backbone

Liquid AI released LFM3, a new generation of open-weight Liquid Foundation Models using a tri-hybrid architecture and new multimodal encoders. The family spans six 2B-class models for text, vision, audio, omni, and decision tasks, each fine-tunable on a single GPU, and runs across GPUs, CPUs, and NPUs including phones and a Raspberry Pi 5.

Artificial Analysis launches Intelligence Index v5, widening the open-weight gap

Artificial Analysis launched Intelligence Index v5, its largest update since January, with two new benchmarks and three upgrades that leave 60 percent of the index built on private datasets. Claude Opus 5.5 leads at 58, ahead of GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol, while the open-weight gap widens from 3 to 12 points.

Anthropic reports early drug-discovery results with six partner organizations

Anthropic shared early results from deep research partnerships with six organizations developing medicines, including BigHat Biosciences and Bristol Myers Squibb, and said it has begun drug discovery efforts of its own. The work spans gene design, manufacturing-document review, and other stages of preclinical development.
