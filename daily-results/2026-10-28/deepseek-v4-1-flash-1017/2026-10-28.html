🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI says its models produced proofs in math, computer science and physics

OpenAI said its models disproved the Exponential Time Hypothesis, proved the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension, which also settles Kakeya and Stein's restriction conjectures, and produced an existence proof for QCD and a unified Standard Model completion including pure Yang-Mills theory. It published the math and computer-science writeups and Lean formalizations, and asked external groups led by Impagliazzo, Paturi, Williams and Gross to verify and explain the results. Independent confirmation is not yet established.

Google DeepMind unveils Genie 4 with native audio and real-time steering

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, a natively multimodal world model that generates photorealistic, walkable environments in real time with ambient sound and interactive character dialogue, built on Gemini Omni with Gemini 3.8 Flash steering scenes as a guiding agent. It is a research preview for trusted testers, with Google AI Ultra subscribers and Gemini API early access to follow. DeepMind notes object permanence and long-session consistency remain limitations.

Anthropic reports early preclinical drug-development results with Claude

Anthropic published early results from research partnerships with BigHat Biosciences, Bristol Myers Squibb, Divergence Labs, Enveda, Generate Biomedicines and The Column Group, and said it has started in-house discovery programs after acquiring one-person company Mithril Medicine. Reported outcomes include a CAR-T design roughly 70 percent shorter that produced about 10 times as many receptors, and documentation review time cut 89 percent. The work is preclinical, with human trials possible as soon as late 2028.

Oracle puts Google TPUs and OpenAI's Jalapeno chip on its cloud

At Oracle AI World, Oracle said its cloud infrastructure will offer Google TPUs for the first time, along with OpenAI's Jalapeno inference chip built with Broadcom, Cerebras accelerators and SambaNova hardware, and that ChatGPT and Codex will be embedded in its applications. CNBC reported it is the first time Google's custom chips run on another hyperscaler's cloud. Cerebras rose about 8 percent and Oracle nearly 5 percent.

Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 widens the open-weights gap

Artificial Analysis released Intelligence Index v5, with 60 percent of the composite now built on private test sets, adding Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0 and a 70-task Terminal-Bench-Science while dropping GDPval-AA. Claude Opus 5.5 leads at 58, ahead of GPT-6 Astra at 57. The open-weights gap widened from 3 to 12 points: Kimi K3.1 Pro scores 46 with weights unreleased, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 and GLM-5.3 lead released-weight models at 32.
