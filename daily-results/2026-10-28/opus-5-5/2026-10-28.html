🌐 TOP STORIES

Google DeepMind unveils Genie 4 world model with native audio and real-time steering

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, a real-time interactive world model that natively generates audio, including ambient sound and character dialogue, and lets users change scenes with text prompts mid-simulation. It builds on Gemini models, with Gemini 3.8 Flash acting as a guiding agent in the loop. Genie 4 is rolling out as a research preview for trusted testers, with Google AI Ultra subscribers and Gemini API early-access partners to follow.

OpenAI says its models disproved the Exponential Time Hypothesis, with Anthropic reporting a separate Claude result

OpenAI announced three AI-produced results: a proof of the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension, which implies the Kakeya conjecture; a Lean-formalized disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis; and an existence proof for QCD that includes pure Yang-Mills theory. Expert groups, including ETH originators Impagliazzo and Paturi, will examine the claims. Anthropic separately published a Claude-authored 3-SAT paper making a similar ETH claim.

Oracle to offer Google TPUs and OpenAI's Jalapeño chip on its cloud

At Oracle AI World, Oracle and Google said Google TPUs will be available through Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, the first time the chips are offered on a hyperscale cloud outside Google. CNBC reported that OCI will also run OpenAI's Jalapeño chip and Cerebras hardware, and that Oracle is integrating ChatGPT and Codex into its applications. An Oracle executive said the move is not a pivot away from Nvidia GPUs.

Anthropic details drug-development partnerships and starts its own drug discovery programs

Anthropic said its scientists have run research partnerships with six drug developers, including Bristol Myers Squibb and Generate Biomedicines, and that it has begun its own drug discovery programs, including work on rare childhood forms of muscular dystrophy. In one reported case, Generate scientists used Claude Code to design a CAR-T component about 70% shorter than the standard version that performed better in lab tests.

Banks and Blackstone arrange $63 billion to finance Broadcom chips leased to Anthropic

Bank of America, Blackstone, Citi and Morgan Stanley announced a $63 billion transaction in which a new special purpose vehicle will buy Broadcom XPU accelerators and networking equipment and lease them to Anthropic, with deliveries expected from calendar 2027. Broadcom is providing residual value support. This is asset financing for compute capacity, not an equity fundraising round by Anthropic.
