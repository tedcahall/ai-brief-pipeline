🌐 TOP STORIES

Google previews Genie 4 with native audio and real-time world steering

Google DeepMind introduced Genie 4, a world model that generates interactive environments with native audio and dialogue. Gemini models help steer scenes and coordinate multi-step events. Access begins with trusted testers; Google AI Ultra and developer access are planned, not generally available. Google acknowledges continuing problems with object permanence, physical interactions and long-term consistency.

Liquid AI releases LFM3 open-weight models for on-device multimodal AI

Liquid AI released six roughly 2B-parameter models spanning text, vision, audio, combined modalities and structured decisions. Their shared architecture combines short convolutions, sliding-window attention and global attention; the text model supports 256K context. Base and post-trained weights are available. Liquid positions the family for local specialization, with performance comparisons coming from its own evaluations.

InclusionAI releases Ling-3.1-flash-1019 and its base model

InclusionAI released an updated open-weight Ling model with 560B total parameters, 25B active per token and a million-token context window, alongside base weights. Its model card reports stronger agentic and scientific performance after additional post-training. Artificial Analysis independently reports a rise from 41 to 44 on its older v4.3 index, alongside higher output-token use and cost per task.

OpenAI releases scientific proof claims for academic scrutiny

OpenAI announced AI-generated results concerning local smoothing, the Exponential Time Hypothesis and quantum field theory. It released natural-language and Lean artifacts for the mathematics and computer-science claims, while a physics working group controls its materials. OpenAI describes the manuscripts as raw research data; independent academic examination remains important. The physics claim concerns existence, not a demonstrated solution to the full Yang-Mills mass-gap problem.

Anthropic details drug-development partnerships and acquires Mithril Medicine

Anthropic disclosed work with six drug-development organizations and its acquisition of Mithril Medicine, a one-person company pursuing rare muscular-dystrophy treatments. Partner case studies span molecule design, lab optimization, manufacturing documentation and trial planning. Anthropic says most work remains preclinical; promising laboratory or mouse results do not establish safe, effective human treatments.
