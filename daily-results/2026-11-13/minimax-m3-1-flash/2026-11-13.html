🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen for Rapid Coding and Agentic Work

New developments: Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12 for rapid coding, multimodal synthesis and everyday agentic workflows. Google says the model handles 1M-token contexts, completes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and is priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens.

Anthropic Files for an IPO, Targeting a November 19 Nasdaq Debut

New developments: Anthropic filed a registration statement with the SEC and published its IPO site, which says the roadshow began November 10. The company says shares under the ticker ANT are expected to start trading November 19, subject to regulatory clearance, and reported $17.0 billion in Q3 2026 revenue.

OpenAI Launches Daybreak AI Tools for Red Teaming and Vulnerability Remediation

OpenAI introduced Daybreak Red Team and Daybreak Defense Factory. Red Team uses coordinated agents to test web applications, validate vulnerabilities and check patches, while Defense Factory investigates findings and prepares patches for engineer review. Red Team is in early access and the Defense Factory is rolling out as a preview.

AI Safety Groups Sue Five Frontier AI Companies for a Development Pause

Four nonprofit groups filed a civil complaint in San Francisco Superior Court against Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceX. The plaintiffs seek an emergency injunction pausing new model training and deployment until safeguards can be demonstrated, and say they will pursue federal evaluators, strict liability and an independent regulator.

SGLang Launches a Consortium Backed by Major AI Infrastructure Companies

LMSYS Org launched the SGLang Consortium with NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Google, Meta, Cloudflare, Modal and RadixArk. The founding members will contribute technical representatives, deployment feedback, testing resources and joint engineering work on frontier-model support, reasoning, multimodal and distributed inference, while SGLang maintainers retain technical decisions and code acceptance.
