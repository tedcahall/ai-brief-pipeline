🌐 TOP STORIES

Four nonprofits sue five AI developers seeking a halt to frontier-model development

Four nonprofits sued OpenAI, Anthropic, SpaceX’s AI division, Meta and Google in San Francisco, asking a court to bar further development of leading AI models. Reuters reports the complaint relies on California unfair-business-practices law and statements by company leaders about potential risks. The requested order is not a ruling, and claims in the complaint remain allegations.

Google launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen for agentic workflows

Google introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen as an everyday model for coding and agentic work, with four reasoning levels and rollout across Gemini, Search, AI Studio and the Gemini API. Google says it completes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash; that comparative figure is the company’s claim. The announcement describes broader access alongside paid-product integrations.

Anthropic brings Claude into live team meetings

Anthropic is rolling out Claude Meetings in beta for Team and Enterprise customers, initially with Google Meet. Claude appears as a visible participant, can follow the conversation and begin creating documents or whiteboards while the call is underway; notes, action items and transcripts are also available afterward. The announcement says additional meeting platforms are planned, not yet supported.

OpenAI opens Business Hub and customer-facing agents in ChatGPT beta

OpenAI is testing Business Hub, where eligible businesses can claim profiles, update details and product feeds, and view aggregated mentions and site clicks. Eligible US ecommerce and online-service businesses can also build Business Agents to answer customer questions. OpenAI says the insights omit individual conversations and require minimum activity thresholds.

Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Small weights under Apache 2.0

Thinking Machines Lab says Inkling 2 Small weights are now downloadable on Hugging Face under the Apache 2.0 license. The release makes the model available for developers to use and adapt under that license, beyond hosted access. The lab’s announcement establishes the availability and license; performance rankings elsewhere are not independently asserted here.
