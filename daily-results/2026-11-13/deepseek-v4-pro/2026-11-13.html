🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a Speed-First Model for Agentic Workflows

Google introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, its new model for everyday coding, multimodal synthesis and agentic workflows. The company says it finishes tasks 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and surpasses Gemini 4 Argon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. It offers four thinking levels and rolls out to Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, the Gemini app, Search, and paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang Resigns; CFO Colette Kress Named Interim CEO

NVIDIA announced Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive officer and a board member, effective immediately, and named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO during the search for a successor. The departure follows reports of extramarital relationships that had prompted the board to weigh leadership changes.

Anthropic Sets IPO Price Range at $70 to $78, Targeting a November 19 Nasdaq Listing

Anthropic set a price range of $70 to $78 per share for its initial public offering, planning to sell 825 million Class A shares, according to an updated SEC filing. The range implies a valuation up to about $1.4 trillion, with trading expected to begin on Nasdaq under ticker ANT around November 19.

OpenAI Ships Daybreak Red Team and Defense Factory Cybersecurity Agents

OpenAI launched Daybreak Red Team and Daybreak Defense Factory, two products that deploy AI agents to pen-test web applications and prepare merge-ready fixes. Red Team is in early access and Defense Factory runs on GPT-6 Cyber, extending OpenAI's push into automated vulnerability discovery and remediation.

Tencent's Hy4 Tops CWE-Bench-AA and Ranks #3 on the AA Cyber Index

Tencent's Hy4 scored 76% on CWE-Bench-AA, the top result on the vulnerability-finding benchmark and six points ahead of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017, according to Artificial Analysis. The model also ranked third on the AA Cyber Index at 58%, trailing Kimi K3.1 Pro and GPT-6 Sol, with API pricing of $0.834 per million input tokens.
