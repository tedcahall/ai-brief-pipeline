🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen With Four Thinking Levels and 1M Context

Google introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a multimodal model built for rapid coding and agentic execution across a 1 million-token context window at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens. Featuring four thinking levels including a new xhigh tier, Hydrogen completes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and scores 51 at high and 54 at xhigh on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.

Anthropic Launches IPO Roadshow With $17B Q3 Revenue and Nasdaq Ticker ANT

Anthropic launched the roadshow for its initial public offering of Class A common stock under the Nasdaq ticker ANT, with final pricing expected on November 18 and trading set for November 19. In its IPO disclosures, the public benefit corporation reported $17.0 billion in third-quarter 2026 revenue, up more than 12 times year over year, alongside over 11,900 customers generating at least $100,000 in annual revenue.

Jensen Huang Resigns as NVIDIA CEO; CFO Colette Kress Named Interim Chief

NVIDIA announced that founder Jensen Huang has resigned as president, chief executive officer, and board member effective immediately. Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress was appointed interim CEO while retaining her CFO duties as the board searches for a permanent successor, and longtime director Harvey C. Jones was named NVIDIA's first Chair of the Board.

OpenAI Unveils Daybreak Defense Factory and Red Team for Agentic Cybersecurity

OpenAI announced two new cybersecurity products under its Daybreak initiative: Daybreak Defense Factory and Daybreak Red Team. Defense Factory deploys AI agents with Codex to trace vulnerabilities across codebases and prepare patches for engineering review, while Red Team uses coordinated agents in isolated environments to conduct web application penetration testing, validate reported bugs, and verify that patches block exploits.

Thinking Machines Releases Open-Weight 594B MoE Inkling 2 Small Under Apache 2.0

New developments: Following last week's preview, Thinking Machines released the open weights of Inkling 2 Small on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license with day-zero vLLM support. The 48-layer multimodal Mixture-of-Experts model features 594 billion total and 17 billion active parameters, a 1 million-token context window, mixed NVFP4/MXFP8 precision to fit on a single GB200/GB300 node, and a native DSpark speculator.
