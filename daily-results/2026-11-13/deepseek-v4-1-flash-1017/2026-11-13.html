🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Replaces CEO Jensen Huang With Interim Chief Colette Kress

Nvidia's board said Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive and board member effective immediately, ending a 33-year tenure at the company he co-founded. Chief financial officer Colette Kress, who joined in 2013, becomes interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor, and investor Harvey Jones was named the board's first chair. Nvidia said operations, customer commitments and its product roadmap continue without interruption.

Anthropic Opens IPO Roadshow at $70 to $78 a Share on Nasdaq

Anthropic launched the roadshow for its proposed initial public offering, offering 825 million Class A shares at an expected $70 to $78 each, 801.35 million from the company and 23.65 million from existing stockholders. Anthropic applied to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market as ANT and expects a 30-day underwriter option for up to 123.75 million more shares. The registration statement is not yet effective, and Anthropic receives no proceeds from selling stockholders.

Google Releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen as a Speed-First Model Priced Below Argon

Hydrogen is a separate model from Gemini 4 Carbon, released two days earlier, and Google says it surpasses Gemini 4 Argon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 51 versus 50 while finishing tasks 2.5 times faster than 3.8 Flash. It carries a 1-million-token context, four thinking levels including a new xhigh setting, and flat pricing of $1.50 per million input and $7.50 per million output tokens.

Four Nonprofits Sue Five AI Labs Seeking an Injunction on Frontier Models

The Alliance for Secure AI, Irreplaceable Education Fund, Public Citizen and the Heat Initiative sued Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceXSI in San Francisco Superior Court, seeking an order halting development and deployment of models more capable than current frontier systems. The complaint invokes California's unfair competition law and public nuisance doctrine and quotes the companies' own executives on catastrophic risk. The defendants did not immediately comment.

Weight Forensics Tie Six Chinese Open Models to Export-Controlled Blackwell GPUs

Unemployment Labs, led by former OpenAI researcher Will DePue, published analysis finding initialization patterns matching Nvidia's Blackwell GPUs in six open-weight models from DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax and Ant Group. The report says the evidence supports but does not prove that Blackwell also ran training, cannot establish chip location, ownership or acquisition, and does not by itself show an export-control violation. Newer Alibaba, Zhipu, Tencent, Xiaomi and StepFun models read as Hopper.
