🌐 TOP STORIES

Google DeepMind Releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen Across API, Gemini App and Search

Google DeepMind rolled out Gemini 4 Hydrogen globally across AI Studio, the Gemini API, the Gemini app, Search and Google Antigravity, priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens across a 1M-token context window. Google reports the model finishes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash, raises Terminal-Bench 4.1 from 18.2% to 57%, and adds an xhigh thinking tier that scores 54 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index.

Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO; CFO Colette Kress Named Interim Chief

Nvidia's board announced that co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive and board member effective immediately amid an independent probe and press reports of extramarital relationships involving a suspected Chinese intelligence asset and two employees. Executive vice president and CFO Colette Kress assumed the interim CEO role during a permanent search, Synopsys veteran Harvey C. Jones became Nvidia's first board chair, and Nvidia shares closed down 4.3% near a $4 trillion valuation.

Anthropic Opens Public IPO Portal Ahead of Expected November 19 Nasdaq Debut

Anthropic launched its public IPO portal with its founders' letter and roadshow presentation ahead of expected November 18 pricing and a November 19 Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT, subject to regulatory clearance. Amended SEC filings offer 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 each alongside a contingent $26 billion placement, while Anthropic highlighted $17.0 billion in third-quarter revenue, over 11,900 six-figure customers and $23.3 billion in GAAP net loss.

Thinking Machines Open-Sources 594B-Parameter Inkling 2 Small Under Apache 2.0

Following last week's hosted launch, Thinking Machines Lab released the open weights of Inkling 2 Small on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license in native NVFP4 and MXFP8 checkpoints with day-zero support in vLLM and SGLang. The 48-layer multimodal mixture-of-experts model pairs 594B total and 17B active parameters with a 1M-token context window and DSpark speculative decoding, debuting as the top US open-weight model on Code Arena WebDev and Agent Arena.

OpenAI Launches Daybreak Defense Factory and Daybreak Red Team for Cyber Defense

OpenAI announced two agentic cybersecurity products aimed at enterprise defense: Daybreak Defense Factory and early-access Daybreak Red Team. Defense Factory connects to code repositories and security scanners so agents can trace vulnerabilities and prepare Codex-tuned patches for engineer review, while Red Team deploys coordinated agents inside isolated environments with Guardian scope controls to test web applications, validate reported flaws and confirm that fixes stop exploits.
