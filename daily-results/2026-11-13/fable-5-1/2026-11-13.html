🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen as Jensen Huang Resigns From Nvidia and Anthropic Opens Its IPO Roadshow

Google introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, built for rapid execution in coding and agentic workflows. Google says it finishes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its default high setting, versus 50 for Gemini 4 Argon. It is priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens and is available in the Gemini API, the Gemini app and Search.

Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO; CFO Colette Kress Named Interim Chief

New developments: Nvidia's board said Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and a board member effective immediately, days after the company opened an independent probe into his admitted affair. CFO Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor, and Harvey C. Jones, a former Synopsys CEO, was appointed Nvidia's first-ever board chair. Nvidia said its third-quarter results will still be reported on November 17 as scheduled.

Anthropic Opens IPO Roadshow With $17.0 Billion Q3 Revenue, Targets November 19 Nasdaq Debut as ANT

Anthropic published its IPO site and founders' letter, reporting third-quarter revenue of $17.0 billion, up more than 12x year over year, with more than 11,900 customers above $100,000 in trailing revenue and a $23.3 billion net loss that includes a $21.9 billion non-cash charge tied to convertible notes, derivative liabilities and SAFEs. The company says pricing is expected on November 18 and Nasdaq trading under ANT on November 19, subject to regulatory clearance.

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Small Weights on Hugging Face Under Apache 2.0

New developments: Thinking Machines released the full Inkling 2 Small weights on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license, after external safety testing. The 594B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 17B active parameters accepts text and image input and ships in native NVFP4 and MXFP8 checkpoints, with day-zero serving recipes in vLLM and SGLang. Arena.ai lists it as the top US open model on both its WebDev and Agent Arena leaderboards.

Nonprofits Sue Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceX to Halt New Frontier AI Models

The Irreplaceable Education Fund, Public Citizen, Heat Initiative and the Alliance for Secure AI filed a civil complaint in San Francisco Superior Court on Thursday asking a judge to stop the five companies from developing or deploying new AI models until they can show the systems remain under human control. The suit alleges violations of California's unfair competition law and cites documented AI-driven cyberattacks alongside the companies' own public warnings about catastrophic risk.
