🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen for Coding and Agentic Workflows

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen across AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business, the Gemini app, Search and paid Antigravity tiers. Google says it completes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash, offers four thinking levels and supports a one-million-token context window. Pricing is $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens.

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Small Weights Under Apache 2.0

New developments: Thinking Machines fulfilled last week’s planned release by publishing Inkling 2 Small’s weights on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0. The multimodal MoE model has 594B total and 17B active parameters, accepts text and images, and supports a one-million-token context. Its model card lists SGLang, vLLM, TokenSpeed and Unsloth local-inference support.

Anthropic Announces Initial Public Offering and Files Preliminary Prospectus

Anthropic announced its initial public offering and pointed investors to its SEC registration statement and preliminary prospectus. The company cautioned that securities cannot be sold and offers to buy cannot be accepted before the registration statement becomes effective. The SEC filing identifies the offering as Class A common stock of Anthropic, PBC.

Anthropic Brings Claude Meetings to Google Meet in Beta

Claude Meetings can join Google Meet calls as a visible participant, take notes, answer questions and create documents, whiteboards and slides during the discussion. The beta is available on Team and Enterprise plans, with administrative controls for outside guests. Anthropic says it stores transcripts, summaries and screen descriptions rather than audio.

Salesforce Releases Open-Weight SOV-Transcribe-1 Speech Model

Salesforce released SOV-Transcribe-1 under Apache 2.0 for non-streaming speech recognition with natural-language control over transcription style and context biasing. The 26.6B-parameter model uses 4.6B active parameters and supports seven languages. Salesforce and Artificial Analysis rank it first among open-weight models for non-streaming speech-to-text as of November 12.
