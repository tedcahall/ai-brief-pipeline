🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen for Fast Coding and Agentic Work

Google introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a speed-focused model built to run everyday coding and agentic tasks alone or as the execution engine beside Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon. Google says it scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its default high setting and 54 at xhigh, versus 50 for Argon, finishes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and costs $1.50 and $7.50 per million input and output tokens.

Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO, CFO Colette Kress Named Interim Chief

New developments: following the board investigation reported earlier this week, Nvidia's board said Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO while the board considers internal and external candidates, and former Synopsys chief Harvey C. Jones becomes the board's first chair. Nvidia said third-quarter results remain scheduled for November 17.

Anthropic Sets $70 to $78 IPO Price Range Ahead of Expected Nasdaq Debut

Anthropic set an expected IPO price range of $70 to $78 per share in an amended SEC filing on Tuesday, implying a fully diluted valuation of roughly $1.3 trillion at the midpoint, CBC reports. The company aims to raise up to $64.4 billion, and a Nasdaq listing notice schedules its debut for November 19, although Anthropic's SEC filings have not yet finalized a date.

OpenAI Launches Daybreak Red Team and Defense Factory Security Agents

OpenAI introduced two Daybreak cybersecurity products at its Cyber at the Frontier event on November 12. Daybreak Red Team uses multiple AI agents in isolated environments to investigate attack paths and confirm web application vulnerabilities, while Defense Factory traces findings through code and drafts patches that engineers review before merging. Red Team is in early access, and Defense Factory ships as a preview ChatGPT plugin.

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Small Weights Under Apache 2.0

New developments: a week after launching Inkling 2 Small on its Tinker platform, Thinking Machines published the model's weights on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license. vLLM, which added day-zero support, describes a 594B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 17B active parameters, text and image input and up to 1 million tokens of context. Arena ranks it the top US open model on Code Arena WebDev.
