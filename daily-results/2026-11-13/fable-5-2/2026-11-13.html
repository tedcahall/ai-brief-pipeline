🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen Across the Gemini API, Gemini App and Search

Google introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, built for speed in everyday coding and agentic workflows. Google says it finishes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its default high thinking level, versus 50 for Gemini 4 Argon, and 54 at a new xhigh level. Pricing is $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens across a 1 million-token context window.

Nvidia's Jensen Huang Resigns as CEO, CFO Colette Kress Named Interim Chief

Nvidia's board announced that Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately. Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO while keeping her CFO role, and the board will consider internal and external candidates for a permanent successor. Harvey C. Jones was appointed the board's first chair. Nvidia said it will report fiscal third-quarter results on November 17 as previously scheduled.

OpenAI Introduces Daybreak Red Team and Defense Factory Security Agents

OpenAI announced two Daybreak cybersecurity products at its Cyber at the Frontier event on November 12, SecurityWeek reported. Daybreak Red Team uses multiple AI agents in isolated environments to investigate attack paths and confirm vulnerabilities in web applications. Daybreak Defense Factory traces findings through code and prepares patches that engineers review before merge, with no automatic deployment. Red Team is in early access, and Defense Factory is a preview plugin in ChatGPT.

Advocacy Groups Sue Five AI Developers to Halt New Models

Four advocacy groups, the Irreplaceable Education Fund, Public Citizen, Heat Initiative and the Alliance for Secure AI, sued Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceXSI in San Francisco Superior Court on Thursday, the Associated Press reported. The complaint asks a judge to stop the companies from developing or deploying new AI models, alleging they cannot prevent their systems from causing catastrophic harm.

Anthropic Opens Public IPO Site, Lists $17.0 Billion in Third-Quarter Revenue

New developments: Anthropic published a public IPO site with its founders' letter, roadshow video and financial highlights, two days after its roadshow began. The site lists third-quarter 2026 revenue of $17.0 billion, up more than 12 times year over year, and a $23.3 billion net loss that includes a $21.9 billion non-cash fair-value charge. Anthropic expects pricing on November 18 and Nasdaq trading as ANT on November 19, subject to regulatory clearance.
