Google Launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a Speed-First Model That Surpasses Argon

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a speed-first model for everyday coding and agentic workflows. Google says it surpasses Gemini 4 Argon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at default high thinking, 51 vs 50 and reaching 54 at the new xhigh level, and finishes tasks 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash. Pricing is flat at $1.50/$7.50 per million tokens, spanning AI Studio, the Gemini API, the Gemini app, Search and Antigravity.

Jensen Huang Resigns as NVIDIA CEO; CFO Colette Kress Named Interim Chief

NVIDIA's board announced on November 12 that Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and director effective immediately, following media reports of extramarital affairs. CFO Colette Kress becomes interim CEO while the board searches internal and external candidates, and Harvey C. Jones was named the company's first-ever board chair. NVIDIA said operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap continue without interruption, with fiscal Q3 results still scheduled for November 17.

Anthropic Sets IPO Price Range of $70 to $78 Per Share Ahead of Nasdaq Debut

Anthropic will offer 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 each in its upcoming IPO, according to an updated SEC filing, implying a fully diluted valuation of up to $1.404 trillion and a raise of up to $64.4 billion. The company announced the offering publicly on X on November 12. It plans to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, with trading expected to begin November 19, per CNBC sources.

OpenAI Launches Daybreak Red Team and Defense Factory Cybersecurity Agents

OpenAI president Greg Brockman announced two new cybersecurity products on November 12: Daybreak Red Team, which uses AI agents to probe web applications and validate vulnerabilities in early access, and Daybreak Defense Factory, where agents trace vulnerabilities through code and prepare patches for engineer review. OpenAI says Defense Factory works with Codex and existing security scanners and supports continuous scanning of new commits.

Nonprofits Sue OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and SpaceX to Freeze Advanced AI

A coalition including Public Citizen, the Alliance for Secure AI, the Irreplaceable Education Fund and the Heat Initiative sued Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceX in San Francisco Superior Court on Thursday, seeking an emergency injunction to freeze training and deployment of next-generation models until the companies prove human control. The complaint alleges the labs cannot prevent catastrophic harm and invokes California unfair-competition law.
