🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Resigns as CFO Colette Kress Becomes Interim CEO

Nvidia's board announced that Jensen Huang has resigned as president, chief executive and director, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO while remaining CFO, and Harvey C. Jones becomes the board's first chair. The board is searching for a permanent CEO, and Nvidia says third-quarter fiscal 2027 results remain scheduled for November 17. The New York Times reported the resignation followed revelations of affairs.

Anthropic IPO Site Lists $17.0 Billion Third-Quarter Revenue and 11,900 Large Customers

Anthropic's IPO site, which it linked on November 12 alongside its SEC registration statement notice, reports third-quarter revenue of $17.0 billion, more than 12 times the prior year, and over 11,900 customers with at least $100,000 in trailing revenue. It lists a $23.3 billion net loss that includes a $21.9 billion non-cash charge for changes in the fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities and SAFEs, and a 15% adjusted operating margin.

Google Releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a Faster Model Priced at $1.50 and $7.50 per Million Tokens

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen across the Gemini API, AI Studio, the Gemini app, Search and Antigravity, positioning it as the fast execution model beside Carbon and Argon. Google says it scores 51 against Argon's 50 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its default setting, with flat pricing of $1.50 input and $7.50 output per million tokens across a 1 million-token context window.

Advocacy Groups Sue Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceXSI to Halt New AI Models

The Irreplaceable Education Fund, Public Citizen, Heat Initiative and the Alliance for Secure AI sued in San Francisco Superior Court, asking a judge to stop Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceXSI from developing or deploying new AI models. They allege violations of California's unfair competition law and cite AI-driven cyberattacks and the companies' own risk acknowledgments. The excerpt read did not include company responses.

OpenAI Launches Daybreak Red Team and Defense Factory for AI-Driven Vulnerability Testing and Patching

OpenAI announced Daybreak Red Team and Daybreak Defense Factory at its November 12 Cyber at the Frontier event. Red Team uses multiple agents in isolated environments to confirm whether reported vulnerabilities are exploitable, while Defense Factory traces flaws through connected GitHub repositories and drafts patches for engineer review before merge. Red Team access is by request, and Defense Factory is a preview plugin in ChatGPT workspaces with Daybreak access.
