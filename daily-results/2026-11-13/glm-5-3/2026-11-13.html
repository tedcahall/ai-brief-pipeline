🌐 TOP STORIES

Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO Amid Spy Scandal; CFO Colette Kress Named Interim Leader

Jensen Huang resigned as CEO of Nvidia, which supplies most of the chips powering advanced AI, after reported affairs with a suspected Chinese spy and a company executive. The board named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO and venture investor Harvey C Jones as its first board chair, and Nvidia's market value fell below $4 trillion amid FBI, congressional and internal investigations plus a shareholder class action.

Anthropic Advances Its IPO, Planning a Nasdaq Listing Under "ANT"

Anthropic filed a Form S-1 registration statement with the SEC for an initial public offering of Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley as underwriters. The registration is not yet effective, and IPO trackers report pricing expected November 18 with trading expected to begin November 19.

Google Launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen for Fast Everyday Coding and Agentic Work

New developments: Google introduced a new Gemini 4 series model, Hydrogen, distinct from the Carbon availability covered November 11. Google says Hydrogen finishes tasks 2.5x faster than 3.8 Flash and surpasses Argon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, and it is rolling out to all users in the Gemini app, Google Search and AI Studio, with all API tiers available to developers.

Nonprofits Sue Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceX to Freeze Frontier AI

Four nonprofits, including the Irreplaceable Education Fund and Public Citizen, filed a civil complaint in San Francisco Superior Court seeking an emergency injunction pausing next-generation model training and deployment at five AI giants until they can prove the technology is under human control. The suit cites California unfair competition law and documented AI-driven cyberattacks.

Hark Introduces Handoff², a Next-Generation Computer Use Model

Hark announced Handoff², its next-generation computer use model, saying it is 2x faster than the previous model, 80% better at solving CAPTCHAs and 10% stronger on end-to-end browser tasks, and claiming speed and capability ahead of Opus 5.5 high. These are company claims and have not been independently verified.
