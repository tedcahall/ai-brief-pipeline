🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Introduces Gemini 4 Hydrogen for Everyday Coding

New developments: Gemini 4 Hydrogen is a separate November 12 release, not the earlier Gemini 4 Carbon rollout. Google says it finishes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash at the default high setting, scores 51 versus Argon's 50 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, and reaches 54 at a new xhigh level. It is priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens across a 1 million-token window.

Anthropic Files for an IPO With Nasdaq Ticker ANT

Anthropic's preliminary prospectus, dated November 10 and marked subject to completion, covers a Class A IPO and a Nasdaq listing application under ANT, at an estimated $70 to $78 a share. It reports $17.0 billion of revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and a contingent $26 billion Class N placement to buyers including Nvidia, Microsoft, Google, and TSMC. The registration is not effective, and the price is not final.

Claude Meetings Joins Calls and Drafts Docs in Beta

Anthropic is rolling out Claude Meetings in beta on Team and Enterprise plans, starting with Google Meet. Claude joins as a visible participant, answers in writing by default, and can draft documents, whiteboards, and notes during the call. The page says notes and in-call answers are free in beta, and call artifacts are free through February 10, 2027. Zero-data-retention, HIPAA-ready, and customer-managed-key organizations are excluded.

Nonprofits Sue Five AI Labs Seeking a Development Freeze

Al Jazeera reported that the Irreplaceable Education Fund, Public Citizen, Heat Initiative, and the Alliance for Secure AI sued Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI, and SpaceX on November 12 in San Francisco Superior Court. They seek a pause on frontier training and deployment until the companies show the systems are under human control, citing California unfair competition law. No ruling has been issued.

Thinking Machines Publishes Inkling Small Weights

Thinking Machines posted on November 12 that Inkling 2 Small weights are on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license. The model card describes a multimodal model that takes text and images and generates text, for agentic tools, coding, and retrieval. It says the main NVFP4 checkpoint needs at least 500GB of aggregated VRAM, such as two B300 GPUs or four H200 GPUs. This weights drop follows the earlier model announcement.
