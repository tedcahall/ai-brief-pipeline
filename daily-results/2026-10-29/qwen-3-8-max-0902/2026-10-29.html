📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Google launched Gemini 4 Carbon, a new frontier model it says is state of the art in cybersecurity and long horizon work, and independent testing from Artificial Analysis puts it at No. 1 on the rebuilt Intelligence Index v5, ahead of Claude Opus 5.5, though public access is still limited.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Google launches Carbon, a frontier model built for cybersecurity and long-horizon agents

Google launched Gemini 4 Carbon, its next frontier model, saying it is state of the art across cybersecurity and long horizon capabilities. Artificial Analysis independently ranked it No. 1 on the rebuilt Intelligence Index v5 with a score of 59, and second only to Claude Opus 5.5 on the Coding Agent Index. Carbon rolls out to selected users with a 50 percent launch discount, and Google will iterate on guardrails with cyber defenders before expanding access.

GitHub launches Copilot Fusion to orchestrate Anthropic and OpenAI coding models

At GitHub Universe, GitHub announced Copilot Fusion, which orchestrates Anthropic and OpenAI models and is described as delivering frontier level quality 40 percent faster at up to 77 percent lower cost. Artificial Analysis measured Fusion two points below Claude Code with Opus 5.5 on its Coding Agent Index at roughly half the cost per task. GitHub also introduced a unified Copilot harness in Azure Foundry and Copilot Projects for multi agent orchestration across repositories.

ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers gain up to $500 in monthly API credits

Eligible ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers can now claim up to $500 per month in OpenAI API credits to build apps and agents. Pro tiers receive $100, $200 or $500 monthly by plan, while Business workspaces get $20 per paid Standard seat or $100 per paid Premium seat, pooled up to $500 per workspace. Credits are claimed through ChatGPT billing after linking the subscription to an API organization the subscriber owns.

Mistral previews an agent-focused Responses API and adds DeepSeek and GLM to its cloud

Mistral AI opened public preview of its Responses API, built for agents, returning the model answer, its reasoning and tool calls as separate items so applications can handle each step more easily than with Chat Completions. It works with all chat models Mistral hosts, including Large 4. Mistral also began serving DeepSeek V4.1 Flash in public preview and GLM 5.3 Flash, now generally available, on Mistral AI Cloud Inference in the EU and US.

Open-weight Nemotron Transcribe leads English speech recognition leaderboard

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open weight, promptable 30B MoE speech model with about 4B active parameters covering transcription in 40 languages plus speaker attribution, translation, contextual prompting and audio understanding in one checkpoint. It ranks No. 1 among open weight models on the Hugging Face Open ASR English leaderboard with 5.33 percent average word error rate, and Artificial Analysis ties it with Voxtral Small for the open weight lead.
