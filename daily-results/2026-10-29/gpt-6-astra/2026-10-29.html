🌐 TOP STORIES

Google previews Gemini 4 Carbon for cyber defenders

New developments: Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon, a frontier model distinct from yesterday’s Genie 4 world-model preview. Artificial Analysis reports a score of 59 on Intelligence Index v5, ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 at 58. Access initially goes to selected cyber defenders, not the general public. Google lists introductory pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.

NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe for multilingual audio

NVIDIA released an open-weight speech and audio model covering transcription in 40 languages, speaker attribution, translation and audio understanding. The model card describes offline processing of recordings up to one hour, a 65,536-token context and 34 billion total parameters including the audio encoder, with about 4.3 billion active. Optimized serving-performance claims include components not yet upstreamed into the open-source release.

Mistral opens Responses API preview for agent applications

Mistral introduced a public-preview Responses API that separates answers, reasoning and tool calls, using the OpenAI Responses format. Its documentation covers streaming, structured output and client-executed functions; applications must retain conversation history because the API is stateless. Mistral also announced hosting for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash in public preview and GLM 5.3 Flash in general availability, served in the EU and US.

GitHub launches Copilot Fusion and cross-repository agent projects

At Universe, GitHub introduced Copilot Fusion to coordinate models for different coding tasks, alongside projects that manage parallel agent sessions across repositories. Artificial Analysis measured Fusion at $5.59 per task, versus $11.42 for Claude Code with Opus 5.5 at maximum effort, with Fusion two index points behind. These are benchmark-specific results, not a guarantee of equivalent savings on every workload.

Formation Bio announces $600 million drug-development fund

Formation Bio announced $600 million in capital commitments for a fund combining its AI development platform with clinical operating infrastructure. The vehicle targets 10 to 15 drug programs over three to five years, housing each in a separate asset company. This is a dedicated drug-development fund, not a $600 million equity round in Formation Bio; improved clinical success remains an objective, not an established outcome.
