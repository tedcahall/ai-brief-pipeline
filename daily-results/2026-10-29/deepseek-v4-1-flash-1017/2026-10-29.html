🌐 TOP STORIES

Google releases Gemini 4 Carbon, a cybersecurity-focused frontier model

Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon on October 28, calling it its strongest model for cybersecurity and long-horizon work. Google says Carbon ranks first on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 at 59, with 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for finding vulnerabilities and 71.0% on CWE-bench for patching. Access begins with a subset of cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program before wider release, and Artificial Analysis notes the model is not yet publicly available. Introductory pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.

Anthropic discloses six drug development partnerships and starts its own discovery programs

Anthropic published early drug development results on October 27, describing research partnerships with BigHat Biosciences, Bristol Myers Squibb, Divergence Labs, Enveda, Generate Biomedicines and The Column Group. Anthropic also said it has begun its own discovery programs, including rare childhood forms of muscular dystrophy. Divergence reported roughly 35,000 antibody designs generated in about a month, and Generate said one Claude-assisted design was about 70% shorter and produced about 10 times as many receptors in lab tests.

NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open-weight 30B speech model

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe on October 28, an open-weight 30B mixture-of-experts speech model with about 4B active parameters. NVIDIA says it handles 40 languages, translation for 31 languages, recordings up to an hour and up to eight speakers, and can run on a single Jetson AGX Thor, DGX Spark or Blackwell chip. TechCrunch notes it works on recorded audio rather than livestreams. NVIDIA and Artificial Analysis both rank it first among open-weight speech models.

Microsoft reports Azure revenue up 48% with commercial backlog at $677 billion

Microsoft reported fiscal first-quarter results on October 28 with Microsoft Cloud revenue of $63.9 billion, up 30% year over year, and Azure revenue up 48%. Commercial remaining performance obligation rose 73% to $677 billion, and revenue in the Agents and Infra segment reached $76.8 billion, up 25%. Net income was $36.6 billion on a GAAP basis, up 32%, with diluted earnings per share of $4.92. Results exclude the impact from investments in OpenAI on a non-GAAP basis.

New York enacts AI chatbot safety law restricting features for minors

New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed S 9051 on October 28, a law barring chatbot operators from offering unsafe features to users under 18, including pretending to be human, building emotional dependency, encouraging self-harm and sexually explicit conversation. The sponsors were Senator Kristin Gonzalez and Assemblymember Alex Bores. Transparency Coalition reports the law takes effect early next year.
