📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Google introduced Gemini 4 Carbon with a staged rollout beginning with cyber defenders, while NVIDIA released an open-weight multilingual transcription model. AssemblyAI also launched a text-to-speech model for voice agents.

GitHub made its multi-model Copilot Fusion system generally available, and Formation Bio announced a $600 million drug-development fund. Separately, researchers report that simple images can steer some vision-language models on unrelated coding tasks, a preliminary result rather than a demonstrated real-world attack.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Google introduces Gemini 4 Carbon for cyber defenders

Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon, initially offering the model to a subset of cyber defenders rather than the general public. Google reports improvements in long-running coding and security tasks. Artificial Analysis ranked Carbon first on its Intelligence Index v5 at announcement; benchmark results measure specific tests, not every real-world workload.

NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe speech model

NVIDIA released an open-weight speech model that combines transcription, speaker attribution and translation across 40 languages. Its research page says the model handles recordings up to an hour and uses roughly 4.3 billion active parameters. NVIDIA provides checkpoints and a technical report; its benchmark claims are vendor-reported.

AssemblyAI launches its first text-to-speech model

AssemblyAI says its TTS-1 model is generally available for voice-agent developers at $15 per million characters. The company offers a voice library and instant voice cloning, and reports a 90-millisecond production time to first audio. Performance and latency depend on the evaluation and network conditions.

GitHub makes Copilot Fusion generally available

GitHub made Copilot Fusion generally available, combining model selection and multi-model orchestration for coding tasks. Users select an effort level while the system routes or coordinates models; administrators can control permitted models. GitHub says users are billed for the underlying models, with a temporary discount for paid plans.

Formation Bio commits $600 million fund to drug development

Formation Bio announced a drug-development fund with $600 million in capital commitments. The company plans to combine the fund with its AI-enabled platform and clinical operations to pursue 10 to 15 drug programs over three to five years. The amount describes commitments to a dedicated investment fund, not an equity financing round.
