🌐 TOP STORIES

Google DeepMind launches Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest frontier model

Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest frontier model with state-of-the-art cybersecurity and long-horizon capabilities, built for agentic work such as root-causing and patching subtle bugs across large codebases. Artificial Analysis measured it as the new #1 on Intelligence Index v5 at 59, leading four of the nine evaluations, and second to Claude Opus 5.5 on the Coding Agent Index. The 1M-token-context model takes text, image, video and speech input and is priced at $4/$20 per million input/output tokens with a 50% launch discount; it is rolling out first to selected users and is not yet publicly available.

GitHub makes Copilot Fusion generally available at Universe 2026

GitHub made Copilot Fusion generally available at GitHub Universe 2026, a runtime orchestration system that evaluates what a task needs across reasoning, code generation, debugging and tool use, then selects and coordinates one or more models from Anthropic and OpenAI. Satya Nadella said it delivers frontier-level quality 40% faster and at up to 77% lower cost, and Artificial Analysis found it scores two points below Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code at about half the cost per task on the Coding Agent Index.

Broadcom arranges $63 billion financing to buy chips for Anthropic

Broadcom arranged a $63 billion financing package through Bank of America, Blackstone, Citigroup and Morgan Stanley to purchase Broadcom AI accelerators and networking equipment that Anthropic will lease through a special purpose vehicle, keeping the debt off Anthropic's balance sheet. The arrangement is the largest AI chip financing deal to date and helps Anthropic lock in an independent compute channel alongside its Google TPU supply.

OpenAI gives ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers up to $500 monthly API credits

OpenAI began letting eligible ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers claim up to $500 per month in API credits to build apps and agents, with credit amounts tied to plan tier and Business credits pooled up to $500 per workspace. Subscribers activate the benefit by linking their subscription to an API organization they own, then use the credits for model calls, agent development and real-time voice interactions.

Nvidia releases open-weight Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe speech model

Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open-weight 30B-parameter mixture-of-experts speech model with roughly 4B active parameters that brings 40-language transcription, speaker attribution, translation, contextual prompting and audio understanding into one checkpoint. It ties Mistral's Voxtral Small for the lead among open-weight speech-to-text models with a 2.8% word error rate, and is released under the OpenMDW-1.1 license with a NeMo Speech fine-tuning toolkit.
