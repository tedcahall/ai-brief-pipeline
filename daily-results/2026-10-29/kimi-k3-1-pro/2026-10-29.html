OCT 29, 2026

📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Google DeepMind launched Gemini 4 Carbon for cybersecurity and long-horizon work, with Artificial Analysis placing it first on Intelligence Index v5 while noting limited availability. GitHub made Copilot Fusion generally available, and NVIDIA released an open-weight speech model covering 40 languages.

AI infrastructure financing also expanded through a $63 billion Broadcom arrangement for Anthropic. OpenAI added subscription-linked API credits, while Mistral, Unsloth, InclusionAI and Grok shipped agent or inference updates.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Google DeepMind launches Gemini 4 Carbon for cyber and long-horizon work

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 4 Carbon as its next frontier model, emphasizing cybersecurity and long-horizon capabilities. Artificial Analysis scored it 59 on Intelligence Index v5, first in that evaluation, and second on its Coding Agent Index. The evaluator said access is limited to selected users, with a 50% launch discount and no confirmed end date.

NVIDIA releases open-weight Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe across 40 languages

New developments: NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open-weight 30B mixture-of-experts model with about 4B active parameters. One promptable checkpoint supports transcription in 40 languages, speaker attribution in 13, translation and audio question answering. NVIDIA also published the Granary v2 data pipeline and fine-tuning recipes. Artificial Analysis reported a 2.8% AA-WER, tied for first among open-weight models.

GitHub makes Copilot Fusion generally available with multi-model orchestration

GitHub made Copilot Fusion generally available across paid Copilot plans in VS Code, its app, CLI and JetBrains IDEs. Fusion routes tasks across enabled models using single, cascade and critique workflows. GitHub said Fusion Max scored 60 on Artificial Analysis’ Coding Agent Index, two points behind Claude Code with Opus 5.5 Max, at about half the average API cost per task.

OpenAI adds up to $500 in monthly API credits to eligible ChatGPT plans

OpenAI said eligible ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers can claim monthly API credits for building apps and agents. Pro tiers receive $100, $200 or $500 per month. Business workspaces receive $20 per paid Standard seat or $100 per paid Premium seat, pooled up to $500 per workspace. Users claim the benefit through ChatGPT billing.

Broadcom and financial partners arrange $63B for Anthropic AI infrastructure

Bank of America, Blackstone, Citi and Morgan Stanley arranged $63 billion in committed financing for a special-purpose vehicle buying Broadcom XPU accelerators and networking equipment for lease to Anthropic. Broadcom is providing residual-value support equal to the first-lien portion. Deliveries are scheduled to begin next year, adding a large debt-financed route to Anthropic’s compute supply.
