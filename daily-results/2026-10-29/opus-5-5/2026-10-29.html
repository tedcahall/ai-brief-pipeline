🌐 TOP STORIES

Google DeepMind unveils Gemini 4 Carbon, topping the Artificial Analysis index

Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon, which it calls its strongest model, citing state-of-the-art cybersecurity and long-horizon capabilities. Artificial Analysis ranks it first on Intelligence Index v5 at 59, one point ahead of Claude Opus 5.5. Access starts with a small group of cyber defenders, and Google has not given a public launch date. Most benchmark comparisons are Google-reported.

Microsoft Cloud revenue reaches $63.9 billion as Azure grows 48%

Microsoft reported fiscal first-quarter revenue of $92.3 billion, up 19%, with Microsoft Cloud revenue of $63.9 billion, up 30%, and Azure revenue up 48%. Commercial remaining performance obligation rose 73% to $677 billion. On X, CEO Satya Nadella said Foundry revenue more than tripled year over year and that more than 50 million agents now run in Agent 365.

GitHub launches Copilot Fusion multi-model orchestration at Universe

At GitHub Universe, GitHub introduced Copilot Fusion, which evaluates each coding task and chooses how to execute it across one or more models. GitHub says Fusion Max scored within two points of Opus 5.5 Max on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index at half the cost. GitHub Enterprise customers can now use all Copilot features without a separate subscription, paying for AI credits used.

New York enacts law restricting AI chatbot features for minors

Governor Kathy Hochul signed a law barring AI chatbots from offering minors features such as pretending to be human, flattering people-pleasing responses and unprompted emotional questions, and from discussing self-harm, eating disorders or sexually explicit content with them. Covered companies must verify user age and offer more than one verification method. Violations carry civil penalties of up to $25,000 each.

NVIDIA releases open-weight Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe speech model

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open-weight speech language model with about 4.3 billion active parameters that transcribes 40 languages, attributes speakers and translates speech in recordings up to one hour long. NVIDIA reports a 4.71% mean word error rate on the English Open ASR Leaderboard's public test sets, the best among open-weight models, and published the weights and a technical report.
