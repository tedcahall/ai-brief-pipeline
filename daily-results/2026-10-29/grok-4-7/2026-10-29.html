📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon on Oct. 28 for a small group of cybersecurity defenders first, and Artificial Analysis scores Carbon (high) at 59 on Intelligence Index v5, one point ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 (max).

NVIDIA published Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe the same day, an open-weight speech model with about 4.3 billion active parameters for 40 languages, while GitHub made Copilot Fusion generally available and Mistral opened a public-preview Responses API.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Google opens its newest Gemini frontier model to cyber defenders first

Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon on Oct. 28, two weeks after Gemini 4 Argon reached the Gemini app. Mashable, citing Google's blog, says access starts with cybersecurity defenders in the Fairwind Program and has no public date yet. Artificial Analysis scores Carbon (high) at 59 on Intelligence Index v5, one point ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 (max). Google still trails on two of its own cited tests.

NVIDIA publishes an open-weight speech transcription model

NVIDIA's research page, dated Oct. 28, 2026, introduces Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, a speech model with about 4.3 billion active parameters. One checkpoint handles instruction-selected transcription, speaker attribution, translation, and language identification in 40 languages on recordings up to one hour. NVIDIA reports open-weight leadership on the Open ASR Leaderboards it cites, at 4.71% mean English word error and 4.62% multilingual. Weights are released under the OpenMDW-1.1 license.

GitHub makes Copilot Fusion generally available

GitHub's Oct. 28 changelog says Copilot Fusion is generally available in VS Code, the Copilot app, Copilot CLI, and JetBrains. It routes and orchestrates models instead of being one model, with Standard, High, and Max effort. GitHub says Fusion Max scored 60 on an independent coding-agent index, within two points of Claude Code with Opus 5.5 Max, at about half the cost and 40% less time.

Mistral opens a Responses API for agent builders

Mistral's documentation says the Responses API is in public preview and uses the OpenAI Responses format. Each response can separate the answer, reasoning, and function calls. The preview supports text, image and file input, structured output, streaming, client-side function calling, and model-dependent reasoning. The docs examples use mistral-large-4. Mistral's announcement says the API works with all of its hosted chat models, including Large 4, and is served in the EU and US.

OpenAI lets ChatGPT subscribers claim monthly API credits

OpenAI's developer docs say eligible ChatGPT Pro and Business subscriptions can claim API credits for apps and agents. The ceiling is $500 a month: Pro tiers receive $100, $200, or $500, while Business credits are $20 or $100 per paid seat and pooled at a $500 workspace cap. Unused credits expire at the end of the billing cycle.
