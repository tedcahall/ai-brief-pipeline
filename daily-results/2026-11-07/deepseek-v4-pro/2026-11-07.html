🌐 TOP STORIES

Alibaba Teases Qwen4 as Nous Research Ships Western-Values Arete-27B

Alibaba's Qwen account posted a cryptic four-word teaser, "The becoming begins.", signaling Qwen4's imminent arrival. Meanwhile, Nous Research released Arete-27B, a Qwen3.8-27B fine-tune designed to reinforce Western values on a Chinese-trained foundation. Using 53 million tokens and an 803-prompt eval across seven value categories, Arete-27B reduced state-aligned propaganda responses from 22% to 1% while maintaining Hermes Bench performance.

Meta Rolls Out Active Memory and Active Focus to Muse and Meta AI

Meta began shipping specialized models trained using Muse Wonder to Muse and Meta AI users, introducing Active Memory, which carries important user details across conversations, and Active Focus, which helps agents persist toward goals when tasks encounter bottlenecks. Meta reports a 2.4x reduction in forgetting critical information and 67% better task completion when things go off-plan.

Anthropic Details Claude's "Dreaming" Memory System for Agents

Anthropic published an engineering post describing four layers of agent memory in Claude and a "dreaming" process where Claude offline-reviews logs and transcripts to find and fix bad notes. The dreaming improved Claude's recall on a wiki benchmark from 13% to 48%, correcting mistakes and improving organization without requiring new training.

Google DeepMind Uses AI Agents to Accelerate RSA Cryptanalysis

Google DeepMind researchers deployed AI agents under human guidance to stress-test legacy 1024-bit RSA encryption, uncovering two weaknesses and discovering a new algorithm with 30x better asymptotic complexity for factoring additional keys. Modern 2048-bit systems remain unaffected, but the work demonstrates how AI can transform cryptographic research.

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Small, Plans Open Weights

Thinking Machines launched Inkling 2 Small, a 594B Mixture-of-Experts model with 17B active parameters scoring 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0. The model is available for fine-tuning and inference on Tinker today; full weights are planned to be released at a later date, which would make it the highest-scoring open-weights model outside of China.
