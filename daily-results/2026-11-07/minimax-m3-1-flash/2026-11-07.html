🌐 TOP STORIES

Nous Research Releases Arete-27B Model With Western Value Alignment

Nous Research released Arete-27B, a post-trained version of Qwen3.8-27B, along with its evaluation, training data and weights. In the company’s 803-prompt test, the model’s measured propaganda rate fell from 22% to 1%, while its Hermes Bench score remained within 0.2 points of the base model. The figures are self-reported by Nous Research.

Anthropic Details Memory Techniques for Claude Agents

Anthropic described how Claude agents can use memory notes, offline “dreaming,” linked wiki pages and a small injected memory layer to retain useful experience across sessions. In internal evaluations, Anthropic reports that dreaming corrected a wrong memory note, and that a wiki built over 128 sessions improved graded criteria from 13% to 48%. The post also documents where memory can distract an agent.

OpenAI Introduces BioBasicsBench for Wet-Lab Analysis

OpenAI and Ligo introduced BioBasicsBench, 100 tasks built from real wet-lab experiments that test whether agents can interpret raw evidence, detect errors and recognize unsupported conclusions. OpenAI reports that GPT-6 Astra at xhigh reasoning averaged 66.7% of available credit, versus 43.8% for GPT-5.6 Sol, while also documenting remaining failures in controls, assumptions and counting.

Frontier Labs Create Common Rating Method for Cyber Jailbreaks

The Frontier Model Forum released version 1.0 of a framework for rating the comparative risk of successful cyber jailbreaks. It scores bypasses across capability gain, capability scope, deployment ease and discovery ease, and opens public comment through December 21, 2026. Contributions came from Amazon Web Services, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Microsoft and OpenAI.

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash Inference Speeds Improve on GB300

Artificial Analysis reported that its DeepSeek V4.1 Flash tests on NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 reached 848 tokens per second for one agent, up from 596, and roughly 1.6 times throughput per GPU at about 80 tokens per second per agent. The benchmark attributes the change to newer vLLM kernels, Dynamo configuration, CUDA graphs, speculative decoding and FlashInfer autotuning.
