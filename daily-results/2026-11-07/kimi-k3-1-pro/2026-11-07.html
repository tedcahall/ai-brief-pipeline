🌐 TOP STORIES

Thinking Machines Launches Inkling 2 Small With Apache 2.0 Weights Planned

Thinking Machines introduced Inkling 2 Small, a 594B-total, 17B-active mixture-of-experts model supporting image inputs and variable effort. It scores 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and is available for Tinker inference and fine-tuning. Weights are closed initially, with a full Apache 2.0 release planned next week after external safety testing and red-teaming.

Nous Research Releases Arete-27B Open Weights and Values Evaluation

Nous Research released Arete-27B, post-training Qwen3.8-27B on about 52.8 million tokens. On its 803-prompt evaluation, Nous reports answers containing propaganda fell from 22% to 0.9% with thinking off, while Hermes Bench changed by less than 0.2 point. The weights, evaluation and training data are publicly available; these results are Nous-reported measurements.

Google DeepMind Uses AI Agents to Find Weaknesses in Legacy RSA-1024

Google DeepMind said human-guided AI models uncovered two major weaknesses in legacy 1024-bit RSA cryptosystems, demonstrating how agents can help stress-test encryption. The company said modern 2048-bit systems remain safe from these specific exploits. The work highlights the need to revisit historical hardness assumptions as AI-enabled mathematics and computation improve.

Anthropic Shows How Dreaming Organizes and Corrects Agent Memory

Anthropic detailed how dreaming lets Claude review transcripts, correct inaccurate memory notes and build linked wikis for later sessions. In one internal evaluation, expanding dreaming from two to 128 sessions raised performance from 13% to 48% of graded criteria. Anthropic also cautioned that wiki benefits varied by task and worked best alongside search.

Meta Brings Active Memory and Active Focus to Muse and Meta AI

Meta is rolling out specialized versions of its existing Muse and Meta AI models trained using Muse Wonder. Active Memory distinguishes lasting information from stale context, while Active Focus helps agents recover from obstacles. Meta reports a 2.4x reduction in forgetting goal-critical information and a 67% improvement on everyday tasks with unexpected issues in Muse.
