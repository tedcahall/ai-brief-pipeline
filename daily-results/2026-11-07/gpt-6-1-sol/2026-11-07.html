🌐 TOP STORIES

Thinking Machines Launches Inkling 2 Small, With Open Weights Planned

Thinking Machines introduced a 594-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 17 billion active parameters, image inputs and adjustable reasoning effort. Inkling 2 Small is available for inference and fine-tuning on Tinker. The company plans Apache 2.0 weights after external safety testing; they are not yet released. Artificial Analysis reports an Intelligence Index score of 31, up from 13 for its predecessor.

Meta Brings Muse Wonder-Trained Models to Muse and Meta AI

Meta says Muse and Meta AI are moving to specialized models trained using Muse Wonder. Its Active Memory feature aims to retain relevant information over time, while Active Focus helps agents continue toward goals when obstacles arise. Meta reports less forgetting and improved recovery from setbacks in its own evaluations; those claims are not independent evidence of general reliability.

Anthropic Explains How Dreaming Repairs and Organizes Agent Memory

Anthropic describes agent memory as persistent context rather than changes to model weights. Its engineering report shows how offline reflection, called dreaming, checks stored notes against transcripts, corrects errors and builds linked representations such as wikis. The examples include database tasks and game navigation. These are Anthropic-run evaluations, not proof that agents have solved continual learning.

DeepMind Reports AI-Assisted Weaknesses in Legacy 1024-Bit RSA

Google DeepMind says human-guided AI research uncovered two weaknesses in legacy 1024-bit RSA systems. Crypto Briefing describes a signature-forgery flaw and a lower-cost method for batch factoring related moduli. The findings concern specific older cryptosystems, not a demonstrated general break of modern encryption. The report explicitly says 2048-bit and larger RSA keys remain secure against this specific threat.

AI Chip Demand Pushes Advanced Packaging Into a Capacity Race

Reuters reports that AI processor demand is driving investment in advanced packaging, which connects compute and memory chips into high-performance systems. An unnamed industry executive says Nvidia is financing additional CoWoS capacity at SPIL; Nvidia did not comment and ASE declined to discuss customer rumors. Yole forecasts advanced-packaging capital spending near $28 billion, while suppliers pursue alternatives to TSMC capacity.
