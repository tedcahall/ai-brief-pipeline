🌐 TOP STORIES

Thinking Machines Ships Second-Generation Inkling Model With Planned Open Weights

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Small, a 594-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 17 billion active parameters that scores 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, 18 points above its predecessor. It is available now through Tinker with closed weights; the company says it intends to publish full Apache 2.0 weights next week after external safety testing and red-teaming.

Google DeepMind's AI Agents Find Two Weaknesses in Legacy 1024-Bit RSA

Google DeepMind said human-guided AI agents found two weaknesses in legacy 1024-bit RSA: a signature-forgery technique it estimates is roughly 1,600 times more efficient, and a batch-factoring algorithm that cuts the marginal cost of each additional key by about 30 times. The company said 2048-bit systems remain safe from these specific exploits.

Anthropic Details How 'Dreaming' Reorganizes Agent Memory

Anthropic published research on the components of agent memory, describing an offline “dreaming” pass in which Claude rereads past session transcripts, corrects mistaken notes and builds linked wikis between sessions. In its evaluations, dreaming raised performance on a wiki-building task from 13% to 48% of graded criteria and improved answers when Claude consulted the wiki rather than search alone.

OpenAI Releases BioBasicsBench to Test Agents on Wet-Lab Data

OpenAI and biotech company Ligo released BioBasicsBench, a 100-task benchmark built from real experiments that tests whether AI agents can read raw wet-lab evidence such as gel images and colony plates, spot errors and recognize when data cannot support a conclusion. At its highest reasoning setting, GPT-6 Astra earned 66.7% of available credit against 43.8% for GPT-5.6 Sol.

Nous Research Releases Arete-27B to Strip State-Aligned Behavior

Nous Research released Arete-27B, a Qwen3.8-27B model post-trained to reduce state-aligned behavior, publishing weights, training data and an 803-prompt evaluation. Nous says the model's rate of adopting China's party line fell from 22% to 1% after fine-tuning on about 53 million tokens, with no significant regression on its Hermes Bench; these are the lab's own measurements.
