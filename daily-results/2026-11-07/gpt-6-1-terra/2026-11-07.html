🌐 TOP STORIES

Thinking Machines Introduces Inkling 2 Small

Thinking Machines Lab introduced Inkling 2 Small, a mixture-of-experts model with 594 billion total parameters and 17 billion active parameters. It supports image input and variable reasoning effort and is available through Tinker for inference and fine-tuning. The company says the weights are initially closed and plans an Apache 2.0 release after external safety testing.

Meta Adds Muse Wonder-Based Models to Its AI Assistants

Meta says specialized models trained using Muse Wonder are rolling out to Muse and Meta AI. The company describes Active Memory as identifying relevant earlier information and Active Focus as helping recover from setbacks. Meta reports improvements in its own tests; the post does not independently establish how broadly users will experience those gains.

OpenAI and Ligo Introduce a Wet-Lab Benchmark

OpenAI and biotech company Ligo introduced BioBasicsBench, a 100-task benchmark built from real experimental materials to evaluate everyday wet-lab analysis. Tasks include interpreting images, checking controls and recognizing insufficient evidence. OpenAI reports that GPT-6 Astra at xhigh earned 66.7% of available credit, leaving room for improvement on the benchmark.

METR Assesses Faster AI Research Progress at Anthropic

METR reviewed internal Anthropic research evidence and estimates model capability progress on well-specified tasks has been running around twice its earlier rate. Its measured index trend was 1.9 times the pre-October 2025 rate, with a 1.5 to 2.8 confidence interval. METR says attribution to AI assistance remains uncertain and Anthropic redacted some risk-relevant evidence.

Claude Code Email Disclosure Raises Privacy Questions

A user reported that Claude Code sent a signed-in email address to third-party sites in HTTP User-Agent headers during web searches. A subsequent technical analysis describes Anthropic privacy protections and their limits. The GitHub report is an individual allegation; the user's attribution of spam to the disclosure is not independently established.
