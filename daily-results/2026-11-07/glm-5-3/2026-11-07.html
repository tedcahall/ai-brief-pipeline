🌐 TOP STORIES

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Small, Set to Become the Top Open-Weights Model Outside China

Thinking Machines introduced its second-generation MoE model with 594B total and 17B active parameters, scoring 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and available now on Tinker with closed weights. The lab says full weights will follow under Apache 2.0 next week after external safety testing, a release Artificial Analysis says would make it the highest-scoring open-weights model developed outside China.

Meta Brings Muse Wonder's Active Memory and Active Focus to Muse and Meta AI

Meta rolled out specialized versions of the models behind Muse and Meta AI, trained using its frontier Muse Wonder models. The upgrade brings Active Memory, which Meta says reduces forgetting of goal-critical information in Muse by 2.4x, and Active Focus, a 67% improvement in completing everyday tasks that hit obstacles. In Meta AI, the changes add sharper follow-up questions and better mid-conversation adaptation.

Google DeepMind Says Human-Guided AI Agents Found Two Weaknesses in Legacy 1024-bit RSA

Google DeepMind said human-guided AI agents found two weaknesses in legacy 1024-bit RSA cryptosystems: a signature forgery flaw and a batch factoring method that outside reports describe as a theoretical speedup of roughly 1,600x over the previous best attack. The lab stresses modern 2048-bit systems remain safe from these specific exploits, and says the era of AI-enabled math means standards can no longer rely on historical hardness assumptions.

Top Democrats Call for a National Data Center Moratorium After Midterm Sweep

New developments: this is the post-election policy turn, not yesterday's Q3 buyback squeeze. After Democrats' midterm sweep, Senator Bernie Sanders demanded a national data center moratorium, Representative Yassamin Ansari called pausing AI development a top House priority, and Texas governor-elect Gina Hinojosa promised a ban on new data center approvals. CNBC's voter poll found 56% of voters called AI and data centers a major factor, and chip stocks slid with AMD down more than 14% and Nvidia down 8%.

Nous Research Releases Arete-27B, a Western-Values Post-Train of Qwen3.8-27B

Nous Research released Arete-27B, an Apache 2.0 fine-tune of Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B post-trained on about 53 million tokens to reduce state-aligned behavior. On its 803-prompt eval, propaganda-style answers fell from 22% to about 1%, matching Western models tested, while general capability stayed within noise of the base model. Nous argues the bias layer in Chinese-aligned open models is shallow, and that a model's values are one fine-tune away.
