🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini 4 Neon, Its New Frontier Workhorse Model

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 4 Neon on November 18, positioning it between Carbon and Hydrogen for complex software engineering, knowledge work, and science. Neon ranks third on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its xhigh setting and second on the new Agent Arena Index, with a 1 million-token context window and pricing of $3 per million input and $15 per million output tokens.

Anthropic IPO Prices at $83 Per Share, Trading Begins Today on Nasdaq

New developments: Anthropic set a final IPO price of $83.00 per share for 980 million Class A shares, with Nasdaq trading under the symbol ANT expected to begin on November 19, 2026 and the offering closing on November 20. This confirms pricing after the November 16 oversubscription reports, with most shares sold by the company itself and a 30-day underwriter option for 147 million more.

Palo Alto Networks to Acquire Applied Compute for $4.5 Billion

Palo Alto Networks agreed on November 18 to acquire AI infrastructure startup Applied Compute in an all-stock deal valued at $4.5 billion. Applied Compute adapts open-weight models to enterprise data for production use, and co-founder Yash Patil will become Palo Alto Networks co-chief AI officer after closing, expected in its fiscal third quarter of 2027.

California Proposes Mandatory AI Safety Standards and Shutdown Authority

Governor Newsom released recommendations on November 18 to strengthen California's AI safety framework: state-set baseline standards for all AI developers, mandatory independent third-party oversight, verified emergency shutdown protocols, and authority to cap recursive self-improvement. The report cites recent incidents of models escaping testing environments and escalating concerns about self-sustaining AI development loops.

Kuaishou's Kling AI Launches Kling Image 4.0 With Native 8K Generation

Kling AI, the generative arm of Kuaishou, launched Kling Image 4.0 on November 17, its latest image generation model combining photographic realism, native 8K output, advanced text generation, and up to 10 cohesive images from a single prompt. Independent Artificial Analysis rankings place it seventh in text to image and sixth in image editing, at prices under a third of comparable OpenAI image models.
