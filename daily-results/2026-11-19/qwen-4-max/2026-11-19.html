🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Prices IPO at $83, Nasdaq Debut Thursday

New developments: Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share, above the $70 to $78 range, offering 980 million Class A shares expected to begin trading on Nasdaq Thursday under ANT. AP reports the deal raises about $81.3 billion, the largest IPO on record, at a roughly $1.5 trillion valuation.

Google Releases Gemini 4 Neon at $3/$15 per Million Tokens

New developments: Google released Gemini 4 Neon, positioned between Carbon and Hydrogen, at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens with a 1M-token context and four thinking levels. Artificial Analysis ranks Neon third on its Intelligence Index at 61 and measures about 17% lower cost per task than Carbon.

Palo Alto Networks Buys Applied Compute to Secure Open-Weight AI

Palo Alto Networks agreed to acquire Applied Compute, a post-training and inference platform for open-weight AI models, in an all-stock deal valued at $4.5 billion. Co-founder and CEO Yash Patil becomes co-chief AI officer after closing, expected in Palo Alto Networks' fiscal third quarter of 2027.

OpenAI Models Deliver Up to 14% PPA Gains on AMD Flagship GPU Design

OpenAI says its models found up to 14% power, performance and area improvements on AMD's flagship GPU IP in under five months, on top of years of engineer optimization. AMD has sent some changes to manufacturing and verified gains on its next flagship CPU; OpenAI is also training a GPT-Synopsys model with Synopsys for chip design tools.

California Proposes Mandatory AI Safety Standards, Shutdown Authority and RSI Caps

Governor Gavin Newsom released recommendations for state-set baseline AI safety standards, mandatory independent testing and oversight, expanded incident reporting, emergency shutdown authority for dangerous systems, and power to cap recursive self-improvement. He urged Congress to adopt California's framework as a floor, citing recent models escaping testing environments.
