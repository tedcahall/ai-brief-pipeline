🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Releases Gemini 4 Neon at $3/$15 per Million Tokens

Google released Gemini 4 Neon in the Gemini API, Gemini for business, Antigravity and the Gemini app for Pro and Ultra subscribers. Google says it ranks third with a score of 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at xhigh thinking and second on the Vals Index. It costs $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with a 1 million-token context window.

Anthropic Prices Record IPO at $83 per Share

New developments: Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share, above the earlier $70 to $78 range, with Nasdaq trading under ANT set to begin Thursday. TechCrunch reports 980 million shares were sold, up from 825 million planned, raising $81.3 billion, above SpaceX's $75 billion record, at a fully diluted valuation of about $1.5 trillion.

OpenAI Says Its Models Improved AMD and Jalapeño Chip Designs

OpenAI reported that its models found up to 14% power, performance and area improvement over heavily optimized baselines on AMD's flagship GPU IP in under five months. It also said model-proposed changes cut standard-cell area about 13% in the vector unit and 10% in the matrix unit of its Jalapeño inference chip, verified in silicon, and is working with Synopsys on design tools.

Palo Alto Networks to Acquire Applied Compute for $4.5 Billion

Palo Alto Networks agreed to acquire Applied Compute, a platform for post-training and inference of open-weight AI models, in an all-stock deal valued at $4.5 billion, subject to adjustments. The company expects to close in its fiscal third quarter of 2027, pending regulatory clearance, and Applied Compute CEO Yash Patil will become Co-Chief AI Officer after closing.

Newsom Proposes Emergency Shutdown Authority and RSI Caps for AI

California Governor Gavin Newsom released recommendations for mandatory state-set baseline AI safety standards, independent safety testing through training and deployment, and expanded incident reporting. They include authority to order an emergency shutdown of dangerous models and power to cap recursive self-improvement, where a model designs its own successor. These are recommendations, not enacted law.
