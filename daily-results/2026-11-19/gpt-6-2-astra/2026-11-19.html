🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Releases Gemini 4 Neon for Faster Work at Lower Cost

Google DeepMind placed Neon between Carbon and Hydrogen for tasks such as coding and scientific work. In Artificial Analysis testing, Neon at its highest effort setting scored one point above Carbon at high effort while averaging 21 minutes per task instead of 36. Its cost advantage uses standard, not discounted, Carbon pricing.

Anthropic Prices Its IPO at $83 a Share

New developments: Anthropic has set the price rather than simply reporting investor demand. The offering comprises 980 million shares, including roughly 24 million sold by existing stockholders whose proceeds will not go to the company. Anthropic expects Nasdaq trading under the symbol ANT; pricing does not establish the opening market price.

OpenAI and AMD Report Chip Design Gains on Existing GPU Designs

OpenAI says its models identified up to a 14% improvement in power, performance and area on flagship AMD GPU designs in less than five months. The distinction between design and hardware matters: OpenAI separately reports that gains on its own Jalapeño inference chip were verified on real silicon.

Palo Alto Networks Agrees to Buy Applied Compute for $4.5 Billion

Palo Alto Networks signed an all-stock agreement to acquire Applied Compute, whose tools let customers adapt and run open-weight models. The buyer plans to offer the platform in public and sovereign clouds. The deal price is subject to adjustments, and the acquisition still requires regulatory clearance.

Mistral Brings Vibe Agents to Mac and Windows Desktops

Mistral has made its Vibe desktop application available for macOS and Windows. Developers can start agents from the desktop and choose whether those agents run locally or remotely. That gives teams a choice about where work takes place rather than tying the desktop application to one execution environment.
