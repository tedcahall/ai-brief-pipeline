🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Prices Record $81.3 Billion IPO at $83 Per Share

New developments: Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share Wednesday, above its raised range, selling 980 million shares to raise $81.3 billion at a $1.5 trillion valuation, the largest offering on record per AP and CNN, surpassing SpaceX's $75 billion listing. Trading begins Thursday on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under ANT, with closing expected November 20.

Google Ships Gemini 4 Neon Between Carbon and Hydrogen

Google released Gemini 4 Neon, a frontier-level model for complex software engineering, knowledge work and science workflows, priced at $3/$15 per million tokens and rolling out in the Gemini API, Antigravity and the Gemini app for paid tiers. Artificial Analysis ranked Neon third on its Intelligence Index at xhigh thinking, a small gain over Carbon at about 17% lower cost per task.

Palo Alto Networks to Acquire Applied Compute for $4.5 Billion

Palo Alto Networks announced a definitive agreement to acquire Applied Compute, a post-training and inference platform for open-weight models, in an all-stock transaction valued at $4.5 billion. The company says the deal brings secure open-weight AI to public and sovereign clouds. Co-founder and CEO Yash Patil becomes Co-Chief AI Officer at close, expected in fiscal Q3 2027.

OpenAI Models Find Up to 14% PPA Gains on AMD Flagship GPU IP

OpenAI said its models, working with AMD, identified up to 14% power-performance-area improvements on AMD's flagship GPU IP in under five months against baselines AMD engineers had spent years optimizing. Greg Brockman said some results are included in a recently taped-out design. OpenAI also reported Jalapeno inference-chip gains verified on real silicon and an EDA collaboration with Synopsys.

California DMV Orders Waymo Off Freeways, Citing Imminent Hazard

California's Department of Motor Vehicles ordered Waymo to stop operating on state freeways, saying its vehicles pose an imminent hazard to public safety after incidents including cars entering a closed construction zone. Waymo then temporarily suspended freeway operations nationwide, according to TechCrunch and the Los Angeles Times. The order followed several recent Bay Area traffic incidents.
