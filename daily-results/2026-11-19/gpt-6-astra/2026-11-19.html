🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini 4 Neon With a Million-Token Context

Google introduced Gemini 4 Neon for software engineering, knowledge work and science, priced at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. Its context window holds one million tokens. Google reports third place on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at xhigh effort; availability includes the Gemini API and paid Gemini app subscriptions.

Cloudflare Opens Clairic Model Weights and Introduces Clairic-flash

Cloudflare released open weights for its updated Clairic extraction model and the new nine-billion-parameter Clairic-flash, with both hosted on Workers AI. The revised Clairic supports extraction from images and PDFs, including tables and dates. Reported accuracy improvements come from Cloudflare’s internal evaluations, not an established public extraction benchmark.

Anthropic Prices Its IPO at $83 Per Share

New developments: Anthropic has priced its initial public offering at $83 per share, following earlier reports about investor demand. The offering comprises 980 million shares, including shares sold by existing stockholders. Nasdaq trading is expected to begin under ANT, while closing remains subject to customary conditions. Anthropic will not receive proceeds from the selling stockholders’ shares.

Palo Alto Networks Agrees to Buy Applied Compute for $4.5 Billion

Palo Alto Networks agreed to acquire Applied Compute in an all-stock transaction valued at $4.5 billion, subject to adjustments. Applied Compute provides post-training and inference infrastructure for open-weight models. The buyer plans to combine that platform with enterprise security capabilities. Completion is expected in its fiscal third quarter of 2027, subject to regulatory clearance and other closing conditions.

OpenAI Reports Rising Agent Use Inside Its Research Organization

OpenAI’s latest internal research-acceleration report says median researcher agent usage reached approximately $2,400 per day at API list prices, up 62% from September. That is an API-equivalent measure, not a statement of actual spending. The company says human involvement remains central and advocates public reporting of indicators showing how AI accelerates AI development.
