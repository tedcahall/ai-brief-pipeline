Google Launches Gemini 4 Neon, Ranking Third on Artificial Analysis Index

Google rolled out Gemini 4 Neon across Google AI Studio, Gemini for business, Google Antigravity, and the Gemini app for Pro and Ultra subscribers at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens with a 1 million-token context window. Independent Artificial Analysis benchmarks place Neon third overall with a score of 61 at xhigh thinking and roughly 110 output tokens per second.

Anthropic Prices Initial Public Offering at $83 Per Share for $81.3B Debut

New developments: Following earlier roadshow demand reports, Anthropic officially priced 980 million Class A shares at $83 per share on November 18, setting an $81.34 billion base offering before fees. Anthropic is selling 956.35 million shares directly, granted underwriters a 147 million-share option, and expects Nasdaq trading to begin November 19 under ticker ANT at roughly a $1.5 trillion reported valuation.

Palo Alto Networks Agrees to Buy AI Startup Applied Compute for $4.5 Billion

Palo Alto Networks signed a definitive agreement to acquire open-weight model post-training and inference startup Applied Compute in an all-stock transaction valued at $4.5 billion, subject to adjustments and regulatory clearance. Expected to close in Palo Alto Networks' fiscal third quarter of 2027, the deal brings Applied Compute's enterprise sovereign-cloud platform and names CEO Yash Patil as Co-Chief AI Officer.

OpenAI Models Drive 14% AMD GPU Design Gain as Synopsys Partnership Launches

OpenAI reported that its models rewrote SystemVerilog RTL circuits to deliver up to a 14% power, performance, and area improvement on flagship AMD GPU IP in under five months, alongside silicon-proven 13% vector-unit and 10% matrix-unit cell-area reductions on OpenAI's custom Jalapeño inference chip. OpenAI also partnered with Synopsys to train a new GPT-Synopsys model on commercial electronic design automation tools.

California Proposes Emergency AI Shutdown Powers and Self-Improvement Caps

California Governor Gavin Newsom and a state-convened expert panel released frontier AI governance recommendations proposing mandatory baseline safety standards, ongoing independent third-party audits across training and deployment, expanded incident reporting, and state authority to order emergency model shutdowns. The framework also seeks statutory power to cap recursive self-improvement, while fast-tracking recently signed auditor-registry and verification laws AB 1405 and SB 813.
