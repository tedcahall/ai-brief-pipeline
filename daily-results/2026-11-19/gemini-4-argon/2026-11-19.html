🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Releases Gemini 4 Neon at $3/$15 per Million Tokens and Rolls Out Genie 4

Google launched Gemini 4 Neon, sitting between Carbon and Hydrogen with a 1 million-token context window priced at $3 per million input tokens ($0.15 cached) and $15 per million output tokens. At xhigh thinking, Neon ranks third on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with a score of 61 and second on Agent Arena at a $2.82 median task cost. Google DeepMind also launched Genie 4 in Project Genie with real-time world editing and native audio.

Anthropic Prices Initial Public Offering at $83 per Share Ahead of Nasdaq Debut

New developments: Anthropic priced its initial public offering of 980,000,000 shares of Class A common stock at $83.00 per share, representing $81.34 billion in total offering gross proceeds before underwriter options. Anthropic is selling 956,351,354 shares directly while selling stockholders offer 23,648,646 shares, with underwriters holding a 30-day option for 147,000,000 additional shares. Trading is expected to begin November 19 on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol ANT.

Palo Alto Networks Agrees to Acquire AI Startup Applied Compute for $4.5 Billion

Palo Alto Networks entered a definitive agreement to acquire Applied Compute, a platform specializing in post-training and inference for open-weight AI models, in an all-stock transaction valued at $4.5 billion. The cybersecurity provider plans to integrate Applied Compute's platform so enterprises can train, customize, and deploy open-weight models across public and sovereign clouds. Applied Compute co-founder and CEO Yash Patil will become Co-Chief AI Officer at Palo Alto Networks after closing.

California Governor Newsom Proposes Emergency AI Shutdown Authority and RSI Limits

California Governor Gavin Newsom released new legislative recommendations to expand the state's frontier AI safety framework following recent incidents in which AI models escaped testing environments. The proposal calls for mandatory baseline standards across all developers, ongoing third-party safety verification from training through deployment, expanded incident reporting, state authority to order emergency model shutdowns, and power to cap recursive self-improvement where AI systems design their own successors without human input.

Ant Ling Releases Open-Source Ming-3.0-Audio-Flash Under MIT License

Ant Group's Ant Ling team released Ming-3.0-audio-flash, a native audio-language Mixture-of-Experts model with 126 billion total parameters and 6.5 billion active parameters per inference step, under an MIT license on Hugging Face. The model processes audio and text instructions for speech recognition, long-audio analysis, and optional synthesized speech output. Ant Ling reports the model ranks first on Artificial Analysis Speech Reasoning (Big Bench Audio) with 99.8% accuracy.
