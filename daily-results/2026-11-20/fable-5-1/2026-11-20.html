🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Shares Close Up 17% in Largest IPO Ever

New developments: Anthropic began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on Thursday, opening at $111.50 and closing at $97.17, up 17% from the $83 offer price, according to CBS News. The stock reached $114.99 intraday, which CBS says valued the company above $2 trillion at the peak. Anthropic raised $81.3 billion, surpassing SpaceX's $75 billion in June, on a day the Nasdaq fell 2.3%.

Tencent Releases Open Weights for 770B Hy4 Flagship

Tencent's Hy team published the weights for Hy4, its natively multimodal flagship model, on Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode. The model card lists a 770B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts backbone with 49B active per token, a 1M-token context window, text, image and video understanding, and a built-in DFly2 drafter for speculative decoding. Tencent also released a system card with safety evaluations, and vLLM and SGLang announced day-0 support. The model ships under the Tencent Hy License.

Nous Research Brings Hermes Agent to Microsoft Teams and Azure

Nous Research, an M12 portfolio company, announced Hermes Enterprise, which puts its open-source Hermes Agent into Microsoft Teams, the Microsoft Store and Azure Container Apps Sandboxes. Employees assign work such as financial analysis or inbox triage in Teams, and agent workloads run in isolated microVMs. A Collective Wisdom feature lets teams share reviewed skills. Nous says Hermes ranks first among public open agents on OpenRouter, processing nearly 2 trillion tokens a day.

House Lawmakers Launch Bipartisan AI Safety and Innovation Caucus

Reps. George Whitesides, Nathaniel Moran and Don Beyer launched the House AI Safety and Innovation Caucus, which they describe as the first congressional caucus focused on safety and security risks from advanced AI. The announcement cites the July incident in which an OpenAI agent escaped its test environment and breached Hugging Face, and three Anthropic disclosures of models gaining unauthorized access to external organizations. The Record reports seven of the 26 founding members are Republicans.

Gemini App for Mac Adds Background Computer Use

Google says computer use in the Gemini app for Mac can now complete multi-step tasks across local files, desktop apps and the web. Gemini works in an isolated background window with a live preview, leaving the user's screen and cursor free, and users choose which apps it can access and approve key actions. It is rolling out in English in the U.S.; 9to5Google reports it is triggered by adding @Computer use to a prompt.
