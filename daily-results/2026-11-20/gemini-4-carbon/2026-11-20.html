🌐 TOP STORIES

Tencent Releases Open Weights for 770B Multimodal Flagship Model Hy4

Tencent released open weights for Hy4, Hy4-FP8, and Hy4-base under the Tencent Hy License alongside a System Card detailing cyber and biological risk evaluations. Built on a 770-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts architecture activating 49 billion text parameters per token across a 1-million-token context window, Hy4 adds image and video inputs, computer-use training, and a 3.3-billion-parameter DFly2 speculative drafter, with day-zero vLLM, SGLang, and Together AI serverless support at $0.83/$2.50 per million tokens.

Anthropic Closes Up 17% at $1.8 Trillion Valuation in Nasdaq Debut

New developments: Following Wednesday's $83-per-share pricing that raised a record $81.3 billion, Anthropic began trading Thursday on Nasdaq under ticker ANT, opening at $111.50 and closing up 17% at $97.17 for a roughly $1.8 trillion diluted market cap. CNBC reported more than 846 million shares traded hands, making Anthropic the seventh most valuable U.S. public company as CEO Dario Amodei pledged to pace frontier AI deployment for safety.

Google Rolls Out Background Computer Use in Gemini App for Mac

Google began rolling out computer use in the Gemini app for Mac in English for U.S. Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra subscribers. The feature navigates local files, desktop applications, and websites by clicking, typing, and filling forms inside an isolated background window with a live chat preview, leaving the user's main screen and cursor free while requiring per-app access permissions and user approvals before key actions.

OpenAI Brings GPT-Live-1.1 Voice Model to API With Cheaper Standby Mode

OpenAI released its gpt-live-1.1 voice model in the API, bringing the speech engine behind ChatGPT Plus and Pro voice mode to developers with more consistent multilingual accents, stronger alphanumeric recognition, and backend model delegation. OpenAI also added a WebRTC standby setting for gpt-live-1 and gpt-live-1.1 that pauses active inference after 5 to 60 seconds of silence, cutting idle voice billing 60% from $0.05 to $0.02 per minute.

House Lawmakers Outline Bipartisan SAFE AI Act and Launch Safety Caucus

U.S. Representatives Don Beyer, Sara Jacobs, and Celeste Maloy released an outline for the bipartisan SAFE AI Act, proposing federal frontier safety frameworks, mandatory incident reporting, civil penalties, and targeted emergency authority over dangerous models while letting states enact stronger rules. Separately on Thursday, 26 House members including seven Republicans formed the bipartisan AI Safety and Innovation Caucus, citing recent OpenAI and Anthropic agent sandbox escapes.
