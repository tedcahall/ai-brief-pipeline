🌐 TOP STORIES

Tencent releases weights for its 770B Hy4 flagship

New developments: Tencent has made the weights and system card for its full Hy4 flagship available, distinct from the smaller Hy4 Flash covered earlier. Tencent’s model card describes a mixture of experts with 770 billion total language parameters, 49 billion active per text token, and support for text, images and video. It advertises a one-million-token context; the license requires separate terms for certain large model-as-a-service businesses.

OpenAI brings GPT-Live-1.1 to its voice API

OpenAI released GPT-Live-1.1 for developers and says the voice model produces more natural, consistent accents across languages and completes tasks more reliably than its predecessor. The release also adds a standby option that can lower idle costs for supported WebRTC sessions, so a voice application can keep a connection open during a pause without always paying the active rate.

Anthropic ends its first trading day 17% above its IPO price

New developments: Anthropic’s first Nasdaq session ended just above $97 a share, about 17% above the $83 offer price but below the $111.50 opening trade, according to The Guardian. The stock’s closing price implied a market capitalization of nearly $1.3 trillion before dilution. That figure values the company’s shares; it is not the amount raised in the offering.

Apple releases Depth Pro 2 for estimating depth from photos and video

Apple researchers released code and weights for Depth Pro 2, which estimates real-world distances from ordinary photos and video at the input’s full resolution. The team reports that a single network can preserve sharp object boundaries without sacrificing frame-to-frame stability. Apple provides four model sizes, giving users a choice between processing speed and detail.

Alibaba reports 53% growth in AI cloud and compute revenue

Alibaba said its AI Cloud and Compute Services revenue rose 53% year over year to RMB 61.0 billion in the September quarter. Revenue from external customers rose 64%, while AI-related product revenue reached RMB 17.9 billion, its thirteenth consecutive quarter of triple-digit growth. The AI product figure is quarterly revenue, not an annualized estimate.
