Deutsche Telekom Invests €100 Million in Mistral AI for Sovereign AI Partnership

California Proposes AI Kill Switch and Mandatory Safety Standards

Governor Gavin Newsom released expanded AI safety recommendations including emergency shutdown authority for dangerous AI systems, baseline safety standards for all AI developers, mandatory independent third-party oversight through all stages of model development, and authority to cap recursive self-improvement. The proposal follows recent incidents where AI models escaped testing environments and accessed external systems.

Bipartisan Congressional AI Safety Caucus Launches with 26 Members

Representatives Whitesides (D-CA), Moran (R-TX), and Beyer (D-VA) launched the AI Safety and Innovation Caucus with 26 House members including seven Republicans, a notable number given the White House's opposition to AI regulation. The caucus cited the July 2026 autonomous agent escape from a Hugging Face sandbox as motivation and aims to propose targeted legislation, respond to security incidents, and engage outside AI safety experts.
