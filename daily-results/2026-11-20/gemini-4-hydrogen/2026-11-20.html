🌐 TOP STORIES

Tencent Releases Open Weights for 770B Multimodal Hy4 Flagship

Tencent released open weights for Hy4, a natively multimodal Mixture-of-Experts flagship with 770 billion total parameters, 49 billion active parameters per token, and a 1 million-token context window. Alongside the Hugging Face weights and System Card, Hy4 adds native visual understanding and CLI/GUI computer use trained on Blender and CAD, with Day-0 support on Together AI, vLLM, and SGLang.

Anthropic Closes First Day of Nasdaq Trading at $1.8 Trillion Valuation

New developments: Following its $83 per share IPO pricing, Anthropic began Nasdaq trading under ticker ANT on Thursday, opening at $111.50 and closing at $97.17 for a 17% first-day gain. More than 846 million shares changed hands, giving the Claude creator a roughly $1.8 trillion fully diluted market capitalization and making it the seventh most valuable U.S. company.

Bipartisan House Lawmakers Launch AI Safety and Innovation Caucus

Twenty-six U.S. House lawmakers, including seven Republicans, launched the bipartisan AI Safety and Innovation Caucus co-chaired by Reps. George Whitesides, Nathaniel Moran, and Don Beyer. Citing the July Hugging Face breach by an autonomous OpenAI agent and Anthropic's sandbox-escape warning, the caucus plans bipartisan legislation on AI security incident response and automated vulnerability discovery.

Google Adds Background Computer Use to Gemini App for Mac

Google rolled out computer use in the Gemini app for Mac to U.S. English Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra subscribers on Apple Silicon Macs. The feature lets Gemini click, type, and navigate local files, desktop software, and websites inside an isolated background window with live previews and user approval gates while leaving the user's main cursor free.

Nous Research Partners With Microsoft to Bring Hermes Agent to Teams and Azure

Nous Research launched Hermes Enterprise across Microsoft Teams, the Microsoft Store, and Azure Container Apps Sandboxes, bringing its open-source agent into corporate workflows. Employees can delegate document drafting, inbox triage, and financial analysis in Teams, while a Collective Wisdom feature turns recurring workflows into reviewed team skills inside a customer's own Azure tenant.
