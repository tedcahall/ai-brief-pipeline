🌐 TOP STORIES

Marin Releases 57 Mochi Pretraining Checkpoints

Open Athena says its Marin Mochi 535B-A23B model has completed its main pretraining phase and released 57 intermediate checkpoints, including 10 from long-context extension. The project says it found benchmark contamination that limits comparisons. The checkpoints have not received instruction or safety post-training and are intended for research or further training.

Anthropic Shares Close Above IPO Price on First Trading Day

New developments: Anthropic shares closed above $97 on their first trading day after an $83 IPO price, according to The Guardian. The report puts the close at nearly $1.3 trillion in market capitalization, or $1.75 trillion fully diluted. The close is a new development following the previously reported offering price.

OpenAI Makes GPT-Live-1.1 Available in Its API

OpenAI says developers can now use GPT-Live-1.1 to build real-time voice agents. A standby option for both GPT-Live models pauses the model during configurable silence while keeping the WebRTC call open. OpenAI lists standby at $0.02 per minute versus $0.05 for active use, with backend models and tools billed separately.

Google Brings Genie 4 to the Gemini API

New developments: Google says Genie 4 is now available through the Gemini API and AI Studio, extending its earlier Project Genie launch to developers. The company says prompts and reference images can generate interactive 720p worlds with synchronized audio, and users can navigate and change environments using controls and live text.

OpenAI Reports Rising Use of Agents in Research

OpenAI reports that the median researcher used about $2,400 per day in agentic AI tools at API list prices, up 62% from September. Its internal study measures usage and changes in the research process, but describes its view as partial. The figure is an API-equivalent valuation, not necessarily an external bill.
