📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Anthropic's first session as a public company gave the AI build-out its clearest market test yet: a record $81.3 billion raise, a close 17% above the IPO price, and a $1.3 trillion market capitalization that faded from an opening print above $2 trillion.

The same session showed how thin sentiment has become: SoftBank fell 14.5% in Tokyo, chip suppliers across Asia sold off, and Nvidia extended a losing streak.

Model releases continued regardless, with Tencent opening 770-billion-parameter Hy4 weights under Apache 2.0 and Google putting Genie 4 into subscribers' hands. In Washington, a bipartisan trio circulated an outline that would make frontier safety frameworks a federal requirement.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Closes First Day at $97, a $1.3 Trillion Valuation

New developments: Anthropic closed its first trading day at $97.17, up 17% from its $83 IPO price, after opening at $111.50. The company sold 980 million shares to raise $81.3 billion, the largest flotation on record ahead of SpaceX's $75 billion, and ended with a market capitalization near $1.3 trillion, or $1.75 trillion fully diluted, per The Guardian. US indexes and most chipmakers closed lower.

Tencent Opens 770B Hy4 Weights Under Apache 2.0

Tencent's Hunyuan team published weights for Hy4, a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 770 billion total and 49 billion activated parameters and a one-million-token context. The release includes an FP8 variant, a system card and vLLM and SGLang deployment recipes. Tencent said 163 internal experts rated Hy4 slightly ahead of GLM 5.3 and Kimi K3 across 203 engineering tasks, a company-run comparison not independently replicated.

Google Puts Genie 4 World Model in Project Genie

Google DeepMind rolled Genie 4 out to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers, adding mid-session natural-language edits and real-time native audio to its interactive world model. A Project Genie agent now orchestrates Gemini models to build and score worlds, and a community gallery lets users publish and remix them. Google said Genie 3 gained three-minute sessions, world chaining and 360-degree panorama seeding.

Bipartisan Trio Outlines Federal Frontier AI Safety Bill

Representatives Don Beyer, Sara Jacobs and Celeste Maloy released an outline of the Safe and Accountable Federal Evaluations of Artificial Intelligence Act, which would impose federal safety requirements on frontier labs. The draft mandates safety frameworks, incident reporting and transparency, backed by civil penalties and targeted emergency authority to stop dangerous models. The sponsors said they are still seeking expert input before introducing legislative text.

SoftBank's Worst Day in Two Years Leads Asian AI Selloff

SoftBank Group closed 14.48% lower in Tokyo, its worst session since August 2024, as Asian technology shares fell the day after Anthropic's Nasdaq debut. CNBC reported that SoftBank's $64.6 billion investment in OpenAI and its ownership of Arm have made the stock particularly sensitive to sentiment toward AI. SK Hynix fell 5.92%, Samsung Electronics 7.01%, TSMC 4.76% and Tokyo Electron 7.06%.
