📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Anthropic closed its first day of Nasdaq trading at $97.17, 17% above its $83 IPO price, after opening at $111.50. CNBC put its fully diluted market capitalization at roughly $1.8 trillion at the close.

The debut did not lift other AI-linked stocks. The Nasdaq Composite fell more than 2% on Thursday, and SoftBank closed 14.48% lower in Tokyo on Friday, its worst day since August 2024.

Tencent released open weights for its 770-billion-parameter Hy4 flagship alongside a safety system card, Google brought computer use to the Gemini app for Mac, and House lawmakers launched a bipartisan AI Safety and Innovation Caucus.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Closes Nasdaq Debut Up 17% at $97.17

New developments: Anthropic shares began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on Thursday, opening at $111.50 and closing at $97.17, 17% above the $83 IPO price. CNBC reports a fully diluted market capitalization of roughly $1.8 trillion at the close, with more than 846 million shares traded. The company raised $81.3 billion in the offering, which CNBC calls the biggest IPO on record.

SoftBank Falls 14.5% as AI Sell-Off Hits Asian Tech Stocks

SoftBank Group shares closed 14.48% lower in Tokyo on Friday, their worst day since August 2024, as a sell-off spread through Asian technology stocks after Anthropic's market debut, CNBC reports. SoftBank has invested $64.6 billion in OpenAI. Tokyo Electron fell 7.06%, Samsung Electronics 7.01%, SK Hynix 5.92% and TSMC 4.76%, after the Nasdaq Composite fell more than 2% on Thursday.

Tencent Releases Open Weights for Its 770B-Parameter Hy4 Flagship

Tencent released open weights for Hy4, which it describes as a natively multimodal flagship mixture-of-experts model with 770 billion total parameters, 49 billion active, and a 1M-token context window. It also published a system card with safety evaluations covering cyber and bio risks. FourWeekMBA reports the Tencent Hy License requires a separate agreement for resellers with more than $50 million in annual revenue.

House Lawmakers Launch Bipartisan AI Safety and Innovation Caucus

More than two dozen House members announced a new AI Safety and Innovation Caucus on Thursday, co-chaired by Reps. George Whitesides, Nathaniel Moran and Don Beyer, The Record reports. Seven of the 26 founding members are Republicans. The caucus plans to push bipartisan AI safety legislation and convene briefings after AI security incidents, citing sandbox escapes involving OpenAI and Anthropic systems.

Google Brings Computer Use to the Gemini App for Mac

Google began rolling out computer use in the Gemini app for Mac, letting Gemini complete multi-step tasks across local files, desktop apps and the web. Google says Gemini works in an isolated background window with a live preview while the user's screen and cursor stay free, and users choose which apps it can access and approve key actions. The rollout is in English in the U.S.
