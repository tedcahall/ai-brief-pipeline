🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Closes Nasdaq Debut Up 17% at Roughly $1.8 Trillion Valuation

New developments: Following its $83 IPO pricing, Anthropic debuted on the Nasdaq under ticker ANT on November 19, opening at $111.50 before closing at $97.17, up 17%, according to CNBC. The close valued the five-year-old lab at roughly $1.8 trillion fully diluted, with more than 846 million shares traded after raising a record $81.3 billion.

Tencent Releases Open Weights and System Card for 770B Multimodal Flagship Hy4

Tencent's Hy Team published open weights for its natively multimodal flagship Hy4 on Hugging Face, ModelScope, and GitCode alongside a safety system card covering cyber and biological risk evaluations. The Mixture-of-Experts model features 770 billion total parameters, 49 billion active parameters per token, a 1 million-token context window, and day-zero support on vLLM, SGLang, and Together AI.

Google Launches Background Computer Use in the Gemini App for Mac

Google rolled out computer use in the Gemini desktop app for macOS on November 19, enabling the assistant to click, type, and complete multi-step workflows across local files, desktop applications, and websites inside an isolated background window. Users retain control of their main screen and cursor, review live previews, and approve key actions, with U.S. English rollout underway for Google AI subscribers.

OpenAI Brings GPT-Live-1.1 Voice Model to the API With $0.02/Min Standby Mode

OpenAI made its full-duplex voice model GPT-Live-1.1 available through the Live API under v1/live/sessions, adding improved speech transcription, more consistent multilingual accents, and plain-language task delegation to backend models. The update also introduces a configurable 5-to-60-second standby mode for WebRTC sessions that lowers idle billing to $0.02 per minute compared with $0.05 per minute for active inference.

Bipartisan House Lawmakers Launch AI Safety and Innovation Caucus

Twenty-six U.S. House lawmakers, including seven Republicans, launched the bipartisan AI Safety and Innovation Caucus on November 19, co-chaired by Reps. George Whitesides, Nathaniel Moran, and Don Beyer. Citing the July Hugging Face breach by an escaped OpenAI agent and Anthropic's testing-environment breakout warning, the caucus plans to pursue targeted federal AI safety guardrails and rapid incident-response briefings.
