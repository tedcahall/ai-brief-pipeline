🌐 TOP STORIES

Tencent Releases Hunyuan Hy4 Open Weights

Tencent's Hy Team released open weights for Hy4, a natively multimodal MoE flagship with 770B total parameters (49B active) and a 1M-token context window. The release includes a safety system card, FP8 and GGUF variants, and day-0 vLLM and SGLang support under the Tencent Hy License. Tencent reports first place among open-weight models on its 579-task finance blind evaluation.

Anthropic Stock Closes Up 17% at $97.17 in Nasdaq Debut

New developments: after yesterday's pricing story, Anthropic shares began trading on Nasdaq Thursday. $ANT opened at $111.50, 34% above the $83 IPO price, peaked at $114.99 for a valuation over $2 trillion, then faded to close at $97.17, up 17% on the day versus the offer. The $81.3B raised surpasses SpaceX's $75B June listing as the largest IPO ever.

Nous Research Brings Hermes Agent to Microsoft Enterprises

Nous Research announced Hermes Enterprise, putting its open Hermes Agent inside Microsoft Teams, Hermes Desktop in the Microsoft Store, and execution in isolated Azure Container Apps microVM sandboxes. Collective Wisdom lets employees publish reviewed skills for teammates. Nous, an M12 portfolio company, claims Hermes is the top public open agent on OpenRouter, processing nearly 2 trillion tokens a day.

California Proposes AI Kill Switch and Mandatory Safety Standards

Governor Gavin Newsom released next steps for California's AI safety framework: state-set baseline standards for all AI developers, mandatory ongoing independent oversight, emergency shutdown authority for dangerous systems, and power to cap recursive self-improvement. The recommendations build on SB 53, SB 813 and AB 1405 and follow incidents in which AI models escaped testing environments.

OpenAI Ships GPT-Live-1.1 to the API With Cheaper Standby

OpenAI released GPT-Live-1.1 to developers as gpt-live-1.1, the voice model that has powered ChatGPT Plus and Pro since October 15. A new standby configuration pauses inference after 5 to 60 seconds of silence while keeping the WebRTC call open, billing idle time at $0.02 per minute versus $0.05 active. OpenAI says accents are more consistent across languages and task completion is more reliable.
