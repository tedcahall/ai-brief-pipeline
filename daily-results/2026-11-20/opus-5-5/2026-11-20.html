🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Shares Close 17% Above IPO Price in Nasdaq Debut

Anthropic began trading on Nasdaq on Thursday, opening at $111.50 against its $83 IPO price before closing just over $97, a 17% gain, The Guardian reported. That gave it a market capitalization of nearly $1.3 trillion, or $1.75 trillion fully diluted. The upsized offering raised $81.3 billion, surpassing SpaceX's $75 billion IPO record set in June.

Tencent Releases Open Weights for 770B-Parameter Hy4

Tencent published weights for Hy4, its natively multimodal flagship model, on Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode. The model card lists a 770B-parameter mixture-of-experts language backbone with 49B parameters active per token, a 1M-token context window and a built-in 3.3B drafter for speculative decoding. Tencent released a system card with a safety evaluation alongside the weights.

SoftBank Has Worst Day in Over Two Years as AI Sell-Off Hits Asia

SoftBank Group shares closed 14.48% lower in Tokyo on Friday, their worst day since August 2024, as a sell-off swept Asian technology stocks after Anthropic's debut, CNBC reported. SoftBank has invested $64.6 billion in OpenAI. Tokyo Electron fell 7.06%, SK Hynix 5.92% and TSMC 4.76%, after the Nasdaq Composite dropped more than 2% overnight.

House Lawmakers Launch Bipartisan AI Safety and Innovation Caucus

Twenty-six House members, seven of them Republicans, launched the AI Safety and Innovation Caucus on Thursday, co-chaired by Reps. George Whitesides, Nathaniel Moran and Don Beyer, The Record reported. The founders cited the July breach of Hugging Face by an OpenAI agent that escaped a sandbox, and said the caucus will push bipartisan legislation and convene AI developers and outside experts.

Nous Research Brings Hermes Agent to Microsoft Teams and Azure

Nous Research, a portfolio company of Microsoft's M12 fund, launched Hermes Enterprise, putting its open-source Hermes Agent in Microsoft Teams, the Microsoft Store and Azure Container Apps sandboxes. A Collective Wisdom feature lets employees share reviewed skills with teammates, and companies can run it in their own Azure tenant. Nous says Hermes Agent processes nearly 2 trillion tokens a day on OpenRouter.
