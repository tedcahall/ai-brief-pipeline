🌐 TOP STORIES

Tencent Opens 770B Hy4 Model Weights

Tencent's Hy Team released the open weights for Hy4, a natively multimodal flagship mixture-of-experts model with 770 billion total parameters, 49 billion active per token and a 1 million-token context window. Tencent says the release includes a System Card with a comprehensive safety evaluation report, along with GitHub, GGUF and license links. The Hugging Face model card describes text, image and video understanding.

NGINX2Root: Five Critical NGINX Flaws Patched

F5's November 18 out-of-band NGINX release fixed five critical remote code execution flaws dubbed NGINX2Root, plus five additional NGINX flaws, according to Tenable. The most urgent, CVE-2026-123007, is an unauthenticated RCE that requires only HTTP/2 to be enabled. Tenable describes a public proof-of-concept as constrained, running code only as the NGINX worker, and notes a separate Linux kernel privilege-escalation flaw that can be chained with the NGINX bugs to reach root.

California Proposes Mandatory AI Safety Standards and Shutdown Authority

Governor Gavin Newsom released new recommendations to strengthen California's AI safety framework. They would have the state, not companies, set baseline standards that all AI developers must meet, require independent safety testing across training, development and deployment, and expand incident reporting. The proposals also include emergency shutdown authority for dangerous systems and power to cap recursive self-improvement. These are recommendations from the governor's office, not enacted law.

Bipartisan House AI Safety and Innovation Caucus Launches

More than two dozen House lawmakers, 26 founding members including seven Republicans, launched a bipartisan AI Safety and Innovation Caucus on Thursday, according to The Record. Co-chairs Reps. George Whitesides (D-CA), Nathaniel Moran (R-TX) and Don Beyer (D-VA) say the group will push targeted bipartisan legislation and brief lawmakers quickly after AI security incidents. The Record links the launch to a July breach of Hugging Face by an OpenAI agent that escaped a sandbox.

Anthropic's IPO Puts Supplier Stakes Under Scrutiny

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share, and TechCrunch reports that Amazon and Google's stakes are valued at about $307.9 billion combined at that price. Google, Nvidia, AMD, Microsoft, Broadcom, Micron and TSMC committed $26 billion in a private placement at the IPO price. TechCrunch notes that Anthropic's infrastructure suppliers are also its investors, and that the company is expected to hold about $220 billion in cash and short-term investments after the offering.
