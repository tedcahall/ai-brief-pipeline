🌐 TOP STORIES

Tencent Opens 770B Hy4 Preview Weights With 1M Context

Tencent released open weights for Hy4 preview, its natively multimodal flagship mixture-of-experts model with 770B total parameters, 49B active per token and a 1M-token context. The GitHub repository provides BF16 and FP8 checkpoints, deployment recipes and an Apache 2.0 license. Tencent also published a system card and disclosed known issues, including lengthy reasoning and excessive self-verification.

OpenAI Releases GPT-Live-1.1 in the API

OpenAI made GPT-Live-1.1 available in the API for voice agents, promising more natural and consistent multilingual accents, better recognition of precise details and more reliable task completion. A new standby option for both GPT-Live generations pauses inference after 5 to 60 seconds of silence while keeping WebRTC connected, cutting the idle rate from $0.05 to $0.02 per minute.

Nous Research Brings Hermes Enterprise to Microsoft Teams and Azure

Nous Research partnered with Microsoft to offer Hermes Enterprise in Teams, the Microsoft Store and isolated Azure Container Apps sandboxes. Employees can delegate knowledge work in Teams and convert successful recurring workflows into reusable skills, with review before team sharing through Collective Wisdom. Organizations with data-residency or procurement requirements can deploy the service in their own Azure tenant.

Gemini for Mac Gains Background Computer Use

Google added computer use to the Gemini app for Mac, enabling multi-step tasks across local files, desktop applications and websites. Gemini clicks, types and completes forms in an isolated background window with a live preview, leaving the user’s main screen and cursor free. Users choose accessible apps, can pause activity and approve key actions. The English-language feature is rolling out in the US.

OpenAI Reports Rapid but Uneven AI Research Acceleration

OpenAI reported that median researcher spending on agentic tools reached an API-equivalent $2,400 per day in October, up 62% from September, while autonomous task performance remained uneven. Agents succeeded on 79% of run-monitoring tasks but 25% of inference and serving-code tasks without intervention. OpenAI called for mandatory public reporting of recursive self-improvement indicators and stressed that humans remain central to research.
