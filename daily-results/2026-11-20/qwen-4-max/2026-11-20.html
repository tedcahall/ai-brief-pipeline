🌐 TOP STORIES

Tencent Open-Sources Hy4 Flagship With 770B Parameters

Tencent released open weights for Hy4, its natively multimodal flagship mixture-of-experts model with 770B total parameters, 49B active per token and a 1M-token context, covering text, image and video understanding. The release includes a system card with safety evaluations that Tencent says involved independent testers, plus day-0 vLLM and SGLang support.

Anthropic Closes First Trading Day Up 17% After Record IPO

New developments: Anthropic's first trading day. ANT opened at $111.50, peaked at $114.99 and closed at $97.17, up 17% from the $83 IPO price, after the company raised $81.3 billion in the largest IPO in history, ahead of SpaceX's $75 billion. The broader market fell sharply the same day.

Nous Research Brings Hermes Agent to Microsoft Teams and Azure

Nous Research announced Hermes Enterprise, bringing its open-source Hermes Agent into Microsoft Teams, the Microsoft Store and Azure Container Apps sandboxes, with an option to run in a customer's own Azure tenant. Employees can delegate knowledge work and share reviewed skills through Collective Wisdom. Nous says Hermes is the top public open agent on OpenRouter, processing nearly 2 trillion tokens a day.

OpenAI Opens GPT-Live-1.1 Voice Model to the API

OpenAI made GPT-Live-1.1 available in the API, a full-duplex voice model that can listen while it speaks, with more accurate transcription and more consistent accents across languages. A new standby option bills idle WebRTC time at $0.02 per minute versus $0.05 active. OpenAI says the model has powered ChatGPT Plus and Pro voice since October 15.

Gemini Computer Use Arrives on Mac

Google rolled out computer use in the Gemini app for Mac in the U.S. in English, letting Gemini operate local files, desktop apps and websites in an isolated background window while the user's screen and cursor stay free. Users choose app access, watch every step live and approve key actions. Google says it is available to Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers.
