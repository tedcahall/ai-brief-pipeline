🌐 TOP STORIES

Tencent Releases Open Weights for 770B Multimodal Hy4 Flagship Model

New developments: Following its November 4 preview, Tencent published the open weights and System Card for Hy4 on Hugging Face, GitHub, and ModelScope under an Apache-2.0 license. The natively multimodal Mixture-of-Experts model totals 770 billion parameters with 49 billion active text parameters, a 1.23-billion-parameter vision encoder, a 3.3-billion-parameter DFly2 speculative drafter, and a 1-million-token context window, with day-zero inference support in vLLM, SGLang, and Together AI.

Anthropic Closes Up 17% in Record $81.3 Billion Nasdaq IPO Debut

New developments: After pricing 980 million shares at $83 on Wednesday to raise $81.3 billion, Anthropic began public trading Thursday on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Shares opened at $111.50 and closed up 17% at $97.17, giving the Claude developer a fully diluted market capitalization of roughly $1.8 trillion as more than 846 million shares changed hands alongside $26 billion in private placements from infrastructure partners.

Google Rolls Out Background Computer Use in Gemini App for Mac

New developments: Google launched computer use in the Gemini app for Mac for U.S. Google AI Plus, Pro, and Ultra subscribers. The feature lets Gemini click, scroll, type, and navigate local files, desktop applications, and web pages inside an isolated background window with a live chat preview while leaving the user's primary screen and cursor free, with user controls for app access and action approvals.

OpenAI Reports Coding Agents Now Run 5.7 Workdays Daily per Researcher

New developments: OpenAI published its second internal research-acceleration report detailing how coding agents affect AI development. At API list prices, median daily agent spend per researcher rose 62% from September to $2,400 (and up 105% to $23,000 at the 90th percentile), while average agent runtime increased elevenfold from 0.5 to 5.7 eight-hour workdays per researcher daily, even as zero-intervention success remained uneven across inference coding (25%) and run monitoring (79%).

Nous Research Partners With Microsoft to Bring Hermes Agent to Teams and Azure

Nous Research announced a partnership with Microsoft to launch Hermes Enterprise across Microsoft Teams, the Microsoft Store, and Microsoft Azure. Employees can assign financial analysis, document drafting, and inbox triage directly in Teams while agent workloads execute inside isolated Azure Container Apps sandboxes, using a Collective Wisdom feature to share reviewed team skills and offering in-tenant deployment for strict enterprise data-residency requirements.
