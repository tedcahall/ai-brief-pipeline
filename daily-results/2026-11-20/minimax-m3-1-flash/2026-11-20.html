🌐 TOP STORIES

Tencent Releases Hy4 Open Weights and System Card

New developments: Tencent released the open weights for Hy4, its natively multimodal flagship mixture-of-experts model, which has 770 billion total parameters, 49 billion active per token, and a 1 million-token context window. Tencent also published a system card with security, safety, and alignment evaluations, including reviews by its Xuanwu, Zhuque, and Keen Labs and independent testing by FangcunLeap and alice_dot_io.

Anthropic Begins Trading After Record IPO

New developments: Anthropic began trading on Nasdaq after pricing 980 million shares at $83, an offering worth about $81 billion and the largest IPO on record. The debut followed the Nov. 19 pricing announcement covered in the prior brief. TechCrunch reported that the company expects about $220 billion in cash, equivalents, and short-term investments after the offering and a $26 billion private placement, while warning that Anthropic remains loss-making and has major compute commitments.

OpenAI Reports Rapid Growth in Agentic Research Work

OpenAI said internal coding agents completed an estimated 46 percent of research tasks expected to take two to four human hours without intervention, up from 23 percent in March. For tasks estimated to require four to eight hours, success rose from 13 percent to 35 percent. The company also reported an increase from 0.5 to 5.7 agent workdays per researcher per day, while researchers said human review and research judgment remained essential.

OpenAI Adds GPT-Live-1.1 to Its API

OpenAI made GPT-Live-1.1 available to developers, building on the model used in ChatGPT Plus and Pro since Oct. 15. The company says the live model produces more natural and consistent accents across languages and completes tasks more reliably. A new standby configuration keeps WebRTC calls open while billing idle time at $0.02 per minute, compared with the standard active rate of $0.05 per minute.

Google Rolls Out Gemini Computer Use on Mac

Google began rolling out computer use in the Gemini app for Mac to English-language users in the United States. Gemini can navigate local files, desktop applications, and websites in an isolated background window while the user keeps the main screen and cursor available. Users can watch each step, pause the process, restrict app access, and approve key actions; Google describes examples including form entry, photo workflows, and building presentations from live dashboards.
