🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic shares open at $111.50, 33% above IPO price

New developments: Anthropic shares began trading on Nasdaq on November 19 at $111.50, 33% above the $83 IPO price, according to TechCrunch. The opening price came after the company set an offering of 980 million shares, including primary and selling-stockholder shares. The market price fluctuates; the opening quote is not a closing price or a fixed company valuation.

Tencent releases Hy4 open weights with system card

Tencent Hunyuan announced downloadable weights for Hy4, its natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model listed as 770 billion total parameters, 49 billion active parameters and a one-million-token context window. The release included a system card with safety evaluations and links to model, GGUF and license resources. Specifications are attributed to Tencent; the post does not independently establish performance.

Meta details production use of AI agents across ads ranking models

Meta Engineering described agents that help propagate machine-learning techniques across a diverse fleet of advertising ranking models. Meta says agents handle over 60% of this work, with early results showing threefold engineering productivity and a median fourfold reduction in training compute for selected combinations; it estimates over $45 million in compute savings by year-end. These are company-reported measurements, and engineers retain review and responsibility for high-revenue models.

OpenAI publishes new measures of AI-assisted research

OpenAI reports median daily agent use among its researchers of about $2,400 at API list prices and about $23,000 for the 90th percentile. It estimates 5.7 agent workdays per researcher per calendar day, while noting this interactive-use metric may undercount automation. The report is based on internal measurements, argues for broader public reporting of research-acceleration indicators, and says human involvement remains central.

Microsoft introduces Canvas, an interactive image workspace

Microsoft announced Canvas, an interactive workspace powered by MAI Image 2.7 Flash, for turning sketches into diagrams and refining images without restarting. The announcement describes the feature but does not provide availability scope or independent evaluation results.
