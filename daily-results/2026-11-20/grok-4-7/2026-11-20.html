🌐 TOP STORIES

Tencent Releases Open Weights for Hy4

Tencent's Hy team published open weights for Hy4, a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 770 billion total parameters and 49 billion active per token, plus a 1 million-token context. The model card describes text, image, and video understanding. Tencent also posted a system card covering safety, security, and alignment evaluations, including cyber and bio reviews. This is the flagship Hy4 release, separate from the earlier Hy4 Flash weights.

Anthropic Closes First Trading Day About 17% Above Its IPO Price

New developments: Anthropic shares finished their first Nasdaq session at just over $97, about 17% above the $83 IPO price, after an early high of $111.50, The Guardian reported. The close implied a nearly $1.3 trillion market capitalization, or $1.75 trillion fully diluted. The company sold 980 million shares to underwriters, raising $81.3 billion. Chief executive Dario Amodei said the listing puts Anthropic in a stronger financial position.

OpenAI Opens GPT-Live-1.1 to the API

OpenAI made GPT-Live-1.1 available in the API on November 20. The company says this is the voice model that has powered ChatGPT Plus and Pro since October 15. The developer announcement says it handles accents, letters, and numbers more reliably and can listen while it speaks. A new standby mode, also available on GPT-Live-1, bills idle time at $0.02 per minute versus $0.05 when the session is active.

Google Adds Computer Use to the Gemini App for Mac

Google said on November 19 that computer use in the Gemini app for Mac can click, type, and move through local files, desktop apps, and the web from an isolated background window. The main screen and cursor stay free. Users choose which apps Gemini can access, can watch each step, and must approve key actions. The feature is rolling out in English in the United States.

Nous Research Brings Hermes Agent to Microsoft Teams

Nous Research said on November 18 that Hermes Agent now runs in Microsoft Teams, the Microsoft Store, and Azure Container Apps sandboxes. Employees can hand it work such as financial analysis or inbox triage and share reusable skills through Collective Wisdom. Nous, an M12 portfolio company, says Hermes ranks first among public open agents on OpenRouter. That ranking is the company's claim, not an independent measurement.
