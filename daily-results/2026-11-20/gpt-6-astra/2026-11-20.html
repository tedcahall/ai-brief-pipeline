🌐 TOP STORIES

Tencent Releases Hy4 Multimodal Open Weights

Tencent released weights for Hy4, a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with a 770-billion-parameter language backbone, 49 billion active parameters per token and a one-million-token context. Its repository describes text, image and video understanding, computer use and deployment options. Tencent also announced a system card; reported benchmark gains remain vendor evaluations, not independently established superiority.

OpenAI Opens GPT-Live-1.1 API With Lower-Cost Standby

OpenAI made GPT-Live-1.1 available through its API, claiming more consistent accents and more reliable task completion. A standby option, also supported by GPT-Live-1, pauses the model after a configurable 5–60 seconds of silence while keeping the WebRTC call open. OpenAI lists standby at $0.02 per minute, versus the standard $0.05.

Perplexity Brain Learns Reusable Skills From Past Sessions

Perplexity extended Brain to create and update personal agent skills from session history and memory. The system changes instructions and context, not model weights, and checks proposed changes before activation. Personal skill improvement is available to US consumer and enterprise subscribers, enabled by default for eligible users with an opt-out in settings.

Nous Brings Hermes Enterprise to Microsoft Teams and Azure

Nous Research announced Hermes Enterprise integrations with Microsoft Teams, the Microsoft Store and Azure Container Apps Sandboxes. Employees can delegate work in Teams and share reusable skills through Collective Wisdom after review. Organizations with residency or procurement constraints can run the service in their own Azure tenant, according to the company.

Bipartisan SAFE AI Outline Proposes Frontier-Lab Oversight

Representatives Don Beyer, Sara Jacobs and Celeste Maloy released an outline for forthcoming SAFE AI legislation. It proposes mandatory safety frameworks, incident reporting and transparency for frontier labs, with government oversight and due process. The lawmakers also describe civil penalties and targeted emergency authority. This is a legislative proposal, not an enacted federal requirement.
