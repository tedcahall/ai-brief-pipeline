🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Shares Begin Trading in Record IPO, Then Fall Back

New developments: Anthropic shares began trading on Nasdaq after the IPO priced at $83, opening at $111.50 before falling back during the session. TechCrunch reports the company raised about $141 billion in capital for roughly $91.7 billion in net proceeds, expects about $220 billion in cash and equivalents, and counts Nvidia, AMD, Microsoft, Broadcom, Micron, TSMC and Google in a $26 billion private placement.

Tencent Releases Open Weights for Hy4 Flagship

Tencent's Hy team released open weights for Hy4, a natively multimodal flagship MoE with 770B total parameters, 49B active per token and a 1M token context window, published on Hugging Face. The release ships with a system card covering safety, security and alignment evaluations, including reviews by Tencent's Xuanwu, Zhuque and Keen labs plus independent testing, and day-0 serving support from vLLM, SGLang and Together AI.

OpenAI Reports Exponential Rise in AI-Assisted Research

OpenAI's updated research acceleration report says the median researcher now uses about $2,400 per day of agentic AI tools at list prices, with the 90th percentile near $23,000, and agent workdays per researcher rose elevenfold to 5.7. Safety and alignment saw the largest absolute increase in documented researcher time, and OpenAI says labs should be required to track and publicly report indicators of recursive self-improvement.

Apple Is Building a Mac-Controlling AI Agent, Bloomberg Reports

Bloomberg reports Apple is working on an AI agent that can use a computer the way a capable person does, based on a now removed job listing for a proactive assistant spanning apps, browsers, command lines and code. Apple has eyed a launch as soon as late 2027, and the macOS 27.3 beta raises the steps needed to let outside agents control a Mac from four to 14, which Apple attributes to security.

Gemini App for Mac Gains Computer Use

Computer use is rolling out in the U.S. in English to Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app for Mac. Gemini works in an isolated background window with a live preview while the user's screen and cursor stay free, and users choose which apps it can access, watch every step live, pause at any time and approve key actions before they happen.
