🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Closes First Trading Day Up 17% After Record $81.3 Billion IPO

Anthropic shares closed at $97.17 on their first day on Nasdaq, 17% above the $83 offer price, after opening at $111.50. The company raised $81.3 billion by selling 980 million shares. CNBC reported a fully diluted market cap of roughly $1.8 trillion at the close, while other outlets cited lower figures, so reported valuations differ by share-count basis.

Tencent Releases Open Weights for 770B-Parameter Hy4 Multimodal Model

Tencent's Hy team released open weights for Hy4, a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 770 billion total and 49 billion active parameters and a 1M-token context window. Its Hugging Face model card describes text, image and video understanding and lists vLLM and SGLang deployment. Tencent also published a system card with a safety evaluation report.

California Proposes Emergency Shutdown Authority and Limits on Recursive AI Self-Improvement

Governor Gavin Newsom's office released recommendations for mandatory state safety standards, independent testing through training and deployment, and verified containment protocols. They would give California authority to order an emergency shutdown of a model and to cap recursive self-improvement, where a model designs its own successor. The report comes from an expert group and is a set of recommendations, not enacted law.

OpenAI Reports Median Researcher Now Spends $2,400 a Day on Coding Agents

In a research acceleration report based on internal measurements, OpenAI says its median researcher uses about $2,400 a day of agentic AI at API list prices, up 62% since September, and the 90th percentile about $23,000. Agent workdays per researcher rose from 0.5 to 5.7. OpenAI argues labs should be required to track and report indicators of recursive self-improvement.

Nous Research Brings Hermes Agent to Microsoft Teams, Store and Azure

Nous Research, an M12 portfolio company, announced Hermes Enterprise on November 18: Hermes Desktop in the Microsoft Store, a Microsoft Teams integration, and agent execution in Azure Container Apps Sandboxes. A Collective Wisdom feature lets employees share learned skills with teammates after review. Nous says Hermes ranks first among public open agents on OpenRouter at nearly 2 trillion tokens a day.
