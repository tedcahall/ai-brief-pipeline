🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, Its New Flagship Models

New developments: OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra, a frontier model for professional work and scientific research, and GPT-6.2 Sol, an efficient everyday-work model. Both are available in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users, and via the API. OpenAI says Astra sets new eval highs at less than a third the cost per task of GPT-6 Astra.

Nvidia Beats Estimates as Board Rules Out Reinstating Jensen Huang

Nvidia reported better-than-expected third-quarter results in its first earnings report since Jensen Huang's resignation, with AI chip sales beating analyst expectations. Board chair Harvey Jones said the board does not intend to reinstate Huang as CEO, days after Huang said he would be honored to return.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion to Advance AI Drug Design

Isomorphic Labs, the AI drug design company led by Demis Hassabis, announced a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with Andreessen Horowitz and continued investment from Alphabet. The company will scale its IsoDDE drug design engine and therapeutic candidate pipeline.

Microsoft Unveils Seven MAI Models at Ignite, Led by Code 2 and Cyber 2 Flash

At Ignite 2026, Microsoft released seven MAI models emphasizing price-performance, including MAI Code 2, which the company says reaches 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol's coding quality at 40% of the price, and MAI Cyber 2 Flash for continuous security work. Microsoft also introduced MAI Licensed Weights for customized, customer-controlled models.

Tencent Opens the Weights of Hy4 Flash, a 224B Multimodal Model

New developments: Tencent released open weights for Hy4 flash, including FP8 and Base variants, under the Tencent Hy License. The 224-billion-parameter model uses 12 billion active parameters, supports a 1-million-token context, and is natively multimodal, following the model's initial launch earlier this week.
