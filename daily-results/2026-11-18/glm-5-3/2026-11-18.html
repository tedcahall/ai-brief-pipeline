🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol

New developments: OpenAI has now released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, its first new frontier models since the GPT-6.1 Astra cancellation. The company says Astra sets new highs on professional and scientific evals at less than a third of GPT-6 Astra's cost per task, including a 22-point jump on ExploitBench Internal Port with tighter cybersecurity safeguards, while Sol targets everyday agentic work. Both are in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users, and in the API with Ultrafast now extended to Pro 200.

Nvidia Board Rules Out Jensen Huang's Return as Revenue Doubles

New developments: after Huang publicly said he would be honored to return as CEO if the board decided it was in the company's interest, board chair Harvey Jones said during the earnings call that the board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate him. Nvidia's first report since the transition beat expectations, with quarterly revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103 percent year over year.

Microsoft Launches Seven MAI Models and Licensed Weights at Ignite

At Ignite, Microsoft AI introduced seven new models, including MAI Code 2, which it says reaches 98 percent of GPT-6.1 Sol's coding quality at 40 percent of the price, plus MAI Cyber 2 Flash, MAI Realtime 1, MAI Image 2.7, Decision 1.1 and MAI-Transcribe-2. The MAI Licensed Weights program lets trusted customers customize a frontier MAI model with their own data and run it in their own Azure environment.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C for AI Drug Design

Isomorphic Labs, the AI drug design company founded by Demis Hassabis after he left the Google DeepMind CEO role, announced a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with existing investors Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek and the UK Sovereign AI Fund participating. Hassabis said the round will accelerate building foundational biology models to cut the time and cost of finding new medicines.

Tencent Releases Hy4 flash Weights Openly

New developments: after launching Hy4 flash as a low-cost everyday model on Monday, Tencent has now released the weights openly, including Hy4 flash-FP8 and Hy4 flash-Base variants. The model is 224 billion parameters total with 12 billion active, natively multimodal with a 1 million-token context, and is released under the Tencent Hy License, with Tencent teasing the larger Hy4 as next.
