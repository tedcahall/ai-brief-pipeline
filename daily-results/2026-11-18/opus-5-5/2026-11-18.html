NOV 18, 2026

📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol to paid ChatGPT users, Codex and the API, cutting Astra's API token prices by 20% and calling it the first model it has released externally that meets its High threshold for AI self-improvement. Artificial Analysis ranks Astra second on its Intelligence Index behind Claude Fable 5.2, though it measured no gain on its Coding Agent Index and several early testers described the model as uneven.

Microsoft used Ignite to launch seven MAI models built to run agents around the clock at lower cost, while Isomorphic Labs raised a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital to advance its AI-designed drug candidates.

Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year earlier, as Vera Rubin systems began shipping, and Tencent released open weights for its Hy4 flash model.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol at Lower Cost

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.2 Astra for complex projects and GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday work, available to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users and in the API. Astra's API prices fall 20% to $8 input and $40 output per million tokens, while Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol pricing. OpenAI says Astra is its first externally released model to meet the High threshold for AI self-improvement.

Microsoft Launches Seven MAI Models at Ignite

Microsoft AI released seven models at Ignite, including MAI Cyber 2 Flash, MAI Code 2, MAI Image 2.7 and MAI Realtime 1. Microsoft says MAI Code 2 reaches 96% of Claude Opus 5.5's performance across four coding benchmarks at 5% of the price, and that MAI Image 2.7 ranks first on Artificial Analysis image editing. MAI Code 2 is available in GitHub Copilot.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C

Isomorphic Labs, the AI drug-design company spun out of Google DeepMind, raised $5 billion in a Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with Andreessen Horowitz joining and Alphabet continuing to invest. The company did not disclose a valuation. TechCrunch reports the round follows a $2.1 billion Series B in May as Isomorphic works to take candidates toward clinical trials.

Nvidia Posts $115.8 Billion Quarter as Vera Rubin Ships

Nvidia reported third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year earlier, including data center revenue of $107.6 billion. Interim CEO and CFO Colette Kress said Vera Rubin is off to an explosive start as NVL72 racks began shipping. Nvidia guided fourth-quarter revenue to $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, assuming no data center compute revenue from China.

Tencent Opens the Weights of Hy4 Flash

Tencent released open weights for Hy4 flash, including FP8 and Base versions, under the Tencent Hy License, days after Tencent launched the model. The model card describes 220 billion total parameters with 12 billion active per token, native text and image input, and a context window of up to 1 million tokens. vLLM and SGLang announced day-zero support.
