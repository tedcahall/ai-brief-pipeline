🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol With Lower Astra API Prices

New developments: OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on November 17, after its November 16 GPT-6 Astra science collaboration. OpenAI says Astra scores 5.2 points higher than GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam at less than a third of the cost, and that Astra's API token prices are 20% lower than GPT-6 Astra's.

Tencent Makes Hy4 Flash Open-Weight, a 224B Model With 12B Active Parameters

New developments: Tencent says Hy4 flash is now open-weight, including Hy4 flash-FP8 and Hy4 flash-Base, after its November 16 launch as a low-cost everyday model. The official post lists 224B total parameters, 12B active, a 1M-token context and native multimodal support, and says the weights are available now.

Microsoft Launches Seven MAI Models at Ignite, Led by Code 2

Microsoft AI released seven in-house models at Microsoft Ignite, including MAI Code 2 for GitHub Copilot and MAI Cyber 2 Flash for security defenders. Microsoft says MAI Code 2 delivers 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol's coding quality at about 40% of the price, and that MAI Cyber 2 Flash beats GPT-6 Astra on CyberGymE2E at under 2% of the cost per task. These are company-reported benchmarks.

Google Launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen Robotics Plugin for Antigravity

Google launched a Robotics plugin for Google Antigravity alongside Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a faster model that Google says can plan a task, write control code and adjust robot-arm actions from a camera view. Google says Hydrogen controls a real 6-axis arm through eight tabletop tasks on the Artificial Analysis Robotics 0.1 benchmark, where it scores similarly to Gemini 4 Argon at a lower cost per token.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion in Series C for AI Drug Design

Isomorphic Labs, the AI drug-discovery company spun out of Google DeepMind, announced a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with a16z and other new investors. Alphabet continued to invest, and existing investors including Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek and the UK Sovereign AI Fund participated. The company says the funds will scale its drug design engine and advance its candidates toward clinical trials.
