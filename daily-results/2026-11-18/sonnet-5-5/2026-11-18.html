📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on Tuesday, cutting Astra's API price 20% while drawing criticism from some early testers. Microsoft answered the same day with seven MAI models pitched at near-frontier quality for a fraction of the cost.

Capital and compute kept flowing: Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion, and Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in quarterly revenue while saying it will stop shipping chips to China.

Tencent also opened the weights of Hy4 flash, a 224B-parameter model with 12B active parameters.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol With a 20% Astra Price Cut

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra, its frontier model, and GPT-6.2 Sol, an efficient everyday model, in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API on Tuesday. Astra's API prices fell 20% to $8 input and $40 output per million tokens, while Sol stays at $2 and $10. OpenAI reports Astra ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 on Agents' Last Exam; VentureBeat reports sharp criticism from some early testers.

Microsoft Launches Seven MAI Models at Ignite

Microsoft AI released seven models at Ignite, including MAI Code 2, MAI Cyber 2 Flash, MAI Image 2.7 and MAI Realtime 1. By Microsoft's own measure, MAI Code 2 reaches 96% of Claude Opus 5.5's coding performance at about 5% of the price. Microsoft says Maia 200, its second-generation accelerator, is now in production and delivers 1.7x more tokens per watt.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C

Isomorphic Labs, the AI drug-discovery company spun out of Google DeepMind, raised $5 billion in a Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with Andreessen Horowitz and existing backers including Alphabet's GV and CapitalG participating. TechCrunch reports the round follows a $2.1 billion Series B in May and that no valuation was disclosed. The funds target clinical trials for its first drug candidates.

Nvidia Reports $115.8 Billion Quarter and Says It Will Stop Shipping Chips to China

Nvidia reported revenue of $115.8 billion and profit of $65.3 billion for the quarter ending in October, and guided to about $130 billion for the current quarter, according to The New York Times. On the call, interim CEO Colette Kress said demand is accelerating, and the company said it will stop shipping chips to China after about $2.4 billion in sales there this quarter.

Tencent Releases Hy4 Flash Open Weights

Tencent published open weights for Hy4 flash, a 224B-parameter model with 12B active parameters, a 1M-token context window and native multimodality. The release includes Hy4 flash, an FP8 variant and the pre-trained Base model on Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode under the Tencent Hy License. Tencent also said a larger Hy4 model is coming next.
