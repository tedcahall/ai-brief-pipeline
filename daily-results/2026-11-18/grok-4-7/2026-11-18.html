NOV 18, 2026

📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, with Astra's API priced 20% below GPT-6 Astra, while Microsoft opened Ignite by announcing seven MAI models and saying Maia 200 is in production.

Isomorphic Labs raised a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia. Tencent published open weights for Hy4 Flash, and Jensen Huang said he would return as Nvidia CEO if the board asked.

OpenAI said both GPT-6.2 models meet its High threshold for biological and chemical capabilities, and that Astra is its first external model to meet the High threshold for AI self-improvement.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol

New developments: OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API for Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users. It is a new model release, not the earlier science blog. Astra is $8/$40 per million input/output tokens, 20% below GPT-6 Astra, and cached input is $0.40. Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol prices. OpenAI says Astra gained 5.2 points versus GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam at under a third of the cost.

Microsoft Launches Seven MAI Models at Ignite

Microsoft said it released seven MAI models at Ignite on Nov. 17, including Code 2, Code 2 Flash, Cyber 2 Flash, Image 2.7, Realtime 1, a Dragon Copilot transcription model, and Decision 1.1. It also said Maia 200 is in production at 1.7 times more tokens per watt, and that MAI Licensed Weights let customers run customized models in their own Azure environment. Benchmark and price figures on the page are Microsoft's claims.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C

Isomorphic Labs said it raised $5 billion in a Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with Andreessen Horowitz and other new investors joining. Alphabet continued a significant investment, and existing backers including Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund participated. The company said the round will scale its AI drug-design engine, IsoDDE, and its candidate pipeline.

Tencent Opens Hy4 Flash Weights

New developments: Tencent released open weights for Hy4 Flash, Hy4 Flash-FP8, and a pretrained base model under the Tencent Hy License. The model card lists 220 billion total parameters, including 64 billion Engram parameters, about 12 billion active per token, text and image input, and a 1 million token context. The company post said 224 billion. This is the weights release, not the earlier product launch.

Jensen Huang Says He Would Return if the Board Asked

Jensen Huang posted his first public statement since resigning as Nvidia CEO. He apologized for personal misconduct, said he will cooperate with the board's independent investigation, and said he would be honored to return as CEO if the board decided that served shareholders, employees, the company, and the country. The post does not say the board has asked him back.
