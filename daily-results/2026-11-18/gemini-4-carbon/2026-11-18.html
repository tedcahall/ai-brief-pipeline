🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol With Lower Per-Task Costs

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API, cutting Astra token prices 20% to $8 input and $40 output per million tokens with $0.40 cached input. OpenAI designated Astra its first external model reaching High on AI self-improvement and added tighter cyber safeguards, while Artificial Analysis ranked it second overall behind Claude Fable 5.2 but flat in coding.

Microsoft Releases Seven MAI Models and Licensed Weights at Ignite

At Ignite 2026, Microsoft AI introduced MAI Code 2, MAI Code 2 Flash, MAI Cyber 2 Flash, MAI Image 2.7, MAI Realtime 1, MAI-Transcribe-2, and generally available Decision 1.1, alongside MAI Licensed Weights for custom Azure deployments. Microsoft claimed 96% of Claude Opus 5.5 coding performance at 5% of the cost, while Artificial Analysis independently ranked MAI-Image-2.7 first in image editing.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C for AI Drug Design

Alphabet's AI drug-discovery company Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion in Series C funding co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, joined by Andreessen Horowitz, Alphabet, Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund. The company did not disclose its valuation and said the capital will scale its proprietary IsoDDE drug-design engine and advance AI-designed therapeutic candidates toward clinical trials.

Nvidia Quarterly Revenue Tops $100 Billion at $115.8 Billion

Nvidia reported third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% year over year, driven by $107.6 billion in Data Center sales and $65.3 billion in GAAP net income as Vera Rubin NVL72 systems began shipping. Interim CEO Colette Kress projected fourth-quarter revenue of $130.0 billion excluding China Data Center compute, hours after former CEO Jensen Huang said he would return if asked by the board.

Google Expands Free Gemini Tasks With Calling and Agent Payments

Google rolled out Tasks in Gemini free of charge across the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India, running background workflows on Gemini 4 Hydrogen inside hardware-isolated Agent Compute Engine virtual machines. Starting in the US, Gemini can place simultaneous calls to local businesses, process inbox tasks forwarded to gemini@google.com, and check out via Google Pay using the Agent Payments Protocol with Visa and Mastercard tokens.
