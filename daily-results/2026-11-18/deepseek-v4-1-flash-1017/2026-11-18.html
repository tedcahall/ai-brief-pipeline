NOV 18, 2026

📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Three frontier labs shipped inside one news cycle: OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol with a 20% price cut on Astra, Microsoft announced seven MAI models at Ignite aimed at agents that run around the clock, and Tencent published open weights for Hy4 flash.

The commercial logic was unusually explicit. Microsoft and OpenAI both sold efficiency rather than raw benchmark gains, and independent evaluators placed GPT-6.2 Astra second on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index while its coding-agent score stayed flat.

Capital followed the same theme: Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion for AI drug design, and Nvidia posted $115.8 billion in quarterly revenue in its first report since Jensen Huang resigned.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol at 20% Lower Token Prices

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra, its new frontier model, and GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday work, saying both use fewer output tokens on demanding tasks. OpenAI cut Astra input and output prices 20% below GPT-6 Astra and reduced cached input to $0.40 per million tokens, while Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol pricing. Both ship in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API.

Microsoft Launches Seven MAI Models at Ignite to Cut Agent Costs

Microsoft released seven MAI models at Ignite, led by MAI Code 2 for coding and MAI Cyber 2 Flash for security, and said Maia 200, its second-generation accelerator, is now in production. Microsoft claims MAI Code 2 reaches 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol coding quality at about 40% of the price and 96% of Claude Opus 5.5 at 5% of the price. The lineup targets agents running continuously rather than peak benchmark scores.

Nvidia Posts $115.8 Billion Quarter in First Results Without Huang

Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in revenue for the quarter ended October 25, up 103% from a year earlier, with data center revenue of $107.6 billion. It was the first earnings report since Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive; Colette Kress, interim CEO and chief financial officer, said Vera Rubin is off to an explosive start. GAAP gross margin was 74.8% and diluted earnings per share $2.70.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C for AI Drug Design

Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion in a Series C round co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with Andreessen Horowitz joining and continued investment from Alphabet. The DeepMind spinout said it will scale IsoDDE, its drug design engine, and move AI-designed candidates toward clinical trials. The round follows a $2.1 billion Series B reported in May.

Tencent Publishes Open Weights for Hy4 Flash at 224B Parameters

Tencent published open weights for Hy4 flash, including FP8 and Base variants, one day after launching the model through its API. The model has 224 billion total parameters with 12 billion active, a 1M-token context and native multimodal input, under the Tencent Hy License. API input is priced at about $0.070 per million tokens. New developments: the open-weight release follows the November 16 API launch.
