📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, while Microsoft introduced seven MAI models across coding, security and other tasks. Tencent also made Hy4 Flash weights available, according to its announcement.

Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion for AI drug design, and Google expanded free Gemini Tasks to six countries. Nvidia introduced a collection of models, data and recipes for scientific agents.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol for professional and everyday work. GitHub says both are generally available in Copilot for eligible plans, with a gradual rollout. OpenAI says Astra costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens via API, down from the preceding Astra model.

Microsoft Introduces Seven MAI Models at Ignite

Microsoft announced seven models spanning coding, cybersecurity, image generation, speech and decision tasks. The company says MAI Code 2 is available in GitHub Copilot and reports that it achieved 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol performance at 40% of its price across four selected coding benchmarks. Those comparisons are Microsoft claims.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion for AI Drug Design

Isomorphic Labs announced a $5 billion Series C round co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with continued investment from Alphabet. The AI drug design company says the capital will support its proprietary design engine and therapeutic candidate pipeline. The $5 billion figure is funding raised, not a valuation.

Google Makes Gemini Tasks Free in Six Countries

Google is rolling out Gemini Tasks free of charge in the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia and India, using Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Google says additional capabilities, including email forwarding and texting, start in the United States. It says the agent is designed to seek confirmation before critical actions such as purchases.

Nvidia Introduces Nemotron for Science

Nvidia introduced Nemotron for Science, combining its existing Nemotron 3.5 Super VL model with a chemistry dataset and post-training recipes for specialized research agents. Its LitmusBench v0.2 dataset contains nearly 50,000 chemistry reasoning questions, according to the company. Nvidia says the model and dataset are available on Hugging Face.
