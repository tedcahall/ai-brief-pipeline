🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol With Lower Costs and Tighter Cyber Safeguards

New developments: OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra for complex professional and scientific work and GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday tasks across ChatGPT Work, Codex and its API. Astra’s standard token prices are 20% below GPT-6 Astra. OpenAI says both meet Critical cybersecurity and High biological and chemical thresholds, while Astra is its first external model rated High for AI self-improvement.

Microsoft Debuts Seven MAI Models and Licensed Weights for Enterprise Control

Microsoft introduced MAI Cyber 2 Flash, Code 2 and Code 2 Flash, Image 2.7, Realtime 1, Transcribe 2 and Decision 1.1 at Ignite, emphasizing task-specific price-performance. MAI Licensed Weights lets customers customize frontier MAI models with proprietary data and run them in their own Azure environments. Microsoft’s benchmark and market-position claims are vendor claims.

Google Expands Gemini Tasks With Calls, Payments and App Actions

Google made Gemini Tasks free for hundreds of millions of eligible users in six countries, powered by Gemini 4 Hydrogen. US features include calling businesses, email and messaging handoffs, Google Pay purchases through AP2, and work across more than 60 connected apps. Google says tasks use isolated virtual machines and request approval for critical steps.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C to Scale AI Drug Design

Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion in Series C funding co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with a16z, continued significant Alphabet investment and participation from existing backers. The company will scale its IsoDDE drug-design engine and AI-designed therapeutic candidate pipeline. This is the amount raised, not a valuation.

Tencent Releases Open Weights for Multimodal Hy4 Flash

New developments: following Hy4 Flash’s November 16 launch, Tencent has now published Hy4 flash-FP8 and Hy4 flash-Base weights under the Tencent Hy License. The 224B-parameter mixture-of-experts model activates 12B parameters, supports a 1M-token context and native multimodality. Tencent linked model weights, code and a research paper; the license is not an unrestricted open-source license.
