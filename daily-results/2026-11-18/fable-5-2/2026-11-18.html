🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol With Tighter Cyber Safeguards

New developments: OpenAI released two new models on November 17, GPT-6.2 Astra as its frontier model and GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday work, in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API. Astra costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, 20% below GPT-6 Astra. OpenAI says Astra is its first externally released model to meet its High threshold for AI self-improvement, and both models ship with tighter cybersecurity safeguards.

Microsoft Launches Seven MAI Models at Ignite

Microsoft AI released seven models at Ignite aimed at lower-cost, always-on agents. Microsoft says MAI Code 2 reaches 96% of Claude Opus 5.5's performance at 5% of the price across four coding benchmarks, and that MAI Cyber 2 Flash beats GPT-6 Astra on CyberGymE2E at under 2% of the cost per task. It also introduced MAI Licensed Weights for customer-controlled deployments in Azure.

Nvidia Reports $115.8 Billion Quarter Under Interim CEO Colette Kress

Nvidia reported third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago, with Data Center revenue of $107.6 billion. It guided to $130.0 billion for the fourth quarter, assuming no Data Center compute revenue from China. CNBC reported that board chair Harvey Jones said the board is not considering bringing Jensen Huang back as CEO and has hired a search firm.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C for AI Drug Design

Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion in Series C funding co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, alongside Andreessen Horowitz and other new investors, with continued investment from Alphabet. All existing investors participated, including Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek and the UK Sovereign AI Fund. The company says it is scaling its IsoDDE drug design engine and its pipeline of AI-designed therapeutic candidates.

Google Makes Tasks in Gemini Free in Six Countries

Google is rolling out Tasks in Gemini free of charge in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India, running on Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Advanced features starting in the US let Gemini call businesses, disclosing that it is an AI, and check out through Google Pay using the Agent Payments Protocol with a user-set spending cap.
