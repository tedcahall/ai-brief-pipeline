🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol With Higher Cyber Safeguards

OpenAI launched GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol for ChatGPT, Codex and its API, describing Astra as a frontier model for demanding work and Sol as a more efficient general-purpose option. The company says Astra improved on selected professional-work and science evaluations at lower cost, while Artificial Analysis places it second on its Intelligence Index and reports no gain for Astra on its Coding Agent Index. OpenAI says both models meet its Critical cybersecurity and High biological-and-chemical capability thresholds, and that it tightened safeguards for higher-risk cyber assistance.

Microsoft Introduces MAI Code 2 and Licensed Model Weights at Ignite

Microsoft announced its MAI Code 2 and Code 2 Flash agentic coding models, alongside Cyber 2 Flash and other MAI systems, at Ignite. Code 2 and Flash were generally available through GitHub Copilot and Azure AI Foundry, while Microsoft said trusted customers could obtain licensed MAI weights through Frontier Co. The company framed the launches around enterprise task performance and cost; its competitive benchmark claims remain vendor-reported.

Anthropic Shortens High-Severity Vulnerability Disclosure Timelines

Anthropic says it will shorten coordinated disclosure deadlines starting January 1, 2027: from 90 to 30 days for high- and critical-severity vulnerabilities, and to 45 days for other issues. It will also seek an external coordinator after 15 days without a maintainer response and publish full technical details 30 days after a patch, rather than 45. Anthropic cites faster AI-assisted vulnerability discovery, while retaining a separate seven-day window for actively exploited flaws and exceptions where remediation needs more time.

Google Adds Robotics Plugin for Gemini 4 Hydrogen in Antigravity

Google introduced an Antigravity Robotics plugin that combines Gemini 4 Hydrogen with hardware setup, MuJoCo simulation and closed-loop robot control. Google’s account says developers can move from a prompt or scene image to a working robot-arm task, and its blog demonstrates calibration and a simulated-to-real snack-sorting conveyor workflow. The examples are Google demonstrations, not independent performance tests; the post also makes Hydrogen available through the Gemini API and AI Studio.

Tencent Releases Open-Weight Hy4 Flash With One-Million-Token Context

Tencent Hunyuan released Hy4 Flash and its FP8 and base variants under the Tencent Hy License, describing the model as natively multimodal with 224 billion total parameters, 12 billion active parameters and a one-million-token context. The announcement links downloadable weights and technical documentation, and positions the model for productivity and agentic tasks. These capabilities and positioning are vendor claims; the saved coverage did not include an independent benchmark assessment.
