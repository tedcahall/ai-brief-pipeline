OpenAI Launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol With Record Scores and Lower Prices

New developments: OpenAI released next-generation GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol across ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API, with Astra token prices 20% below GPT-6 Astra. OpenAI reports new highs on Agents' Last Exam and Terminal-Bench Science 0.1; ARC Prize verified 100% on ARC-AGI-3 with a provider adapter (70.3% standard). Astra is its first public model rated High for AI Self-improvement; early testers call it uneven.

Microsoft Launches Seven MAI Models and Licensed Weights at Ignite

Microsoft AI released seven models at Ignite, led by MAI Code 2, which Microsoft says reaches 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol's coding quality at 40% of the price and 96% of Claude Opus 5.5's at 5%. MAI Image 2.7 tops Artificial Analysis's image-editing ranking. The MAI Licensed Weights program lets enterprises customize frontier models and run them in their own Azure environments.

Isomorphic Labs Raises Record $5 Billion Series C for AI Drug Design

Alphabet-backed Isomorphic Labs announced a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with a16z and continued Alphabet investment; BioSpace calls it a biotech single-round record. The DeepMind spinout will scale its IsoDDE drug-design engine and therapeutic pipeline toward clinical trials. Demis Hassabis said the round accelerates the mission to solve all disease.

California Orders Waymo Off Freeways, Prompting Nationwide Pause

California's DMV restricted Waymo's freeway operations after three robotaxis entered an active construction closure on U.S. 101 near Burlingame and a separate incident involving CHP vehicles, citing an imminent hazard to public safety. The order, issued Nov. 11 and publicized Tuesday, runs until corrective actions are proven. Waymo paused freeway rides in all 15 U.S. markets; surface-street service continues.

Tencent Open-Weights Hy4 Flash, a 220B Multimodal Model

New developments: Tencent released open weights for Hy4 Flash, including FP8 and Base versions, under the Tencent Hy License. The model card lists 220B total parameters (12B active per token), 1M-token context and native image input, with hybrid sparse attention and a built-in speculative-decoding drafter. vLLM and SGLang shipped day-0 support; Tencent says the larger Hy4 is next.
