📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, cutting Astra’s API token prices while keeping Sol’s rates unchanged. Microsoft went after the cost of running agents continuously with seven new models, and Tencent made Hy4 flash weights available for teams that want to run the model themselves.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, With Lower API Prices for Astra

New developments: OpenAI released the new GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol in the API, Codex and ChatGPT Work for eligible plans. Astra’s API input and output token prices are 20% lower than GPT-6 Astra’s; Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol’s rates. OpenAI says both produce fewer tokens on demanding tasks and reports that Sol came within half a point of Astra on Cognition’s FrontierCode 2.0 at about one-fifth the cost at maximum effort.

Microsoft Introduces Seven MAI Models for Lower-Cost AI Workloads

Microsoft introduced seven models at Ignite, including MAI Code 2 and Code 2 Flash for software work, MAI Image 2.7 for image editing and MAI Realtime 1 for voice. The company says Realtime 1 starts at one cent per audio minute. The launch targets workloads that run often enough for small differences in per-task cost to matter.

Tencent Opens Hy4 Flash Weights, Including Base and FP8 Checkpoints

New developments: Tencent published downloadable Hy4 flash Base and FP8 weights after introducing the model earlier this week. The model accepts text and images, generates text, and supports a context window of up to one million tokens, according to its model card. The checkpoints are distributed under Tencent’s Hy License.

Nvidia Reports $115.8 Billion in Quarterly Revenue as Data Center Sales Double

Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in quarterly revenue, up 103% year over year, with data center revenue up 110% to $107.6 billion. The company said it had begun shipping Vera Rubin NVL72 systems. Nvidia forecast about $130 billion in revenue for the next quarter, which contains an extra week, without assuming any data center compute sales in China.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion for AI Drug Design

Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion in a Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with participation from Andreessen Horowitz and continued investment from Alphabet. The company says it will use the money to expand its AI drug-design platform and develop more therapeutic candidates. The $5 billion is new financing, not a valuation or proof of clinical results.
