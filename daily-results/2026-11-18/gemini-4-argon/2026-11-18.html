📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Frontier labs are pairing capability updates with steep per-task cost reductions as OpenAI launches GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol alongside Microsoft's seven-model MAI rollout at Ignite. Meanwhile, Chinese labs continue expanding open-weight multimodal infrastructure as Tencent releases weights for its 224-billion-parameter Hy4 Flash and Ant Group's inclusionAI open-sources the Ming-3.0-audio-flash speech model.

Capital and hardware scale remain central across the sector, highlighted by Isomorphic Labs' $5 billion Series C for AI drug discovery and Nvidia reporting $115.8 billion in quarterly revenue while its board rules out a return for former CEO Jensen Huang.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol With Lower API Token Pricing

New developments: OpenAI launched GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol across ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API, cutting Astra token pricing by 20% to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. OpenAI reports Astra leads Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 and Agents' Last Exam at under one-third the per-task cost of GPT-6 Astra, while Artificial Analysis ranks Astra second overall behind Claude Fable 5.2 and flat on its Coding Agent Index.

Microsoft Unveils Seven MAI Models and Licensed Weights Program at Ignite 2026

Microsoft AI introduced seven models at Ignite 2026, including MAI Code 2, MAI Code 2 Flash, MAI Cyber 2 Flash, MAI Image 2.7, MAI Realtime 1, MAI Transcribe 2, and Decision 1.1, alongside a MAI Licensed Weights program for customer Azure deployments. Microsoft states MAI Code 2 reaches 96% of Claude Opus 5.5 coding performance at 5% of the price, while Artificial Analysis ranks MAI Image 2.7 first in image editing.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C for AI Drug Discovery

Alphabet-backed Isomorphic Labs announced a $5 billion Series C funding round co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with participation from Andreessen Horowitz, Alphabet, Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund. CEO Demis Hassabis stated the capital will scale the company's IsoDDE drug design engine and advance its AI-designed therapeutic pipeline as candidates approach human clinical trials.

Tencent Open-Sources 224B Multimodal Hy4 Flash Weights as Ant Releases Ming-3.0-Audio-Flash

New developments: Following Monday's API launch, Tencent released open weights for Hy4 Flash, including Hy4-flash-FP8 and Hy4-flash-Base checkpoints, with Day-0 support in vLLM and SGLang. The 224-billion-parameter hybrid Mixture-of-Experts model activates 12 billion parameters per token across a 1-million-token context window under the Tencent Hy License. Separately, Ant Group's inclusionAI open-sourced Ming-3.0-audio-flash under the MIT license, a 126-billion-parameter native audio-language model.

Nvidia Quarterly Revenue Reaches $115.8 Billion as Board Rules Out Jensen Huang Return

Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in quarterly revenue and $65.3 billion in profit for the three months ended in October, more than doubling year-over-year sales and projecting roughly $130 billion for the current quarter. Earlier Tuesday, former CEO Jensen Huang stated he would return if asked by the board, but board chair Harvey C. Jones told investors the board unanimously accepted Huang's resignation and has no plans to reinstate him.
