🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Ships New Astra and Sol Flagship Models at Lower Cost

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on Tuesday across ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API. Astra scores 5.2 points above GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam at less than a third of the cost per task, and its API prices fall 20 percent to $8/$40 per million tokens with cheaper cached input. Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol pricing. Artificial Analysis ranks Astra second overall behind Claude Fable 5.2 and notes a flat Coding Agent Index score.

Nvidia Posts Record Quarterly Revenue in First Report Since CEO Departure

Nvidia reported fiscal Q3 revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103 percent year over year and its first quarter above $100 billion, with Data Center revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110 percent. The Tuesday release, issued under interim CEO and CFO Colette Kress, was the first since founder Jensen Huang resigned. Q4 guidance is $130 billion plus or minus 3 percent with no China Data Center compute revenue assumed, and Vera Rubin NVL72 racks are now shipping.

Jensen Huang Breaks Silence and Offers to Return as Nvidia CEO

In his first public statement since resigning on November 12, Jensen Huang wrote that he made serious mistakes in his personal life, deeply regrets the problems he created for Nvidia's board, and would be honored to return as CEO if the board judges it in the company's interest. The Guardian, citing FT and WSJ reports, says the board is not considering reinstatement. US authorities confirmed a related Commerce and Justice investigation into Singapore-based Megaspeed, suspected of diverting US AI chips to China.

Microsoft Ignite Debuts Copilot Workspaces and Seven New MAI Models

At Ignite 2026 in San Francisco, Satya Nadella unveiled Copilot Workspaces for multiplayer human-agent collaboration, the Microsoft IQ organizational-intelligence platform, and seven new MAI models spanning code, cyber, image and audio workloads. Microsoft-Decision-1.1 reached general availability in Foundry with more than 4,000 customers, and GPT-6.2 plus Meta's latest models are coming to Foundry. Microsoft AI says MAI Code 2 delivers 98 percent of GPT-6.1 Sol performance at 40 percent of the cost.

Isomorphic Labs Lands $5 Billion to Scale AI Drug Discovery

Isomorphic Labs announced a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, alongside Andreessen Horowitz and other new investors, with continued significant investment from Alphabet and participation from all existing investors including Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek and the UK Sovereign AI Fund. The company founded by Demis Hassabis will scale its IsoDDE drug-design engine and advance AI-designed therapeutic candidates toward clinical evaluation.
