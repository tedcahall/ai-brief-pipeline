🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol

New developments: OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol across ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API, following its Nov 16 GPT-6 Astra physics blog post. OpenAI says Astra cuts per-task costs by over two-thirds versus GPT-6 Astra while setting new highs on Agents' Last Exam, Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, and Harvey LAB-AA, though independent replication is pending. API input pricing drops 20% to $8 per million tokens.

Nvidia Tops $100 Billion in Quarterly Revenue as Board Rules Out Huang Return

Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in quarterly revenue and $65.3 billion in profit for the period ending in October, projecting $130 billion next quarter while announcing it will stop shipping chips to China. Board chair Harvey C. Jones ruled out reinstating former CEO Jensen Huang, who resigned last week over code-of-conduct violations and offered Tuesday to return. CFO Colette Kress is serving as interim chief executive during the board's search.

Microsoft Unveils Seven MAI Models and Copilot Workspaces at Ignite 2026

At Ignite 2026 in San Francisco, Microsoft introduced seven proprietary MAI models, led by MAI Code 2 and MAI Code 2 Flash for agentic software engineering, alongside MAI Image 2.7, MAI Cyber 2 Flash, MAI Realtime 1, MAI-Transcribe-2, and Decision 1.1. The company also launched MAI Licensed Weights for enterprise customization, Copilot Workspaces for multi-user agent collaboration, and Microsoft IQ to unify organizational data across Fabric and Foundry.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C for AI Drug Design

Alphabet spinout Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion in Series C funding co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with participation from Andreessen Horowitz, Alphabet, Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund. The London-based company plans to use the capital to scale its proprietary IsoDDE drug design engine and advance its AI-designed therapeutic candidate pipeline toward clinical trials.

Google Rolls Out Free Gemini Tasks in Six Countries With AP2 Checkout

Google began rolling out Gemini Tasks free of charge to users in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India, running on its Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. The update adds U.S. capabilities including automated phone calls to local businesses, email forwarding to gemini@google.com, Google Password Manager integration, and Google Pay checkout via the Agent Payments Protocol with user-defined spending caps.
