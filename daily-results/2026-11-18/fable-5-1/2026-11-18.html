📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on Tuesday, cutting Astra's API prices 20% and claiming new highs on professional and scientific benchmarks, while Artificial Analysis found no gain on its Coding Agent Index and early testers described the model as uneven. Hours later Microsoft AI launched seven MAI models at Ignite, pitching MAI Code 2 at 96% of Claude Opus 5.5's coding performance for 5% of the price.

Isomorphic Labs raised $5 billion in a Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia, and Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion under interim CEO Colette Kress as former CEO Jensen Huang said he would return if the board asked.

Tencent released open weights for Hy4 flash under its own license, and Google made Gemini Tasks free in six countries, with calling, texting and payment actions starting in the US.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol at Lower Per-Task Cost

New developments: OpenAI launched GPT-6.2 Astra, successor to GPT-6 Astra, and GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday work, available in ChatGPT, Codex and the API. Astra's API prices drop 20% to $8 input and $40 output per million tokens, with cached input at $0.40. OpenAI says Astra sets new highs on Agents' Last Exam and Terminal-Bench-Science and is its first released model to reach the High threshold for AI self-improvement under its Preparedness Framework.

Microsoft AI Launches Seven MAI Models at Ignite

Microsoft AI released seven models at Ignite, including MAI Cyber 2 Flash, MAI Code 2, MAI Image 2.7, MAI Realtime 1 and Decision 1.1. Microsoft claims MAI Code 2 reaches 96% of Claude Opus 5.5's coding performance at 5% of the price, and that MAI Cyber 2 Flash beats GPT-6 Astra at under 2% of the cost per task. MAI Licensed Weights lets enterprises run customized MAI models in their own Azure environments.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C

Isomorphic Labs, the Alphabet drug-design company led by Demis Hassabis, announced a $5 billion Series C co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, alongside Andreessen Horowitz and continued investment from Alphabet. Existing investors Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek and the UK Sovereign AI Fund also participated. The company says the funding will scale its IsoDDE drug design engine and its pipeline of AI-designed therapeutic candidates.

Nvidia Posts $115.8 Billion Quarter Under Interim CEO Kress

New developments: Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago, with data center revenue of $107.6 billion, in its first earnings since Jensen Huang's resignation. Interim CEO Colette Kress said Vera Rubin NVL72 is shipping in the fastest ramp in company history. Nvidia guided fourth-quarter revenue to $130 billion and assumes no data center compute revenue from China. Separately, Huang said he would return as CEO if the board asked.

Tencent Releases Open Weights for Hy4 flash

New developments: Two days after launching Hy4 flash as a low-cost everyday model, Tencent Hunyuan published the weights on Hugging Face and GitHub under the Tencent Hy License, including FP8 and Base variants. The model card describes about 220 billion total parameters with 12 billion active per token, native text and image input, a 1M-token context window and a built-in DFly2 drafter for speculative decoding. vLLM and SGLang announced day-0 support.
