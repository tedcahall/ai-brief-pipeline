🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol With Lower Task Costs

New developments: Unlike the earlier science-blog announcement, OpenAI has now released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol across ChatGPT Work, Codex and its API. Astra’s input and output token prices are 20% below GPT-6 Astra’s; Sol retains prior rates. Artificial Analysis reports stronger general intelligence and cost efficiency, but no Coding Agent Index improvement. OpenAI reports tighter safeguards for higher-risk cybersecurity requests.

Microsoft Launches MAI Models for Coding, Cyber Defense and Voice

Microsoft announced its new MAI lineup at Ignite, covering coding, cybersecurity, image editing, live voice, transcription and routing. The company says MAI Code 2 reaches 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol’s quality across four coding benchmarks at about 40% of the price. Availability varies by product, including GitHub Copilot, Microsoft Foundry and limited previews; these are workload-specific comparisons, not universal performance guarantees.

Tencent Releases Hy4 Flash Open Weights and Base Model

New developments: Following the earlier Hy4 Flash launch, Tencent has released instruction-tuned, FP8 and base weights under the Tencent Hy License. The model accepts text and images and supports a one-million-token context. Tencent reports lower per-task costs on its internal WorkIndex evaluation. Its announcement says 224 billion parameters, while the current model card says 220 billion; that discrepancy remains unresolved.

Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion for AI Drug Design

Isomorphic Labs announced $5 billion in Series C funding, co-led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with participation from Alphabet, a16z and existing investors. The company says it is scaling its proprietary drug-design engine, IsoDDE, and its AI-designed therapeutic pipeline. The figure is newly raised capital, not a valuation, and the announcement does not establish clinical efficacy or regulatory approval.

Reflection Opens Trusted Access to Laser Security-Testing Preview

Reflection introduced a Trusted Access Program offering selected commercial partners a research preview of Laser, its autonomous penetration-testing harness for the Mirror terminal agent. The company describes scoped engagements, a shared evidence ledger and a separate supervisor reviewing the executor’s findings. Access is restricted to accepted organizations conducting authorized testing; this is not an unrestricted public release or independently demonstrated security guarantee.
