🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Mathematician Claims AI-Assisted Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

Levent Alpöge, a mathematician at Anthropic, wrote that working with Claude he discovered a proof of the Riemann hypothesis, first posed in 1859 and one of the Clay Institute's million-dollar problems. He posted a Lean-coded argument, which scientists say should allow experts to verify it. Mathematicians quoted by Scientific American call the claim plausible but unverified, and checking is underway.

OpenAI Details Defenses Against Adversarial Distillation

OpenAI published research on combatting adversarial distillation, where actors use a model's outputs and reasoning traces to train rival models without safeguards. In adaptive red-teaming, the success rate for extracting protected reasoning from an earlier request fell from 54.0 percent against GPT-6 Astra to 0.2 percent against GPT-6.2 Astra with updated safeguards. OpenAI also described campaign enforcement and industry information sharing.

Thinking Machines Releases Open-Weights Inkling 2 Mini

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weights mixture-of-experts model with 26.4B total and 3.4B active parameters whose middle layers loop three times per token. The company says it leads open-weights models under 100B parameters on agentic coding benchmarks including DeepSWE v1.1 at 48.2 percent, with a roughly 20GB memory footprint that runs on consumer laptops. A variable-loop research variant ships alongside it.

SpaceX Sells $32 Billion of AI Chip Debt at Yields Above 8%

SpaceX completed a $32 billion debt sale on Thursday to fund Nvidia AI chip purchases through the Saturn Funding Trust, a special-purpose vehicle managed by an Apollo Global Management entity that keeps the borrowing off SpaceX's balance sheet. Goldman Sachs and Apollo ran the three-part deal, and the $25.2 billion Class A3 notes priced at a yield of 8.259 percent, far above the 5.95 percent US high-grade average.

Nvidia Nears $500 Million-Plus Deal for Diffusion Startup Inception

The Information reports that Nvidia is in advanced talks to acquire Inception, a two-year-old startup building language models with diffusion processes that refine whole responses rather than generating token by token, for at least $500 million plus performance bonuses. The report, citing three people familiar with the talks, says the deal would bolster Nvidia's open-source Nemotron models. Neither company commented and no agreement is signed.
