🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Mathematician Claims Riemann Hypothesis Proof With Claude

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge published a four-page note and a 17.4-million-line Lean formalization claiming a proof of the 167-year-old Riemann Hypothesis and the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis developed with Claude. Alpöge stated the argument chains roughly square-root-of-X theta lifts across totally definite unitary groups to extend Stepanov's determinant method. Independent mathematicians are still reviewing the Lean files to verify whether the formalization is sound and complete.

Thinking Machines Lab Releases Open-Weights Inkling 2 Mini

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini, an Apache 2.0 open-weights looped Mixture-of-Experts model with 26.4 billion total parameters and 3.4 billion active parameters per pass. Running its middle 12 layers three times per token yields 5.4 billion weight activations, allowing the model to run locally on 32GB Macs or Nvidia DGX Spark while scoring 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1 and 20.1 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.

OpenAI Details Defenses Against Adversarial Model Distillation

OpenAI published research on detecting and disrupting adversarial distillation, warning that unauthorized extraction of frontier chain-of-thought traces can transfer dual-use cybersecurity and biology capabilities to models lacking safety guardrails. Using layered training, classifiers, and API controls to shield protected reasoning in GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, OpenAI reported reducing automated red-team extraction success from 54.0% on GPT-6 Astra to 0.2% on GPT-6.2 Astra in internal adaptive evaluations.

SpaceX Closes $32 Billion Bond Offering to Lease Nvidia AI Chips

SpaceX completed a $32 billion debt sale at yields exceeding 8% to finance Nvidia AI chip acquisitions, part of a broader $40 billion financing package that includes bank loans. Structured off SpaceX's balance sheet through the Saturn Funding Trust managed by an Apollo Global Management entity, the transaction purchases semiconductors and leases them back to SpaceX for roughly five years as unsecured corporate debt capacity tightens.

California Panel Recommends Emergency Shutdown Power Over Frontier AI

California Gov. Gavin Newsom released AI safety policy recommendations drafted by a state-appointed expert panel proposing emergency shutdown authority over dangerous AI systems when developers fail to contain them. The framework also recommends mandatory independent third-party safety audits, whistleblower protections, and caps on recursive self-improvement for frontier labs such as OpenAI and Anthropic, with state lawmakers preparing legislative proposals for the upcoming January session.
