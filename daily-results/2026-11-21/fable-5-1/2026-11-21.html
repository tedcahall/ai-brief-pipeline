🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Mathematician Claims Riemann Hypothesis Proof Found With Claude

Levent Alpöge, a mathematician at Anthropic, posted a statement on Friday saying he believes he has, working with Claude, discovered a proof of the Riemann hypothesis and its generalized form, released as Lean code. Scientific American reported that MIT mathematician Andrew Sutherland independently compiled the Lean proof, while researchers still await a comparator run confirming the formal statement matches the hypothesis.

Thinking Machines Releases 26B Looped Transformer Built to Run on a Laptop

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weights mixture-of-experts model with 26.4B total and 3.4B active parameters that runs its middle layers three times per token. The lab reports 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1 and says the model leads open-weights models under 100B parameters on that benchmark. Weights are mostly NVFP4 with a roughly 20GB footprint, and vLLM, SGLang and llama.cpp announced day-zero support.

OpenAI Details Defenses Against Adversarial Distillation of Its Models

OpenAI published research on how it detects and disrupts adversarial distillation, where outside actors use a model's outputs to train a copy without its safeguards. The company says protections against extracting protected reasoning were strengthened in GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, and reports that in an internal adaptive evaluation the average attack success rate fell from 54.0% against GPT-6 Astra to 0.2% against GPT-6.2 Astra.

Nasdaq Erases 2026 Gains as Anthropic and Nvidia Slide in Global AI Selloff

New developments: Anthropic shares fell 9% to $88.30 on their second trading day, near the $83 IPO price, as the Nasdaq Composite erased its 2026 gains and closed 5% below its January 1 level, the Guardian reported. Nvidia dropped 14% after California served it a subpoena over chips reaching China and, per the Motley Fool, after Reuters reported the SEC had opened an accounting investigation.

SpaceX Sells $32 Billion of Bonds to Finance Nvidia Chips at Yields Above 8%

SpaceX sold $32 billion of bonds on Thursday through a trust that buys Nvidia chips and leases them back to the company, the Motley Fool reported, citing Bloomberg. The largest piece, about $25.2 billion, priced at a yield of 8.259%, against a 5.95% average for high-grade corporate debt that day. The deal is part of a financing package of more than $40 billion announced in October, with chips expected from January.
