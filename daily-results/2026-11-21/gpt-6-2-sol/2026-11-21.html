🌐 TOP STORIES

Thinking Machines Lab Releases Open Weights for Inkling 2 Mini

The 26.4-billion-parameter model reuses its middle layers three times per token, letting it spend more computation on reasoning without adding more weights. Thinking Machines reports a roughly 20 GB memory footprint and a 48.2% score on DeepSWE v1.1; benchmark setups vary across models. The lab also released a variable-loop research variant and made the main model available on Tinker.

Anthropic Mathematician Claims Claude-Assisted Riemann Hypothesis Proof

Levent Alpöge says he and Claude found a proof of the Riemann hypothesis that extends to its generalized form. He posted a summary and links to a Lean formalization. The claim is not a confirmed result: experts still need to check that the formalization captures the intended mathematical statements and that the argument is sound.

SpaceX Sells $32 Billion of Debt to Finance AI Chips, Bloomberg Reports

Bloomberg reports that a special-purpose vehicle will use the proceeds to buy Nvidia chips and lease them to SpaceX for about five years. The bond sale is part of a financing package exceeding $40 billion, including bank loans. About $25.2 billion of the notes priced at an 8.259% yield. This is debt financing, not an equity round.

Alibaba Reports 53% Growth in AI Cloud and Compute Revenue

Alibaba says September-quarter revenue from its AI Cloud and Compute Services unit reached 61.0 billion yuan, about $9.1 billion, with sales to external customers up 64% year over year. Cloud adjusted EBITA rose 121%. The company also reiterated that its T-Head Zhenwu V900 chip for AI training and inference is scheduled for commercial release in the first quarter of 2027.

Reflection AI Tests Beam’s Autonomy in an Office Vending Business

Reflection and Andon Labs gave Beam discretion over stock, pricing and orders within preset budgets and safety rules. In the New York trial, the model resisted direct attempts to get it to break its rules, but it struggled to complete some multi-step tasks and acted on fabricated claims. These are initial findings from a controlled deployment, not evidence of a containment escape.
