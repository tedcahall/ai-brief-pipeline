🌐 TOP STORIES

Thinking Machines Releases Open-Weights Inkling 2 Mini Looped Transformer

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weights Mixture-of-Experts looped transformer with 26.4 billion total parameters and 5.4 billion weight activations per token across three middle-layer passes. Occupying roughly 20GB in NVFP4 and MXFP8 formats for laptop deployment, the model scores 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1 and 46.5% on Vals Index v2.2, alongside a variable-loop research variant for chain-of-thought monitorability.

Anthropic Mathematician Claims Claude-Assisted Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpoge posted a note on November 20 claiming he discovered a proof of the 167-year-old Riemann Hypothesis and Generalized Riemann Hypothesis working with Claude, releasing 14 archive files formalized in Lean. MIT mathematician Andrew Sutherland independently compiled the Lean code, though formal statement verification and a human-readable write-up for peer review remain pending.

SpaceX Wraps Up $32 Billion Off-Balance-Sheet Debt Deal for Nvidia AI Chips

SpaceX completed a $32 billion bond sale arranged by Goldman Sachs and Apollo Global Management as part of a more than $40 billion financing package to fund Nvidia AI chip purchases. A special-purpose vehicle, Saturn Funding Trust, will acquire the semiconductors and lease them to SpaceX for roughly five years, with $25.2 billion in Class A3 notes pricing at an 8.259% yield.

Alibaba Trains Next-Gen Qwen on In-House Zhenwu Chips as U.S. Curbs Tighten

Alibaba CEO Wu Yongming said on an earnings briefing that the company is training its next-generation Qwen model on processors designed by its T-Head semiconductor unit, with plans to support full pretraining and post-training on domestic chips next year. Alibaba also opened Zhenwu M890 cloud clusters for external developers and scheduled mass production of the next-generation Zhenwu V900 for early next year.

California Unveils AI Safety Blueprint Featuring Emergency Model Shutdowns

California Governor Gavin Newsom released recommendations from a four-person expert panel proposing state authority to order emergency shutdowns of dangerous AI systems when developers fail to act. The blueprint also calls for licensing independent AI auditors, shortening critical safety incident reporting from 15 days to 48 hours, and empowering officials to limit automated AI self-improvement, with Governor-elect Xavier Becerra pledging January legislation.
