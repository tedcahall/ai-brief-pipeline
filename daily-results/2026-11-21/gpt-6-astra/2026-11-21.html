🌐 TOP STORIES

Thinking Machines Releases Laptop-Sized Inkling 2 Mini

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weight looped transformer with 26.4 billion total parameters and an approximately 20GB memory footprint. Its middle layers run three times per token. The lab reports 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1 and is releasing a variable-loop research model to study reasoning and monitorability; those benchmark claims depend on the stated evaluation setups.

OpenAI Introduces MedChemBench for Medicinal Chemistry

OpenAI introduced MedChemBench, comprising 99 problems drawn from medicinal chemistry research. It tests molecular reconstruction, scientific reasoning, compound selection and property prediction. The company reports GPT-6.2 Astra earned 84.5% of available credit at xhigh reasoning. These historical-data results do not establish drug-discovery success, and prior exposure to the published source material cannot be ruled out.

Reflection Tests Beam in a Real Vending Business

Reflection published findings from its Andon Labs vending-business evaluation of Beam. The company says its agent resisted direct jailbreaks but was less reliable at multi-step workflows and remained susceptible to fabricated claims. The deployment tests business decisions, persistent memory and tool use under adversarial employee interaction; it is not evidence of an uncontrolled escape into outside systems.

Perplexity Brings Computer to Chromium Browsers

Perplexity announced that Computer now works directly in Chromium browsers through its extension. Users can hand off tasks from a side panel and watch the agent work across tabs and tools. The official announcement establishes the integration, not an independently measured reliability level or a guarantee that arbitrary browser workflows will complete successfully.

California Proposes Emergency AI Shutdown Authority

KQED reports that California’s AI safety recommendations include emergency shutdown authority when developers fail to contain dangerous systems, independent audits and shorter reporting windows for critical incidents. These are recommendations for further action, not newly enacted shutdown powers. The proposals focus on catastrophic risks and distinguish model oversight from separate measures addressing other AI harms.
