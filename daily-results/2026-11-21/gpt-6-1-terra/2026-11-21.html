🌐 TOP STORIES

Thinking Machines Releases Laptop-Sized Inkling 2 Mini

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, a 26.4-billion-parameter open-weights model that repeats its middle layers three times per token. The lab reports a 48.2% DeepSWE v1.1 score and an approximately 20 GB memory footprint. Its own tests found shorter but still legible chains of thought as looping increased; benchmark comparisons are the lab’s claims.

OpenAI Details Defenses Against Adversarial Distillation

OpenAI described efforts to detect and disrupt unauthorized distillation, where another model learns from a frontier model’s outputs without necessarily inheriting its safeguards. In an internal adaptive test, the company reports that extraction of prior protected reasoning fell from 54% on GPT-6 Astra with original safeguards to 0.2% on GPT-6.2 Astra with updated safeguards.

OpenAI Introduces a Medicinal Chemistry Reasoning Benchmark

OpenAI introduced MedChemBench to assess AI reasoning on medicinal chemistry tasks drawn from historical research. The company says its results show progress alongside remaining weaknesses in compound selection and numerical prediction. It cautions that the benchmark does not establish that models can discover drugs or improve laboratory decisions, and prior exposure to source material cannot be ruled out.

Merck and Isomorphic Labs Partner on Cancer Drug Candidates

Merck and Isomorphic Labs announced a collaboration to discover small-molecule candidates for an oncology target. Isomorphic will use its AI drug-design engine and Merck data to make predictions and molecular designs; Merck will handle subsequent development and commercialization. The agreement includes an upfront payment, potential milestones and royalties, but the announcement does not disclose amounts.

SpaceX Completes $32 Billion AI Chip Debt Deal

SpaceX completed a $32 billion debt sale to finance access to Nvidia chips, according to Bloomberg reporting published by Taipei Times. The chips will be acquired by a special-purpose trust and leased to SpaceX for about five years. The report says most of the offering carried yields above 8%, and the broader financing also includes bank loans.
