🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Mathematician Claims Claude-Assisted Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge posted a statement and Lean code on November 20 claiming he and Claude discovered a proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, encompassing the 1859 Riemann Hypothesis Millennium Prize problem. MIT mathematician Andrew Sutherland independently compiled the Lean code, though mathematicians cautioned that checking the formalization and understanding the mathematical argument will still take time, according to Scientific American.

Thinking Machines Lab Releases Open-Weights 26.4B Looped Transformer Inkling 2 Mini

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini, an Apache 2.0 open-weights Mixture-of-Experts looped transformer with 26.4 billion total parameters, 3.4 billion active parameters, and 5.4 billion weight activations per token across three middle-layer passes. With a roughly 20GB memory footprint for laptops, the lab reports it leads open-weights models under 100 billion parameters on DeepSWE v1.1 (48.2%) and Vals Index v2.2 (46.5%), alongside a variable-loop research model.

SpaceX Wraps Up $32 Billion AI Chip Debt Sale at Yields Topping 8%

SpaceX completed a $32 billion off-balance-sheet bond sale arranged by Goldman Sachs and Apollo Global Management as part of a more than $40 billion financing package to fund Nvidia AI chip purchases via the Saturn Funding Trust special-purpose vehicle, Bloomberg reported. SpaceX will lease the chips for roughly five years, with about $25.2 billion in Class A3 notes pricing at an 8.259% yield, well above average high-grade yields.

OpenAI Details Defenses Against Adversarial Model Distillation

OpenAI published research on detecting and disrupting coordinated adversarial distillation, warning that unauthorized extraction of chain-of-thought reasoning traces can transfer dual-use cybersecurity and biology capabilities without inherited safeguards. Combining adversarial training, input and output classifiers, and API controls, OpenAI reported that its internal automated red-teaming attack success rate for recovering protected reasoning dropped from 54.0% on GPT-6 Astra to 0.2% on GPT-6.2 Astra.

TSMC Tightens China AI Chip Curbs as New US Export Rules Close Loophole

Following new US Department of Commerce rules that took effect November 19 and closed a compliance verification route, TSMC halted affected Chinese chip orders at 16 nanometers and below unless customers obtain US export licenses, according to Reuters reporting in The Straits Times. Impacted Chinese designers are generally restricted to 28-nanometer and 40-nanometer processes without high-bandwidth memory or advanced packaging, while TSMC said it will comply with the regulations.
