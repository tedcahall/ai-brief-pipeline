🌐 TOP STORIES

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Mini as an Open-Weights Local Model

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini, a 26.4-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 3.4 billion active parameters and an approximately 20 GB memory footprint. Its middle layers loop three times per token, and the company says the design improves reasoning efficiency on coding tasks. The weights are open, and the model is available on Tinker; benchmark comparisons are the lab’s own and use differing evaluation setups.

Pro² Agent Uses Explicit Problem States to Guide AI Research

A November 19 arXiv preprint introduces Pro² Agent, which represents research blockers and candidate fixes explicitly, then uses controlled experiments to update their importance. On the complete MLE-Bench, the authors report matching the best previously reported 70.7% medal rate under a 24-hour budget and reaching 76.0% with 48 hours. The longer budget matters when comparing the headline result, and the paper also says the agent produces reusable research notes.

OpenAI Introduces MedChemBench for Medicinal-Chemistry Reasoning

OpenAI introduced MedChemBench, a benchmark built from 99 problems in 117 papers and patents, covering 761 molecular structures. It tests structure reconstruction, evidence-based reasoning, compound ranking and property prediction, including tradeoffs among activity, permeability and breakdown. OpenAI reports GPT-6.2 Astra earned 84.5% of available credit at its xhigh setting; the benchmark and scores are company-produced and do not establish clinical drug-discovery outcomes.

SpaceX Completes $32 Billion Chip-Backed Debt Financing

SpaceX sold $32 billion in debt to finance access to Nvidia AI chips, with the bulk priced at yields above 8%, according to reporting originally published by Bloomberg. The Taipei Times account describes a three-part transaction involving a special-purpose trust and says the equipment will be leased for about five years. Bloomberg’s direct page was blocked during verification; the accessible report corroborates the amount and structure but the financing terms remain attributed reporting, not an AI-company forecast.

Report: Nvidia in Talks to Acquire Diffusion-Model Startup Inception for $500 Million or More

The Information reported that Nvidia is in advanced talks to acquire Inception, a startup developing diffusion-based language models, for at least $500 million plus performance-related awards. This is a reported proposed transaction, not an announced or completed acquisition. The Information’s page was accessible only as a paywall shell during verification; The Decoder independently repeated the report and attributed it to three people familiar with the discussions.
