🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Mathematician Claims Claude-Assisted Proof of Riemann Hypothesis

Levent Alpöge, a mathematician at Anthropic, said Friday he proved the Riemann hypothesis working with a non-public version of Claude over about six weeks. He released a Lean proof exceeding 250,000 pages. Terence Tao said verification will take time. Alpöge said the timing, one day after Anthropic's stock debut, was coincidental.

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Mini, a Laptop-Sized Looped Transformer

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, an Apache 2.0 open-weights mixture-of-experts model with 26.4B total and 3.4B active parameters that loops its middle layers three times per token. The lab reports 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1 and 20.1 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, leading open models under 100B parameters. It runs in about 20GB of memory.

OpenAI Says New Defenses Cut Reasoning-Extraction Attacks to 0.2%

OpenAI published research on adversarial distillation, where attackers train copycat models on frontier outputs without safeguards. In internal adaptive red-teaming, the average success rate for extracting protected reasoning fell from 54.0% against GPT-6 Astra to 0.2% against GPT-6.2 Astra with updated defenses. The company also detailed campaign-level detection of coordinated extraction.

SpaceX Sells $32 Billion in AI-Chip Bonds at Yields Above 8%

SpaceX sold $32 billion of bonds Thursday to fund access to AI chips, with the bulk priced at yields above 8%, according to Bloomberg. The off-balance-sheet structure, part of a financing exceeding $40 billion, buys Nvidia chips through a trust that leases them to SpaceX. The high yields signal investor concern about returns on AI spending.

Newsom Proposes Emergency AI Shutdown Authority and RSI Caps

California Governor Gavin Newsom released recommendations November 18 for mandatory AI safety standards, independent third-party oversight, emergency state shutdown authority over dangerous systems, and caps on recursive self-improvement. The proposals build on SB 813 and AB 1405 and require further action; they are not yet law.
