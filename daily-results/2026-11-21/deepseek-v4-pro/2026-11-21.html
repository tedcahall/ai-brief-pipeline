AI Claims Second Millennium Prize: Generalized Riemann Hypothesis Proof Generated with Claude

Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge, using Claude, produced a 250,000+ page Lean-only proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis. Terence Tao confirmed the proof uses modern Langlands program and arithmetic geometry developments and is now being verified by the Lean community. Separately, Alpöge, Buckmaster, and Coiculescu published human-readable AI-assisted proofs of blowup for both the unforced Euler and Navier-Stokes equations in three dimensions, also formalized in Lean.

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Mini: Looped Transformer Runs on Laptops, Leads Open-Weights Under 100B

Inkling 2 Mini is an Apache 2.0-licensed Mixture-of-Experts model with 26.4B total parameters using a looped transformer architecture where middle layers run three times per token for deeper reasoning. Memory footprint is roughly 20GB, enabling laptop execution. Scores 20.1 on the AA Intelligence Index, 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1, and leads all open-weights models under 100B parameters on agentic coding benchmarks. A variable-loop research variant is released for monitorability studies.

Alibaba Deploys Homegrown Zhenwu Chips to Train Qwen as US Export Controls Tighten

Alibaba CEO Wu Yongming confirmed the company is training its next-generation Qwen model on domestically designed Zhenwu chips from subsidiary T-Head, with plans to use Chinese-made chips for the entire training process next year. The Zhenwu 810E matches Nvidia's H20 on some inference tasks at 8-bit precision. Alibaba released SAIL, a free CUDA-compatible software stack, and plans to source most additional AI chips from T-Head, with mass production of the next-gen V900 chip beginning Q1 2027.

Newsom Proposes AI 'Kill Switch' for California: Emergency Shutdown Authority for Dangerous Systems

California Governor Gavin Newsom released sweeping AI safety recommendations including emergency shutdown authority for dangerous AI systems, mandatory independent audits by licensed evaluators, and a shortened 48-hour safety incident reporting window. The proposals also include whistleblower protections for contractors and computing resource disclosure requirements, coming alongside a state lawsuit against OpenAI over Hugging Face cyberattacks.
