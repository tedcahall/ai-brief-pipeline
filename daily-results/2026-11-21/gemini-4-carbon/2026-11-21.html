🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Mathematician Claims Proof of Riemann Hypothesis With Claude

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge posted a statement and 14-part Lean formalization on Friday claiming he and Claude discovered a proof of the 1859 Riemann Hypothesis, one of the Clay Mathematics Institute's $1 million Millennium Prize Problems, and extended it to the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis. MIT mathematician Andrew Sutherland told Scientific American he independently compiled the Lean code, while experts still must verify the definitions and formalization.

Thinking Machines Lab Releases Open-Weights Looped Model Inkling 2 Mini

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini under an Apache 2.0 license, a 26.4B-parameter mixture-of-experts model that loops its 12 middle layers three times per token for 5.4B weight activations. Trained natively in NVFP4 with a roughly 20GB footprint for laptops, the lab reports 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1, topping open-weights models under 100B parameters, alongside a variable-loop research variant showing reasoning traces stay legible across extra passes.

OpenAI Details Safeguards Against Adversarial Model Distillation

OpenAI published research on detecting and disrupting unauthorized distillation campaigns that can transfer dual-use cyber and biological capabilities to models lacking safety controls. An internal study found student models fine-tuned on Astra-level exact reasoning traces generally gained stronger cyber skills than those trained only on visible summaries. Pairing adversarial training, tool-argument classifiers and conversation-bound encrypted reasoning cut prior-turn adaptive extraction success from 54.0% on GPT-6 Astra to 0.2% on GPT-6.2 Astra.

SpaceX Sells $32 Billion in AI Chip Bonds at Yields Above 8%

SpaceX completed a $32 billion bond sale through a special-purpose trust that will buy Nvidia AI chips and lease them back for terrestrial data centers, part of a broader financing package exceeding $40 billion. The largest $25.2 billion tranche priced at an 8.259% yield, compared with a 5.95% high-grade US corporate average that day, as SpaceX's AI division spent $16.4 billion on third-quarter capital expenditures against $7.6 billion in revenue.

Nvidia Nears $500M+ Deal for Diffusion-LLM Startup Inception

Nvidia is in advanced talks to acquire Palo Alto startup Inception for more than $500 million plus performance earnouts tied to post-close employee targets, The Information reported. Co-founded by professors Stefano Ermon, Aditya Grover and Volodymyr Kuleshov, Inception builds parallel diffusion language models such as Mercury 3 and is expected to bolster Nvidia's open-weight Nemotron and robotics models. Neither company has publicly confirmed a final agreement.
