🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Mathematician Claims a Claude-Assisted Riemann Proof

Levent Alpöge, a mathematician at Anthropic, wrote Friday that he believes he and Claude discovered a proof of the Riemann hypothesis, and that the argument confirms the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis. Scientific American reported that he released a Lean formalization. MIT mathematician Andrew Sutherland said he compiled it and called the moment historic, while the details are still being checked. The Clay prize has not been awarded.

Thinking Machines Releases Laptop-Sized Inkling 2 Mini

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weights looped transformer with 26.4 billion total parameters and 3.4 billion active. Middle layers run three times, which the lab counts as 5.4 billion weight activations per token. It says the model leads open-weights models under 100 billion parameters on DeepSWE v1.1 at 48.2%. That score is the lab's claim. Weights are on Hugging Face, and it runs on Macs with at least 32GB of RAM.

SpaceX Sells $32 Billion of Bonds to Buy Nvidia Chips

SpaceX sold $32 billion of bonds on Thursday through a trust that buys Nvidia chips and leases them back, Motley Fool reported. The largest piece, about $25.2 billion, was priced at a yield of 8.259%, above that day's 5.95% average for high-grade corporate debt. Fool said the Financial Times reported that lenders wanted the chips kept on Earth. SpaceX says the chips are for terrestrial data centers and should start arriving in January.

OpenAI Reports New Defenses Against Model Distillation

OpenAI published research on adversarial distillation, where another model is trained on a frontier model's outputs without inheriting its safeguards. The company said updated protections in GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol target extraction of protected chain-of-thought. In an internal adaptive test, average success at recovering protected reasoning from an earlier prompt fell from 54.0% on GPT-6 Astra to 0.2% on GPT-6.2 Astra. Those figures are OpenAI's own evaluation, not an independent audit.

Apple Publishes Depth Pro 2 Metric Depth Model

Apple's machine learning group published Depth Pro 2, a network that estimates metric depth in meters for a photo, a video, or a live camera feed at full input resolution. Apple says the maps are metrically accurate, sharp at object edges, and steady across frames, and that it is releasing code and weights. The research page does not include an independent benchmark table.
