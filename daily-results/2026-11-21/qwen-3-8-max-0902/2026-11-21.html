🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Mathematician Claims Claude-Assisted Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

Levent Alpöge, a mathematician at Anthropic, announced Friday that he believes he has discovered a proof of the Riemann hypothesis and its generalized form while working with Claude. He posted a four-page note and a Lean formalization on his personal site. The problem, concerning the hidden order of prime numbers, has stood since 1859 and carries a $1 million Clay Mathematics Institute prize. Expert verification is still underway.

Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Mini, a 26B Looped Transformer for Laptops

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weights mixture-of-experts model with 26.4B total and 3.4B active parameters that runs its middle layers three times per token. The lab says it leads every open-weights model under 100B parameters on DeepSWE v1.1 (48.2%) and Vals Index v2.2 (46.5%). It ships under Apache 2.0 with a ~20GB footprint that runs on MacBook Pros and NVIDIA DGX Spark.

SpaceX Completes $32 Billion AI Chip Debt at Yields Above 8%

SpaceX wrapped up $32 billion of debt to fund its access to AI chips, with the bulk sold at yields above 8%, levels not typically associated with investment-grade borrowers, according to Bloomberg Law. The pricing reflects market concern that AI spending will not pay off. The off-balance-sheet deal is part of a more than $40 billion package and closed a day after Anthropic's record IPO.

Newsom Proposes AI Kill Switch in California Safety Framework

California Governor Gavin Newsom released expert-panel recommendations Thursday that would let state officials order emergency shutdowns of AI systems deemed dangerous when companies refuse to contain them. The framework also calls for independent auditor testing and safety and security standards for frontier models. Newsom is pitching it as a blueprint for state legislation while federal AI rules remain absent.

OpenAI Details Defenses Against Adversarial Distillation

OpenAI published research describing how it detects and disrupts unauthorized attempts to distill its models' capabilities and protected chain-of-thought reasoning. In an internal adaptive red-team evaluation, the average attack success rate fell from 54.0% against GPT-6 Astra with original safeguards to 0.2% against GPT-6.2 Astra with updated protections. The company says the defenses mirror its biology and cybersecurity safeguards.
