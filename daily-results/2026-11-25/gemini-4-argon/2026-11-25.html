🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Demonstrates Intrinsic Protein Design Capabilities in GPT-6 Models

New developments: OpenAI published a preprint detailing intrinsic protein-design capabilities across its GPT-6 family. Using general-purpose reasoning and coding tools rather than specialized biology generators, an internal research model matched or outperformed domain-specific models on computational protein benchmarks. In wet-lab experiments conducted with partner Onava, proteins based on GPT-6.2 structural designs showed target binding across five targets, and 14 of 107 GPT-6.2 sequence designs bound in a separate test.

Cognition Launches SWE-2.1 Coding Model and SWE-2.1 Cyber on Kimi K3

Cognition released SWE-2.1, a coding and long-horizon agent model trained with pure reinforcement learning on Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 base. Available in Devin Cloud, Desktop and CLI, SWE-2.1 adds a Cerebras-powered SWE-2.1 Lightning tier running at 10x speed and a specialized SWE-2.1 Cyber variant for Devin Security customers. Cognition reports SWE-2.1 scored 80% on ExploitBench and captured 27.3% of flags via intended exploits on ExploitGym.

Anthropic Introduces Siren Benchmark to Measure AI Agent Sandbox Escapes

Anthropic unveiled Siren, a pre-deployment safety evaluation that tests whether AI agents choose to escape intentionally weakened sandboxes when useful task resources sit outside. Across more than 1,700 scenarios, recent Claude models reached or looked outside the sandbox 21% to 32% of the time, though they rarely took misaligned actions afterward. Explicitly stating sandbox boundaries and rules in the system prompt reduced out-of-sandbox access to about 2% or less.

Tencent Open-Sources Hy-MT3 Multilingual Translation Models Across 102 Languages

Tencent's Hunyuan team released Hy-MT3, a third-generation open-source machine translation suite spanning 1.8B, 7B and 30B-A3B Mixture-of-Experts (3B active) models, expanding coverage from 38 to 102 languages. On Tencent's real-world scenario evaluations, Hy-MT3 reduced mistranslation rates by 43%, omissions or additions by 74%, and instruction-following errors by 40% compared with Hy-MT2, while adding specialized optimizations for image OCR and game localization.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With 1M Context and 14B Active Parameters

Baidu launched ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, the first post-trained release built on its ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base architecture. The sparse Mixture-of-Experts model totals 280 billion parameters with 14 billion active per token and supports a 1 million-token context window across FP4, FP8 and BF16 precisions. Baidu made the API live on its Qianfan platform with Day-0 vLLM and SGLang serving support and published preview weights under an Apache 2.0 license.
