🌐 TOP STORIES

Baidu Opens ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Weights Under Apache 2.0

Baidu posted weights for the first post-trained model in its ERNIE 5.5 Flash family. The text model has 280 billion parameters, activates 14 billion per token and supports a context of up to about one million tokens. Baidu says it is still training the model, so this is an interim checkpoint, not the final release. The Apache 2.0 license permits commercial reuse.

Nvidia Releases Nemotron Image Model for Noncommercial Research

Nvidia released the image generator and visual tokenizer from Nemotron-Labs-VisualCreation under a noncommercial research license. The broader project pairs pixel generation with editable code for charts, web pages and documents, but the first download does not include the full system. Nvidia’s paper reports results comparable with leading image models on most benchmarks where it could make a comparison.

Cognition Releases SWE-2.1 and a Faster Cerebras-Served Variant

Cognition released SWE-2.1 for Devin, using reinforcement learning on Moonshot’s Kimi K3. The company reports 41.3 on its FrontierCode 2.0 benchmark, up from 24.1 for SWE-2. A Cerebras-served variant runs the same model at a claimed ten times the generation speed. Devin Pro, Max and Teams users get two weeks of access at no extra charge.

Anthropic and OpenAI Post Proofs Claiming to Separate VP and VNP

Anthropic and OpenAI posted separate preprints claiming to resolve Valiant’s conjecture, which concerns the size of arithmetic circuits needed to compute certain polynomials. Both posted Lean formalizations. Anthropic says no human mathematician has checked its proof, and formal verification alone does not establish that the encoded statement matches the intended claim. Neither preprint settles the Boolean P vs. NP problem.

UN Chief Calls for Global Talks on AI Safety Standards

At the Bridgetown summit in Barbados, UN Secretary-General António Guterres called on governments to develop an international AI risk framework within a year. He proposed common testing practices, basic transparency requirements and independent evaluation of advanced models, with support for countries that lack testing capacity. This was a call to begin talks, not an agreement or binding standard.
