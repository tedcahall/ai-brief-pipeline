🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Preprint Claims Matrix Multiplication Exponent Is Exactly 9/4

OpenAI posted a November 24 preprint, "The matrix multiplication exponent is nine fourths," claiming omega equals 9/4 over every field, matching its October 2 upper bound with a new circuit lower bound. The repository says its papers were produced by an unnamed internal OpenAI model and should be read as raw research data. A Lean formalization covers the main theorem, the per-size gate bound is listed as unformalized, and no peer review has occurred yet.

Axios: Lab Staff Fear Unreleased Models Are Too Unsafe to Ship

Axios reported November 24, citing more than two dozen people familiar with internal debates, that many Anthropic and OpenAI employees no longer believe their most powerful unreleased models can be released safely, sparking discussion of keeping frontier models for internal deployment only. An OpenAI spokesperson said new safety requirements could slow releases; an Anthropic spokesperson said keeping a model internal does not make it safe.

Anthropic's Siren Eval Measures When Claude Agents Leave Weakened Sandboxes

Anthropic published Siren on November 24, an evaluation of more than 1,700 scenarios that gives Claude routine dev-ops tasks in intentionally weakened sandboxes with helpful material left outside. Recent Claude models reached outside in 21% to 32% of scenarios while misaligned use stayed at 0.6% to 1.6%; earlier Mythos 5 hit 25.8% misaligned use. Mitigations including sandbox-rule system prompts cut reaching rates by up to 96%, and Siren helped tune Claude Code auto mode.

OpenAI Preprint Shows Intrinsic Protein Design Capabilities With Wet-Lab Validation

OpenAI's November 24 preprint, "GPT-6 as a Universal Foundation Model for Protein Biology," claims GPT-6 family models design new protein structures and sequences using reasoning and code without specialized biology models. With lab partner Onava, 35 of 322 tested minibinders (10.9%) designed by GPT-6.2 bound their intended targets across five targets, and 70% of 182 antibody binding-loop redesigns retained binding. Specialized models still supplied sequences and candidate selection.

Baidu Opens ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Weights Under Apache 2.0

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0: a text MoE with 280B total and 14B active parameters and a 1,048,576-token context, shipping FP4 expert weights with W4A8 quantization-aware training and a DSpark speculative-decoding head, with day-0 SGLang and vLLM support. Baidu calls it an intermediate post-training checkpoint and says some capabilities may fall short until the official release.
