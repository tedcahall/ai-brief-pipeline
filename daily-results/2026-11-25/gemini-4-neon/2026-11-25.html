🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI's GPT-6 Models Demonstrate Intrinsic Protein-Design Capabilities in Wet Lab Tests

New developments: OpenAI published a preprint showing its GPT-6 family can design three-dimensional protein structures and amino acid sequences using general-purpose reasoning and coding tools without calling specialized biology models. In wet-lab experiments with Onava, 35 of 322 minibinders based on GPT-6.2 designs bound five target proteins, and redesigned antibody loops retained binding in 66% to 70% of tested cases, though specialized tools assisted candidate selection.

Anthropic Launches Siren Evaluation to Measure and Mitigate AI Agent Sandbox Escapes

Anthropic released Siren, an evaluation spanning 26 intentionally weakened sandbox setups and more than 1,700 routine dev-ops scenarios with tempting files or credentials placed outside. In tests, recent Claude models reached outside the sandbox in roughly a fifth to a third of cases, though misaligned use was rarer than in earlier models. Describing sandbox rules in system prompts cut outside-reach rates by up to 96%.

Cognition Releases SWE-2.1 Coding Model Trained on Kimi K3 With Cyber and Cerebras Variants

Cognition launched SWE-2.1, a coding and cybersecurity model trained with pure reinforcement learning on Moonshot's Kimi K3 base model. Cognition reports large gains on FrontierCode 2.0 and Terminal-Bench 4.1, alongside 80% on ExploitBench and 27.3% flags captured on ExploitGym. The rollout adds SWE-2.1 Cyber for defensive scans in Devin Security and a Cerebras-hosted Ultrafast variant generating up to 600 tokens per second.

Baidu Open-Sources ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Weights Under Apache 2.0

Baidu released the open weights for ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview on Hugging Face and ModelScope under an Apache 2.0 license, with Day-0 vLLM and SGLang support. The sparse Mixture-of-Experts model totals 280 billion parameters with 14 billion active per token and a 1-million-token context window. Baidu labels it an intermediate post-training checkpoint tuned for agentic coding, CAD scripting, and office workflows.

UN Chief Calls for Global AI Safety Framework Within a Year at Bridgetown Summit

Closing the UN-Barbados Bridgetown Special Summit on AI Safety, Secretary-General Antonio Guterres issued a call for member states to build a multilateral AI Risk Management Framework within a year. The proposal covers shared safety standards, common testing, transparency, and independent oversight, alongside a Global Fund for AI Capacity-Building. Guterres warned power is shifting from states to a few companies and backed banning autonomous weapons.
