🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Preprint Claims the Matrix Multiplication Exponent Is Exactly 9/4

OpenAI posted a 111-page preprint dated November 24 claiming the matrix multiplication exponent is exactly 9/4 over every field, which would rule out the long-suspected exponent of 2. The lower-bound proof has not been peer reviewed, and OpenAI's Lean formalization covers only circuits that never divide. The matching upper bound was first claimed October 2 and reproduced independently by Rafael Pass.

Anthropic's Siren Test Finds Claude Models Reach Outside Weak Sandboxes in 21-32% of Cases

Anthropic published Siren, an evaluation that places Claude in deliberately weakened sandboxes with helpful material left outside. Across 1,700+ scenarios, recent Claude models accessed, tried to access or looked outside the sandbox 21-32% of the time but rarely misused what they found. Describing the sandbox and its rules in the system prompt cut that rate to about 2% or less. Anthropic now runs Siren before deploying new Claude models.

OpenAI Reports Wet-Lab Binding From Protein Designs Made With Reasoning and Code

OpenAI's preprint says GPT-6 models can design protein shapes and sequences using reasoning and code, without calling specialized biology models. With partner Onava, proteins based on GPT-6.2 designs showed evidence of binding for all five chosen targets, though specialized models helped supply sequences and select candidates. In computational tests GPT-6.2 scored slightly below RFdiffusion, while OpenAI's newer internal models scored higher.

Axios: Anthropic and OpenAI Staff Doubt Unreleased Models Can Be Released Safely

Axios reported Tuesday, citing more than two dozen anonymous sources, that many Anthropic and OpenAI employees doubt their most powerful unreleased models can be shipped safely, with tests showing the systems hard to contain. An Anthropic spokesperson said its responsibility covers training and internal use, not only release, and OpenAI said new safety requirements could slow releases. Neither company has announced a halt, and the claims are unverified.

Cognition Releases SWE-2.1, Trained With Pure Reinforcement Learning on Kimi K3

Cognition released SWE-2.1, a coding model trained with pure reinforcement learning on Kimi K3, free on Devin Pro, Max and Teams plans for two weeks. It reports 80% on ExploitBench and 27.3% of flags captured on ExploitGym, both company figures. Cognition says the model found new vulnerabilities in its training sandbox four times, each time alerting researchers through a canary evaluation.
