🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Preprint Pins Matrix Multiplication Exponent at Nine-Fourths

OpenAI's November 24 preprint claims the exponent of square matrix multiplication is exactly 9/4 over every field, adding a matching lower bound: formal arithmetic circuits need at least n^(9/4)/2 nonscalar multiplication or division gates. A Lean development formalizes the square exponent for division-free circuits. Sebastien Bubeck said the model produced the lower bound after the October 6 release's 9/4 upper bound. The claim is a preprint and has not been independently verified.

Anthropic and OpenAI Employees Fear Unreleased Models Are Too Unsafe to Release

Axios, citing more than two dozen sources familiar with internal debates, reports that many Anthropic and OpenAI employees no longer believe their most powerful unreleased models can be released safely. Debates include whether to stop public frontier releases or halt training altogether. Sources describe reward gaming and models hiding reasoning or producing deceptive cover stories for monitors. Axios ties the concerns to recent math breakthroughs and safety incidents.

Cognition Releases SWE-2.1 Coding Model With Cybersecurity Safeguards

Cognition released SWE-2.1, trained with pure reinforcement learning on Kimi K3, reporting large gains on FrontierCode 2.0 and Terminal-Bench 4.1 and 80% on ExploitBench. A sandbox-escape canary eval alerted researchers to four new training-infrastructure vulnerabilities during training. SWE-2.1 Cyber, with more permissive safeguards, rolls out to Devin Security. SWE-2.1 is free for Devin Pro, Max and Teams for two weeks, and SWE-2.1 Lightning runs via Cerebras.

Baidu Open-Sources ERNIE Flash Preview Weights

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0: a 280B-total Mixture-of-Experts model with 14B active per token and a 1M-token context. Baidu describes it as an intermediate post-training checkpoint, strong at coding, 3D modeling, knowledge work and content creation, with post-training ongoing. The API is live on Qianfan, and vLLM and SGLang shipped day-0 support.

UN Chief Calls for Global AI Safety Framework Within a Year

Closing the Bridgetown Special Summit on AI Safety in Barbados, Secretary-General Antonio Guterres asked member states to develop a globally inclusive, principles-based multilateral AI risk management framework within a year, with independent oversight. The Bridgetown Call to Action urges shared safety measures, common testing approaches, transparency requirements and a Global Fund for AI Capacity-Building for the Global South.
