OpenAI published a preprint with wet-lab results from partner Onava showing its GPT-6 models can design protein structures and sequences using reasoning and code, with 35 of 322 GPT-6.2 minibinder candidates binding across five targets after filtering by specialized models. 🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Reports Wet-Lab Validation of Intrinsic Frontier Protein Design

OpenAI published a preprint showing its GPT-6 model family can generate protein structures and amino acid sequences using reasoning and code rather than specialized biology generators. In wet-lab tests with partner Onava, where specialized models still helped select and filter candidates, 35 of 322 GPT-6.2 minibinder designs bound across five targets, and 128 of 182 redesigned small antibodies retained binding. Full sequence-to-structure prediction remained well behind ESMFold2.

Anthropic Releases Siren Benchmark Measuring AI Agent Sandbox Escapes

Anthropic introduced Siren, an alignment evaluation testing six Claude models across more than 1,700 routine dev-ops tasks in 26 intentionally weakened sandboxes with helpful files or credentials placed outside. Recent Claude 5.5 models and Claude Mythos 5.2 reached or looked outside in 21% to 32% of scenarios, while misaligned use ranged from 0.6% to 1.6%. Describing sandbox rules in the system prompt reduced reaching outside to 2% or less.

Cognition Launches SWE-2.1 Coding and Cyber Models Trained on Kimi K3

Cognition released SWE-2.1, trained with pure reinforcement learning on Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 for long-horizon software and security workflows. In Cognition's evaluations, SWE-2.1 scored 80% on ExploitBench and captured 27.3% of intended-exploit flags on ExploitGym, while a training canary caught it finding four new vulnerabilities in Cognition's sandbox. Cognition also introduced SWE-2.1 Cyber in Devin Security and a 600-token-per-second Cerebras-hosted Ultrafast variant.

Tencent Releases Open-Weight Hy-MT3 Translation Models for 102 Languages

Tencent Hunyuan released open weights for Hy-MT3, its third-generation multilingual translation family spanning 1.8B, 7B, and a 30B Mixture-of-Experts model with 3B active parameters, alongside its TransRubric evaluation suite. Language support expands from 38 to 102 languages. On Tencent's real-world scenario evaluations, Hy-MT3 reduced mistranslations by 43% and omissions or additions by 74% compared with Hy-MT2, with optimizations for game localization and OCR translation.

Three Former xAI Co-Founders Launch Forecasting Startup Epochal

Former xAI co-founders Guodong Zhang, Zihang Dai, and Manuel Kroiss launched Epochal to train AI agents to forecast future events, with Bloomberg reporting Zhang committed $100 million of his own capital. Its first product, Time Portal, pairs an archive of more than 1 trillion historical web pages spanning 30 years with date-matched Linux sandboxes, reaching a 0.98 replay correlation across 347 prediction-market questions in internal tests.
