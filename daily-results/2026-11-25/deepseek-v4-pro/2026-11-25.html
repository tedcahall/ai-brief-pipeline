🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI's GPT-6 Models Design Proteins With Wet-Lab Validation

New developments: OpenAI shared a preprint showing its GPT-6 family can design novel protein shapes and sequences using reasoning and code, without calling specialized biology models. With partner Onava, 35 of 322 GPT-6.2-designed proteins showed binding evidence in the lab across five targets. OpenAI calls it a proof of concept and its goal is for scientists to one day design working medicines by prompting.

Anthropic and OpenAI Staff Fear Unreleased Models Are Too Unsafe to Release

Axios reported, citing more than two dozen sources, that many Anthropic and OpenAI employees no longer believe their most powerful unreleased models can be released safely, prompting internal debate about whether to reserve future frontier models for internal deployment only.

Cognition Launches SWE-2.1 Coding Model Trained With RL on Kimi K3

Cognition released SWE-2.1, trained with pure reinforcement learning on Moonshot's Kimi K3, reporting large gains over SWE-2 across benchmarks including FrontierCode 2.0 and Terminal-Bench 4.1. It is free for Devin Pro, Max and Teams for two weeks, with a faster SWE-2.1 Lightning via Cerebras and a SWE-2.1 Cyber variant.

NVIDIA Puts Vera, Its First CPU for AI Agents, Into Full Production

At GTC Taipei, NVIDIA said Vera, the first CPU it built for AI agents, is now in full production, claiming 1.8x faster task completion than x86 for agentic AI, reinforcement learning and data processing. Anthropic, OpenAI and SpaceXAI are among the labs planning to adopt it, alongside hyperscalers ByteDance, CoreWeave and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.

Google DeepMind Launches Humanity's First Exam Reliability Benchmark

Google DeepMind launched Humanity's First Exam (HFE) on Kaggle, a 544-question benchmark measuring whether models reliably answer simple everyday text and image prompts, not just once. Visual perception accounted for 74% of wrong answers, and DeepMind released a 112-prompt public subset under CC-BY-4.0 with the rest held out privately.
