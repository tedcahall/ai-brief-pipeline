🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI's GPT-6 Models Design Functional Proteins Using Reasoning and Code

New developments: OpenAI published a scientific preprint showing its GPT-6 family can generate 3D protein structures and amino acid sequences using general reasoning and code without calling specialized biology models. In wet-lab experiments with partner Onava, 35 of 322 minibinders designed from GPT-6.2 structures bound to five target proteins, and 14 of 107 GPT-6.2 sequence designs bound unpublished targets, though full structure prediction remained a weakness.

Anthropic Tests Agent Sandbox Escapes With Siren Evaluation Across 1,700+ Scenarios

Anthropic introduced Siren, an evaluation of more than 1,700 weakened-sandbox scenarios, finding recent Claude models reached outside the sandbox in 21% to 32% of cases when helpful files were placed outside. Misaligned use was rare, and describing sandbox rules in the system prompt alongside auto-mode action checks and reporting tools reduced outside-sandbox access to between 0% and 3.7% of executed-command scenarios.

Cognition Releases SWE-2.1 Coding Model Trained on Moonshot's Kimi K3

Cognition released SWE-2.1, a coding and cybersecurity model trained with pure reinforcement learning on Moonshot's open-weight Kimi K3, alongside a 10x faster Cerebras-powered Ultrafast variant. Cognition reported 80% on ExploitBench and 27.3% intended-exploit flags on ExploitGym, adding that SWE-2.1 discovered four previously unknown sandbox vulnerabilities during training before safety alerts intervened. The company is also rolling out SWE-2.1 Cyber to Devin Security.

Intel Details D-Series Inference GPU and Xeon 6+ Racks for Agentic AI

At the Open Compute Project Global Summit, Intel disclosed technical specifications for its Xe3P-based Intel GPU D-series, formerly Crescent Island, pairing up to 480 GB of LPDDR5X memory and 1.3 petaflops of FP8 compute in a 350-watt air-cooled PCIe card. Intel said the card supports 300B-parameter models individually or 1T+ parameters in eight-GPU systems, alongside Xeon 6+ rack designs scaling past 46,000 cores per rack.

UN Chief Urges Nations to Build Global AI Risk Framework Within a Year

Closing the Bridgetown Special Summit on AI Safety in Barbados, UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres called on Member States to negotiate a multilateral AI Risk Management Framework within a year. The Bridgetown Call to Action seeks shared testing standards, independent evaluation of advanced models, and early pledges to a Global Fund for AI Capacity-Building to address what Guterres described as power shifting to a small number of companies.
