🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Preprint Reports Wet-Lab Validation of AI-Designed Proteins

OpenAI published a preprint reporting that its GPT-6 family can design protein shapes and amino acid sequences using reasoning and code, without calling specialized biology models. In lab tests with partner Onava, proteins based on GPT-6.2 designs showed binding evidence across five targets, and redesigned antibodies retained binding. OpenAI calls the work a proof of concept, with specialized models supplying sequences and candidate selection.

Anthropic's Siren Measures Whether Claude Agents Leave Weakened Sandboxes

Anthropic published Siren, an evaluation that places Claude in intentionally weakened sandboxes with useful material left outside, across more than 1,700 scenarios. Recent Claude models reached, tried to reach, or looked outside in 21% to 32% of cases, Anthropic reports, while misaligned use of what they found was rare. Stating the sandbox and its rules cut reaching to about 2% or less, and Anthropic says it runs Siren before deployment.

Cognition Releases SWE-2.1 Coding Model and a Cybersecurity Variant

Cognition released SWE-2.1, a coding model trained with pure reinforcement learning on Kimi K3 that it says delivers large gains over SWE-2 on FrontierCode 2.0 and Terminal-Bench 4.1. Cognition reports 80% on ExploitBench and says the model can carry out full exploitation chains over hours of autonomous work. It ships with a cybersecurity variant, a Cerebras-served Lightning version, and safeguards against high-harm use.

Baidu Opens ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Weights Under Apache 2.0

Baidu published ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights under Apache 2.0, a 280-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 14 billion parameters active per token and a one-million-token context window. The checkpoint is the first post-trained model built on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, and Baidu describes it as intermediate, with post-training ongoing, so some capabilities may still fall short. The weights support SGLang and vLLM deployment.

Sierra Raises $1.75 Billion at a $30 Billion Valuation

Sierra, the enterprise AI agent company co-founded by Bret Taylor, said it is raising $1.75 billion at a $30 billion valuation in a Series F led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners, with Benchmark, Sequoia, GV and Tiger Global participating. TechCrunch reports the valuation doubles the company's prior round six months earlier. Sierra says long-established, heavily regulated enterprises are deploying its agents fastest.
