🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Makes Gemini 4 Carbon Broadly Available at Argon's Introductory Price

Google released Gemini 4 Carbon to all paying developers and enterprises through the Gemini API and Gemini for business, two weeks after limiting it to cybersecurity defenders. It carries the same introductory price as Gemini 4 Argon, $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, and is rolling out to Google AI Ultra subscribers. Google also previewed /swarm, a multi-agent feature in Antigravity.

Anthropic Sets IPO Range at $70 to $78, Implying Up to $1.4 Trillion

Anthropic launched its IPO roadshow and said in an updated filing that it will offer 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 each, raising $57.75 billion to $64.35 billion, TechCrunch reported. The top of the range implies a fully diluted valuation of $1.404 trillion. Seven companies including Google and Nvidia agreed to buy $26 billion of non-voting stock, and run-rate revenue reached $81 billion by the end of October.

Alibaba Opens Qwen4-Max Preview With a Million-Token Context Window

Alibaba opened a preview of Qwen4-Max, the flagship of its next-generation Qwen family, TechNode reported. The model accepts image and video inputs and handles up to one million tokens of context. Developers reach it through Alibaba's Token Plan subscription, with early access in the Qwen app, Qoder, QwenWork and QwenStudio. QwenCloud documentation lists a maximum output of 393,216 tokens shared between thinking and the final answer.

Nvidia Shareholders Sue Huang as FBI Examines Spy Allegations

New developments: Nvidia shareholders filed a class action in California federal court accusing the company and CEO Jensen Huang of misleading investors about his relationship with Alice Huang, whom US authorities concluded was a Chinese intelligence asset, according to New York Times reporting cited by the Guardian. President Trump said the FBI is investigating. Nvidia shares closed down 4.3 percent on Monday, cutting about $190 billion from its market value.

OpenAI Opens ChatGPT to Business Profiles and Branded Agents

OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, letting companies claim a profile through Business Hub, update details, add product feeds with pricing and availability, and see aggregated data on mentions and website clicks. Eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses can also launch a Business Agent that answers customer questions in a clearly labeled, separate conversation. OpenAI says 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week.
