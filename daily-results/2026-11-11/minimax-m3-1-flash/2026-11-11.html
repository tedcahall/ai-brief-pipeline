🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Makes Gemini 4 Carbon Generally Available to Developers and Enterprises

New developments: after a two-week preview limited to cybersecurity defenders, Google opened Gemini 4 Carbon to all paying developers and enterprises on the Gemini API at introductory pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, the same rates as Gemini 4 Argon until November 30. Google says Carbon ranks first on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR and Zapier's AutomationBench at more than twice lower cost per task than the next best model, and blocked 99 percent of attack trajectories in adaptive red-team testing.

Anthropic Launches IPO Roadshow, Pricing Shares at $70 to $78

New developments: Anthropic started the roadshow for its proposed initial public offering on November 10, offering 825 million Class A shares at an expected $70 to $78 range and applying to list on Nasdaq under the symbol ANT. CNBC and Yahoo Finance report the offering could raise up to $64.35 billion at the top of the range, with trading expected around November 19 or 20. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are lead underwriters, and the registration statement is not yet effective.

Alibaba Previews Qwen4, With Qwen4-Max on Token Plan

Alibaba's Qwen team said Qwen4 is coming, with deeper knowledge, richer world understanding and stronger coding and agentic capabilities aimed at complex coding, knowledge work, front-end builds and professional software workflows. Qwen4-Max is in preview on Alibaba's Token Plan, with early access on QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen app and QwenStudio. Alibaba published no benchmark scores or prices with the announcement.

Baidu Ships ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview and Promises Apache 2.0 Weights

New developments: Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, the first post-trained model built on the ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base it shipped earlier, and said the weights will be open-sourced under Apache 2.0 by November 25. The mixture-of-experts model has 280 billion total parameters with 14 billion active, a 1 million token context, and serves above 200 tokens per second on Baidu's Qianfan platform. The API is available now.

OpenAI Opens Business Hub and Business Agents in ChatGPT

OpenAI put Businesses in ChatGPT into beta, letting eligible businesses claim a profile, manage product feeds with pricing and availability, and see aggregated mentions and website clicks, while publishing a Business Agent that answers customer questions in a clearly labeled conversation. Agents are available to eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the United States, and businesses can sponsor their agent as a ChatGPT Ads destination. OpenAI says ChatGPT now has 1.2 billion weekly users.
