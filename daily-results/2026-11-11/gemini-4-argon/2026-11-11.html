🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini 4 Carbon and Formally Verifies Signal Protocol Code

Google released Gemini 4 Carbon in Google AI Studio, Gemini for business, and Google AI Ultra at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, plus a preview of the /swarm multi-agent feature in Google Antigravity. Google claims Carbon ranks first on CursorBench 4.0 and Perplexity WANDR, while DeepMind used Carbon in AlphaProof Code to generate over 6 million lines of Lean proofs verifying Signal's compiled cryptographic code.

Anthropic Launches IPO Roadshow Targeting Up to $64.35 Billion in Proceeds

New developments: Following prior unconfirmed valuation reports, Anthropic officially launched its IPO roadshow and filed an amended SEC registration statement offering 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 per share under the Nasdaq ticker ANT. CNBC reports the range implies up to $64.35 billion in proceeds and a $1.405 trillion diluted valuation, alongside a $26 billion private placement from Nvidia, Google, Microsoft, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, and TSMC.

MiniMax Open-Sources 33.1B H3-Image Model for 10-Second Native 4K Generation

MiniMax released open weights on Hugging Face for MiniMax-H3-Image, a 33.1-billion-parameter single-stream diffusion transformer that unifies text-to-image generation and multi-reference editing in one checkpoint. Distilled to six sampling steps without classifier-free guidance and paired with a Qwen3-VL text encoder, the model generates native 16-megapixel images up to 4096 by 4096 resolution without an upscaler and accepts up to 16 reference images, with MiniMax claiming 4K generation in about 10 seconds.

Nvidia Releases Open GR00T 2 World-Action Robotics Model and 100K-Hour Dataset

Nvidia GEAR Labs introduced GR00T 2, an open-weight foundation world-action model pretrained on 402,730 hours of egocentric human video, 10,184 hours of handheld gripper demonstrations, and 23,500 hours of robot teleoperation. A diffusion transformer jointly denoises future video and motor actions across parallel grippers, dexterous hands, and humanoids, while the smaller GR00T 2 Turbo runs onboard a Unitree G1's Jetson Thor at 124 milliseconds per prediction alongside 100,000 open-sourced video hours.

OpenAI Launches Businesses in ChatGPT Beta With Customer-Facing Business Agents

OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, introducing a Business Hub where verified organizations can manage company profiles, upload product feeds with pricing and availability, and connect Shopify catalogs across ChatGPT's 1.2 billion weekly users. Companies also receive aggregated mention and citation analytics, while eligible US e-commerce and online service firms can deploy clearly labeled Business Agents to answer customer questions and serve as destinations for sponsored ChatGPT Ads.
