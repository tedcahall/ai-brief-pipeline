🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Makes Gemini 4 Carbon Generally Available

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available on November 10 to developers, enterprises, scientists, and consumers after a two-week limited release to cybersecurity defenders. Google says Carbon is its strongest model for cybersecurity and long-horizon work, ranking first on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity WANDR, and Zapier's AutomationBench at more than 2x lower cost per task than the next external model. API price matches Argon at $2 and $10 per million input and output tokens.

Alibaba Opens a Qwen4-Max Preview on Token Plan

Alibaba's Qwen account said on November 11 that Qwen4-Max is in preview on Alibaba's Token Plan, with early access also on QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen app, and QwenStudio. The post describes Qwen4 as having deeper knowledge, richer world understanding, and stronger coding and agentic capabilities for coding, knowledge work, and professional software workflows. The announcement did not state weights, benchmark scores, context length, or general API availability.

DeepMind's AlphaProof Code Verifies Signal Protocol Code

Google DeepMind said on November 10 that AlphaProof Code, an agent framework using Gemini Carbon, formally verified key security properties of the compiled code behind the Signal protocol. DeepMind says agents generated more than 6 million lines of machine-checked Lean proofs in four weeks, and that reviewers needed to inspect about 1 percent of the codebase. The lab said the system flagged two previously fixed bugs when reintroduced, and it is open-sourcing the artifacts.

MiniMax Releases Open-Weight H3-Image

MiniMax released H3-Image on November 10 as an open-weight model for text-to-image generation, reference-based generation, and editing. The company says it can produce a 4096 by 4096 image in under 10 seconds, accept up to 16 reference images, and lead open-weight models on prompt adherence. Weights are on Hugging Face, with API, MiniMax Code, and Hailuo access, plus a launch-week 50 percent discount on 4K and higher generations.

Anthropic Starts IPO Roadshow at $70 to $78 a Share

Anthropic said on November 10 that it launched the roadshow for a proposed IPO of 825 million Class A shares, including about 801.35 million offered by the company and 23.65 million by existing stockholders. The expected price is $70 to $78 per share, and Anthropic has applied to list on Nasdaq as ANT. The registration statement is filed but not yet effective, so the shares are not yet for sale.
