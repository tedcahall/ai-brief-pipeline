🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Releases Gemini 4 Carbon With Agent Swarms and Formal Code Proofs

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon generally available via the Gemini API at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, alongside Google AI Ultra and a new multi-agent swarm preview in Google Antigravity. Google reports top scores on CursorBench 4.0, WANDR, and AutomationBench, while DeepMind's AlphaProof Code used Carbon to generate over six million lines of Lean proofs formally verifying Signal's compiled cryptographic code.

Anthropic Launches Nasdaq IPO Roadshow to Raise Up to $64.35 Billion

New developments: Following preliminary valuation discussions last week, Anthropic formally opened the investor roadshow for its Nasdaq listing under ticker ANT, offering 825 million Class A shares priced between $70 and $78 to raise up to $64.35 billion before underwriter options. Updated SEC prospectus filings reported by Reuters imply a fully diluted valuation up to $1.4 trillion alongside a separate $26 billion cornerstone placement and $81 billion run-rate revenue.

Alibaba Opens Qwen4-Max Preview With Million-Token Multimodal Context

Alibaba opened early access to Qwen4-Max Preview through November 25 across Alibaba Cloud's subscription Token Plan, Qwen Chat, QwenWork, Qoder, and QwenStudio. Documentation for the qwen4-max-preview endpoint specifies text, image, and video inputs, a one-million-token context window, a 393,216-token combined reasoning and output ceiling, and default extra-high reasoning, though Alibaba has not yet published benchmark scores, weights, or pay-as-you-go token pricing.

MiniMax Releases Open-Weight 33.1B H3-Image Model for Native 4K Generation

MiniMax released open weights on Hugging Face under a community license for MiniMax-H3-Image, a 33.1-billion-parameter single-stream diffusion transformer paired with the Qwen3-VL text encoder. Distilled to six sampling steps without classifier-free guidance, the unified text-to-image and editing checkpoint generates native 16-megapixel images up to 4096 by 4096 pixels without an upscaler, accepts up to 16 reference images, and runs in vLLM-Omni and SGLang.

OpenAI Launches Businesses in ChatGPT With Verified Profiles and Shopping Agents

OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, giving organizations a Business Hub to claim verified profiles, upload self-service product feeds with live pricing and availability, connect Shopify catalogs, and view aggregated mention and click analytics across ChatGPT's 1.2 billion weekly users. Eligible U.S. ecommerce and online-service merchants can also deploy clearly labeled Business Agents for product questions and sponsored ad destinations.
