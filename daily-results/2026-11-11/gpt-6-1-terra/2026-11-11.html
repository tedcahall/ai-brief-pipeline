📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Alibaba has opened a preview of Qwen4-Max, while Google is expanding access to Gemini 4 Carbon beyond its initial cybersecurity rollout. MiniMax released an image model with downloadable weights, although its license has geographic restrictions.

In physical AI, DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5 for its robotics partners. Anthropic also added collaborative whiteboards and spreadsheets to Claude, alongside a broader release of its data product.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Alibaba Opens Qwen4-Max Preview

Alibaba says Qwen4-Max is available in preview on its Token Plan, with early access also named for QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen app and QwenStudio. The company positions the forthcoming Qwen4 family for coding, agentic tasks and professional software workflows. The announcement describes a preview, not a full family launch or open-weight release.

Google Broadens Access to Gemini 4 Carbon

Google is making Gemini 4 Carbon available to paying developers and enterprises through its API, and rolling it out to Google AI Ultra subscribers. Previously limited to selected cybersecurity defenders, the model targets long-horizon coding, security and scientific tasks. Google is also previewing a multi-agent swarm feature in Antigravity.

MiniMax Releases H3-Image With Open Weights

MiniMax has released H3-Image for image generation and editing, with weights available for download and access through its API. The company says it supports roughly 16-megapixel outputs and up to 16 reference images. Crypto Briefing reports that its community license excludes use in the US, EU, UK and South Korea.

DeepMind Introduces Gemini Robotics 2.5

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5, a vision-language-action model rolling out to robotics partners. The company says it evaluated the system across 200 distinct tasks and tested it with Apptronik and Mercedes-Benz on automotive production lines. These are company-reported results; a new benchmark also targets robotics-specific jailbreaks.

Anthropic Adds Whiteboards, Sheets and Data to Claude

Anthropic launched Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets in beta and made its former Dashboards product generally available as Claude Data. The tools support collaborative boards, spreadsheet generation and data storytelling across paid plans. Anthropic says spreadsheet functionality is currently simple, with export to Excel or CSV for fuller workflows.
