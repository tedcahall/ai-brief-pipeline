📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon broadly available on November 10, a day after Anthropic's Fable 5.2 launch, pricing it at Argon's introductory $2 per million input and $10 per million output tokens and claiming first place on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR and Zapier's AutomationBench, while Anthropic launched its IPO roadshow for 825 million shares at $70 to $78, which TechCrunch says implies a fully diluted valuation of up to $1.404 trillion.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Makes Gemini 4 Carbon Available to Developers, Enterprises and Consumers

Google released Gemini 4 Carbon to developers, enterprises, scientists and consumers on November 10. It is available through the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and Gemini for business at $2 per million input and $10 per million output tokens, matching Gemini 4 Argon's introductory price, and to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Antigravity, the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search. Google says Carbon ranks first on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR and Zapier's AutomationBench.

Anthropic Launches IPO Roadshow: 825 Million Shares at $70 to $78 Under Ticker ANT

New developments: Anthropic launched the roadshow for its proposed IPO on November 10, offering 825 million Class A shares at an expected $70 to $78 each and applying to list on Nasdaq as ANT. TechCrunch reports the range implies a fully diluted valuation of up to $1.404 trillion and that the updated filing adds a $26 billion private sale of Class N stock to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC.

OpenAI Launches Businesses in ChatGPT Beta With Business Agents

OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, letting companies claim their business through Business Hub, update details, add product feeds and view aggregated insights on mentions and website clicks. Eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses can also launch a Business Agent that answers customer questions in a labeled conversation separate from ChatGPT's own answers, and can sponsor it as a ChatGPT Ads destination. OpenAI says ChatGPT has 1.2 billion weekly users.

Alibaba Opens Qwen4-Max Preview With a 1M-Token Context

Alibaba's Qwen team said Qwen4-Max is now in preview on Alibaba's Token Plan, with early access in QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen app and QwenStudio, and described stronger coding and agentic capabilities for real-world software work. QwenCloud documentation lists a 1M-token context, image and video input, tool calling and compatibility with OpenAI and Anthropic API formats. The announcement did not include benchmark results or pricing.

Baidu Unveils ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With Open Weights Promised Later This Month

Baidu announced ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, the first post-trained model built on its ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, a 280B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 14B active parameters and a 1M-token context. Baidu says it serves over 200 tokens per second per user on its Qianfan platform and that the weights will be open-sourced by November 25. AlphaSignal notes limited independent testing and license restrictions on the earlier base checkpoint.
