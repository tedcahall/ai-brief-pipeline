🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Rolls Out Gemini 4 Carbon as Its Strongest Frontier Model

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available to developers, enterprises, and AI Ultra subscribers, calling it its strongest model for cybersecurity and long-horizon agentic work. Google says Carbon ranks #1 on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR, and Zapier's AutomationBench at more than 2X lower cost per task than the next best external model, and reached a 0% prompt injection attack success rate on Gray Swan's IPI benchmark. API pricing matches Argon at $2/$10 per million tokens.

Alibaba Opens Qwen4 Preview Led by Qwen4-Max Flagship

Alibaba's Qwen team announced Qwen4, saying the next-generation family brings deeper knowledge, richer world understanding, and stronger coding and agentic capabilities built for real-world professional software workflows. The flagship Qwen4-Max is now in preview on Alibaba's Token Plan, with early access also available on QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen APP, and QwenStudio. Pricing and benchmark details have not yet been published.

Anthropic Launches Roadshow for Proposed IPO on Nasdaq

New developments: Anthropic launched the roadshow for its proposed initial public offering, moving past the valuation uncertainty reported earlier this month. The company is offering 825,000,000 Class A shares, 801,351,354 from Anthropic and 23,648,646 from existing stockholders, at an expected $70 to $78 per share, listing on Nasdaq under ANT. Underwriters hold a 30-day option for up to 123,750,000 additional shares.

OpenAI Launches Businesses in ChatGPT Beta with Business Agents

OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, giving companies a way to connect with the 1.2 billion people who use ChatGPT each week. Through Business Hub, businesses can claim their profile, manage information, and see aggregated engagement insights. Eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses can also build Business Agents that answer customer questions, with Shopify verification support and sponsored placements tied to ChatGPT Ads.

NVIDIA Introduces GR00T 2 Open World Action Model at CoRL

At the Conference on Robot Learning in Austin, NVIDIA introduced GR00T 2, an open frontier world action model built on NVIDIA Cosmos and trained on robot and human data including 100,000 hours of open-source egocentric video. NVIDIA says it completed 40 of 50 evaluated tasks at least once without task-specific demonstrations and averaged 71% success across those tasks with one demonstration each, rising to 95% with five.
