📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Google released Gemini 4 Carbon for complex, long-horizon agentic work, while Alibaba opened a Qwen4-Max preview and MiniMax shipped its open-weight H3-Image model. The releases span frontier reasoning, Chinese flagship models and high-resolution image generation.

Robotics also advanced: NVIDIA introduced its open GR00T 2 world-action models, and Google DeepMind paired Gemini Robotics 2.5 with a Mercedes-Benz factory proof of concept and a new jailbreak benchmark. Anthropic separately launched the roadshow for its proposed IPO.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Releases Gemini 4 Carbon for Long-Horizon Agentic Work

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available to paying API developers and enterprises and to Google AI Ultra subscribers on November 10. Google positions it above Argon for extended coding, cybersecurity, scientific and knowledge-work tasks. Introductory API pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens through November 30, after which Google lists $4 and $20 respectively.

Alibaba Opens Qwen4-Max Preview Across Its AI Products

Alibaba’s Qwen team announced Qwen4-Max in preview on November 11, the first public step in its Qwen4 family. The announcement emphasizes deeper knowledge, coding and agentic capabilities. Access is available through Alibaba’s Token Plan, with early access in QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen app and QwenStudio. The post supplies no benchmark results, context-window specifications or general-availability date.

MiniMax Ships Open-Weight H3-Image With 4K Generation

MiniMax released H3-Image as an open-weight model for text-to-image generation, reference-based generation and editing. The company says it can produce a 4096 by 4096 image in under 10 seconds and accept up to 16 reference images. Weights are on Hugging Face, with hosted access through the MiniMax API, MiniMax Code and Hailuo AI; launch-week 4K requests receive 50 percent off.

NVIDIA Introduces GR00T 2 Open Robotics Foundation Models

NVIDIA Research introduced GR00T 2 world-action models that jointly predict future video and robot actions across grippers, dexterous hands and humanoids. On 50 tasks unseen by the target robot, NVIDIA reports 40 percent zero-shot success and 95 percent after five demonstrations and a 30-minute fine-tune. The release includes an open stack, while X-Adapt recipes are scheduled for December.

Google DeepMind Launches Gemini Robotics 2.5 With Factory Pilot

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5, its latest vision-language-action model, and tested it across more than 200 tasks. In a Mercedes-Benz production-line proof of concept, Apptronik’s Apollo 2 used the system to sort automotive parts with a reported 96 percent success rate. DeepMind also released ASIMOV-Jailbreak, an open benchmark containing hundreds of adversarial robotics prompts.
