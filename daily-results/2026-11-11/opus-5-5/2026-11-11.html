🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Opens Gemini 4 Carbon to Developers and Enterprises at Argon Pricing

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest model, available to all paying developers and enterprises through the Gemini API and Gemini for business at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, matching Gemini 4 Argon's introductory price. It is also rolling out to Ultra subscribers in Antigravity, the Gemini app and AI Mode. Google says Carbon ranks first on CursorBench 4.0, WANDR and AutomationBench.

Anthropic Launches IPO Roadshow at $70 to $78 a Share

Anthropic launched the roadshow for its proposed initial public offering of 825 million Class A shares, about 801.4 million offered by the company and 23.6 million by existing stockholders, at an expected $70 to $78 per share. It has applied to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley named for the prospectus. The SEC registration is not yet effective.

Alibaba Opens Qwen4-Max Preview, First Model of Its Qwen4 Generation

Alibaba opened trial access to Qwen4, its next-generation Qwen family, starting with a Qwen4-Max preview aimed at complex coding, front-end design and agentic professional work. The preview is available through Alibaba's Token Plan, QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen app and Qwen Studio. Alibaba has not disclosed parameter count, benchmark results or a general release date, and BigGo reports Flash, Plus and 27B on-premises versions are planned.

MiniMax Releases Open-Weight H3-Image for 4K Images in Under 10 Seconds

MiniMax released H3-Image, an open-weight model built on its H3 family that handles text-to-image generation, generation from up to 16 reference images, and editing. The company says it renders a 4096 by 4096 image in under 10 seconds, at up to five times the speed of mainstream alternatives. Weights are on Hugging Face under a MiniMax community license, and API pricing is 50 to 70 percent of comparable offerings.

OpenAI Opens ChatGPT to Businesses With Verified Profiles and Business Agents

OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, letting companies claim and verify a profile through Business Hub, update product feeds and see aggregated insights such as mentions and website clicks. Eligible U.S. ecommerce and online service businesses can also build Business Agents that answer customer questions in a separate, labeled conversation. OpenAI says ChatGPT reaches 1.2 billion weekly users, and businesses can sponsor agents through ChatGPT Ads.
