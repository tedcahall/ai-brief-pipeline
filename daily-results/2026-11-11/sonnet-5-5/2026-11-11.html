🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Makes Gemini 4 Carbon Generally Available at Gemini 4 Argon Pricing

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available to developers, enterprises and consumers, two weeks after a limited release to cybersecurity defenders through its Fairwind Program. API pricing matches Gemini 4 Argon at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Google says Carbon ranks first on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR and Zapier's AutomationBench at more than 2X lower cost per task than the next best external model. These are Google's own claims.

Anthropic Launches IPO Roadshow With $70 to $78 Price Range

Anthropic launched the roadshow for its IPO, offering 825,000,000 Class A shares, of which 801,351,354 come from the company, at an expected $70.00 to $78.00 per share. It has applied to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market as ANT. Fortune reports the range values Anthropic at up to $1.4 trillion fully diluted and that an amended filing puts its annualized revenue run rate at about $81 billion at the end of October.

OpenAI Launches Businesses in ChatGPT in Beta

OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT in beta. Through Business Hub, companies can claim and verify a profile, add product feeds with pricing and availability, and see aggregated insights such as mentions and website clicks. Eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses can also build Business Agents that answer customer questions. OpenAI says it does not share users' conversations or Business Agent chats with businesses, and cites 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users.

MiniMax Releases H3-Image Open Weights With 4K Output

MiniMax released H3-Image, a 33.1 billion parameter diffusion transformer for image generation and editing, with open weights on Hugging Face. MiniMax says it generates a 4096 by 4096 image in under 10 seconds and accepts up to 16 reference images. The community license excludes the European Union, United Kingdom, South Korea and the United States, which must request a separate license. The speed claims come from MiniMax.

Google DeepMind Releases Gemini Robotics 2.5 With 96% Factory Pilot Result

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5, a vision-language-action model it says achieves a 30% to 100% higher success rate than prior baselines and finishes tasks 20% to 30% faster across over 200 tasks. In a proof of concept on Mercedes-Benz lines in Hungary and Germany, Apptronik's Apollo 2 humanoid kitted automotive parts with a 96% success rate. DeepMind says it is rolling out to robotics partners.
