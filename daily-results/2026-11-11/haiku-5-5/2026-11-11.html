🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Opens Gemini 4 Carbon to Developers and Consumers

Google said on Nov 10 that Gemini 4 Carbon is available to developers, enterprises, scientists and consumers. Google calls it its strongest model, with frontier cybersecurity capability and long-horizon agentic work. It was first released two weeks earlier to cybersecurity defenders through the Fairwind Program. Pricing and performance claims are company statements.

Alibaba Previews Qwen4-Max on Its Token Plan

Alibaba's Qwen team said on Nov 11 that Qwen4-Max is in preview on Alibaba's Token Plan, with early access on QwenWork, Qoder, Qwen APP and QwenStudio. The announcement claims deeper knowledge, stronger coding and agentic capability, and better front-end and professional software workflows. These are company claims, and no independent benchmark was verified in this run.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With Open Weights Planned

Baidu announced ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview on Nov 11 as the first post-trained model built on the ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. Baidu lists 280B total and 14B active parameters, a 1M-token context, and more than 200 tokens per second per user on Baidu Qianfan. The company says weights will be open-sourced by November 25.

Anthropic Launches Roadshow for Proposed IPO

New developments: Anthropic on Nov 10 launched the roadshow for a proposed IPO of its Class A common stock, to list on Nasdaq under the symbol ANT. The offering covers 825,000,000 shares, of which 801,351,354 are sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 by existing stockholders, at an expected price of $70.00 to $78.00 per share. The registration statement has been filed with the SEC but is not yet effective.

OpenAI Launches Businesses in ChatGPT in Beta

OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT in beta on Nov 10. Businesses can claim their profile through Business Hub and see how people engage with them. Eligible ecommerce and online-service businesses in the U.S. can build a Business Agent to answer customer questions, and Shopify merchants can connect their store. OpenAI cites 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users.
