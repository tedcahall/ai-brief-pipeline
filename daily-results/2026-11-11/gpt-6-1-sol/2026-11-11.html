🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Opens Gemini 4 Carbon to Developers and Ultra Subscribers

Google is making Gemini 4 Carbon broadly available after an earlier restricted cybersecurity preview. The model targets sustained coding, knowledge work and scientific reasoning, with API access for paying developers and rollout to Ultra subscribers. Introductory API pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens; Google says those rates rise after November 30.

Alibaba Opens Qwen4-Max Preview Across Its Developer and Consumer Tools

Alibaba says Qwen4-Max is now in preview on its Token Plan, with early access through QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen app and QwenStudio. The announcement emphasizes coding, knowledge work and agentic workflows. TechNode corroborates the rollout and reports image and video inputs with a million-token context window. This is preview access, not an announced open-weight release.

Baidu Introduces ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, With Weights Promised Later

Baidu introduced its first post-trained model built on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, reporting 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active parameters and a million-token context window. The company claims more than 200 tokens per second per user on Qianfan. The API is available, but the Preview weights are promised by November 25 rather than released now.

MiniMax Releases Open-Weight H3-Image for 4K Generation and Editing

MiniMax released H3-Image for text-to-image generation, reference-based creation and editing, with open weights and access through its API, MiniMax Code and Hailuo AI. The company says it handles up to 16 reference images and produces 4096-by-4096 images in under ten seconds. Those speed and quality statements are vendor claims, not independently reproduced results in this brief.

NVIDIA Unveils GR00T 2 Robot Models, With Weights Due in December

NVIDIA introduced GR00T 2, a world-action model that jointly predicts future video and robot actions, and a Turbo variant designed for onboard humanoid control. Its research describes transfer from human demonstrations to robot tasks and reports 95% success after five demonstrations per task in one evaluation. Jim Fan says weights and X-Adapt recipes are coming in December; supporting tools and data are available now.
