🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Opens Gemini 4 Carbon to Developers and Ultra Subscribers

Google is expanding Gemini 4 Carbon beyond its initial cybersecurity-defender access to paying API customers, enterprises and Google AI Ultra subscribers. The company positions it for extended coding, research and verification tasks. Its launch announcement lists introductory API prices of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens; these are introductory rates, not permanent pricing.

Alibaba Opens Qwen4-Max Preview Across Its AI Products

Alibaba says Qwen4-Max is now in preview on its Token Plan, with early access also available through QwenWork, Qoder, Qwen APP and QwenStudio. The announcement emphasizes coding, knowledge work and agentic workflows. This is a preview-access announcement, not confirmation of a complete Qwen4 family release or downloadable model weights.

Baidu Introduces ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, With Weights Still to Come

Baidu introduced the first post-trained model based on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. It reports 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active parameters and a one-million-token context window, with throughput above 200 tokens per second per user on Qianfan. Baidu says weights will be open-sourced by November 25; that remains a future commitment.

MiniMax Releases H3-Image Weights for Native 16-Megapixel Generation

MiniMax released H3-Image for image generation and editing, with native outputs around 16 megapixels and support for up to 16 reference images. Its model card describes a 33.1-billion-parameter diffusion transformer and local serving options. The weights use MiniMax’s community license, not MIT; the company’s roughly ten-second generation claim should not be assumed for every hardware setup.

Meituan Opens LongCat-2.5 Weights Under the MIT License

Meituan has made LongCat-2.5 weights available under the MIT License, following the model’s earlier introduction. VentureBeat reports the weight release on November 9. The official model card describes a native multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 1.6 trillion total parameters and about 48 billion active per token, designed for visual understanding and extended coding tasks. Published benchmark results include vendor-run evaluations.
