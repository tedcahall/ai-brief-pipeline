📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Google DeepMind says its AlphaProof Code framework machine-checked selected security properties of Signal’s compiled implementation, while Alibaba placed Qwen4-Max in preview and Baidu launched ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview. Meituan also published LongCat-2.5 weights under MIT, and OpenAI opened Business Hub for businesses to manage their presence and agents in ChatGPT.

The releases differ in evidence and access: several model specifications are company-reported, Qwen4-Max remains a preview, and DeepMind’s proof covers specified properties rather than guaranteeing the security of the whole Signal system. OpenAI says Business Hub provides aggregated engagement information, not access to individual user conversations.

🌐 TOP STORIES

DeepMind Uses AlphaProof Code to Verify Selected Signal Protocol Code

Google DeepMind says AlphaProof Code, a multi-model agent framework, produced more than six million machine-checked Lean proof lines for selected security properties of Signal’s compiled implementation. Researchers defined and reviewed the specifications, while agents generated and audited proofs; DeepMind says about one percent of the codebase needed human review. This verifies specified properties, not every aspect of Signal’s security.

Alibaba Puts Qwen4-Max Into Preview as It Teases the Qwen4 Family

Alibaba’s Qwen team announced Qwen4-Max preview access through its Token Plan and early access in QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen app and QwenStudio. The same post describes Qwen4 as forthcoming and lists intended improvements in knowledge, coding and agentic work. The announcement did not include model weights, a model card or independent performance results, so those capabilities remain the company’s characterization.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With 1M-Token Context

Baidu introduced ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview as the first post-trained model based on its earlier Flash Base. The company lists 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active parameters and a one-million-token context, and says the Qianfan API is available now. Baidu says weights will follow under Apache 2.0; its reported speed and task capabilities are vendor claims, not independent benchmark results.

Meituan Publishes LongCat-2.5 Weights Under the MIT License

Meituan announced the release of LongCat-2.5 weights under the MIT license, describing the model as natively multimodal with 1.6 trillion total parameters and about 48 billion active parameters. The company positions it for visual understanding, agentic coding and longer tasks. Those specifications and capability descriptions come from Meituan’s release post; this run did not independently benchmark the model or verify its weights repository.

OpenAI Opens Business Hub for ChatGPT Business Profiles and Agents

OpenAI’s Business Hub lets organizations claim and maintain business information that ChatGPT may use, including product details and retailer feeds with current pricing and availability. Businesses can create a separately labeled Business Agent with company-provided information and view aggregated mentions and discovery topics. OpenAI says users’ conversations are not shared with businesses; its announcement does not provide evidence yet about how these agents affect customer conversion.
