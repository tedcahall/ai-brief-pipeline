🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Releases Gemini 4 Carbon, Its Strongest Frontier Model

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon generally available to developers, enterprises and Ultra subscribers, positioning it as its strongest model for long-horizon agentic work in software engineering, cybersecurity and scientific reasoning. It leads CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity WANDR and Zapier AutomationBench at more than 2x lower cost per task than the next-best external model, and is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.

Anthropic Launches IPO Roadshow With a $70 to $78 Price Range

Anthropic launched the roadshow for its proposed initial public offering and plans to list Class A common stock on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. The offering spans 825 million shares at an indicative price of $70 to $78 per share, with a 30-day underwriter option for 123.75 million additional shares. Anthropic will not receive proceeds from the portion sold by existing stockholders.

MiniMax Releases Open-Weight H3-Image, Generating 4K Images in Under 10 Seconds

MiniMax released H3-Image, an open-weight image model built on H3 that supports text-to-image generation, reference-based generation and image editing. It generates a 4096x4096 image in under ten seconds, at up to five times the speed of mainstream alternatives, and handles up to 16 reference images. Weights are on Hugging Face, with API access through the MiniMax platform, MiniMax Code and Hailuo AI.

Alibaba Previews Qwen4-Max, the Next Generation of Its Frontier Models

Alibaba announced Qwen4-Max is now in preview on its Token Plan, with early access across QwenWork, Qoder, Qwen App and QwenStudio. The company says Qwen4 brings deeper knowledge, richer world understanding and stronger coding and agentic capabilities for complex coding, knowledge work, front-end builds and professional software workflows.

Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Code Formally Verifies the Signal Protocol

Google DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code, an agentic framework that mathematically proved key security properties of the code underlying the Signal protocol, which secures conversations in Signal and Google Messages. Agents powered by Gemini 4 Carbon generated over six million lines of Lean proofs in about four weeks, a task that historically takes expert teams years, with roughly one percent of the codebase requiring human review.
