🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Releases Gemini 4 Carbon to All Developers and Ultra Subscribers

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest frontier model, available to developers, enterprises, scientists and consumers after a two-week early window for cybersecurity defenders. Google says it ranks first on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR and Zapier's AutomationBench at more than 2X lower cost per task than the next best external model, and it is priced at the same introductory rate as Argon, $2 per million input and $10 per million output tokens.

Anthropic Launches IPO Roadshow at $70 to $78 a Share

New developments: after last week's valuation disputes, Anthropic formally launched the roadshow for its proposed IPO on November 10. The offering is 825 million Class A shares, 801.4 million from Anthropic and 23.6 million from existing stockholders, priced between $70.00 and $78.00, with a Nasdaq listing under the symbol ANT; press reports put the implied valuation near $1.4 trillion.

OpenAI Launches Businesses in ChatGPT in Beta

OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT, a beta that lets companies claim a profile in Business Hub, manage their information and product feeds, and see aggregated insights on how ChatGPT mentions them. Eligible US ecommerce and online-service businesses can publish a Business Agent that answers customer questions from supplied information in a clearly labeled conversation.

MiniMax Open-Sources H3-Image for 4K Generation and Editing

MiniMax released H3-Image, an open-weight image model built on H3 that generates and edits 4K (4096x4096, about 16 megapixel) images in under 10 seconds with up to 16 reference images. Weights are on Hugging Face, and the model is served through the MiniMax API, MiniMax Code and Hailuo AI, with 50 percent off 16MP-plus generations during launch week.

Alibaba Previews Qwen4-Max Ahead of Qwen4

Alibaba's Qwen team opened preview access to Qwen4-Max, the flagship of the coming Qwen4 generation, on Alibaba's Token Plan, with early access through QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen app and QwenStudio. The company pitches deeper knowledge, richer world understanding and stronger coding and agentic capabilities for real-world professional workflows; no benchmarks or pricing for the final release were published yet.
