🌐 TOP STORIES

Apple Puts Alibaba's Qwen Inside Apple Intelligence in China

Alibaba announced on October 31 that its Qwen model will power Apple Intelligence features in China, including Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence. According to Alibaba, testing begins in the iOS developer beta on November 4, with rollout to Chinese users in iOS 27.3 in December. Users can choose whether to enable it, and Alibaba says it will not train on user requests or responses.

Google's Project Suncatcher Satellite Returns First AI Response From Orbit

Google says its Project Suncatcher prototype satellite, launched October 1, has run its first prompt on a data-center-class TPU in orbit. During a ground-station pass the team uplinked a request to the onboard Gemma 4 open model and received a haiku back as the satellite passed Svalbard. Google plans thermal and radiation tests next, ahead of twin interconnected satellites in 2027.

OpenAI Releases GPT-Rosalind-6 for Vetted Life Sciences Research

OpenAI published a system card addendum for GPT-Rosalind-6, a biology and drug-discovery model that uses the same weights as GPT-6 Astra with no additional training. Offered through trusted access, it is designed to handle some dual-use topics Astra may refuse while still refusing severe bioweaponization requests. OpenAI reports GeneBench Pro single-attempt success of 37.7%, up from 16.7% for GPT-Rosalind-5.5.

DeepSeek Open-Sources Super-agent Coding Agent Prototype

DeepSeek published dsh-superagent, a proactive coding agent for a single GitHub repository, packaged as a bundle for its DeepSeek Harness scaffolding. It listens to GitHub and optionally Feishu, opens work items in separate sessions and replies with results such as pull requests. DeepSeek labels it a research prototype rather than an official product; ShiftDelete reports an October 31 release under the MIT license.

OpenAI Pares Funding Round Target to $20 Billion

OpenAI is seeking to raise up to $20 billion in its latest funding round, down from an earlier target of up to $30 billion, CNBC confirmed on Saturday following a Financial Times report. The FT said OpenAI is seeking a $1.25 trillion post-money valuation. A person familiar with the deal said the round has not closed and figures may change; UAE investors and Coatue are leading.
