🌐 TOP STORIES

Apple to Bring Alibaba Qwen to Apple Intelligence in China

Alibaba announced that its Qwen model will integrate with Apple Intelligence in China, according to Xinhua. The company says users will be able to choose whether to enable the integration and share information. A developer beta is planned for November 4, followed by a December iOS 27.3 rollout.

OpenAI Details GPT-Rosalind-6 for Trusted Life Sciences Research

OpenAI says its GPT-Rosalind-6 uses GPT-6 Astra weights and is designed for advanced biological research under trusted access. The system card describes support for some dual-use topics that general-access Astra may refuse, alongside security requirements and safeguards intended to limit dangerous biological weaponization.

Google Receives Suncatcher Satellite’s First AI Response

Google says its Project Suncatcher prototype received a prompt from a ground station, ran the Gemma 4 open model on a data-center-class TPU in orbit, and returned a haiku to Earth. The experiment is an early research milestone; Google plans further thermal and radiation testing, not a commercial deployment.

TBC Releases Mischief 1 Video Model Informed by Neuron Research

The Biological Computing Co. released Mischief 1, an image-to-video model built on Wan 2.2. Research on living neurons informs a software layer, but customer inference runs on conventional hardware, not living cells. TBC claims five times faster generation at comparable quality; independent confirmation of that comparison was not established.

Google’s PlanAssist Expands UK Planning Pilot

Google DeepMind says its Gemini-based PlanAssist reduced planning officers’ average assessment time by 58% in an early three-council pilot. The tool checks documents and helps draft reports, while officers retain final decisions. Twelve additional English councils will join a year-long beta using live applications.
