🌐 TOP STORIES

Google's Project Suncatcher Runs First AI Inference on an In-Orbit TPU

Google said its Project Suncatcher prototype satellite returned the first AI response from a data-center-class TPU in orbit, running an onboard Gemma 4 open model to generate a haiku during a ground-station pass. The company launched the satellite on October 1 and next plans to test how TPUs perform under thermal stress and radiation, ahead of twin interconnected satellites in 2027.

Apple to Integrate Alibaba's Qwen Into Apple Intelligence in China

Alibaba announced a partnership with Apple to integrate its Qwen model into Apple Intelligence in China, with testing in the iOS developer beta from November 4 and a rollout to Chinese users with iOS 27.3 in December. Qwen will power Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence, marking the first time a global hardware maker has used a Chinese large model as its system-level AI backbone.

OpenAI Releases GPT-Rosalind-6 for Biology and Drug Discovery

OpenAI published a system card addendum for GPT-Rosalind-6, a frontier reasoning model for biology, drug discovery and translational medicine that uses the same weights as GPT-6 Astra with no additional training. The trusted-access deployment relaxes refusals on some dual-use biological topics, and the company reports higher single-attempt success than GPT-Rosalind-5.5 on GeneBench Pro v13.

Musk Says Colossus 2 Will Hold About 1.2 Million Blackwell GPUs by Year-End

Elon Musk said SpaceX's Colossus 2 site will hold around 1.2 million Blackwell GPUs by the end of the year. The Macroharder and Minihard buildings each run 220,000 GB300s configured for training, while the Gun Range and Spam clusters are targeting November and December operational dates, with Spam configured for inference using simpler networking.

Anthropic IPO Draws Datacenter Developers Awaiting New Compute Demand

SemiAnalysis said Anthropic's IPO is the moment datacenter developers have been waiting for, citing the lab's outsized compute demand. The commentary, quoting datacenter-market sources, notes Anthropic is on par with hyperscalers but cannot contract directly with datacenter builders, and that a public listing is contingent on Anthropic reaching investment-grade status.
