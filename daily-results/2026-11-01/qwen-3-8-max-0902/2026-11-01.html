🌐 TOP STORIES

Alibaba's Qwen to Power Apple Intelligence in China

Alibaba announced a partnership with Apple on Saturday to integrate its Qwen large language model into Apple Intelligence for users in China. Coverage of the announcement says Qwen will power Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence for text and image understanding and content generation. Developer beta testing starts November 4, with the public rollout shipping alongside iOS 27.3 in December.

Project Suncatcher Receives First AI Response From a TPU in Orbit

Google Research said its Project Suncatcher prototype satellite returned what Google calls the first AI response from a data center class TPU in orbit. During a ground station pass the team uplinked a prompt to the onboard Gemma 4 open model, and a haiku about being in space was downlinked over Svalbard, Norway. Next tests cover thermal stress and radiation, with twin interconnected satellites targeted for 2027.

OpenAI Releases GPT-6 Rosalind Research for Biology and Drug Discovery

OpenAI released GPT-6 Rosalind Research on October 30, a life sciences model for biology and drug discovery that appears in the API changelog as gpt-6-rosalind. According to analysis of its system card, the model reuses the same weights as flagship GPT-6 Astra with no additional training, relaxes refusals on dual use biology queries under a trusted access program, and carries a higher price than Astra.

AMD's MI355X Beats NVIDIA B200 on Cost per Kimi K3 Token in SemiAnalysis Benchmark

SemiAnalysis reported that AMD's MI355X achieved up to 2x better performance per dollar than NVIDIA's B200 serving Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 for agentic inference, citing AITER kernel tuning, MonoKernel decode and 2,304GB of HBM per server that avoids slow scale-out networking. FourWeekMBA's arithmetic on the InferenceX benchmark put costs at $0.077 per million tokens on MI355X versus $0.179 on B200.

Karpathy Ships HackerTube, Built With Claude Opus 5.5 in About Two Hours

Andrej Karpathy published HackerTube, a YouTube style site seeded with 6,454 videos that people recommended in Hacker News comments between 2007 and 2026. In his blog post Karpathy said he handed the task to Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 on Saturday morning and the model returned hackertube.org, built in about two hours according to coverage. Karpathy framed it as an alternative to recommendation algorithms that do not work for him.
