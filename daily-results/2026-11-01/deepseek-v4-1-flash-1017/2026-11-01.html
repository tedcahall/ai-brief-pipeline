🌐 TOP STORIES

Google's Project Suncatcher prototype returns first AI response from orbit

Google said its Project Suncatcher prototype satellite, launched October 1, finished commissioning and reached a Hello World milestone: during a ground-station pass it uplinked a prompt to an onboard Gemma 4 open model running on a data-center-class TPU, and the satellite downlinked a haiku as it passed Svalbard, Norway. Google calls it the first-ever AI response from a data center class TPU in orbit and says it will next test how the TPUs perform under thermal stress and radiation, with twin interconnected satellites targeted for 2027. These are Google's claims about its own hardware, and the company does not describe a commercial service.

OpenAI opens GPT-6 Rosalind Research to vetted labs for biology work

OpenAI's system card addendum for GPT-Rosalind-6 states the model uses the same weights as GPT-6 Astra, with no added training data and no additional post-training, and is reserved for trusted access rather than general release. On GeneBench Pro v13 the company reports single-attempt success of 37.7% against 16.7% for GPT-Rosalind-5.5, and rates the launch High capability in the biological and chemical domain while keeping the Critical cybersecurity rating from Astra. Approved customers must meet identity, least-privilege and access-removal requirements.

Apple and Alibaba confirm Qwen will power Apple Intelligence in China

Alibaba announced October 31 that its Qwen model will be integrated into Apple Intelligence for users in China, covering Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence for text and image understanding and content generation. Testing begins in the iOS developer beta on November 4, with public rollout in iOS 27.3 in December. Users choose whether to enable the integration and what to share, and Alibaba said it will not use requests or generated responses for model training.

DeepSeek publishes MIT-licensed Super-agent coding prototype

DeepSeek's Harness team released dsh-superagent, a proactive coding agent for a single GitHub repository that listens to GitHub and optionally Feishu, opens a work item for each allowed request, works it in its own session and directory, and answers in the original conversation, often with a pull request. The repository labels it research code for reference only rather than an official DeepSeek product, and says some designs in the accompanying paper are not implemented in the released prototype. RuntimeWire reported the release and cited Bloomberg Technology as its primary source.

Five hyperscalers set to spend up to $828B on AI as Nasdaq 100 sheds $1T

Fortune reported that Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft, Meta and SpaceX are on track to spend as much as $828 billion on capital expenditure in 2026, split roughly $220 billion, up to $205 billion, about $170 billion, up to $145 billion and up to $88 billion, according to company earnings releases and calls. Four of the five reported negative free cash flow in the third quarter, Microsoft was the exception and separately announced a $25 billion bond offering, and the Nasdaq fell 2.9% while Nasdaq 100 companies lost more than $1 trillion in market value. Rising cloud sales do not by themselves validate the spending.
