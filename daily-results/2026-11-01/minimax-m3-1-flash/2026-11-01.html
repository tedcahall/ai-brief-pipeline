🌐 TOP STORIES

Apple Intelligence Reaches China in December With Alibaba's Qwen

Apple said on Saturday that its first Apple Intelligence features will launch in mainland China with iOS 27.3 in December, with a developer beta opening on 4 November. Alibaba's Qwen model will run inside Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence, and Live Translation, Clean Up and Genmoji ship on Apple's own models. The rebuilt Siri AI shown at WWDC 2026 is excluded from the launch.

Google's Suncatcher Satellite Returns Its First AI Response From a TPU in Orbit

Google said its Project Suncatcher prototype satellite produced what it calls the first AI response from a data center class TPU in orbit. During a ground station pass over Svalbard, the team uplinked a prompt to the onboard Gemma 4 open model and received a generated haiku, then tested limericks and telemetry prompts. Google describes the 1 October launch as the first step toward twin interconnected satellites in 2027.

OpenAI Publishes a System Card for GPT-Rosalind-6 Life Sciences Model

OpenAI published a system card addendum for GPT-Rosalind-6, a frontier reasoning model for biology, drug discovery and translational medicine. The addendum states that GPT-Rosalind-6 uses the same weights as GPT-6 Astra, with no added training data and no further post-training for this release. OpenAI treats the launch as High capability in the Biological and Chemical domain and limits it to trusted access deployments.

AMD Reaches Up to Twice the Performance per Dollar of an Nvidia B200 on Kimi K3

SemiAnalysis reported that AMD's team, led by Anush Elangovan, achieved up to twice the performance per dollar of an Nvidia B200 on Kimi K3 agentic inference. The post credits tuned AITER kernels, MonoKernel decode work and 2304GB of HBM per server, which avoids crossing slow 8x400Gbit scale-out networking. The figures come from SemiAnalysis's own testing and have not been independently verified.

Big Tech's $828 Billion AI Capex Plan Helped Wipe $1 Trillion From the Nasdaq 100

Fortune reports that Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft, Meta and SpaceX plan as much as $828 billion of 2026 capital spending, roughly $220 billion from Amazon, up to $205 billion from Alphabet, about $170 billion from Microsoft, up to $145 billion from Meta and up to $88 billion from SpaceX. Four of the five reported negative free cash flow last quarter, and the Nasdaq closed down 2.9 percent on Friday.
