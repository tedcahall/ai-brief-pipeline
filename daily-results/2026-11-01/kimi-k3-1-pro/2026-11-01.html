🌐 TOP STORIES

Apple and Alibaba Bring Qwen to Apple Intelligence in China

Apple will integrate Alibaba's Qwen into Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence for Chinese users. Developer testing begins November 4, with public availability through iOS 27.3 in December. Apple says its own generative models power the wider first feature set, while Qwen adds text and image understanding and content generation.

OpenAI Releases GPT-Rosalind-6 for Trusted Life-Sciences Research

OpenAI's new life-sciences deployment uses the same weights as GPT-6 Astra, with no additional training or post-training. It permits more legitimate dual-use biological research under institutional trusted access while retaining weaponization refusals. OpenAI rates biological and chemical capability High and reports GeneBench Pro v13 success rising to 37.7% from 16.7% at the highest tested reasoning budget.

Google's Project Suncatcher Returns First Response From Orbiting TPU

Google says its Project Suncatcher prototype uplinked a prompt to Gemma 4 running on a data-center-class TPU in orbit, then received a haiku back on Earth near Svalbard. Follow-up tests covered limericks and telemetry. The result is a research milestone; thermal and radiation testing remains ahead, with twin interconnected satellites planned for 2027.

DeepSeek Open-Sources Proactive Super-Agent Research Prototype

DeepSeek's Harness team published an MIT-licensed proactive coding agent for a single GitHub repository. It accepts authorized work from GitHub and optionally Feishu, executes tasks in separate agent sessions and directories, and can respond with pull requests. The team explicitly labels it research code rather than an official product or production-ready software.

Cisco Talos Finds Infostealer Using AI to Rank Victims

Cisco Talos disclosed that VOLTA packages stolen identity and asset data into an evidence bundle, then asks an LLM to rank monetization value and assess security posture. The malware defaults to Claude Opus 4.6 through OpenRouter and falls back to Kimi K2 and Gemini 2.5 Flash when it detects refusals. Talos says this displaces operator analysis rather than improving theft.
