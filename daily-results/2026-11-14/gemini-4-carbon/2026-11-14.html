🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet With Amex, Mastercard and Visa for Agent Purchases

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa, letting its "dots" personal agents buy items for US ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and older without exposing raw card numbers to agents or merchants. Users approve each transaction via Face ID or fingerprint to issue a scoped payment token while keeping card rewards and dispute protections, and OpenAI published a Commerce Partner Playbook with Stripe, Adyen, and fraud-prevention vendors.

DeepSeek Opens Full App Suite in Its API With Server-Side Search, Files and Voice

DeepSeek brought the core features behind its consumer app to the DeepSeek API, adding server-side web search, multi-step Deep Thinking Search, PDF and Office file parsing, automatic URL reading, and safety checks on its V4.1-Flash model across Messages, Responses, and Chat Completions. Developers can also call standalone Search, Fetch, OCR, ASR, and TTS endpoints with tool fees billed atop token charges, alongside a new open-source frontend demo.

Thinking Machines Releases 594B Inkling 2 Small Open Weights and 4-Bit Training Recipe

New developments: Following last week's initial model preview, Thinking Machines Lab released the Apache 2.0 open weights for its 594B-parameter (17B active) multimodal MoE model Inkling 2 Small on Hugging Face and detailed its end-to-end native NVFP4 4-bit forward-pass recipe on NVIDIA GB300 GPUs. The lab reports 8% faster pretraining and 20% faster reinforcement learning than MXFP8, while Arena ranked it first among US open models on WebDev and Agent leaderboards.

Ex-Anthropic Researchers' Startup Mirendil Raises $1 Billion Series A for Agentic AI R&D

San Francisco AI lab Mirendil, founded by former Anthropic researchers, announced a $1 billion Series A led by Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital with Google, Battery Ventures, and Pear VC participating, just five months after its $200 million seed round. The company disclosed no valuation, and while it states one human researcher using agents pretrained Mirendil-Base in 3.5 months for under $10 million in compute, TechTimes noted no independent benchmarks have verified its models.

White House AI Task Force Urges 12-Month Liability Relief for Voluntary Cyber Reports

Following its second meeting with Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross, and Pentagon research chief Emil Michael, the White House AI task force urged federal regulators to limit enforcement liability for at least 12 months when organizations voluntarily report cyber vulnerabilities and incidents in good faith. The readout said AI-driven threats have outdated prior enforcement standards and noted that NSA and NIST are testing frontier models with major AI labs.
