📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

DeepSeek opened its app’s search, file, link, safety and voice capabilities to developers through its API, while OpenAI introduced a wallet that lets agents make tokenized purchases with per-transaction user approval. Both launches move agentic systems beyond generating answers toward completing multi-step digital tasks inside existing developer and payment infrastructure.

Mirendil raised $1 billion to expand agent-driven AI research, NVIDIA introduced the open GR00T 2 robotics model, and Cohere added AISLE and cubic to its sovereign AI platform. Together, the announcements span research automation, robotics, cybersecurity and enterprise deployment.

🌐 TOP STORIES

DeepSeek Opens Full App Capabilities to Developers Through API

DeepSeek made its app capabilities available through its API, including web and image search, file parsing, URL reading, safety checks, OCR, speech recognition and text-to-speech. The features work with V4.1-Flash across Messages, Responses and Chat Completions, with server-side tool calls and context assembly. DeepSeek also released an open-source frontend demo. Tool usage carries fees beyond standard token charges, and public-facing apps must enable safety checks.

OpenAI Introduces ChatGPT Wallet for Agent-Led Purchases

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, letting agents make purchases without receiving users’ actual card details. Users review each transaction and approve it with a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint. Payment networks issue tokens for existing checkout flows while preserving card rewards and eligible dispute protections. The wallet is rolling out through dots to eligible US ChatGPT Pro subscribers, alongside guidance for businesses.

Mirendil Raises $1 Billion Series A for Agent-Driven AI R&D

Mirendil announced a $1 billion Series A led by Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital, with participation from Google and other investors. The company is building agent-driven tools intended to make frontier AI research accessible to smaller teams. It cited its Mirendil-Base coding model, the Fern post-trained model with up to 10 million tokens of context, and the Lor private beta, which coordinates hundreds of agents on extended coding and research tasks.

NVIDIA Unveils Open GR00T 2 Model for Robotics Research

NVIDIA introduced GR00T 2, an open System One model for robotics research that learns skills from human demonstrations and predicts how actions affect the physical world. NVIDIA says those skills can transfer across different robot bodies, reducing the need to teach each robot every task separately. The accompanying research paper describes cross-embodiment teleoperated demonstrations and a pretraining mixture spanning multiple robot platforms.

Cohere Completes AISLE Acquisition and Adds Cubic Team

Cohere completed its acquisition of autonomous vulnerability-discovery company AISLE and welcomed the team behind AI code-review platform cubic. AISLE will anchor a new Cohere Cyber business, with its founders taking security and research leadership roles, while cubic will establish a secure coding vertical. Cohere also plans a long-term investment in Prague as its global center for AI cybersecurity and autonomous security systems.
