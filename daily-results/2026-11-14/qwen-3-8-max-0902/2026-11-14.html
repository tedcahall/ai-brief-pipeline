The fallout from Jensen Huang's resignation deepened: the BBC reports Huang is willing to answer Congressional questions about national security, Nvidia shed over half a trillion dollars in market value, and officials cited by David Sacks have reached no conclusion on the Alice Huang spy allegation.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Jensen Huang Willing to Face Congress as US Officials Question Spy Allegations

New developments: After Jensen Huang's November 12 resignation, the BBC reports he is willing to appear before Congress on whether US national security was compromised. Nvidia closed down more than 4%, shedding over half a trillion dollars in market value, with CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO. David Sacks cites the AG, CIA and DNI as having reached no conclusion that Alice Huang acted as a Chinese spy; Beijing calls the allegations a smear.

Thinking Machines Ships Open Weights for Inkling 2 Small and Details 4-Bit Training

New developments: Following the November 5 launch, Thinking Machines released open weights for Inkling 2 Small, including an NVFP4 checkpoint on Hugging Face, and published its training recipe: NVFP4 forward passes in MoE expert layers with MXFP8 backward passes, yielding 8% pretraining and 20% RL wall-clock speedups on GB300. NVIDIA says the 594B model pretrained in 68 hours, and Arena ranks it the top US open model.

DeepSeek Opens Its Full App Toolkit Through the API

DeepSeek made the capabilities behind its consumer app available through the API on V4.1-Flash, with server-side tool calls: web search with citations, Deep Thinking Search across multi-step reasoning, file input for PDFs, Office documents and images, and URL reading, across Messages, Responses and Chat Completions. Standalone OCR, ASR and TTS APIs are offered, tool usage is billed on top of tokens, and an open-source frontend demo shipped.

Mirendil Raises $1B Series A for Agent-Driven AI R&D

Mirendil announced a $1B Series A led by Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital, with participation from Google, Battery Ventures, Doerr Capital, M13, Pear VC and S32. The lab says one researcher working with agents pretrained Mirendil-Base in 3.5 months for under $10M of compute, post-trained it into Fern with 10M-token context, and runs its Lor agent product in private beta. No valuation was stated and the results are company-run.

White House AI Task Force Urges 12-Month Liability Relief for Cyber Reporting

After its second meeting, the administration's AI task force said prior standards for regulatory action after cyber incidents may be outdated because AI-driven attacks can overwhelm reasonable defenses. It urged agencies to limit liability for good-faith voluntary reporting of vulnerabilities and incidents for at least 12 months, with possible extension. Officials described a voluntary AI-incident reporting channel, and NSA and NIST are testing frontier models with major labs.
