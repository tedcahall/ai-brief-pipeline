🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet So Dots Agents Can Pay With Tokenized Cards

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, which lets its dots agents complete purchases without receiving a user's actual card details. Users add a card once and approve each purchase with a security check such as Face ID; the network then issues a transaction-specific payment token. OpenAI says card rewards and dispute protections are preserved, it takes no cut at launch, and access starts with US ChatGPT Pro subscribers.

DeepSeek Opens Its Full App Toolkit to Developers Through the API

DeepSeek said the capabilities behind its consumer app are now available through its API. V4.1-Flash (deepseek-flash) supports web search with citations, deep-thinking search, file input, URL reading and safety checks across the Messages, Responses and Chat Completions endpoints, with tool calls assembled server-side. Search, Fetch, OCR, ASR and TTS are also offered as standalone APIs, and extended capabilities carry tool-usage fees on top of token pricing.

llama.cpp and ggml Reach v1.0.0 With a Stable API Commitment for Local AI

Georgi Gerganov's ggml-org released llama.cpp v1.0.0 and ggml v1.0.0, the first major versions of the local-inference libraries after roughly 3.5 years of development. The team, now part of Hugging Face, said both projects will signal API and ABI breaks only through major-version bumps under semantic versioning, cites support for more than 150 model architecture variants, and lists distribution through Docker, Debian, Homebrew, conda-forge and Winget.

Mirendil Raises $1 Billion Series A to Automate AI Research With Agents

Mirendil, an AI lab founded by two former Anthropic researchers, announced on November 12 that it raised $1 billion in a Series A led by Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital, with Google, Battery Ventures, Pear VC and S32 among participants; no valuation was disclosed. It says one researcher working with agents pretrained its Mirendil-Base coding model in 3.5 months on under $10 million of compute, and its Lor agent platform is in private beta.

Thinking Machines Details Native 4-Bit Training Behind Now Open-Weight Inkling 2 Small

New developments: Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Small's weights on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0 on November 12, after its November 5-6 launch, and on November 13 published a Connectionism post on training the 594B-parameter model with a native NVFP4 4-bit forward pass from the first pretraining step through the final RL rollout on NVIDIA GB300 systems. It reports an 8% pretraining and 20% RL speedup over full MXFP8 training.
