🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Closes Below $4 Trillion Market Cap as Huang Fallout Widens

New developments: Nvidia closed below a $4 trillion market capitalization for the first time since July 2025, sliding 2.01% on Friday and 12.75% for the week, its sixth straight weekly decline and longest such streak since 2012. Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive and director effective immediately; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and Harvey C. Jones is the board's first chair.

Mirendil Raises $1 Billion Series A to Automate Frontier AI Research

Mirendil raised $1 billion in a Series A led by Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital, with Battery Ventures, Doerr Capital, Gil & Co, Google, M13, Pear VC and S32 participating. The company says one researcher working with agents pretrained its Mirendil-Base coding model from scratch in 3.5 months for under $10 million in compute. It disclosed no valuation.

DeepSeek Opens Its Full App Stack to Developers Through the API

DeepSeek exposed the capabilities behind its consumer app through its API, with V4.1-Flash supporting web search, deep thinking search, file input, URL reading and safety checks across Messages, Responses and Chat Completions, with tool calls and context assembly handled server-side. Search and link reading are enabled by parameter, public apps must enable safety checks, and OCR, ASR and TTS are sold separately.

OpenAI Introduces ChatGPT Wallet for Agent-Led Purchases

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, which lets its dots agents complete purchases without access to a user's actual card details and requires a security check for every transaction. Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, it starts with ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US, using payment tokens so existing card rewards and dispute protections still apply. A Commerce Partner Playbook covers merchant readiness.

LMSYS Launches SGLang Consortium With Eight Founding Members

LMSYS Org launched the SGLang Consortium with founding members NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Google, Meta, Cloudflare, Modal and RadixArk, announced at the opening keynote of the first SGLang Summit in San Francisco. Members designate technical representatives and join two roadmap meetings a year while SGLang's maintainers keep responsibility for technical decisions and code acceptance.
