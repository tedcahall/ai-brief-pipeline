Thinking Machines Lab Details End-to-End 4-Bit Training for Open-Weights Inkling 2 Small

Thinking Machines Lab released open weights for Inkling 2 Small and detailed how it trained the 594B-parameter model end-to-end with a native 4-bit forward pass on NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 racks in 68 hours. Pairing NVFP4 forward passes with MXFP8 backward passes and stochastic rounding delivered an 8% pretraining speedup and 20% reinforcement-learning speedup over full MXFP8 training, while reaching the top US open-model spot on Agent Arena.

DeepSeek Opens Full App Experience and Standalone Voice, OCR, and Search APIs

DeepSeek made its full DeepSeek App toolkit available through the DeepSeek API for V4.1-Flash (deepseek-flash), handling server-side web search, deep-thinking search, file parsing, URL reading, and safety screening with a few parameters. Developers can also call Search, Fetch, OCR, automatic speech recognition (ASR), and text-to-speech (TTS) as standalone APIs, alongside an open-source chat frontend demo supporting conversation history imports.

Llama.cpp Reaches v1.0.0 With Strict Semantic Versioning for Local AI

Three and a half years after its launch, the llama.cpp and ggml projects reached version 1.0.0, committing to strict semantic versioning for public C APIs and ABIs. Supporting over 150 model architectures and mainstream speculative decoding methods across hardware from NVIDIA, AMD, Qualcomm, Arm, IBM, and Intel, the release gives downstream local-AI builders stable library boundaries across major platforms.

White House AI Task Force Urges 12-Month Liability Relief for Cyber Incident Reporting

The Trump administration's AI task force urged federal regulators to grant at least 12 months of regulatory liability relief for organizations voluntarily reporting AI-fueled cyberattacks and vulnerabilities in good faith. Following its second meeting, led by officials including Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross, the group said outdated cyber enforcement rules risk chilling critical threat sharing as NSA and NIST test frontier models.
