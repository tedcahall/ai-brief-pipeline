NOV 14, 2026

📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

OpenAI pushed further into agentic commerce and security this week. ChatGPT Wallet lets approved agents pay without seeing card numbers, and Daybreak adds AI-driven red teaming and remediation tools. Benchmark results also moved, with Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Opus 5.5 tied for first on Artificial Analysis's refreshed coding index at 58.

Funding stayed heavy, led by Mirendil's $1 billion Series A. In Washington, an AI task force recommended looser liability for companies that report AI-enabled cyber incidents in good faith.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet for Agent Purchases

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, which lets agents in its dots product buy things for users without exposing card numbers to the agent or merchant. Each purchase requires a biometric approval, and users keep their card rewards and eligible dispute protections. Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, the wallet is initially available only to US ChatGPT Pro subscribers.

White House AI Task Force Urges Liability Relief for Good-Faith Cyber Reporting

At its second meeting on Thursday, November 12, the White House AI task force recommended that federal agencies limit regulatory liability for good-faith voluntary reporting of cybersecurity vulnerabilities and incidents. The recommended relief would last at least 12 months, with possible extension. The group also backed long-term reauthorization of the 2015 Cybersecurity Information Sharing Act. These are recommendations to agencies, not binding rules.

Claude Fable 5.2 and Opus 5.5 Tie at 58 on Artificial Analysis Coding Index

Artificial Analysis released Coding Agent Index v2.1, which upgrades FrontierCode to 2.0 and Terminal-Bench to 4.1. Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Opus 5.5 share the lead at 58 points. The firm says average scores fell about 5 points from the prior version, and cost per task ranges from $1.51 to $44.22 among configurations scoring 48 or higher.

Mirendil Raises $1 Billion Series A for Agentic R&D

Mirendil, a developer of agentic AI for research and development, raised a $1 billion Series A led by Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital. Crunchbase ranked it first among the week's announced US funding rounds, ahead of Mercor's $750 million Series D. The roundup does not report a valuation for Mirendil in the portion verified.

OpenAI Unveils Daybreak Red Team and Defense Factory Security Tools

Greg Brockman announced Daybreak Defense Factory and Daybreak Red Team, two cyber products that OpenAI says make frontier cyber capabilities easier to use for defending companies. SecurityWeek reports that Red Team uses AI agents to find web application flaws and that Defense Factory helps develop fixes.
