🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Introduces ChatGPT Wallet for Agent Purchases

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet so its dots agents can complete purchases without receiving a user's actual card number. The user reviews the items, total, and selected card, then approves each purchase with a check such as Face ID. Payment networks issue a token, and American Express, Mastercard, and Visa are partners. Availability starts with ChatGPT Pro subscribers age 18 and older in the United States. OpenAI also published a commerce playbook for businesses.

DeepSeek Opens App Search, Files, and Voice in Its API

DeepSeek said the search, file, link, safety, and voice tools behind its app are now available through the DeepSeek API. V4.1-Flash, also called deepseek-flash, can use web search, page reading, file input, and safety checks inside Messages, Responses, and Chat Completions. Search, Fetch, OCR, ASR, and TTS are also standalone APIs. Public-facing apps must enable safety checks. DeepSeek published an open-source chat demo that can import conversation history.

Thinking Machines Details Native 4-bit Training of Inkling-2-Small

Thinking Machines published a November 13, 2026 account of training Inkling-2-Small with a native 4-bit forward pass from the first pretraining step through reinforcement-learning rollouts. The post says NVFP4 stores values in fewer bits than BF16 and is faster on NVIDIA GB300 systems, and it describes how the lab tried to keep quantization error from interfering with learning. NVIDIA linked an NVFP4 checkpoint the same day, and the model card says the weights are open.

DeepMind Releases PARDIG and SatWeave Weather Datasets

Google DeepMind on November 13, 2026 released PARDIG and SatWeave, datasets it says supported WeatherNext 3. SatWeave combines imagery from six geostationary satellites into an hourly global mosaic across 17 channels, with a live feed and a historical archive spanning more than a decade. PARDIG is an AI precipitation dataset used to train the model's precipitation forecasts. DeepMind is publishing historical archives and live feeds for outside research.

Mirendil Raises a Billion-Dollar Series A for AI Research Tools

Mirendil said in a November 12, 2026 release that it raised $1 billion in a Series A led by Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital, with Google among the participants. The company said the money will fund tools meant to let smaller teams run frontier AI research and development. It pointed to agent-built results including Mirendil-Base, a long-context model called Fern, and a private-beta system called Lor. The release states the amount raised, not a valuation.
