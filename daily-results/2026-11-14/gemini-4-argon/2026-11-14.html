🌐 TOP STORIES

NVIDIA Releases Open GR00T 2 System One Robotics Model for Cross-Embodiment Skills

NVIDIA unveiled GR00T 2, an open System One foundation model for robotics research designed to run in real time on onboard computers. The company says the model learns physical skills from human demonstrations, predicts how its actions will alter the physical world, and transfers learned skills across different robot bodies so developers do not have to train each robot on every task from scratch.

DeepSeek Brings Full App Capabilities and Built-In Tools to the DeepSeek API

DeepSeek expanded its API to expose the core capabilities behind the DeepSeek App, including server-side web search, deep-thinking multi-step search, file parsing, automatic URL reading, and safety checks on V4.1-Flash (deepseek-flash). Developers can enable these built-in tools across Messages, Responses, and Chat Completions or call standalone OCR, ASR, and TTS APIs, while public-facing applications are required to keep safety screening enabled.

OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet With American Express, Mastercard and Visa for Agent Purchases

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, a payment system built with American Express, Mastercard, and Visa that lets AI agents complete purchases without exposing a user's actual card details. Rolling out initially through dots for US ChatGPT Pro subscribers, the wallet requires biometric or device verification before each transaction, issues single-use network payment tokens, and preserves existing cardholder rewards and dispute protections.

AI Lab Mirendil Raises $1 Billion Series A to Scale Agent-Driven Frontier R&D

AI research lab Mirendil announced a $1 billion Series A round led by Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital, with participation from Google, Battery Ventures, Doerr Capital, and S32, without disclosing a valuation. The company stated that a single researcher working with AI agents pretrained its Mirendil-Base coding model in 3.5 months for under $10 million in compute before post-training its 10M-context Fern model and launching its Lor research platform in private beta.

Anthropic Publishes Ten Engineering Recommendations for Rigorous AI Safety Evaluations

Anthropic published a technical framework outlining ten engineering recommendations for designing, executing, and reporting AI model safety evaluations. Drawing parallels to replication reforms in psychology, the research notes that poorly designed evaluations are easily gamed or sensitive to prompt formatting, and details methods for measuring a model's propensity to cause harm so teams can make reproducible deployment and safeguard decisions.
