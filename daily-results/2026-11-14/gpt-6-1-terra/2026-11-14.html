🌐 TOP STORIES

DeepSeek Opens App Features to Developers Through Its API

DeepSeek has made features from its consumer app available to developers through its API, including search, file handling, safety checks and voice. It also offers standalone OCR, speech recognition and text-to-speech interfaces. The company says public-facing applications must include safety checks; extended tool calls have charges beyond token usage.

Thinking Machines Details Four-Bit Training for Inkling 2 Small

Thinking Machines described how it trained Inkling 2 Small using a native four-bit forward pass from pretraining through reinforcement-learning rollouts. The lab says its approach uses higher precision for backward passes to limit quantization bias. It reports an 8% pretraining and 20% reinforcement-learning wall-clock speedup against its full MXFP8 baseline in its largest jobs.

DeepMind Opens WeatherNext Data Sets for Researchers

Google DeepMind released historical archives and live feeds of PARDIG and SatWeave, two datasets used in WeatherNext 3. PARDIG provides AI-based precipitation data, while SatWeave combines geostationary satellite imagery into a global view. The release is intended to support researchers building weather, hydrology and climate applications.

Cognition Launches Private Devin Apps

Cognition introduced Devin Apps, private web applications built and hosted on Devin’s cloud computer. Team members access them through authenticated links, and administrators can control sharing. The company says the machines sleep when idle and wake when accessed; billing depends on awake time, and the product is intended for internal tools rather than high-traffic production services.

White House AI Task Force Recommends Cyber Reporting Relief

A White House task force urged agencies to limit regulatory liability for good-faith voluntary reporting of cyber vulnerabilities and incidents where lawful and practical. It recommended relief for at least 12 months, subject to possible extension. The recommendation is not itself a new law, and officials also discussed voluntary reporting of AI-related incidents.
