🌐 TOP STORIES

Jensen Huang Resigns as NVIDIA CEO Amid Scandal

Jensen Huang resigned as NVIDIA's President, CEO, and Board member effective immediately on November 12, following reports of extramarital affairs with an alleged Chinese spy and two company employees. CFO Colette Kress was named interim CEO. The departure of the 33-year NVIDIA veteran injects uncertainty into the AI chip supply chain, while Elon Musk publicly backed a petition to reinstate Huang.

Anthropic Pitches Record IPO at $1.4 Trillion Valuation

Dario Amodei appeared with Jamie Dimon at JPMorgan's Manhattan headquarters on November 13 to pitch Anthropic's IPO to Wall Street. The Claude developer expects to price its offering November 18 and begin trading November 19, seeking up to $90.4 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation. Amodei called compute the company's deepest business dilemma, highlighting the capital intensity of frontier AI development.

OpenAI Launches Daybreak Cybersecurity Agents

OpenAI introduced Daybreak Red Team and Daybreak Defense Factory at its Cyber at the Frontier keynote on November 12. Red Team runs automated penetration tests on web applications and confirms whether reported flaws are exploitable. Defense Factory, built on GPT-6 Cyber, scans codebases and delivers merge-ready pull requests for vulnerability fixes, marking OpenAI's first dedicated enterprise cybersecurity offering.

MiniMax Releases H3-Image as #1 Open-Weight Image Model

New developments: Independent benchmark results now confirm MiniMax's H3-Image, released November 10 as a 33.1B-parameter distilled diffusion transformer, ranks #1 among open-weight models on both the Artificial Analysis Image Editing v2.1 and Text-to-Image v2.1 leaderboards. The model generates and edits 4K-resolution images in six sampling steps, with a lower-cost Highspeed variant running roughly 1.3x to 1.4x faster.

DeepSeek Brings Full App Experience to API

DeepSeek announced that V4.1-Flash now supports built-in tools — search, file handling, link processing, safety filtering, and voice — across Messages, Responses, and Chat Completions endpoints. The extended capabilities incur tool usage fees in addition to standard token charges, bringing DeepSeek's API offering closer to parity with Western frontier labs.
