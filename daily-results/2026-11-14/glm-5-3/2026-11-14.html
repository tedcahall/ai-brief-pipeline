🌐 TOP STORIES

DeepSeek Opens Its Full App Experience to Developers Through Its API

DeepSeek announced that the capabilities behind its app, including web search, file input, link reading, safety and voice, are now available through the DeepSeek API. Developers can enable built-in features with a few parameters or call standalone OCR, ASR and TTS APIs, with tool usage fees charged beyond standard token pricing. V4.1-Flash supports the features across Messages, Responses and Chat Completions, and an open-source frontend demo was released.

Agentic AI Funding Peaks: Mirendil Raises $1 Billion Series A, Mercor $750 Million

Mirendil, a San Francisco developer of agentic AI for research and development, raised a $1 billion Series A led by Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital, Crunchbase News reported. Mercor followed with $750 million in Series D funding at a $20 billion valuation for its platform connecting AI developers with human experts, and LangChain closed a $300 million Series C at a $3 billion valuation led by CapitalG. The amounts are capital raised, separate from valuations.

White House AI Task Force Urges 12-Month Liability Relief for Good-Faith Cyber Reporting

After its second meeting, the White House AI task force said federal agencies should limit regulatory liability for good-faith voluntary reporting of cybersecurity vulnerabilities and incidents for at least 12 months, arguing prior enforcement standards may be outdated as AI raises cyber risk. The NSA and NIST are testing frontier models with major labs, and the task force backs CISA's forthcoming incident-reporting rule while urging minimal duplication.

Cohere Deepens Japan Sovereign AI Commitment With Local Entity and In-Region Inference

Cohere announced plans for a Japanese legal entity and expanded sovereign AI investments, including a Fujitsu partnership on Takane, a Japanese-market LLM selected for the Digital Agency's GENAI evaluation, and Model Vault in-region inference backed by local compute. Japan is one of Cohere's fastest-growing markets; the company separately cited its Series E targeting up to CAD $7 billion at a CAD $35.7 billion valuation.

FAA Rules Out SMART AI System as Cause of Nationwide Ground Stop

The FAA's first nationwide ground stop since January 2023 halted departing flights for more than four hours before being lifted Friday evening. Officials attributed the disruption to overloaded switches and routers in the agency's aging analog telecom infrastructure. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said the SMART AI recommendation system is not connected to STARS or ERAM, and investigators reported no evidence of a cyberattack or AI-related interference.
