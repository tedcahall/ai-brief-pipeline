📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

NVIDIA has released weights for a 30-billion-parameter image generator that activates 3 billion parameters per token, while fresh benchmarks put Amazon's Trainium3 and Google's TPU against NVIDIA systems on agentic inference cost. The results are vendor or benchmark-provider claims, but they show competition shifting toward usable visual workflows and workload-specific economics.

Japan is considering binding safety duties and penalties for advanced AI providers, and Canada is expanding its compute-access fund by C$700 million. Separately, the Bank of England says hyperscalers' more than $4 trillion of debt and off-balance-sheet commitments could transmit stress if expected AI revenue weakens.

🌐 TOP STORIES

NVIDIA Releases Open Weights for 30B-A3B Image Model

NVIDIA has published Nemotron-Labs-Image-30B-A3B, an autoregressive image model with 30 billion total parameters and 3 billion active per token. It is the raster generator for the Pixel as Code visual-creation framework, which also produces editable webpages, slides and charts. The initial release includes weights, tokenizer and inference code under a noncommercial license; the broader harness and VisionCode models remain pending legal clearance.

Trainium3 Claims 2.28x Agentic-Inference Cost Advantage

SemiAnalysis says Amazon's first Trainium3 UltraServer submission to its Agentic Inference Benchmark delivered 2.28 times better performance per dollar than NVIDIA's GB300 NVL72, B300 and AMD's MI355. A follow-up also put Trainium and Google TPU on InferenceX and AgentX. These are SemiAnalysis benchmark results for particular agentic workloads, not evidence of a universal advantage across AI inference.

Japan Weighs Binding Safety Duties for Advanced AI Providers

Japan is reviewing AI Act revisions that would require qualifying advanced-AI providers to implement safeguards, report exploitable flaws and verify certain users of cyber-capable systems. The proposal would escalate from recommendations to corrective orders and penalties, with the specific punishments still undecided. The government aims to present revisions to an expert panel this year and submit a bill next year.

Bank of England Flags More Than $4 Trillion in AI Exposure

The Bank of England says hyperscalers' financial exposure has surpassed $4 trillion through debt and off-balance-sheet commitments, creating increasingly complex and circular links across markets. Its financial stability report warns that these arrangements could come under strain if AI revenue expectations fall. Bloomberg data cited in the report coverage puts hyperscaler bond issuance this year above $300 billion across nine currencies.

Canada Adds C$700 Million to AI Compute Access Fund

Canada is adding C$700 million to its AI Compute Access Fund, bringing total support to C$1 billion and opening a health and life-sciences call. The program offsets compute costs and gives stronger incentives for infrastructure owned, operated and governed in Canada. Minister Evan Solomon also announced more than C$52 million for 45 companies under the fund's initial phase, separate from the expansion.
