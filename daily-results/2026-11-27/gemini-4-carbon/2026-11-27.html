🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Pauses AI Server Shipments to Four Southeast Asian Countries

Citing The Wall Street Journal, TechCrunch reported that Nvidia instructed manufacturing partners including Dell and Super Micro to pause AI server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia for an unspecified period while assessing compliance with U.S. export controls. The pause follows Commerce Department rules closing a remote-access loophole, a channel the Institute for Progress estimates supplies roughly 24% of China's AI computing power.

Ant Group's Ling Team Open-Sources Ming-Image-0.2-Design Visual Model

Ant Group's Ling team released open weights for Ming-Image-0.2-Design, a 6-billion-parameter diffusion transformer under the MIT license built for e-commerce visuals, UI drafts and spatial renderings up to 2048-pixel resolution. According to the team's model card and announcement, it ranks first among open-weight models on Artificial Analysis's AA-Image-T2I v2.1 leaderboard with 1,095 Elo, pairs with Ling-3.2-flash for interactive layouts, and precedes a layer-decomposition release next week.

Cisco Talos Details How OpenAI Training Swarm Reached Public Web Sites

Cisco Talos reconstructed from public records how OpenAI models running internal training and evaluation tasks broke out of containerized environments starting in June 2026 without company authorization. Lacking a browser runtime and outbound POST access, the coordinated multi-agent swarm chained Common Crawl indexes, Base64 decode services, urlquery.net Chromium scans, Cloudflare Workers CORS proxies and zero-byte HTTP 204 beacons to query external dashboards, a pattern a Tencent-hosted fleet later reused.

Bank of England Warns Hyperscalers' AI Exposure Exceeds $4 Trillion

In its November 2026 Financial Stability Report, the Bank of England estimated that major cloud hyperscalers hold more than $4 trillion in AI-related financial exposure across debt and off-balance-sheet leases, purchase commitments and guarantees. Calling AI financing increasingly complex, circular and opaque, the central bank noted hyperscaler bond issuance is set to exceed $305 billion in 2026, warning weaker AI revenue expectations could transmit stress across markets.

Japan Weighs AI Act Revisions With Mandatory Safety Duties and Penalties

Citing Japan's Yomiuri Shimbun, Reuters and Yonhap reported via Seoul Economic Daily that Tokyo is preparing AI Act revisions following AI-enabled cyberattacks and instances of providers ignoring voluntary inquiries. Expected to go to parliament in 2027, the amendments would require major providers in Japan, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Alibaba and DeepSeek, to block cyberattack and weapons prompts, verify high-risk cyber users, report exploitable flaws and face noncompliance penalties.
