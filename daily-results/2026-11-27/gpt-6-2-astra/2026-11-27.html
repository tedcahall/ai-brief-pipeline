🌐 TOP STORIES

Ant Ling Opens Its Ming Image Design Model, With Layer Editing Still to Come

Ant Ling released the weights for Ming-Image-0.2-Design and put a browser demo on Hugging Face for tasks such as product mockups and interior renderings. A separate model splits an image into editable layers. Ant showed a character separated into 35 parts, but said the weights for that model would follow next week.

Tencent Releases DFly2 to Speed Up Hy4 Flash

Tencent released a companion model that predicts upcoming tokens for Hy4 flash to check. DFly2 reuses the main model’s cache rather than allocating a second one as the conversation grows. Tencent reports up to a 4.2-fold speedup on long-context and agent workloads; that peak is not a guarantee for every prompt.

MAGI-2 Ties for First in Image-to-Video Testing at $4.80 a Minute

Artificial Analysis placed Sand AI’s MAGI-2 in a statistical tie with Dreamina Seedance 2.5 on its image-to-video leaderboard. At the prices the evaluator cited, a minute of MAGI-2 output costs $4.80 versus $34.12 for Seedance. The result depends on the input: when starting from text alone, MAGI-2 ranked seventh.

Moonshot Offers Kimi API Credits to Scientific Research Teams

Moonshot opened applications worldwide for free Kimi API credits and access to its latest models. The offer covers selected projects in fundamental science and engineering, including theoretical and experimental work. Researchers from doctoral students to principal investigators can apply; the first round closes December 8.

Canada Adds C$700 Million to Its AI Compute Access Fund

Canada expanded its AI Compute Access Fund to C$1 billion and opened a call for health and life sciences projects, Unite.AI reports. The program helps pay for training, fine-tuning and inference. It can cover up to 80% of eligible costs at Canadian-owned and controlled providers, compared with 50% at other qualifying Canadian-based providers.
