🌐 TOP STORIES

Amazon's Trainium3 UltraServer Tops NVIDIA's GB300 on Cost in Agentic Benchmark

Amazon submitted its first Trainium3 UltraServer results to SemiAnalysis's AgentX agentic inference benchmark, showing up to 2.28x better performance per dollar than NVIDIA's GB300 NVL72, B300 and AMD's MI355. SemiAnalysis attributed the result to a wide expert-parallel approach, with hourly costs near 45% of GB300 rates. The figures come from Amazon's submission and SemiAnalysis's own benchmark, not an independent third-party test.

Sand AI Launches MAGI-2, a 480B-Parameter Video Generation Model

Sand AI released MAGI-2, a 480B-parameter (28B active) Multi-Head Latent MoE video model that generates up to 30 seconds of 1080p, 25 fps video with synchronized audio. Artificial Analysis ranks it first in image-to-video, statistically tied with Dreamina Seedance 2.5, at $0.08 per second or $4.80 per minute, roughly 7x cheaper than Seedance. It is available through Higgsfield, VidMuse and the Sand AI Open Platform under a proprietary API, with Sand AI stating it plans to follow with open-source releases.

Moonshot AI Offers Free Kimi API Credits to Scientific Researchers

Moonshot AI opened its Kimi for Science program, offering selected research teams worldwide free Kimi API credits worth up to $20,000, roughly 7 billion tokens, plus access to its latest models and collaboration opportunities. The first-round application deadline is December 8, with further rounds promised. Applicants describe their research questions, how Kimi could help, and how they will validate results.

Tencent Open-Sources DFly2, the Speculative Decoder Behind Hy4 Flash

Tencent open-sourced DFly2, the speculative-decoding drafter for its Hy4 flash model, claiming up to 4.2x speedup on long-context and agentic workloads. The drafter shares the target model's sparse KV cache and top-k token selection, keeping drafting cost flat by context length with no extra KV memory. Tencent says DFly2 is about 30% faster than a same-recipe DSpark drafter on agentic tasks and accepts more draft tokens.

Mistral Says Large 4 'Le Chonk' Beats Closed Models on Visual Grounding

Mistral demonstrated its Large 4 model, in a "Le Chonk" configuration, locating and outlining the exact match within an image on visual-grounding tasks, which the company says beats closed frontier models. The claim is specific to visual grounding: an independent evaluation aggregator ranked Mistral Large 4 51st of 73 on broader vision benchmarks, so the advantage does not extend to general vision capability.
