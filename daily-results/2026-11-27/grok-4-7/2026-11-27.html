🌐 TOP STORIES

Artificial Analysis Ranks Sand AI's MAGI-2 First in Image-to-Video

Artificial Analysis said on November 26 that Sand AI's MAGI-2 debuted at number one on its image-to-video board, statistically tied with Dreamina Seedance 2.5. It listed the model at $0.08 per second, about seven times below Seedance 2.5's listed rate, and seventh in text-to-video. The firm described a 480-billion-parameter, 28-billion-active model released November 24. The ranking is Artificial Analysis's measurement, not an independent replication.

Ant Ling Open-Sources Ming-Image-0.2-Design

Ant Ling said on November 26 that it was open-sourcing Ming-Image-0.2-Design and would open-source the Layer variant the next week. It said Design ranks first among open-weight models on Artificial Analysis's text-to-image board at 1,095 Elo. The Hugging Face model card lists 6 billion DiT parameters and a November 25 snapshot of that open-weight lead. The Elo score is the lab's citation of Artificial Analysis, not a result replicated for this brief.

Tencent Open-Sources DFly2 Drafter for Hy4 Flash

Tencent Hunyuan said on November 26 that it was open-sourcing DFly2, the speculative-decoding drafter for Hy4 flash. The lab said DFly2 reached up to a 4.2x speedup on long-context and agentic workloads, reuses the target model's KV cache, and adds no extra KV memory. It also said agentic tasks ran about 30 percent faster than a same-recipe DSpark drafter. Those speed figures are Tencent's tests.

Canada Adds $700 Million to Its AI Compute Access Fund

On November 26, 2026, in Toronto, Minister Evan Solomon announced a $700 million expansion of Canada's AI Compute Access Fund, bringing it to $1 billion. The release opens a health and life sciences call and says the money will offset compute costs for Canadian companies. It also announced more than $52 million for 45 companies. The amounts are Canadian government figures, not a private financing round.

SemiAnalysis Reports Trainium3 Ahead of GB300 on AgentX Cost

SemiAnalysis said on November 26 that Amazon's Trainium3 UltraServer submission to its AgentX benchmark showed 2.28x better performance per dollar than Nvidia's GB300 NVL72. OfficeChai, citing the same results, said SemiAnalysis called the figures an official preview that may change, and that the run used Z.ai's GLM-5.2/GLM-5.3 on vLLM in FP4. This is SemiAnalysis's benchmark and cost model, not an Nvidia-confirmed measurement and not a public cloud list price.
