🌐 TOP STORIES

Ant Ling releases open-weight Ming image model

Ant Ling released Ming-Image-0.2-Design, a 6-billion-parameter text-to-image model aimed at visual design. Its model card reports a top open-weight position on the Artificial Analysis text-to-image leaderboard as of November 25. A separate layer-decomposition model was announced for release the following week, not as part of this download.

Tencent opens DFly2 inference accelerator

Tencent says it has open-sourced DFly2, a speculative decoding component for Hy4 flash. The company reports up to 4.2 times faster generation in a specified long-context benchmark; results depend on the workload and configuration. The approach reuses the main model’s cache and sparse index to reduce draft-model overhead.

Canada adds C$700 million to AI compute fund

Canada announced a C$700 million expansion of its AI Compute Access Fund, bringing the total to C$1 billion. The first new sector-specific call targets health and life sciences. Separately, the government announced more than C$52 million for 45 companies from the initial phase of the fund.

Moonshot offers Kimi API credits to researchers

Moonshot AI opened applications for Kimi for Science, offering selected research teams API credits, model access and potential collaboration. RuntimeWire reports that selected projects can receive up to $20,000 in credits, which are not cash grants. The first application round closes December 8, according to the announcement.

OpenAI corrects GPT-6.2 usage accounting

OpenAI corrected an error that had discounted GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage for most users. Its Thibault Sottiaux said users would reach limits 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol compared with the previous week. OpenAI said it would not charge retroactively and would add a banked reset for eligible plans.
