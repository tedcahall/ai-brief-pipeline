📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Custom cloud silicon challenged Nvidia's inference moat on public benchmarks this week as Amazon's Trainium3 UltraServer and Google's TPUv7 beat Nvidia's GB300 NVL72 on performance per dollar in SemiAnalysis's AgentX suite. Meanwhile, OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra claimed the top spot on the GDPx agentic leaderboard at 19.3%, though independent analysis showed its lead relies heavily on alignment tasks while trailing Claude Fable 5.2 in software engineering.

Financial and geopolitical pressures around AI infrastructure also sharpened. 🌐 TOP STORIES

Amazon Trainium3 and Google TPU Beat Nvidia GPUs on SemiAnalysis AgentX

SemiAnalysis added Amazon's Trainium and Google's TPU to its InferenceX and AgentX benchmarks, seven months after Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang predicted neither would test publicly. Amazon's preview Trainium3 UltraServer delivered up to 2.28x better performance per dollar than Nvidia's GB300 NVL72 and B300 on Z.ai's 744B GLM-5.2/5.3 model in FP4 precision, while Google's TPUv7 also outperformed GB300 NVL72 across most of the curve.

GPT-6.2 Astra Tops SemiAnalysis GDPx Leaderboard With 19.3% Overall Score

New developments: SemiAnalysis added OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra to its 206-task GDPx agentic benchmark on November 26, where it debuted first at 19.3% ahead of Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 at 16.4% and Gemini 4 Neon at 10.1%. An independent domain recount by i10X found Astra's 2.9-point lead narrows to 0.3 points outside alignment tasks, where Astra scored 37.6%, and Astra trailed Fable 5.2 in software engineering.

Bank of England Warns Hyperscalers' $4 Trillion AI Exposure Amplifies Market Risks

The Bank of England warned in its November 26 financial stability report that hyperscalers' AI-related financial exposure now exceeds $4 trillion, heightening systemic risks if future AI revenue expectations fall short. The central bank noted hyperscalers raised over $300 billion in bond debt this year alongside off-balance-sheet deals such as SpaceX's $32 billion debt package, while Nvidia's supply-chain backstopping deepens circular financing links.

Nvidia GPU Distributors Warn Neoclouds Against Renting Capacity to Chinese Firms

GPU distributors have warned specialized neocloud providers that Nvidia chip supplies will be withheld if any rented compute capacity goes to Chinese firms, according to insiders cited on November 26. The warning follows a U.S. Bureau of Industry and Security interim final rule that took effect November 19 requiring licenses for China-linked remote access to advanced U.S. AI chips, redirecting Asian capacity toward Western buyers.

Shadowserver Finds 4,200 Exposed AI Hacking Servers Actively Probing Networks

Shadowserver Foundation scans identified roughly 4,200 internet-exposed instances of agentic AI attack frameworks on November 23, up from nearly 3,700 a day earlier, with ARTEX accounting for about 70% and its fork AntiFox reaching around 200 instances. Between November 9 and 23, Shadowserver honeypots logged over 10,000 events from more than 80 IPs targeting VPN gateways, Jenkins, and .env secrets.
