🌐 TOP STORIES

Amazon's Trainium3 Beats Nvidia's GB300 on Cost in SemiAnalysis AgentX Benchmark

SemiAnalysis reported November 25 that Amazon's first Trainium3 UltraServer submission to its AgentX agentic inference benchmark showed up to 2.28x better performance per dollar than Nvidia's GB300 NVL72, B300 and AMD's MI355X, crediting Amazon's wide expert-parallelism design. The benchmark replays 393 real Claude Code sessions running Z.ai's GLM-5.3 on vLLM. Nvidia countered that its Vera Rubin NVL72 delivers 3.3x Trainium3's tokens per dollar; the results are an official preview.

Bank of England Warns AI Financing Is Deepening Global Market Risk

In its Financial Stability Report published Thursday, the Bank of England said hyperscalers' financial exposure to AI has exceeded $4 trillion and that its rapid, increasingly complex, circular and opaque financing is deepening interconnections across the global financial system. Hyperscalers raised more than $300 billion of bond debt across nine currencies this year, per Bloomberg data cited in the report, and some private credit funds are growing more selective over AI deals.

Ant Group Open-Sources Ming-Image-0.2-Design, Now the Top Open-Weight Image Model

Ant Group's Ant Ling team (inclusionAI) released Ming-Image-0.2-Design, a 6B-parameter text-to-image model built for design work, with weights on Hugging Face under an MIT license. It ranks first among open-weight models on Artificial Analysis's AA-Image-T2I v2.1 board at 1,095 Elo, placing 24th overall as the only open-weight entry in the top 25. A layer-editing companion, Ming-Image-0.2-Design-Layer, is announced for open-sourcing next week.

Japan Weighs Safety Duties and Penalties for AI Providers

Japan is preparing revisions to its AI Act that would require providers of advanced AI services with a certain scale of Japanese users, likely including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Alibaba and DeepSeek, to block responses about cyberattack methods and harmful substances, prevent likely malfunctions and verify the identity of users with strong cyber capabilities. A new enforcement ladder moves from recommendations to corrective orders to penalties, with a bill targeted for next year's ordinary parliamentary session.

Moonshot Opens Kimi for Science With Free API Credits for Researchers

Moonshot AI opened applications on November 27 for Kimi for Science, offering selected research teams up to 150,000 yuan (about $20,000, roughly 7 billion tokens) in free Kimi API credits, access to its latest models and long-term collaboration opportunities. The first round welcomes theoretical and experimental work in fundamental science and engineering at all career stages, including PhD students, postdocs and PIs, with applications due December 8.
