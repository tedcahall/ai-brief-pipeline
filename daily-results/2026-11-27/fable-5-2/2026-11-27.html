🌐 TOP STORIES

Stanford Physicist Proposes Quantum Gravity Theory Built With GPT-6 Astra

New developments: Stanford physicist Venkatesa Chandrasekaran posted an arXiv preprint on November 25 proposing a background-independent quantization of vacuum general relativity in four dimensions. He said several calculations and proofs came from months of work with OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, using about 400 million tokens, with computing resources provided by OpenAI. The Quantum Insider notes the paper is theoretical and presents no experimental test.

Anthropic Researchers Use Claude to Cut Riemann Hypothesis Proof to 76,000 Lines

Anthropic researcher Luke Sernau published grh-lean, a Lean 4 proof of the Riemann Hypothesis and its generalization for Dirichlet L-functions in about 76,000 lines, a 99.56% cut from the 17.4 million lines Levent Alpöge released on November 20. The README says it was produced with Anthropic colleagues and Claude, has passed machine checks only, and that no mathematician has read it through.

GPT-6.2 Astra Tops SemiAnalysis GDPx Benchmark as Gemini 4 Neon Ranks Sixth

New developments: SemiAnalysis updated its 206-task GDPx benchmark for this month's model releases. OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra leads at 19.3%, Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 is second at 16.4%, and Google's Gemini 4 Carbon and Neon rank fifth and sixth at 10.9% and 10.1%. The firm says Astra trails Fable on software engineering, 13.3% to 24.4%, and that safety classifiers lower some scores in cyber, biology and alignment.

Bank of England Says Hyperscaler AI Exposure Tops $4 Trillion

In its financial stability report on November 26, the Bank of England said AI financing is expanding in a form that is increasingly complex, circular and opaque, deepening links across the global financial system. Bloomberg reported hyperscalers' financial exposure has grown past $4 trillion, with more than $300 billion of bonds raised this year. The bank said debt and off-balance-sheet commitments could come under strain if AI revenue expectations fall.

Ant Group Open-Sources Ming-Image-0.2-Design Under MIT License

Ant Group's Ling team released Ming-Image-0.2-Design, a 6 billion parameter text-to-image model for design work, with weights on Hugging Face under an MIT license. i10x reports it scores 1,095 Elo on Artificial Analysis's text-to-image board, the top open-weight entry and 24th overall. The minimum validated setup is a GPU with at least 80 GiB of memory, and a companion layer-splitting model is planned for next week.
