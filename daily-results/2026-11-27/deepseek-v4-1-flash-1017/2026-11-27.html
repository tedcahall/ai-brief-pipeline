🌐 TOP STORIES

Swiss Cyber Agency Confirms First AI-Agent Attacks on Critical Infrastructure

Switzerland's National Cyber Security Centre said in a November 24 advisory that it had received its first reports of cyberattacks on Swiss organisations carried out with the assistance of an AI agent, and that in all cases known to it the AI agent led to a successful compromise of the victim's IT systems. Organisations and critical-infrastructure operators across at least two sectors were affected. The NCSC described agentic offensive frameworks that search for vulnerabilities, select tools, evaluate results and plan next steps, and said the number of internet-accessible agents such as ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI has almost doubled since October 2026.

Sber Releases 708B GigaChat 4 Open Weights With a 1M-Token Window

Sber published two open-weight GigaChat 4 models on November 26 at its AI Journey conference in Moscow: a 708-billion-parameter flagship with about 41 billion parameters active per token, and a 37-billion-parameter sibling, both with a 1,048,576-token context window. The flagship uses Gated DeltaNet linear attention in 48 of 64 layers plus DeepSeek Sparse Attention, and ships under the MIT licence. In Sber's own table the 708B scores 35.40% on DeepSWE against 74.34% for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, and independent results are not yet available.

Japan Drafts AI Act Amendment With Safety Duties and Penalties for Frontier Providers

Japan's Cabinet Office is preparing an amendment to the AI Promotion Act that would require frontier AI providers to adopt safety measures such as refusing attack-related queries and to report accidents and dangerous defects, according to Yomiuri reporting relayed on November 26. Enforcement would escalate from a recommendation to a corrective order, with penalties only for violating an order. Duties would fall mainly on upstream model providers, and foreign providers would be covered when their services are used in Japan at a certain scale. Nothing has been enacted; a bill is targeted for the 2027 ordinary Diet session.

Amazon Trainium3 UltraServer Posts 2.28x Better Performance per Dollar Than GB300

SemiAnalysis said Amazon submitted its first Trainium3 UltraServer results to its AgentX agentic inference benchmark, showing up to 2.28 times better performance per dollar than Nvidia's GB300 NVL72, B300 and AMD's MI355X on GLM-5.2 and GLM-5.3 744B workloads. The firm attributed the result to a wide expert-parallel approach and said Google TPU and Trainium now both appear on InferenceX at 1.2x to 2.5x the performance per dollar of Nvidia GPUs. These are vendor-submitted results to a third-party benchmark, not independent replication.

DeepL Launches Aure v2 With End-to-End Speech Translation

DeepL launched Aure v2 on November 26 at its Unmute Summit in Tokyo, an end-to-end speech-to-speech translation model that skips separate transcription, text translation and speech generation stages. DeepL reports a 50% reduction in median translation delay from the original Aure, and says its internal November 2026 study of 10 language pairs found 32% fewer English to Japanese errors than Google Gemini 3.5 Live Translate. Aure v2 ships in six languages across DeepL Voice for Online Meetings, In-Person Conversations and the Voice API.
