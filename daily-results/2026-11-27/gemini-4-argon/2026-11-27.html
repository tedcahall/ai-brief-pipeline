🌐 TOP STORIES

Ant Group Open-Sources Ming-Image-0.2-Design for Visual and UI Generation

Ant Group's Ant Ling team open-sourced Ming-Image-0.2-Design, a 6-billion-parameter diffusion transformer built for e-commerce hero images, UI design mockups, and spatial renderings. The release improves complex composition, lighting, and text rendering, and pairs with Ling-3.2-flash to turn visual drafts into interactive web pages. According to Ant Ling, the model ranks first among open-weight image models on Artificial Analysis's AA-Image-T2I v2.1 leaderboard with a 1,095 Elo score, with a companion layer-decomposition model due next week.

Tencent Open-Sources DFly2 Speculative Decoding Drafter for Sparse Frontier Models

Tencent Hunyuan open-sourced DFly2 and its AngelSpec PyTorch training framework to accelerate long-context speculative decoding on sparse models. By sharing the target model's sparse KV cache and top-k token selection, DFly2 keeps drafting cost flat across context lengths without extra KV memory. Tencent reports up to 4.2x decoding speedups on long-context and agentic workloads for Hy4 flash, alongside released MoE drafter weights for Hy4 flash, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, and GLM-5.3-Flash.

Canada Expands Sovereign AI Compute Access Fund by $700 Million to $1 Billion

Canadian AI and Digital Innovation Minister Evan Solomon announced a CA$700 million expansion of Ottawa's AI Compute Access Fund, lifting total program funding to CA$1 billion and opening a priority call for health and life sciences projects. The fund offsets up to 80% of eligible cloud-compute costs at Canadian-owned sovereign data centers and up to 50% at other domestic providers. Ottawa also awarded over CA$52 million to 45 companies under the fund's initial phase.

Japan Weighs AI Act Revisions With Penalties for Unsafe Model Outputs

Citing the Yomiuri Shimbun, Reuters and Yonhap report that Japan is preparing revisions to its AI Act that would impose mandatory safety duties on major global AI providers, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Alibaba, and DeepSeek. Following rising AI-assisted cyberattacks, the proposed rules would require providers above a user threshold to block instructions for cyberattacks, bombs, and hazardous substances, and to verify user identities on high-capability systems, backed by corrective orders and penalties.

Bank of England Warns Hyperscalers' $4 Trillion AI Exposure Amplifies Market Risks

In its latest financial stability report, the Bank of England warned that hyperscalers' AI-related financial exposure has exceeded $4 trillion, deepening interconnections across global capital markets. According to Bloomberg data cited in the report, hyperscalers have issued more than $300 billion in bonds across nine currencies in 2026, alongside off-balance-sheet financing such as SpaceX's $32 billion chip-debt deal. The central bank cautioned that complex, circular AI financing could face strain if revenue expectations fall.
