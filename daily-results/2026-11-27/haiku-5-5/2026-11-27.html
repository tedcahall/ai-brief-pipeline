🌐 TOP STORIES

GPT-6.2 Astra Tops SemiAnalysis GDPx Leaderboard at 19.3%

New developments: SemiAnalysis's November 26 GDPx leaderboard debuts GPT-6.2 Astra first at 19.3%, ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 at 16.4%. GDPx launched November 1 with 206 tasks built with 14 data partners. SemiAnalysis says safety classifiers have sandbagged the overall scores of Astra and Fable 5.2.

Mistral Large 4 Claims Visual-Grounding Lead Over Closed Models

Mistral says its open-weight Large 4 scores 42% on Dense200 against 41% for GPT-6 Astra, claiming an edge over closed frontier models on visual grounding. The company-reported figures have not been independently verified, and Roboflow Vision Evals places the model mid-tier. The public preview opened October 6 and open weights were released October 29.

Moonshot Opens Kimi for Science Free API Credit Program

Moonshot says it is offering free Kimi API credits, access to its latest models and collaboration opportunities to selected scientific research teams worldwide. The first-round application deadline is December 8. Third-party reports put grants at up to $20,000, about 7 billion tokens, a figure that does not appear in Moonshot's own post.

Japan Weighs Safety Duties and Penalties for AI Firms

Yomiuri Shimbun reporting, carried by Reuters-Yonhap, says Japan is revising its AI Act to require safety measures from large providers, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Alibaba and DeepSeek. Proposed duties include blocking cyberattack and harmful-substance answers, reporting exploitable flaws and verifying users of strong cyber-capable systems. Penalty details are still to be worked out, and the bill is targeted for next year's session.

Canada Expands AI Compute Access Fund by $700 Million

Canada's November 26 expansion adds $700 million to the AI Compute Access Fund, bringing it to $1 billion. Eligible proposals can receive up to 80% of eligible cloud-compute costs from sovereign providers and up to 50% from Canadian-based providers. A health and life-sciences call takes statements of interest until December 17.
