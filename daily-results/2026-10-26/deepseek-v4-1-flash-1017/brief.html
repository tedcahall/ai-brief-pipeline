🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic files S-1 for Nasdaq IPO under ticker ANT

Anthropic filed a Form S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission for a proposed initial public offering of Class A common stock, to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, with share count and price range undetermined. The filing reports $16.3 billion of first-half revenue against an $80.7 billion net loss driven largely by noncash convertible-debt accounting, and a founder LLC holding 50.1 percent of voting power.

SpaceX locks in $40 billion GPU lease with Apollo for Vera Rubin chips

SpaceX reached a binding commitment for an approximately $40 billion master finance lease for GPUs and related equipment with an entity managed by Apollo Global Management, which Apollo called the largest committed GPU financing. The lease will deliver NVIDIA Vera Rubin chips for SpaceX's terrestrial compute buildout, with closing expected before year-end and lease schedules starting in the first half of 2027 as equipment arrives.

Nanbeige publishes Nanbeige4.3-33B, a 33B Apache 2.0 agent model with 512K context

Nanbeige LLM Lab, the research team inside Chinese recruitment platform BOSS Zhipin, published Nanbeige4.3-33B on Hugging Face on October 24 under Apache 2.0, a 33.1-billion-parameter dense model with a 524,288-token context window built for agentic coding and office-style tasks, alongside a base checkpoint. The model card reports 80.9 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 65.5 on DeepSWE, figures the lab has not had independently reproduced.

Musk says Grok Bot can now phone businesses and summarize the call

Elon Musk said Grok Bot can now call a person or a business on a user's behalf, make a request, take notes on the response and send back a summary of the conversation. xAI had not published which subscription plans or countries include the capability, and the official bot account had not posted rollout details when Musk announced it on October 25.

SoftBank's Son picks Malaysian sites for about $50 billion in AI investment

Malaysia's Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim said Masayoshi Son chose Kuching in Sarawak and peninsular Malaysia for about $50 billion of investment in artificial intelligence, new technology and data centers after a videoconference last week. SoftBank representatives did not respond to a request for comment. Malaysia recorded 385.7 billion ringgit, about $94 billion, of data-center-related investment from 2021 through the first half of 2026.
