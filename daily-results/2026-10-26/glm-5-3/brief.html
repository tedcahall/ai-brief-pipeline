📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Anthropic filed for its initial public offering on Monday, disclosing $16.3 billion in first-half 2026 revenue, up from $1.2 billion a year earlier, alongside an $80.7 billion net loss driven largely by noncash convertible-debt adjustments. The filing also details a Founder LLC through which seven co-founders would hold 50.1 percent of shareholder voting power.

AI infrastructure financing diverged sharply. 🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic files for IPO with $16.3 billion in first-half revenue

Anthropic filed its S-1 on Monday and plans to list under the ticker ANT. The filing reports $16.3 billion in first-half 2026 revenue, up from $1.2 billion a year earlier, an operating loss of $693 million, and an $80.7 billion net loss driven largely by noncash convertible-debt adjustments. Seven co-founders would control 50.1 percent of shareholder voting power through a Founder LLC.

SpaceX signs roughly $40 billion GPU lease financing led by Apollo

SpaceX disclosed a binding commitment for an approximately $40 billion master finance lease for GPUs and related equipment, which it calls the largest committed GPU financing to date. An Apollo-managed entity will buy the chips and lease them to SpaceX, delivering NVIDIA's next-generation Vera Rubin chips. The company expects the deal to close by year-end, with lease schedules starting in the first half of 2027.

Meta plans up to $95 billion in stock sales to fund AI spending

Meta said it plans to raise $95 billion to support its AI infrastructure and personal superintelligence work, in two parts: a $35 billion underwritten public offering and a $60 billion at-the-market program selling new nonvoting class C stock. Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom indicated interest in buying up to a combined $5 billion as cornerstone investors.

Apollo economist says AI has delivered zero payoff outside tech

Apollo Global Management's chief economist Torsten Slok says AI has delivered zero financial payoff to corporate America outside the tech sector. Focusing on what he calls the S&P 493, the S&P 500 minus seven tech giants, he finds profit margins and revenue per worker flat, with no evidence AI is improving productivity. He argues it will be hard for companies to turn increasingly cheap intelligence into higher revenue growth and margins.

Nanbeige open-sources Nanbeige4.3-33B with a 512K context window

Chinese lab Nanbeige posted Nanbeige4.3-33B on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0, a 33-billion-parameter dense model with a 524,288-token context window and reasoning enabled by default. The lab calls it the flagship teacher model for the Nanbeige4.3 series and reports 80.9 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 versus 73.0 for Qwen3.8-27B. All benchmark figures are self-reported, with no independent reproduction attached to the release.
