🌐 TOP STORIES

Inside China’s 800MW Galaxy AI Campus: 120MW Built in a Year

SemiAnalysis toured Envision’s Galaxy campus in Ulanqab, Inner Mongolia, in September. Planned for 800MW of IT capacity, its first 120MW phase was delivered within a year at a reported one-fifth of US costs. The site pairs dedicated wind generation and batteries with a live 5MW solid-state-transformer 800V DC pilot and chiller-free shared water loop.

OpenAI Clarifies ChatGPT Pro Usage: 10x, 20x and 50x Plus

OpenAI’s Tibo Sottiaux said Pro 100, Pro 200 and Pro 500 provide 10x, 20x and 50x Plus usage, respectively, with no five-hour limits and Ultrafast on Pro 500. The statement clarifies relative allowances rather than publishing absolute token or request totals; weekly quotas and separate product counters still matter.

Judge Lets Key Claude Max Usage-Advertising Claims Proceed

A California magistrate judge allowed negligent-misrepresentation and breach-of-contract claims over Claude Max’s “5x or 20x more usage” marketing to proceed, finding the advertising plausibly misleading because the session disclosure sat two links from sales pages. Several other claims were dismissed. The ruling permits the case to continue; it does not establish Anthropic’s liability.

MCPHallu Finds Agents Rarely Recognize Impossible Goals

Researchers evaluated 14 frontier agents from eight vendors across 358 tasks on 36 production MCP servers and 5,012 trajectories. The average score was 0.642, but only 23.2% of unreachable-goal runs passed, with agents often continuing tool calls or claiming success. Model rankings shifted sharply by failure subtype, arguing for workload-specific evaluation.

US AI Safety Center’s White House Reporting Raises Independence Concerns

AP reports that CAISSI staff were asked to informally report to a White House office, citing two former government officials. The Commerce Department center lacks a permanent leader and has stopped publishing its research. Critics warned about political influence over safety findings, while supporters said closer White House ties could bring prominence and funding.
