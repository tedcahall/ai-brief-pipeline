🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Reportedly Closes $11 Billion of New Round at $1.25 Trillion Valuation

Bloomberg reported Friday, citing people familiar with the matter, that OpenAI has received about $11 billion, the first part of a round that values the company at $1.25 trillion. The round is expected to close in the coming weeks and could total about $20 billion. Bloomberg reported in September that OpenAI was seeking $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion value, so the current figures fall well short of that target. OpenAI had not commented.

Anthropic IPO Valuation Talk Falls to $1.2 Trillion to $1.3 Trillion After AI Stock Selloff

Bloomberg reports that potential buyers of Anthropic shares are discussing a valuation of about $1.2 trillion to $1.3 trillion ahead of formal IPO marketing expected next week, citing people familiar with the matter. TechCrunch's summary adds that some buyers see $1.1 trillion, against roughly $2 trillion expected a month earlier. These are investor discussions after a sharp AI stock selloff, not an offer price, and Anthropic has set no IPO date or price.

Staff at US AI Safety Center Asked to Report to White House, Raising Independence Concerns

AP reports that officials at the Commerce Department's Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence were recently asked to report informally to a White House office, according to two former officials speaking anonymously. The center has no permanent director, has stopped publishing its research and has a $10 million annual budget. Critics fear findings could be muted; the White House did not answer specific questions about the center.

Judge Lets Claims Over Anthropic's Claude Max Usage Advertising Proceed

Magistrate Judge Alex Tse ruled Friday that Anthropic must face negligent misrepresentation and breach of contract claims in Kahn v. Anthropic, a proposed class action over its "5x or 20x more usage" Claude Max marketing. Plaintiffs say weekly limits fall well short of those multiples. Tse dismissed several other claims and rejected Anthropic's argument that a help-page disclosure settled the question. TNW has not read the ruling itself.

SemiAnalysis Details 800MW Wind-Powered AI Campus in Inner Mongolia

SemiAnalysis toured Galaxy, an Envision Group campus in Ulanqab planned for up to 800MW of IT capacity, and says its first 120MW phase was delivered within a year at a fifth of US costs. The report describes a 5MW 800V DC pilot using solid-state transformers and cooling with no central chiller plant. The visit took place in September, and the figures are the firm's own observations.
