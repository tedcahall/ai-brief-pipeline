🌐 TOP STORIES

Investors Cut Anthropic IPO Valuation Expectations as Even $1.5 Trillion Looks Like a 'Stretch'

After the Nasdaq's 5% drop on Thursday, Anthropic stakeholders told CNBC that a $1.5 trillion IPO valuation is now in doubt; one investor called it a "stretch" and another cited $1 trillion to $1.25 trillion as more likely. The company had sought up to $2 trillion, was privately valued at $965 billion in May, plans to list on Nasdaq as ANT, and is expected to start its roadshow next week. Anthropic declined to comment.

Nous Research Releases Arete-27B, a Qwen3.8-27B Fine-Tune Targeting State-Aligned Answers

Nous Research post-trained Alibaba's open-weight Qwen3.8-27B into Arete-27B, publishing the weights, an 803-prompt Propaganda_Eval dataset and the training data on Hugging Face. On Nous's own evaluation, judged by Grok 4.7, answers adopting the Chinese state's framing fell from 22% to 0.9% and the fact score rose from 2.32 to 2.98 out of 5. Nous reports no significant Hermes Bench regression; these are the developer's own measurements, not independent replication.

Microsoft Is Winding Down Its Oracle AI Cloud Capacity Deal, Bloomberg Reports

Microsoft is winding down an arrangement to rent AI computing capacity from Oracle as part of a larger reassessment of billions of dollars in cloud partnerships, Bloomberg News reported on Friday, citing people familiar with the matter. Oracle shares fell about 4% on the report. Oracle has said demand for its AI infrastructure remains strong. The move adds to investor scrutiny of AI infrastructure spending after a week of sharp declines in AI-linked stocks.

Apple Ships an Official Xcode Plugin for Claude Code

Anthropic merged an Apple-authored xcode plugin into its claude-plugins-official marketplace on November 6, exposing Xcode capabilities to Claude Code through MCP for native Apple-platform development. According to the plugin listing and developer reports, it lets Claude build, run and test apps, render SwiftUI previews, drive simulators and devices, and read build logs. It arrives days after Apple's Xcode 27.3 beta added Coding Intelligence support for ChatGPT, Codex and Claude.

US AI Safety Center Staff Now Report Informally to the White House, AP Reports

Officials at the Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence (CAISSI), the Commerce Department division that evaluates new AI systems, were recently asked to informally report to a White House office, two former officials told the Associated Press. The $10 million-a-year center has had no permanent leader since its director resigned and stopped publishing safety reports in August. Critics worry about politicized findings; others say White House proximity could bring prominence and funding.
