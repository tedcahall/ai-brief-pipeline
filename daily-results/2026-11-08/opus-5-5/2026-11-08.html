🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Raises $11 Billion at a $1.25 Trillion Valuation as Anthropic IPO Bids Slip

OpenAI has raised $11 billion so far in a round valuing it at $1.25 trillion, Bloomberg reported Friday, according to TechCrunch. The round has not closed and could reach about $20 billion, short of a reported $30 billion target. Bloomberg separately reported that potential buyers in Anthropic's IPO are discussing a valuation as low as $1.1 trillion, versus roughly $2 trillion expected a month ago.

NVIDIA Releases Four Open-Weight Cosmos 3 Models for Robot and Driving Simulation

NVIDIA added four open-weight checkpoints to its Cosmos 3 world-model family, each a post-trained version of Cosmos 3 Nano with 16B trainable parameters. They generate multi-view driving data with varied weather and lighting, stream synchronized camera and LiDAR observations, turn simulator controls into photorealistic video, and predict video from robot actions. The models use the OpenMDW 1.1 license, which permits commercial use.

Apple's Official Xcode Plugin Lands in Claude Code's Plugin Marketplace

Anthropic merged an Xcode plugin into its official Claude Code plugin marketplace on November 6, listing Apple Inc. as the author. The pull request says the plugin exposes Xcode capabilities over MCP, and the marketplace entry says it lets Claude Code build, run and test schemes, render SwiftUI previews, drive simulators and devices, read build diagnostics, and search Apple developer documentation.

Staff at US AI Safety Center Asked to Report Informally to the White House

Officials at the Commerce Department's Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence were recently asked to report informally to a White House office, two former government officials told the AP. The center has no permanent leader, has stopped publishing its AI safety reports and receives $10 million a year. Critics fear politicized findings, while others say closer ties could bring more funding.

Judge Says Anthropic Must Face Claims Its Claude Max Ads Misled Customers

Magistrate Judge Alex Tse ruled Friday that Anthropic must face negligent misrepresentation and breach of contract claims in the proposed class action Kahn v. Anthropic, TNW reported, citing Courthouse News. Plaintiffs say Max plans advertised as giving five or 20 times Pro usage deliver far less over a week because the multiplier applied per five-hour session. Several other claims were dismissed.
