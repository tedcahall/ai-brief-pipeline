🌐 TOP STORIES

Nous Research Releases Arete-27B, Re-Aligning Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B to Western Values

Nous Research post-trained Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B with supervised fine-tuning on about 52.8 million tokens distilled from an Inkling teacher, then published the weights, an 803-prompt propaganda evaluation and the training data. On that evaluation the fact score rose from 2.32 to 2.98 out of 5 and propaganda-framed answers fell from 22 percent to about 1 percent, while Hermes Bench stayed within 0.2 points of the base model.

OpenAI Closes $11 Billion First Portion of Funding at $1.25 Trillion Valuation

Bloomberg reported Friday that OpenAI closed roughly $11 billion in new funding, the initial portion of a round valuing the company at $1.25 trillion, with the money wired as of Friday. Reuters and TechCrunch corroborated the first close, and multiple outlets reported the raise fell short of the company's earlier, larger target amid a broad AI stock selloff. The round remains partially open.

Meta Rolls Out Muse Voice and Realtime Avatar Video Chat Across the US

Meta announced Thursday that Muse Voice, calling and video chat with its consumer AI agent Muse, is now available to everyone in the US on mobile, web and Mac. The feature runs on new Muse Realtime Avatar and Muse Realtime Voice models with customizable, expressive avatars, and arrives ahead of Meta's planned year-end Muse Charm wearable and AI Glasses support.

AP: US AI Safety Center Staff Now Informally Report to the White House

The Associated Press reported that officials at CAISSI, the Commerce Department's independent AI safety division, were recently asked to informally report to a White House office. The center has no permanent leader, has stopped publishing its research and runs on $10 million a year, about one-eighth of its UK counterpart's budget, fueling concern that findings could be politicized or muted.

SemiAnalysis Visits China's Stargate-Scale Wind-Powered Campus in Inner Mongolia

SemiAnalysis published a site visit to the Galaxy campus in Ulanqab, planned by Envision Group for up to 800MW of IT capacity, whose first 120MW phase was delivered within a year at about a fifth of US costs. The wind-powered campus runs a 5MW solid-state-transformer 800V DC pilot and chiller-less cooling, with 420MW of dedicated wind farms targeting over 60 percent of early-phase power.
