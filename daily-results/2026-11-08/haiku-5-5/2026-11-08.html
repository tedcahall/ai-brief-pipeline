🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Reports $11 Billion Raised at $1.25 Trillion Valuation as Round Stays Open

OpenAI has raised $11 billion so far in a funding round valuing the company at $1.25 trillion, Bloomberg reported on Friday, November 6, as relayed by TechCrunch. The round has not closed and is expected to close in the coming weeks, with a source saying it could total about $20 billion. That falls well short of the $30 billion target Bloomberg reported in late September.

CAISSI Staff Reportedly Asked to Report to White House Office

CAISSI, the Commerce Department division that assesses new AI offerings, lacks a permanent leader and has stopped publishing its research, AP reported. Two former officials told AP that its staff were recently asked to informally report to a White House office, an arrangement critics say could politicize or mute its findings. The account relies on anonymous former officials.

Meta Opens Muse Voice to Everyone in the US With Real-Time Spoken Conversation

Meta announced Thursday, November 5, that Muse Voice is available to all US users. The feature lets users call and video-chat with Muse, Meta's consumer AI agent, using new Muse Realtime Voice and Muse Realtime Avatar models. The animated avatar, nicknamed Jolly, can be customized, and Meta first introduced the feature at its September Connect conference.

Artificial Analysis Ranks OpenAI's GPT Image 2.6 First on Text-to-Image and Editing Leaderboards

New developments: Artificial Analysis says GPT Image 2.6 takes #1 and #2 on its AA-Image-T2I v2.1 and AA-Image-Editing v2.1 leaderboards. GPT Image 2.6 Flare generates almost twice as fast as GPT Image 2.5 Flare with slightly better quality, while Sunburst takes the top spot at about 2 minutes 15 seconds per image. Both models cost $30 per 1M image output tokens, unchanged from GPT Image 2.5.

Arena Ranks Kimi K3.1 Pro Top Open Model and Tencent Hy4 Top Chinese Model in Agent Arena

New developments: Arena's weekly update says Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro ranks #1 among open models in Agent Arena at +5.3% net improvement. Tencent's Hy4 debuted as the #1 Chinese model in Agent Arena at +5.4% net improvement and a $0.57 median cost per task, with open weights coming soon. These are Arena's own evaluation results, not independent replication.
