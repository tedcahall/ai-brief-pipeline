🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Completes $11 Billion First Close at $1.25 Trillion Valuation

OpenAI has raised $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation in the first close of an ongoing funding round that could reach about $20 billion in the coming weeks, according to Bloomberg reporting cited by TechCrunch. The total sits below the $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation OpenAI sought in late September, though the valuation is up 46.7 percent from the $852 billion level reached in March.

Nous Research Releases Open-Weight Arete-27B and 803-Prompt Values Benchmark

Nous Research released Arete-27B, an open-weight model post-trained from Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B on 52.8 million tokens using Thinking Machines' Inkling as a teacher, alongside its open 803-prompt Propaganda_Eval dataset and training pairs. Across seven value categories, Arete-27B raised factual recall from 2.32 to 2.98 out of 5 and reduced Chinese state-aligned propaganda responses from 22 percent to 0.9 percent without hurting its 65.60 percent Hermes Bench score.

Claude Code Permission Files Expose 1,379 Secrets Across 1,122 Public GitHub Repos

New developments: Separate from Friday's User-Agent email leak, Apiiro researchers scanned 104,185 public GitHub copies of Claude Code's .claude/settings.local.json file and found 1,122 files exposing 1,379 plaintext secrets saved when users selected 'Yes, and don't ask again.' Exposed credentials across 63 organizations included 379 database passwords, 43 SSH logins and 23 AWS keys because Claude Code adds the file only to local global Git excludes rather than project .gitignore files.

Anthropic Study of 207,000 Claude Sessions Estimates AI Could Handle 38% of Research Time

Anthropic analyzed 207,000 privacy-preserved Claude and Claude Code sessions alongside a researcher survey to measure how scientists use AI across disciplines. Data analysis and writing account for most usage, with data analysis representing nearly half of Claude Code sessions, and Anthropic's model estimates that widespread adoption of current AI capabilities could take on 27 to 52 percent of researcher time per study, with a 38 percent median.

US AI Safety Center Staff Asked to Report to White House Amid Leadership Vacuum

Officials at the Commerce Department's Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence, formerly the U.S. Center for AI Standards and Innovation, were recently asked to informally report to a White House office, two former officials told the Associated Press. Allocated $10 million annually, about one-eighth of its UK counterpart's budget, the division lacks a permanent director after two departures since April and stopped publishing safety reports by August.
