🌐 TOP STORIES

AI System at Physical Superintelligence Proves Aaronson-Ambainis Conjecture, Lean-Verified

Physical Superintelligence says an internal research system running instances of a non-public Anthropic model proved the Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture, open since 2008, with Lean accepting a formalization of 648 machine-checked theorems. Stanford mathematicians who proved a special case in August called it a huge result. The model is unreleased, the process cannot be reproduced, and human reviewers must still confirm the formal statement matches the original conjecture.

OpenAI Closes $11 Billion First Tranche at $1.25 Trillion Valuation

OpenAI has reportedly received about $11 billion as the first close of a round priced at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation, with Bloomberg reporting the cash arrived on Friday. The round is expected to total roughly $20 billion, below the $30 billion the company was reported in September to be seeking. Anthropic IPO valuation expectations fell in the same selloff.

Nous Research Releases Arete-27B, Cutting Measured Pro-Beijing Framing From 22% to 0.9%

Nous Research post-trained Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B on about 52.8 million tokens and released the result as Arete-27B. On its own 803-prompt evaluation across seven value categories, answers carrying pro-Beijing framing fell from 22% to 0.9% and fact scores rose from 2.32 to 2.98 of 5. General capability held: Hermes Bench 65.60% against the base model's 65.77%. The evaluation is the lab's own, judged by Grok 4.7.

AWS Makes Kiro Bots Generally Available With Per-Bot Memory and Permissions

AWS made Kiro bots generally available on November 5, adding persistent named agents to its Kiro Crew workspace. Each bot keeps its own conversation, notes, memory, dashboard and scoped permissions, and a coordinator can supervise parallel worker sessions while running a different model from its workers. AWS published no comparative benchmarks or measured error rates, and developers still define each bot's job and completion criteria.

US AI Safety Center Now Reports Informally to the White House as Independence Questions Mount

The Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence, an independent Commerce Department division, was recently asked to report informally to a White House office, two former officials told the Associated Press. The center has no permanent director, stopped publishing its research in August and receives $10 million a year, about one eighth of its UK counterpart. The White House did not answer specific questions.
