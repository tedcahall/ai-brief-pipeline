🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Chief Scientist Proposes Perplexity-Based Safety Limits as Pretraining Gains Accelerate

OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki published internal log-perplexity measurements from GPT-3.5 (0.99) and GPT-4 (0.91) down to 0.76, where models began resolving open math problems. Warning that AI-assisted pretraining research could trigger rapid advances by 2027 while alignment monitoring lags, Pachocki proposed shared industry limits before crossing the 0.68 to 0.70 window. Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman endorsed the effort.

Z.ai Launches GLM-5.4, a 750B Loop Transformer Flagship for Coding and Vision

Chinese lab Z.ai (Zhipu AI) launched GLM-5.4 on November 23 for GLM Coding Plan users, calling it its first Loop Transformer flagship. The 750B-parameter model loops 28 of its 78 layers twice per forward pass to add roughly 36% effective depth and vision capabilities without extra parameters. Z.ai claims intelligence comparable to 2T to 3T models, with API access and open weights pending safety evaluations.

Ant Group Open-Sources 560B Medical MoE Model LingSante-3.1-flash

Ant Group's Ant Ling team released LingSante-3.1-flash (560B total, 25B active parameters, 1M context) and multimodal LingSante-3.1-air-VL (124B total, 5.5B active) under MIT weights on November 22. Ant reports leads over closed frontier models on HealthBench Professional and HLE with tools, though it trails on other medical benchmarks and its model card warns the systems must not independently diagnose or prescribe.

Meta AI Chief Alexandr Wang Warns Superintelligence Is Arriving and Control Is Not Inevitable

Meta Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang warned on November 22 that superintelligence is arriving, arguing that unconstrained recursive self-improvement could yield systems impossible for humans to control. Citing recent AI math breakthroughs, Wang urged frontier labs to abandon the industry's default path, prioritize alignment and security over the next year, and direct compute toward serving users rather than recursive self-improvement.

Apple Recruits Leadership for Autonomous Computer-Use AI Agent

Apple is recruiting a senior engineering manager to build an autonomous, general-purpose computer-use AI agent capable of browsing the web, operating across apps, running terminal commands, and debugging code. The early-stage project, potentially targeting a 2027 debut alongside OS 28, follows Apple's Siri AI rebrand across iOS 27 and its iOS 27.3 beta expansion under CEO John Ternus.
