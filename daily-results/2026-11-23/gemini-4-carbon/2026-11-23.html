🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Shares Pretraining Loss Curve and Proposes Perplexity Safety Limits

OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki published internal log-perplexity progression from GPT-3.5 near 0.99 to recent models below 0.80, warning that AI-assisted pretraining research could trigger rapid breakthroughs as soon as 2027. He proposed industry-wide perplexity thresholds gated on stronger behavior-monitoring tools before entering the 0.68 to 0.70 window. Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman endorsed the proposal and reported MAI-2 Base at 0.819 on OpenAI's evaluation set.

Ant Group Releases Open-Weight LingSante-3.1 Healthcare Model Family

Ant Group's inclusionAI team released open weights on Hugging Face and ModelScope for LingSante-3.1-flash, a 560-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts healthcare model with 25 billion active parameters and a 1-million-token context window, alongside multimodal 124-billion-parameter LingSante-3.1-air-VL. In company-reported benchmarks, LingSante-3.1-flash scored 65.72 on HealthBench Professional and 74.40 on EHR-Complex. Ant Group specifies both models are for research only and are not medical devices.

AWS Reports Autonomous AI Agents Found a Medical App Zero-Day in 11 Minutes

Amazon Threat Intelligence reported that an operator assessed with moderate confidence as Chinese-nexus pointed an unauthenticated Cairn AI penetration-testing server at East Asian universities with the prompt "obtain shell access." After running up to 16 hours unattended on hardened targets, the agents mapped an exposed MONAI Label medical-imaging server, found an unpatched path-traversal zero-day (CVE-2026-133968) in 11 minutes, and later gained root access.

Microsoft President Warns AI Patching Race Risks Months of Tech Outages

Speaking on CBS's Face the Nation, Microsoft President and Vice Chair Brad Smith said Microsoft's November 12 outage stemmed from software update interactions rather than AI or a cyberattack, but warned that AI tools have accelerated vulnerability discovery worldwide. Because defenders are rushing to patch legacy infrastructure with less testing time, Smith said elevated outage risks will likely persist for months.

US Governors Issue Bipartisan Principles to Preserve State AI Oversight

The National Governors Association's bipartisan Governors Council to Secure American AI Dominance, led by Governors Wes Moore, Mike Braun, Josh Stein, and Spencer Cox, released AI governance recommendations ahead of the incoming Congress. The framework calls for federal oversight of national and international AI risks while preserving state authority over local deployment, worker transition support, consumer safety, and open market competition.
