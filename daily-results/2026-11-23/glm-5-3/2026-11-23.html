🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Proposes Perplexity as a Shared Frontier Metric and Safety Limit

OpenAI's post argues that tracking pretraining progress, measured by perplexity or cross-entropy loss, predicts emergent capabilities, citing cybersecurity and mathematics gains after losses fell below 0.8 and math breakthroughs from a model near 0.76. It proposes common limits that developers commit not to cross until monitoring reaches concrete milestones such as a successor to chain-of-thought monitors.

Ant Group's Ant Ling Open-Sources LingSante-3.1-flash, a 560B-Parameter Medical MoE

Ant Ling released LingSante-3.1-flash, an open-weight MoE model with 560B total and 25B active parameters, under an MIT license through the inclusionAI organization on Hugging Face. The team reports HealthBench Professional at 65.72 and BrowseComp at 94.10 with context, claiming it outperforms GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 4 Argon on those benchmarks; these are vendor claims not independently verified.

Databricks in Talks to Raise at a $225 Billion Valuation, WSJ Reports

The Wall Street Journal reports Databricks is in early talks with investors to raise several billion dollars at a $225 billion valuation. The talks are unconfirmed fundraising discussions rather than a closed round, and terms could still change. Databricks' most recent confirmed round was $5 billion in August 2026, per Tracxn.

FT: AI Models Speed Up Operational-Technology Attack Planning in Lab Tests

An FT report describes tests in which Forescout's Vedere Labs used Anthropic's Claude to port a WAGO PLC remote-code-execution exploit, with the final stage taking 8 hours 32 minutes and $535.74 in API tokens. In a separate Booz Allen Hamilton exercise, models moved from perimeter compromise to actions inside an industrial control network in just over 16 minutes. These are lab and simulation results, not real-world attacks.

Apple Is Recruiting to Build an Autonomous Computer-Use AI Agent

A senior engineering manager job listing describes a general-purpose Apple agent that browses the web, works across apps, runs commands, writes and debugs code, and chooses the right tool for each step. The project is early stage with no announced release date, and third-party app support for the rebranded Siri AI is not expected before spring 2027.
