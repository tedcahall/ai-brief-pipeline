NOV 23, 2026

📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki published the company's internal pretraining perplexity figures and proposed shared industry limits, based on perplexity and other measurements, that labs would not cross until AI monitoring reaches a concrete milestone. Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman backed the initiative and reported that MAI-2 Base scored 0.819 log-perplexity on OpenAI's evaluation set, which he put in the same ballpark as the GPT-5.6 Sol base model.

Meta chief AI officer Alexandr Wang published an essay arguing that superintelligence is arriving and that the industry should not accept a handover of society to AI. On the model front, Ant Group released open-weight LingSante medical models and India's Sarvam AI released a cheaper open-weight Sarvam-105B checkpoint.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Chief Scientist Proposes Shared Perplexity Limits for Frontier AI

In "Perplexity and Intelligence," OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki published internal pretraining log-perplexity figures, from about 0.99 for GPT-3.5 and 0.91 for GPT-4 to below 0.8 for recent models. He proposed common limits that developers would not cross until AI monitoring reaches a concrete milestone, expects new transformative capabilities in a 0.68 to 0.7 window, and said pretraining research could accelerate as soon as 2027.

Meta's Alexandr Wang Warns Superintelligence Is Arriving

In an essay on X titled "Superintelligence is Arriving," Meta chief AI officer Alexandr Wang argued that the remaining gaps for models to surpass humans are domain know-how, plumbing and experimentation rather than intelligence, and that models will be able to automate AI research. He said the industry should not accept a "handover" of society to AI and must change course from its default path.

Ant Group Releases Open-Weight LingSante Medical Models

Ant Group's Ant Ling team released LingSante-3.1-flash, a 560B-parameter mixture-of-experts medical model with about 25B active parameters and a 1M-token context, plus the 124B multimodal LingSante-3.1-air-VL, both under MIT licenses. Ant reports leading scores on HealthBench Professional and HLE with tools, but no independent evaluations have been published, and the model cards prohibit independent diagnosis or treatment decisions.

Sarvam Releases Open-Weight Sarvam-105B-1711 at $0.15 per Million Tokens

Indian lab Sarvam AI released Sarvam-105B-1711, a new open-weight checkpoint of its 105B model trained on upgraded B200 infrastructure. Sarvam says the model's average across eight conversational agent benchmarks rose from 42.6 to 73.9 since its February release and that blended pricing fell from $0.44 to $0.15 per million tokens. A 65 GB NVFP4 version runs on a single workstation GPU.

OpenRouter Off-Boards Three Inference Providers After Data Policy Findings

OpenRouter said it off-boarded three inference providers and removed 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention eligibility after Dragos researchers' canary-token testing found some providers had compromised data or were not upholding their data policies. OpenRouter said its own systems were not breached. Novita, Phala and Together have posted incident notices, and OpenRouter plans to run similar checks on an ongoing basis.
