📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki published the lab's internal log-perplexity figures from GPT-3.5 onward and proposed that frontier labs agree on shared development limits tied to perplexity until monitoring tools improve. 🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Publishes Pretraining Perplexity Curve and Proposes Shared Industry Limits

OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki published the lab's log-perplexity on a held-out dataset, citing about 0.99 for GPT-3.5 and 0.91 for GPT-4, with transformative cybersecurity and math capabilities emerging below 0.8. He proposed common limits, based on perplexity and other measurements, that developers would not cross until oversight of AI behavior reaches a concrete milestone, and noted that comparing labs remains difficult.

Meta's Alexandr Wang Warns Superintelligence Is Arriving, Says Loss of Control Is Not Inevitable

In a post titled "Superintelligence is Arriving," Meta's Alexandr Wang wrote that models will be intelligent enough to automate all AI research, and that human control over unrestricted recursive self-improvement is hard to imagine. He then posted that this path is not inevitable, citing Meta's months-long delay of Muse and its signing of the SI Accords.

Ant Group Releases LingSante-3.1-flash, a 560B Open-Weight Medical Model

Ant Group's Ant Ling team released two medical models with MIT-licensed weights, including LingSante-3.1-flash, a mixture-of-experts model with 560 billion total and about 25 billion active parameters and a 1 million token context window. Ant says it beats GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 4 Argon on three benchmarks; these are vendor claims, and the model cards say it is not a medical device.

Tesla Begins Rolling Out FSD V15 With Generative Reality View

Tesla started rolling out Full Self-Driving version 15 on November 21, with Teslarati reporting that the headline feature is Reality View, a real-time AI video generation of the car's surroundings. Elon Musk said it runs on half of one AI4 TRIP accelerator and that wide release is planned for December. Teslarati noted some input lag and withheld judgment on driving performance.

Apple Is Hiring to Build an Autonomous Computer-Use AI Agent

An Apple job listing for a senior engineering manager describes an agent that browses the web, works across apps, runs commands, writes and debugs code, and calls services, choosing the right tool for each step. Dataconomy reports Apple has not announced a release date and the project is early stage, with earlier reports pointing to a possible 2027 debut alongside OS 28.
