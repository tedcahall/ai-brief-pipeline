🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Proposes Perplexity-Based Safety Limits as Microsoft Discloses MAI-2 Base Score

OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki published an essay proposing pretraining perplexity as a shared cross-lab metric to pace frontier AI development until monitoring advances beyond chain-of-thought monitors. He disclosed internal log-perplexity milestones from GPT-3.5 (0.99) to math breakthroughs (~0.76) and warned of potential AI-driven pretraining acceleration by 2027. Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman endorsed the effort and disclosed MAI-2 Base at 0.819 log-perplexity on OpenAI's evaluation set.

Ant Group Releases Open-Weight Medical AI Models LingSante-3.1-flash and LingSante-3.1-air-VL

Ant Group's Ant Ling team released MIT-licensed open weights on Hugging Face for LingSante-3.1-flash, a 560B-parameter MoE text model with 25B active parameters and a 1M-token context window, and LingSante-3.1-air-VL, a 124B-parameter multimodal MoE model with 5.5B active parameters. Ant Group also launched the InclusionMed benchmark with 29 research institutions, though model cards specify the downloadable weights are for research and evaluation rather than clinical diagnosis.

Amazon Threat Intelligence Says Autonomous Cairn AI Agents Exploited a Medical-Imaging Zero-Day

Amazon Threat Intelligence reported that a Chinese-nexus threat actor used the open-source Cairn multi-agent framework to discover and exploit a previously unknown path traversal vulnerability in the MONAI Label medical-imaging platform, gaining root access on an East Asian university server in roughly 11 minutes from a single 'obtain shell access' prompt. Amazon notified project maintainers, who patched the flaw, while noting that two other agentic intrusion attempts failed against stronger defenses.

Meta Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang Warns Superintelligence Is Arriving and Urges Industry Shift

Following the weekend's Claude-assisted Riemann Hypothesis proof, Meta Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang warned in a November 22 X post that superintelligence is arriving and that humanity is unlikely to control unconstrained recursive self-improvement. In a follow-up post, Wang emphasized that this trajectory is not inevitable, pointing to Meta's months-long delay of Muse, industry safety accords, and Mark Zuckerberg's call to direct most compute toward serving users rather than recursive self-improvement.

Apple Recruits Engineering Leader to Build Autonomous Computer-Use AI Agent

Apple posted a job listing seeking a senior engineering manager to lead development of a general-purpose autonomous AI agent capable of browsing the web, operating across apps, running terminal commands, and writing or debugging code. The early-stage effort reflects CEO John Ternus's push for a more ambitious Siri AI strategy beyond iOS 27.3 app integrations, as Apple looks to compete with autonomous agent offerings from OpenAI, Meta, and xAI.
