🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Proposes Perplexity-Based Pretraining Limits as Microsoft Shares MAI-2 Score

In a November 22 essay titled Perplexity and Intelligence, OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki proposed industry-wide pretraining limits gated on safety and monitoring milestones, disclosing that OpenAI's log-perplexity dropped from roughly 0.99 on GPT-3.5 to 0.91 on GPT-4 and below 0.8 on recent models. Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman backed the proposal and reported that MAI-2 Base scored 0.819 on OpenAI's evaluation set, near the GPT-5.6 Sol base model.

Ant Group Open-Sources 560B LingSante-3.1-flash and 124B Multimodal Medical Models

Ant Group's Ant Ling and Afu health teams released the LingSante medical AI model family on Hugging Face and ModelScope. LingSante-3.1-flash is a 560-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 25 billion active parameters and a 1-million-token context window, while LingSante-3.1-air-VL is a 124-billion-parameter multimodal model with 5.5 billion active parameters. Ant reports leading internal scores on HealthBench Professional and medical imaging tasks, while labeling both models strictly for non-clinical research.

vLLM and AMD Show Direct NVMe KV Cache Offloading Matches DRAM Throughput on Kimi-K3

The vLLM team, collaborating with AMD and EmbeddedLLM, reported that skipping host DRAM and placing a 1.8 TB KV cache pool directly on NVMe drives matched a 1.8 TB DRAM pool within 1% throughput on Kimi-K3 across 16 AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs using AMD's MoRI UMBP library. On WEKA's 40-day trace at 70 concurrent sessions, a 4.6 TB NVMe pool delivered 5.1 times the throughput of a 1.0 TB DRAM pool.

Meta AI Chief Alexandr Wang Warns on Superintelligence Timeline and Defends Muse Delay

In an essay titled Superintelligence is Arriving and follow-up statements on November 22 and 23, Meta Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang pointed to the certified Claude-assisted Riemann hypothesis proof as evidence of accelerating AI capabilities and warned against ceding societal control to autonomous systems. Wang said Meta delayed its Muse model launch for months to prioritize safety evaluations and noted Mark Zuckerberg's call to direct compute toward customer applications rather than recursive self-improvement.

National Governors Association Releases Principles Preserving State AI Oversight Authority

The National Governors Association, led by Chair Maryland Governor Wes Moore and Vice-Chair Indiana Governor Mike Braun, published a bipartisan framework on November 22 calling for a shared national approach to AI regulation ahead of the incoming Congress in January. The principles state that the federal government should address national and international AI risks while states retain authority to govern AI deployment, workforce impacts, and consumer protections within their jurisdictions.
