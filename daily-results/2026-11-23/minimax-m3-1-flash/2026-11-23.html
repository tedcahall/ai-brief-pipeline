🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Publishes Model Perplexity Scores and Proposes Shared Development Limits

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki published internal pretraining perplexity measurements for OpenAI models from GPT-3.5 onward, reporting log-perplexity of about 0.99 for GPT-3.5, about 0.91 for GPT-4 and below 0.8 for current frontier models, and argued that alignment and monitoring progress is unlikely to be fast enough for pretraining. He proposed that frontier labs agree on a first shared limit measured by perplexity, with concrete exit criteria such as a successor to chain-of-thought monitors. Microsoft AI said it would publish pre-training progress on a common held-out set and reported MAI-2 Base at log-perplexity 0.819.

Amazon Nears $7.5 Billion Purchase of AI Startup Decart

The Financial Times reported that Amazon is in advanced talks to acquire Decart, an Israeli AI startup whose software lets models run more efficiently and free up chip capacity, for roughly $7.5 billion, with a deal possible within days but not guaranteed. Decart was an early adopter of Amazon's Trainium3 chips, and the talks follow a reported approach to Anthropic earlier this year that Anthropic walked away from. Amazon declined to comment.

Amazon Threat Intelligence Reports AI Agents Found and Exploited a Zero-Day

Amazon Threat Intelligence said a Chinese-nexus actor ran autonomous Cairn agents against universities in East Asia with one instruction, to obtain shell access, and the agents planned, executed and adjusted on their own. The successful run found a path traversal flaw in an open-source medical imaging AI application that had no advisory and no fix, was used to reach a second university, and was patched by its maintainers after AWS contacted them.

InclusionAI Releases Open-Weight LingSante-3.1-Flash Medical Model

Ant Group's InclusionAI released LingSante-3.1-flash, an open-weight mixture-of-experts model with 560 billion total parameters, 25 billion active per token and a 1 million token context window, built on Ling-3.1-flash for medical reasoning and long-horizon clinical workflows. The team says it leads open-weight models on HealthBench Professional, HLE with tools and BrowseComp with context, and states the model is for research and evaluation only, not for diagnosis or treatment.

AI Speeds Planning of Industrial Cyberattacks, Forescout Ports an OT Exploit

The Financial Times reported that Forescout's Vedere Labs used Anthropic's Claude to port a known pre-authentication remote code execution exploit between WAGO programmable logic controllers, a final stage that ran 8 hours 32 minutes and cost $535.74 in API tokens, with one payload writing to flash and bricking a device. In a separate Booz Allen Hamilton exercise, models moved from perimeter compromise to actions inside an industrial control network in just over 16 minutes across eight scenarios, and Dragos chief executive Robert Lee said the situation is worse than most people realise.
