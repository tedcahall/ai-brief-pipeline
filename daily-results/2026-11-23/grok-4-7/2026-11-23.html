🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI's Chief Scientist Proposes Perplexity Limits on Pretraining

Jakub Pachocki, OpenAI's chief scientist, published Perplexity and Intelligence on November 22. He reported log-perplexity of about 0.99 for GPT-3.5, about 0.91 for GPT-4, and below 0.8 for recent models, and said a model near 0.76 produced groundbreaking math results. He proposed shared limits labs would not cross until monitoring reaches a concrete milestone, such as a successor to chain-of-thought monitors. The essay proposes that standard. It does not say other labs have agreed.

Microsoft Reports MAI-2 Base Log-Perplexity of 0.819

Mustafa Suleyman said Microsoft AI supports OpenAI's transparency effort and, with OpenAI's permission, reports MAI-2 Base at 0.819 log-perplexity on OpenAI's held-out set. He placed that in the same range as the GPT-5.6 Sol base model and said a shared tokenizer makes the figures comparable. The number is Microsoft's report, not an independent audit. The technical page linked from his post is dated October 15, 2026, and does not contain this figure.

Ant Ling Releases Open-Weight LingSante-3.1-flash

Ant Ling announced LingSante-3.1-flash on November 22. Its model card describes an MIT-licensed open-weight model with 560 billion total parameters, 25 billion active per token, and a 1 million-token context, and says it is for research, not diagnosis or treatment. Vendor benchmark claims, including a second-place professional-behavior ranking behind GPT-6 Astra, are the lab's measurements, not independent replications. The same day Ant Ling announced multimodal LingSante-3.1-air-VL, with 124 billion total and 5.5 billion active parameters.

AWS Says Cairn Agents Found a Medical-Imaging Zero-Day

AWS reported on November 20 that Cairn agents, told to obtain shell access, found an unpatched path-traversal flaw in an open-source medical imaging app. AWS says they went from mapping the API to writing files in about 11 minutes, and later wrote a Python reverse shell that ran as root. AWS calls the zero-day finding high confidence and a Chinese-nexus link moderate confidence. Maintainers shipped a fix. Indicators date to September 8-10, 2026.

Meta's Alexandr Wang Says Muse Launch Was Delayed for Safety

Alexandr Wang said on November 23 that the path he described is not inevitable. He said Meta delayed the Muse launch for months, took a leading role in the SI Accords, and is adding safety reviews. He also said Mark Zuckerberg has called for labs, including Meta, to put most compute toward serving customers rather than recursive self-improvement. Those are Wang's statements, not an independently checked schedule.
