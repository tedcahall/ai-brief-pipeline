🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Proposes Perplexity-Based Limits on Frontier AI as Microsoft Shares MAI-2 Data

OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki published "Perplexity and Intelligence" on November 22, proposing common cross-lab limits on pretraining progress measured by log-perplexity and gated on concrete monitoring milestones, and warning AI could drive rapid pretraining gains as early as 2027. Sam Altman called it the most important thing OpenAI has published in a while, and Microsoft's Mustafa Suleyman said MAI-2 Base scores 0.819 log-perplexity on OpenAI's evaluation set, with permission.

AWS Says Chinese-Nexus Actor's AI Agents Found and Exploited a Zero-Day Autonomously

Amazon Threat Intelligence found a Chinese-nexus actor ran Cairn, an open-source autonomous penetration-testing framework, against universities in East Asia in September with minimal human involvement. Given only the goal "obtain shell access," the agents mapped an open-source medical imaging application, found a path-traversal zero-day and gained full control of a server in about 11 minutes, AWS said in a November 20 post by CJ Moses.

Ant Ling Releases LingSante-3.1-flash Open-Weight Medical Model

Ant Group's Ling team released LingSante-3.1-flash, an open-weight mixture-of-experts model with 560B total parameters and 25B active per token, aimed at professional medical workflows including report generation, imaging analysis and long-horizon task execution. The company reports leads on HealthBench Professional (65.72), HLE with tools (72.22) and BrowseComp with context (94.10) over GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 4 Argon; these are company evaluations, not independently replicated. Weights are public.

SGLang Rubin NVL72 Submission Shows 6.5x Performance per Dollar Over GB300

SemiAnalysis reported that a new SGLang submission on Nvidia's Rubin NVL72 platform delivered 6.5x better performance per dollar than GB300 NVL72, running Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro open model architecture, which is less than three weeks old with over 2.8 trillion parameters, similar in parameter count to Anthropic Fable 5. The figures come from SemiAnalysis's InferenceX benchmarking platform and have not been independently replicated.

Citi CEO Jane Fraser Says AI Market Reset Brings Healthy Selectivity

In a Semafor interview published November 23, Citigroup CEO Jane Fraser said the AI market had been priced to perfection and that the current reset brings good selectivity, with capital a tad more scarce than before. She expects consolidation among neoclouds, warned of a large refinancing wall arriving with higher rates and tighter liquidity, and said the Nasdaq's correction has been technical rather than fundamental so far.
