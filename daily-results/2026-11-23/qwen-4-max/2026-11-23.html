📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

OpenAI published its internal pretraining perplexity curve and proposed industry-wide development limits tied to the metric, and Microsoft responded by reporting its MAI-2 Base model's score on OpenAI's evaluation set.

🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Publishes Pretraining Perplexity Curve, Proposes Shared Safety Limits

Chief scientist Jakub Pachocki published 'Perplexity and Intelligence,' disclosing OpenAI's internal held-out log-perplexity measurements from GPT-3.5 at about 0.99 to below 0.8 today, and proposing common development limits that labs would not cross until monitoring reaches concrete milestones, such as a successor to chain-of-thought monitors. Microsoft's Mustafa Suleyman endorsed the initiative and reported MAI-2 Base at 0.819 on OpenAI's evaluation set.

Ant Group Open-Sources LingSante Medical Models

Ant Group's Ant Ling team released LingSante-3.1-flash, a 560B-parameter mixture-of-experts medical model with 25B active parameters per token and a 1M-token context window, under an MIT license, plus LingSante-3.1-air-VL, a 124B multimodal medical model. Ant claims leads on HealthBench Professional and other benchmarks against GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 4 Argon; the models are for research, not diagnosis.

Amazon: AI Agents Found and Exploited a Zero-Day Autonomously

Amazon Threat Intelligence said a Chinese-nexus actor, assessed with moderate confidence, gave Cairn AI agents the goal 'obtain shell access'; the agents found a path-traversal zero-day in MONAI Label, went from API mapping to arbitrary file writes in about 11 minutes, and later gained root. MONAI Label's advisory rates the bug critical, affecting versions 0.8.5 and earlier.

vLLM and AMD Show NVMe Can Match DRAM for Agentic KV Cache

A vLLM post with AMD's MoRI team showed a 1.8 TB NVMe KV-cache pool matched an equal-size DRAM pool within 1% throughput on Kimi-K3 across 16 MI355X GPUs replaying AgentX traces. On WEKA's kvlab workload at 70 sessions, a 4.6 TB NVMe pool delivered 5.1 times the throughput of a 1.0 TB DRAM pool.

Databricks Reportedly in Talks to Raise at $225 Billion Valuation

The Wall Street Journal reported Databricks is in early talks to raise several billion dollars at about $225 billion, roughly 18% above its August valuation of $190 billion. No investors, final amount or terms are confirmed. Databricks said in August it passed a $7 billion annualized revenue run-rate, growing more than 80% year over year.
