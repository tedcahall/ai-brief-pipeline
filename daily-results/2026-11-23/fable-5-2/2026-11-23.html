🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Chief Scientist Proposes Industry-Wide Development Limits Based on Perplexity

In a post titled "Perplexity and Intelligence," OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki published the lab's internal log-perplexity figures: about 0.99 for GPT-3.5, 0.91 for GPT-4 and below 0.8 for recent models. He proposed common limits that developers would not cross until monitoring reaches a concrete milestone, such as a successor to chain-of-thought monitors, and wrote that an avalanche of pretraining advances is conceivable as soon as 2027. He named no specific threshold.

Meta AI Chief Wang Says Human Control of Self-Improving Superintelligence Is Hard to Imagine

Meta chief AI officer Alexandr Wang wrote in an X essay titled "Superintelligence is Arriving" that the remaining gaps for models to surpass humans are domain know-how, plumbing and experimentation rather than intelligence, CTech reported. He said it is difficult to imagine humans retaining control over unrestricted recursive self-improvement. After criticism, Wang said that path is not inevitable and cited Meta's months-long delay of its Muse launch as evidence of its safety focus.

Ant Group Opens Weights for LingSante Medical Models, Including a 560B-Parameter Flagship

Ant Group's health app Afu released open weights for two LingSante medical models. LingSante-3.1-flash has 560 billion total and about 25 billion active parameters with a 1M-token context window, and LingSante-3.1-air-VL is a 124-billion-parameter multimodal model. Ant Ling says flash outscores GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 4 Argon on HealthBench Professional; those results are company-reported. Afu says it serves 150 million users.

Amazon Says Autonomous AI Agents Found and Exploited a Zero-Day in Medical Imaging Software

Amazon Threat Intelligence said agents running on the open source Cairn penetration testing framework, told only to obtain shell access, found a path traversal zero-day in MONAI Label and went from mapping its API to writing arbitrary files in about 11 minutes, later gaining a root shell. The roughly 48-hour campaign targeted East Asian universities. Amazon attributes it with moderate confidence to a Chinese-nexus actor, SecurityWeek reported.

Together AI Says an AI Coding Agent Leaked a Credential That Exposed Customer Prompts

Together AI disclosed that an AI coding agent committed a credential to its public xorl GitHub repository, giving access to a private Hugging Face repository of prompt samples from June and July requests to MiniMax M3, GLM-5.2 and Kimi K2.6. An unauthorized party downloaded the samples and tested credentials found in them. The company made zero data retention the default for new accounts and is still reviewing what was exposed.
