🌐 TOP STORIES

Ant Ling Releases Open Weights for Two LingSante Medical Models

LingSante-3.1-flash activates 25 billion of its 560 billion parameters per token and targets tasks such as finding evidence and working through medical records. Its companion, air-VL, can interpret images and generate reports. Ant Ling reports an advantage on several medical benchmarks; those tests do not establish safety in clinical practice.

OpenAI Scientist Proposes Shared Limits on Pretraining

Jakub Pachocki proposes that frontier labs track how well their base models predict unseen text and agree not to cross certain thresholds until they can better monitor model behavior. The proposal sets neither a numerical ceiling nor an effective date. Microsoft’s Mustafa Suleyman says MAI-2 Base scored 0.819 log-perplexity on OpenAI’s evaluation set.

Apple Recruits a Lead for a Computer-Use Agent

A job listing describes an Apple assistant that could choose when to switch between a browser, terminal, editor and other tools to complete a task. The hiring notice gives a concrete picture of what Apple wants to build, but does not announce a product or commit to a shipping date.

vLLM Tests SSDs Instead of DRAM for Inference Cache

On 16 AMD MI355X GPUs running Kimi-K3, vLLM says a 1.8 TB NVMe pool came within 1% of an equally large DRAM pool on its cache-offloading tests. A separate test favored a larger SSD pool. That comparison illustrates the value of extra capacity, not a general speed advantage for flash over memory.

NoPeWorld Helps Video Models Stop at Walls

Researchers found that six video world models already encoded clues about whether a proposed move would pass through a solid obstacle. Reading those signals and substituting a permissible move cut the average violation rate from 74% to 16% in their tests, without retraining the underlying video generators.
