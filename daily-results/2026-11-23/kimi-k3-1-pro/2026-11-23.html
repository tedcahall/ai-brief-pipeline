🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Proposes Shared Perplexity Limits for Frontier Model Development

OpenAI published internal held-out pretraining measurements and proposed common limits based on perplexity and other measurements. Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki argued developers should pause at agreed thresholds until monitoring reaches concrete milestones. The article acknowledges comparability, task-relevance and post-training blind spots, so the proposal is a framework for coordination rather than a comprehensive capability benchmark.

Ant Ling Releases Open-Weight LingSante Medical Models

Ant Ling released LingSante-3.1-flash, a medical mixture-of-experts model with 560 billion total and 25 billion active parameters. The team also introduced the 124-billion-parameter multimodal air-VL model. Ant reports leading results on several medical and agent benchmarks, but these are vendor-reported evaluations rather than independent verification. Both releases emphasize open weights and healthcare workflows.

Microsoft Shares MAI-2 Base Perplexity on OpenAI's Evaluation Set

Microsoft AI chief Mustafa Suleyman backed OpenAI's transparency initiative and reported a 0.819 log-perplexity for MAI-2 Base on OpenAI's held-out evaluation set. He said the shared tokenizer makes that result directly comparable within this dataset. He also cautioned that broader cross-lab comparisons may require bits-per-byte and different evaluation sets, illustrating both the value and limits of the proposal.

vLLM Reports NVMe KV-Cache Offload Matching DRAM Within 1%

vLLM reported that a 1.8-terabyte NVMe pool matched an equal DRAM pool within 1% for Kimi-K3 KV-cache offload on 16 AMD MI355X GPUs. At 70 sessions, a larger 4.6-terabyte NVMe pool delivered 5.1 times the throughput of a capacity-constrained 1-terabyte DRAM pool. The results are workload-specific tests using AgentX and WEKA traces.

Databricks Reportedly Discusses New Funding at $225 Billion Valuation

Databricks is in early talks to raise several billion dollars at about a $225 billion valuation, according to a Wall Street Journal report covered by TNW. The terms could change and no round has closed. The reported valuation would be nearly 20% above August's $190 billion mark, when Databricks raised $5 billion and disclosed a $7 billion revenue run rate.
