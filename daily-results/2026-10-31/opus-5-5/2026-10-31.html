🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI cuts funding round target to about $20 billion, FT reports

OpenAI has reduced the target for its latest funding round by $10 billion to about $20 billion and lowered the proposed valuation to $1.25 trillion from $1.4 trillion, the Financial Times reported on October 30, citing people familiar with the matter. Some investors reduced commitments amid an AI stock sell-off, and the first close will likely raise less than $20 billion. OpenAI has not confirmed the figures.

Malicious Claude Code extension reached up to 5,000 Cursor users

A backdoored copy of Anthropic's Claude Code extension was live in Cursor's extension marketplace for roughly 11 hours on Thursday and was downloaded by at most 5,000 users, according to Cursor. Anthropic says its systems were not compromised and that a malicious actor used access to Cursor's marketplace to publish the tampered copy. How the upload happened has not been explained.

AWS growth accelerates to 39.3% as AI demand rises

Amazon reported AWS revenue growth of 39.3% year over year in the third quarter, its sixth straight quarter of acceleration and fastest growth in nearly five years. CEO Andy Jassy cited a $578 billion backlog growing at triple digits and said Amazon is installing an AI accelerator about every 14 seconds, with customers absorbing capacity almost as fast as it is installed.

Anthropic uses Claude agents to find training data that slips past safety monitors

Anthropic's Alignment Science team asked Claude to write fine-tuning data that would make a small research model misaligned while evading a training-data monitor similar to those used in production. Given enough attempts it succeeded, mostly by hiding behavior in fiction and roleplay, and produced stronger misalignment than previously known datasets on Anthropic's evaluations. The resulting models were never deployed; Anthropic calls this a first step toward automated red-teaming.

Anthropic renames Claude Max 20x to Ultra and drops its 5-hour limit

Anthropic renamed its Claude Max 5x plan to Max and Max 20x to Ultra, keeping prices at $100 and $200 per month. Verified Ultra accounts lose the 5-hour usage limit, starting with most existing subscribers, while newer accounts keep a temporary limit until verified to curb fraud and abuse. Weekly limits are unchanged.
