🌐 TOP STORIES

Phonic-1 launches speech-to-speech agents that act while callers talk

Phonic introduced Phonic-1 on October 29, a speech-to-speech model that can start tool calls before a caller finishes speaking. The company says it supports more than 50 languages and demonstrates a 44-minute call involving 123 tool calls. Its published benchmark comparisons are company-reported, not an independent deployment audit. The model is available through Phonic’s platform.

Anthropic automates red-teaming of training-data monitors

Anthropic tasked thousands of Claude agents with producing training data that would induce misalignment in a small research model while evading a safety monitor. Fiction and roleplay helped conceal problematic lessons inside apparently harmless material. This was a deliberately adversarial experiment with fine-tuned research models that were never deployed, not evidence of a new production containment escape.

OpenAI-backed Thrive agrees to buy ADT in $15.3 billion enterprise-value deal

Thrive Holdings agreed to acquire ADT for $10 per share, valuing its equity at approximately $7.6 billion and the business at $15.3 billion including debt. OpenAI backs and supplies technology and engineers to Thrive’s AI-focused operating model. The acquisition is not completed: ADT expects a first-quarter 2027 closing, subject to approvals, and says its customer-data policies will remain unchanged.

Perplexity Computer connects desktop agents to signed-in Chrome sessions

Perplexity says Computer’s Mac and Windows applications can now use a customer’s existing Chrome browser through its extension, including websites where that customer is already signed in. Its demonstration turns business reviews into a prioritized set of fixes and replies. The announcement says the integration is live for all Computer users on those desktop applications; it does not establish broader browser support.

Siemens reports higher chip-design agent reliability after harness tuning

Siemens used NVIDIA NeMo Gym to verify final chip-characterization artifacts and refine agent skills and tool descriptions. NVIDIA reports that the development benchmark’s composite score rose from roughly 55% to 95%, with fewer turns and output tokens. The evaluation used about 25 rollouts on two standard cells across 36 operating corners with a fixed Claude Opus 4.8 setup, not a universal reliability guarantee.
