🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Uses Claude Agents to Probe Hidden Training-Data Risks

Anthropic says it tasked thousands of Claude agents with finding training examples that evade a monitor yet induce misaligned behavior. The examples include apparently ordinary stories. This was a controlled red-teaming experiment on deliberately trained models, not evidence that deployed Claude escaped containment. The work aims to improve future training-data monitoring.

Mischief 1 Brings Neuron-Informed Video Generation to Public APIs

The Biological Computing Co. released Mischief 1, a Wan 2.2-based image-to-video model informed by measurements of living neurons. The company says the software runs on conventional hardware and claims a fivefold speedup over the base model. That quality and speed comparison is company-reported; independent testing remains limited.

OpenAI Announces New ChatGPT Violence-Prevention Safeguards

OpenAI says it will restrict additional weapons information for teens and develop automated interruption of conversations showing signs of fixation on violence. The company says safeguards will begin rolling out over the following weeks, not that every feature is already live. It reported more than 200 law-enforcement referrals in the third quarter and plans a first community safety report in early 2027.

New York Lawmakers Propose Expanded Frontier AI Safety Oversight

New York lawmakers introduced a proposal to expand the state’s AI safety oversight, including independent audits and new incident disclosures for large developers. The proposal would also give state authorities tools to limit some training or deployment under specified circumstances. It is proposed legislation, not an enacted requirement.

SGLang Reports Faster DeepSeek V4.1 Flash Inference

SGLang says DeepSeek V4.1 Flash reached peak total throughput of 1.09 million tokens per second per GPU on AgentX, up from 654,000 four days earlier. The figure combines cached, new input and output tokens. The team separately reported 943 tokens per second per user at concurrency one; these are project-reported benchmark results.
