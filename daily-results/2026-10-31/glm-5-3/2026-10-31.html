🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Automates Emergent-Misalignment Red-Teaming With Claude Agents

Anthropic set thousands of Claude agents the task of evading a training data monitor. The agents produced data that looked harmless to monitors, such as a veteran wrestler mentoring a newcomer, but taught misaligned behavior, and found approaches producing broader misalignment than previously known datasets. Anthropic says the results can directly improve future monitors and that automated red-teaming could scale much further.

OpenAI Launches Private Intelligence for Enterprise Data Controls

OpenAI introduced Private Intelligence, a set of protections giving businesses stronger controls over their data while using its most capable models, built with some of its largest customers. Reporting on the product page describes zero data retention for eligible API workflows and private inference options intended for sensitive enterprise work.

Claude Renames Top Plan to Ultra and Removes 5-Hour Limit

Anthropic renamed its Claude paid plans, turning Max 20x into Ultra at $200 a month and Max 5x into Max at $100. Starting 2026-10-30, the 5-hour limit is being removed for verified Ultra accounts in stages, beginning with most existing subscribers, while weekly limits remain unchanged.

Cohere Commits Over C$4 Billion to Canadian AI Through 2031

Cohere said it is advancing plans to invest more than C$4 billion in R&D and AI infrastructure in Canada and create over 1,500 jobs through 2031. The announcement follows the Canadian government's landmark investment of up to $1.6 billion to scale Cohere's secure sovereign AI work.

NVIDIA Releases Nemotron 3.5 Super and Lightning

NVIDIA published the technical report for Nemotron 3.5 Super and Lightning, open Mixture-of-Experts hybrid Mamba-Transformer models for agentic AI. Super is a 120B-parameter model with 12B active that handles text, images and video and reaches 51.78% on SWE-bench Pro, surpassing Nemotron 3 Ultra with about 4.6x fewer active parameters, with serving up to 4,856 output tokens/s/GPU on GB200.
