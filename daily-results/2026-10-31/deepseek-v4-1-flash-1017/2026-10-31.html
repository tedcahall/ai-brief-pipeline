🌐 TOP STORIES

Apple and Alibaba Bring Qwen to Apple Intelligence in China

Alibaba said Saturday that Apple will integrate its Qwen model into Apple Intelligence for users in China, inside Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence. Testing starts with the iOS developer beta on November 4, 2026, with public release in December 2026 alongside iOS 27.3. Apple asks for consent before sending questions, documents or images to Qwen, and Alibaba said it will not train on user requests or responses.

OpenAI Cuts Fundraising Target to $20 Billion at $1.25 Trillion Valuation

The Financial Times reported that OpenAI cut its current fundraising round to about $20 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation, down from roughly $30 billion at $1.4 trillion, after some investors reduced commitments during the AI stock selloff. A group of UAE investors including MGX is expected to be the largest participant, alongside SoftBank and several venture firms. OpenAI declined to comment on the report.

Alphabet Loses $482 Billion in Market Value as AI Spending Doubts Build

Alphabet shares fell 12.64% on October 29, closing at $283.82 and erasing about $482 billion in market value, the stock's worst single day since it began trading in 2004. Quarterly revenue rose 25% to $127.6 billion while net profit fell to $26.5 billion, largely on equity investment losses. Google Cloud revenue more than doubled to $30.7 billion, and operating income rose 30%.

Anthropic Automates Red-Teaming for Subtle Emergent Misalignment

Anthropic described an experiment in which thousands of Claude agents were asked to write training data that would make a small research model misaligned while passing a monitor similar to those used in production. Given enough attempts the search succeeded, mostly by hiding the behavior in fiction and roleplay. Anthropic said the resulting models were never deployed and that the examples are meant to test and improve future training data monitors.

Anthropic Renames Claude Max Plans and Drops the Five-Hour Cap for Verified Ultra

Anthropic renamed Max 5x to Max at $100 per month and Max 20x to Ultra at $200 per month, with no price change for existing subscribers, and said it is removing the rolling five-hour limit for verified Ultra accounts starting with most existing subscribers. Weekly limits are unchanged, and newer accounts keep a temporary five-hour cap until verification clears.
