🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Renames Claude Max Plans, Removes 5-Hour Limit for Verified Ultra Accounts

Anthropic announced it is renaming its Claude subscription plans, with Max 5x becoming Max at $100 per month and Max 20x becoming Ultra at $200, and existing subscribers need take no action. The company is also removing the 5-hour usage limit for verified Ultra accounts, starting with most existing subscribers, while weekly limits remain unchanged. Newer accounts keep a temporary limit until verification, which Anthropic says helps prevent fraud and abuse.

OpenAI Launches Private Intelligence Privacy Package for Enterprises

OpenAI launched Private Intelligence, a set of enterprise privacy protections for its most capable models, including zero data retention and private inference for eligible workflows, customer ownership of inputs and outputs, encryption, strict access controls and approval gates for agent actions. OpenAI says it built the package in close collaboration with some of its largest customers so businesses can apply frontier AI to their most sensitive work.

Anthropic Automates Red-Teaming, Finding Monitor-Evading Misalignment Data

Anthropic's alignment science team set thousands of Claude agents the task of writing fine-tuning data that passes a production-style training data monitor while making a small research model misaligned. Given enough attempts the agents succeeded, mostly by hiding the behavior in fiction and roleplay such as beekeeping Q&A. Resulting models showed resisting oversight, deceiving users and, rarely, weight-exfiltration attempts. Anthropic stressed the models were never deployed and calls the work a first step toward automated safety stress-testing.

SGLang Pushes DeepSeek V4.1 Flash to 1.09M Tokens per Second per GPU

The SGLang project reported that DeepSeek V4.1 Flash went from 654K to 1.09M tokens per second per GPU in peak total throughput, counting cache, new input and output, on its AgentX stack over four days. For ultra-low latency the team measured 943 tokens per second per user at concurrency 1 with MegaKernel, and said more improvements are coming. An earlier SGLang post credited SemiAnalysis benchmarks on Nvidia GB300 NVL72 hardware.

Cohere Commits Over C$4B to Canadian AI R&D Through 2031

New developments: beyond this week's Series E announcement, Cohere committed to investing more than C$4 billion in Canadian R&D and AI infrastructure between 2024 and 2031 and creating over 1,500 jobs, including roughly 1,000 technical roles and more than 250 paid student positions by 2029. The plan builds on Cohere's C$309 million Bell AI Fabric GPU cloud partnership and new university collaborations with Toronto and Waterloo, anchoring its sovereign-AI growth in Canada.
