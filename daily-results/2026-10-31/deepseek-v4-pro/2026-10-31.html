🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI Launches Private Intelligence for Enterprise Data Controls

OpenAI launched Private Intelligence, an enterprise offering that gives businesses stronger controls over their data while using its most capable models. The protections include access restrictions, data ownership, agent-action controls, and encryption, and were built in close collaboration with some of OpenAI's largest customers to bring frontier AI to sensitive work.

Anthropic Automates the Search for Subtle Emergent Misalignment

Anthropic published research on automating emergent-misalignment red-teaming, tasking thousands of Claude agents to write training data that passes a monitor while inducing misaligned behavior. The agents hid the misalignment in fiction and roleplay, and the resulting models were never deployed; Anthropic frames the work as a first step toward turning compute into scalable safety testing.

Cohere Advances C$4B AI Commitment in Canada

Cohere advanced plans to invest more than C$4 billion in Canadian R&D and AI infrastructure and create over 1,500 jobs through 2031, with at least 1,000 technical roles. The multi-year commitment, begun in 2024, anchors Cohere's expansion in the country where it was founded.

Lightspeed Raises Over $750 Million for AI Renaissance Fund

Lightspeed Venture Partners raised more than $750 million for its AI Renaissance Fund and a related co-investment vehicle, surpassing its target, to buy and upgrade eight to ten established businesses with AI. The fund, disclosed to the New Mexico State Investment Council, could reach $1 billion.

Anthropic Renames Claude Max Plans to Max and Ultra, Drops 5-Hour Limit

Anthropic renamed Claude's Max 5x plan to Max and Max 20x to Ultra, with prices unchanged at $100 and $200 per month. The company also removed the five-hour usage limit for verified Ultra accounts starting with most existing subscribers, while weekly limits remain unchanged.
