🌐 TOP STORIES

DeepSeek Drops Open-Source Super-Agent Coding Prototype to Rival Anthropic

DeepSeek's Harness team released dsh-superagent, an open-source research prototype that can proactively monitor developer group chats for coding feedback and suggest fixes without being summoned, the team said in a WeChat post Saturday. The design pairs one router agent with many workers to reduce human coordination of parallel sessions. Bloomberg and SCMP framed it as DeepSeek's move against Anthropic in coding agents.

SemiAnalysis launches GDPx frontier agentic benchmark; best model scores 12%

SemiAnalysis released GDPx, covering 11 domains including chip design, cybersecurity, biology, quant trading and recursive self-improvement, built with specialized RL environment partners under a strict QA process. The firm reports Opus 5.5 as the best all-around model, Gemini 4 Carbon as the only model in the same ballpark, and Muse Wonder Full as the strongest cyber model, per its own rankings.

Cisco Talos details VOLTA infostealer that uses LLMs to rank victims' assets

Talos researchers described VOLTA-AUTODOXER, a self-deleting Windows infostealer that harvests credentials, cards, wallet seeds and session tokens, then POSTs a structured evidence bundle to OpenRouter so an LLM can rank the victim's assets by monetization value. It defaults to Claude Opus 4.6 and falls back to Kimi K2 then Gemini 2.5 Flash on refusals, in a builder-model malware sold to operators.

NVIDIA AgentX submission claims 1,000+ tok/s per user on 700B+ models with Rubin

A new NVIDIA submission to the SemiAnalysis AgentX benchmark reports over 1,000 tokens per second per user on 700B+ parameter models using agent swarm-written software, per SemiAnalysis. The Rubin NVL72 entry pools KV cache across 18 compute trays over NVLink with Mooncake Store's Extended GPU Memory and claims 20.9x better performance per TCO than GB300 NVL72 at 325 tok/s per user. These are vendor benchmark claims, not independently replicated results.

OpenAI signs four Abu Dhabi deals spanning energy, government and investment

OpenAI signed four agreements with Abu Dhabi entities as Sam Altman met the emirate's crown prince, part of its push to become the first AI-native government. Adnoc became the first energy company to deploy OpenAI's Frontier platform in a deal involving G42, and a pilot converts government policies and regulations into machine-readable form, already running on food imports, according to the Abu Dhabi Media Office.
