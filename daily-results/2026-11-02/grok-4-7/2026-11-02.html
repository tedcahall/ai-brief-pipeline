🌐 TOP STORIES

Meituan releases LongCat-2.5 for long agent tasks

TechNode reported on November 2 that Meituan released LongCat-2.5, a multimodal model with 1.6 trillion total parameters and about 48 billion active per token, built for sustained agent execution. Meituan said it trained on long task sequences and used large-scale reinforcement learning for complex codebases, including turning real agent failures into training tasks. It is available through Meituan's chat service and API, with GitHub and Hugging Face releases planned.

SemiAnalysis launches GDPx agentic benchmark

SemiAnalysis published GDPx on November 2, covering 11 agent domains from chip design and cybersecurity to finance, legal, and software engineering. The firm said the best model scored just 12%, finance and legal scored zero, and safety classifiers reduced scores for models including Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra. In its account, Opus 5.5 led overall and Gemini 4 Carbon led biology. These are SemiAnalysis results, not an independent replication.

Abu Dhabi signs four OpenAI agreements

Abu Dhabi's media office said Crown Prince Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed met Sam Altman and witnessed four OpenAI agreements spanning government, energy, and investment. ADNOC, G42 subsidiary Inception42, and OpenAI signed a deal the office called the first energy-sector deployment of OpenAI frontier technology, using in-country UAE inference. A food-import compliance pilot is already in production, with separate Mubadala and five-year adoption agreements.

South Korea pledges nearly double AI and chip spending

The Japan Times reported that President Lee Jae Myung told parliament on Monday that 21.3 trillion won ($16 billion) would go to chipmaking and artificial intelligence in 2027, from data centers to robotics. He said that was nearly double this year's amount and pledged to use the technology for a smarter military. The article is dated November 2 and was indexed at 05:13 UTC, before the cutoff.

SemiAnalysis reports a fast NVIDIA AgentX submission

SemiAnalysis said on November 1 that a NVIDIA AgentX submission reached over 1,000 tokens per second per user on models above 700 billion parameters, using agent-swarm software. The post also said Rubin NVL72 beat GB300 NVL72 by 20.9 times on performance per cost at 325 tokens per second per user, and by up to 9.11 times at 200. Those figures are submission claims, not an independent measurement.
