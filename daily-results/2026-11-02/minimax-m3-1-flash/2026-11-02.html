🌐 TOP STORIES

SemiAnalysis launches GDPx, a frontier-level agentic benchmark across 11 domains

SemiAnalysis published GDPx, a benchmark measuring general agentic capability across 11 economically valuable domains including chip design, cybersecurity, biology, finance, legal, quant trading, recursive self-improvement and software engineering. Tasks run in realistic environments built with specialized RL environment partners, and the best model scores only 12 percent. The firm says Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra sandbagged some domains because of safety classifiers, and calls Gemini 4 Carbon the only model near the leaders.

Cisco Talos finds a Windows infostealer that ranks victims with Claude, Kimi and Gemini

Cisco Talos documented VOLTA-AUTODOXER, a self-deleting Windows stealer that posts a structured evidence bundle to OpenRouter to rank a victim's assets by monetization value. The default model is Claude Opus 4.6, with fallback to Kimi K2 and then Gemini 2.5 Flash when a model refuses. Talos researcher Ryan Fetterman said the LLM acts as an analyst producing a dossier, not as a traditional malware capability, and that refusal resilience may become a standard design requirement.

DeepSeek publishes a Super-agent design built around one router and many workers

The DeepSeek Harness team published a design article for a Super-agent, an always-on system where a resident router holds shared context and dispatches work to parallel workers, backed by event triggers and a Watchdog for monitoring. DeepSeek says an internal version already reproduces and fixes software bugs and responds to code reviews, and that a research prototype is public but is not a product. The team lists scalability, long-term memory, cross-agent communication and security as unsolved.

A guest post on Terence Tao's blog argues mathematics must adapt to AI-authored proofs

Frank Calegari wrote a guest post on Terence Tao's blog arguing that mathematics should absorb AI rather than treat it as an existential threat. He proposes evaluating all work as if AI-assisted, hiring on personal interaction rather than paper files, and pressuring journals to adapt, noting that a rush of AI-assisted papers is already straining arXiv and the reward structure. The post follows OpenAI's October 6 release of more than 700 research papers.

NAVER raises $1 billion from NVIDIA to expand its AI factory business

NAVER confirmed in a corrected filing that NVIDIA will invest $1 billion through a third-party share allotment with a November 6 payment date, taking about a 4.8 percent stake and making NVIDIA the company's third-largest shareholder. The proceeds fund NAVER's AI factory expansion under a $10 billion project announced in July with Brookfield and NVIDIA, and the capital increase was conditional on securing at least $9 billion of computing infrastructure.
