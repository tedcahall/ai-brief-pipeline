🌐 TOP STORIES

SemiAnalysis launches GDPx benchmark; best frontier model scores 12%

SemiAnalysis released GDPx, built with specialist data partners across 11 domains including chip design, cybersecurity and biology. It says even the best model scores 12%, calls Opus 5.5 the best all-around model and puts Gemini 4 Carbon first in biology and software engineering. It says safety classifiers depress some scores; Ethan Mollick noted the absence of human baselines.

DeepSeek open-sources Super-agent research prototype for proactive coding

DeepSeek's Harness team published a Super-agent design and an MIT-licensed prototype that works on one GitHub repository and acts when asked or when team rules allow. The design separates a Router handling people from parallel Workers. The repository calls it research code, not an official product, and says some designs in the accompanying article are not implemented.

Musk says Grok Bot serving costs were unsustainably high relative to revenue

Elon Musk wrote on Sunday that "the cost of serving Grok Bot was unsustainably high relative to revenue," replying to a user as subscribers complained about usage limits. Subscriber posts described weekly meters jumping from as low as 50% to 100% over the Halloween weekend. His reply gave no detail on how limits will change.

OpenAI signs Abu Dhabi deals spanning government, energy and investment

Abu Dhabi and OpenAI announced agreements after Sam Altman met Crown Prince Sheikh Khaled, including a Machine-Readable Government initiative whose first pilot covers food imports on GPT models using UAE-based inference. Inception42 signed a five-year Frontier agreement, and Mubadala joined an investment-analysis deal. CNBC reported on October 31 that UAE investors would co-lead OpenAI's $20 billion round.

South Korea budgets 21.3 trillion won for AI, chips and data centers

President Lee Jae Myung proposed a record 820.9 trillion won 2027 budget to the National Assembly on Monday, including 21.3 trillion won for semiconductors, physical AI and AI data centers, nearly double the 2026 level. A separate 4.7 trillion won would fund a sovereign AI model that Lee described as having the "world's highest performance."
