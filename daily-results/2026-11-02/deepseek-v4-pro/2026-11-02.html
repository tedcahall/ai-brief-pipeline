🌐 TOP STORIES

DeepSeek open-sources Super-Agent, a coding agent that reads team chats

DeepSeek released the code for Super-Agent, an experimental open-source coding agent that proactively monitors developer group chats for feedback and suggests fixes without being summoned. Bloomberg reports the DeepSeek Harness team announced it on WeChat on Saturday, positioning the prototype against OpenAI and Anthropic in the coding-agent race.

SemiAnalysis launches GDPx benchmark; Claude Opus 5.5 leads at 12%

SemiAnalysis released GDPx, a frontier benchmark measuring agentic capabilities across 11 high-value domains and 206 tasks. On the debut leaderboard, Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 leads with a 12.0% success rate, followed by Gemini 4 Carbon at 10.9% and GPT-6 Astra at 9.6%. Even the best model scores only 12%, leaving substantial headroom.

OpenAI signs four Abu Dhabi deals toward an AI-native government

OpenAI signed four agreements with Abu Dhabi spanning energy, government, investment and education as Sam Altman met the emirate's crown prince. The deals include a pilot that turns government policies into machine-readable form, inside Abu Dhabi's Dh13 billion ($3.5 billion) strategy to become the world's first AI-native government.

South Korea pledges $16B for AI chips and sovereign models in 2027 budget

South Korea's 2027 budget allocates about 21.3 trillion won ($16 billion) to semiconductors, physical AI and AI data centers, nearly double the 2026 amount. CNBC reports the government is targeting what it calls the world's highest performance sovereign AI model. The overall budget rises 12.8% to 820.9 trillion won.

Cisco Talos: VOLTA infostealer uses LLMs to rank stolen victims

Cisco Talos documented VOLTA-AUTODOXER, a self-deleting Windows infostealer that follows credential theft with a large language model stage that ranks each victim's monetizable assets. The malware routes a compiled dossier through OpenRouter, defaulting to Claude Opus 4.6 and falling back to Kimi K2 then Gemini 2.5 Flash on refusal.
