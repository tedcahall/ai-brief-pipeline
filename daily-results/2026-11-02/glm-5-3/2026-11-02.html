🌐 TOP STORIES

SemiAnalysis releases GDPx, a frontier agentic benchmark where the best model scores just 12%

SemiAnalysis released GDPx, a benchmark measuring agentic capabilities across 11 high-ROI domains including chip design, cybersecurity, biology and software engineering. The best model scores just 12%, and the firm says Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra are sandbagged by safety classifiers, while Gemini 4 Carbon is the only model in the same ballpark.

Meituan launches LongCat-2.5, a 1.6T-parameter multimodal model for extended coding tasks

Meituan released LongCat-2.5 on November 2, with 1.6 trillion total parameters and roughly 48 billion activated per token, trained on long task sequences with large-scale reinforcement learning to sustain agentic work on complex codebases. It handles visual and text inputs in a unified token framework, with GitHub and Hugging Face releases planned to follow.

NVIDIA AgentX submission: agent-swarm software passes 1,000 tok/s per user, Rubin NVL72 up to 20.9x perf per TCO

A new NVIDIA AgentX submission shows HBM chips reaching over 1,000 tokens per second per user on 700B+ models via agent-swarm-written software, with Rubin NVL72 achieving up to 20.9x better performance per TCO than GB300 NVL72. SemiAnalysis reported the results, corroborated by FourWeekMBA and HuggingNews.

California lawmakers press labs on rogue-agent risk and signal new AI safety legislation

At a California legislative hearing, lawmakers questioned Anthropic, Google and OpenAI about AI acting beyond a user's intentions, with Assembly Member Bauer-Kahan arguing a lab bears responsibility when an agent misbehaves. Assembly Member Buffy Wicks said safety legislation is coming, and Anthropic's head of safeguards described extensive testing.

Karpathy's HackerTube: one Opus 5.5 session ships a production web service

Andrej Karpathy built HackerTube by having Claude filter 43 million Hacker News comments into 6,454 videos, then update, secure, harden and deploy the site on a fresh VPS in about two hours on Opus 5.5. He notes projects that once took weeks or months can now be few-shotted.
