🌐 TOP STORIES

GDPx tests AI agents across 11 expert domains

SemiAnalysis introduced GDPx, an agent benchmark covering 11 domains, from chip design and biology to software engineering. Its creators report that the highest overall score is 12%, and note that safety classifiers affect some results. The benchmark uses domain-specific tasks and environments; its scores are measurements under its own methodology, not a general measure of economic impact.

Cisco finds malware designed to use AI for victim profiling

Cisco Talos analyzed VOLTA-AUTODOXER, a Windows information stealer with an optional AI stage that ranks stolen information by potential value. The malware is configured to try Claude, then Kimi and Gemini if a model refuses. Talos describes the code and its intended workflow; the finding does not establish that every configured model generated a useful response.

Nvidia submission reports faster large-model inference

SemiAnalysis reported an Nvidia AgentX submission that surpassed 1,000 tokens per second per user on models with more than 700 billion parameters. Its comparison also cited gains in performance per total cost of ownership for Rubin NVL72 against GB300 at specified speeds. These are benchmark-specific submission results, not a guarantee of application performance.

MiniMax highlights local quantization of M3.1 Flash

MiniMax highlighted community work quantizing its 428-billion-parameter M3.1 Flash model for local deployment. The company cited Unsloth’s 143.9 GB two-bit weights alongside other hardware-specific approaches. Unsloth’s documentation describes tradeoffs between memory and fidelity, with additional runtime memory needed beyond the weight download.

Karpathy builds a curated video site with Claude

Andrej Karpathy described building HackerTube with Claude to surface videos repeatedly recommended in Hacker News comments. He said the pipeline narrowed 43 million comments to 6,454 videos and that the project took about two hours of his time. He later clarified that filtering included conventional preprocessing before sending the remainder to a model.
