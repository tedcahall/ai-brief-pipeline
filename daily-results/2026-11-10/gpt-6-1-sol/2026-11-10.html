🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2

Anthropic released Fable 5.2 for paid Claude plans and its platform, with Mythos access subject to verification for beneficial cyber and biomedical work. The models share an underlying model but differ in safeguards. Standard pricing falls to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens; reported scientific and engineering gains remain evaluation-specific.

Meta Launches Muse Ember for Low-Cost Agent Work

Meta introduced Muse Ember on Muse Code and the Meta Model API at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens. It supports a one-million-token context and native text, image, audio and video input. Meta positions it for repeated agent subtasks, with larger models handling planning; its performance comparisons depend on the cited evaluations.

Google Adds Confidential and High-Context TPU Systems

Google announced TPU 8tx for confidential inference and TPU 8i Max for large-context workloads. These are specialized systems built around existing eighth-generation silicon, not two wholly new chip designs. Google says 8tx pairs TPU 8t silicon with AMD EPYC hosts and hardware data protection, while 8i Max increases compute and memory resources for demanding agent workloads.

Baidu Reports Verified Inference Gains From ERNIE Evolve

Baidu unveiled ERNIE Evolve, an automated research system that edits code in isolated environments and retains changes after hidden evaluation and review. For the in-development ERNIE-5.5-Flash, Baidu reports 33.7% higher deterministic GPU throughput and generation speed reaching 623.6% of its initial Kunlunxin P800 baseline. The latter included enabling unused optimized paths; these are company-reported results, not equivalent hardware comparisons.

OpenAI Details Control Room for Stopping Research Inference

OpenAI described Control Room, a centralized authorization system for research inference workloads. It says further model output stops within 35 seconds after an instruction to stop a job, rather than instantly upon detection of a problem. The inference layer denies requests for stopped jobs. Fail-closed protection covers frontier research workloads, with remaining research workloads still being added.
