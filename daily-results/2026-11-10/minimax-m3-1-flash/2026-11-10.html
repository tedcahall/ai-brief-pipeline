📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, while Meta introduced Muse Ember, a smaller model built for repeated agent work. Both launches target long-running technical tasks, with Anthropic emphasizing open-ended work and Meta emphasizing cost and throughput.

Google expanded its eighth-generation TPU portfolio with confidential-inference and large-context systems, while OpenAI detailed Control Room, a centralized mechanism for stopping research workloads after a safety decision. Mistral also expanded its enterprise platform with managed agents, workflows, and apps.

Baidu reported that ERNIE Evolve beat published baselines on public automated-research tasks and improved inference for an in-development ERNIE model. These are vendor-reported results, and the evidence does not establish independent replication.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2

Anthropic launched Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 as the same underlying model with different safeguards. The company says outputs are up to 30% faster than version 5.1 and priced at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. Anthropic reports improvements in coding, reasoning, vision, robotics, and scientific work, along with external pre-release safety evaluation.

Meta Launches Muse Ember for Low-Cost Agent Work

Meta launched Muse Ember as its smallest model, aimed at tool use, coding subtasks, routing, extraction, and long-document parsing. It offers a 1M-token context window and up to 512K output tokens, and is available on Muse Code and the Meta Model API at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens. Meta's efficiency claims cite Artificial Analysis results.

Google Adds TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max for Agentic AI

Google added TPU 8tx for secure and confidential inference and TPU 8i Max for massive context windows, completing four eighth-generation systems in one year. Google says 8tx protects data and model weights in use, while 8i Max doubles compute, SRAM, and HBM bandwidth relative to TPU 8i. The post also says Gemini is now used to produce chip layouts in hours rather than weeks.

OpenAI Deploys Control Room to Stop Research Model Workloads

OpenAI says Control Room is deployed across its research infrastructure to track inference workloads and stop output after safety or security concerns. Engines check the service before producing tokens, and the system fails closed if the service is unavailable. OpenAI states the maximum delay between a stop instruction and halted progress is 35 seconds, and says it is working to reduce that to zero.

Baidu's ERNIE Evolve Automates AI R&D and Inference Tuning

Baidu introduced ERNIE Evolve, an automated research system that chooses directions, edits code, runs experiments, and applies independent evaluation and review. Baidu reports better-than-published results on NanoChat AutoResearch and NanoGPT Speedrun, plus gains on SOL-ExecBench. It also says the system passed production checks while optimizing the in-development ERNIE-5.5-Flash.
