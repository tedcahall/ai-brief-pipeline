🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 Take Top Spot on Artificial Analysis Index

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, which the company says improve coding, reasoning and vision and generate outputs up to 30 percent faster than the 5.1 models. Pricing is $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. Artificial Analysis reports Fable 5.2 scores 65 on its Intelligence Index v5, ahead of Gemini 4 Carbon at 60 and Claude Opus 5.5 at 59.

Meta Launches Muse Ember, Its Smallest and Most Efficient Model

Meta introduced Muse Ember, built for complex reasoning execution and agent work such as tool calls, coding subtasks, routing and long document parsing. The model has a 1M-token context window and accepts text, image, audio and video input, according to Meta. Artificial Analysis scores Muse Ember at 36 on its Intelligence Index, matching Claude Haiku 5.5 (max).

OpenAI Details Control Room, a System to Halt Research Model Workloads

OpenAI says Control Room is deployed across its research infrastructure to track model workloads and stop them when safety or security concerns arise. It connects every research inference workload and works with OpenAI’s misalignment monitoring systems, halting model output within 35 seconds of a decision to intervene. OpenAI says the system keeps working when other systems fail and stops model output if Control Room itself goes down.

Google Expands Eighth-Generation TPU Lineup With TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max

Google added TPU 8tx for secure and confidential inference and TPU 8i Max for massive context windows and the highest compute density per rack. Google says this is the first year it has introduced four TPU systems and that TPUs now serve nearly 100 customers, including frontier labs, trading firms and universities. The company also says customers can now deploy TPUs directly into their own data centers.

Bank of America, Blackstone, Citi and Morgan Stanley Arrange $63 Billion Financing for Anthropic Compute

Bank of America, Blackstone, Citi and Morgan Stanley announced a $63 billion financing structure to support AI computing capacity for Anthropic using Broadcom accelerators and networking equipment. A newly established special purpose vehicle will buy the equipment and lease it to Anthropic, with first-lien financing from Bank of America, Citi and Morgan Stanley and second-lien from Blackstone. Equipment deliveries are expected to begin in 2027.
