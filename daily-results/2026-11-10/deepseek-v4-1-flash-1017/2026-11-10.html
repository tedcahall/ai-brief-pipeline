🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 on Nov. 9, its most capable models for demanding technical work, with gains in coding, reasoning and vision. Anthropic reports outputs up to 30% faster than the 5.1 versions and lower prices at $8 and $40 per million input and output tokens. Mythos access is limited to verified organizations through cyber and life-sciences programs.

Meta Releases Muse Ember, a Low-Cost Model Built for Agent Work

Meta Superintelligence Labs introduced Muse Ember, its smallest and most cost-efficient model, aimed at the tool calls, routing, extraction and coding subtasks agents repeat all day. Meta priced it at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output, with a 1M-token context window. The company claims more intelligence per dollar than Claude Haiku 5.5 or GPT-6 Luna.

Google Adds TPU 8i Max and TPU 8tx to Its Eighth-Generation Lineup

Google expanded its eighth-generation TPU portfolio with TPU 8i Max for large context windows and high rack density, and TPU 8tx for confidential inference on AMD EPYC hosts. Google says this is the first time it has introduced four TPU systems in one year, and that TPUs now serve nearly 100 customers, including frontier labs and trading firms.

OpenAI Builds Control Room to Halt Model Workloads Within 35 Seconds

OpenAI detailed Control Room, an internal system that tracks research inference workloads and halts model output within 35 seconds of a decision to stop or pause a job. Inference engines must check with the service before fulfilling a request, and the system is designed to keep enforcing stops if it fails. OpenAI says it is already deployed across its research infrastructure.

Nvidia's GR00T 2 Robot Model Learns New Tasks From a Handful of Demonstrations

Nvidia introduced GR00T 2, a world-action model trained on 100,000 hours of egocentric video, at the Conference on Robot Learning in Austin. Nvidia reports 71% average success across 50 tasks with one demonstration per task and 95% with five, plus skill transfer from human and handheld demonstrations to different robot bodies. Weights and license terms were not announced.
