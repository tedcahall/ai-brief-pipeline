🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2

Anthropic introduced Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 for ambitious technical work, citing gains in coding, reasoning and vision. Fable is available on paid Claude plans and major clouds at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. Anthropic says the models share an underlying model, with Mythos access restricted to verified cybersecurity and biomedical organizations.

Meta Introduces Muse Ember for Efficient Multimodal Agent Work

Meta launched Muse Ember, its smallest and most efficient model, for complex reasoning execution and everyday agent work. The company says it can chain tool calls, run tests and make targeted code fixes. Muse Ember supports a 1M-token context window and native text, image, audio and video inputs; Artificial Analysis measured pricing of $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens.

Google Adds TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max to Eighth-Generation Lineup

Google expanded its eighth-generation TPU family with 8tx for confidential inference and 8i Max for massive context windows. TPU 8tx pairs TPU 8t silicon with AMD EPYC hosts for hardware-level data protection. TPU 8i Max combines two 8i chips, doubling compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth while fitting twice the silicon into the existing rack footprint.

OpenAI Control Room Can Halt Research Model Output Within 35 Seconds

OpenAI disclosed Control Room, a central authorization layer for research inference workloads that is already deployed across its research infrastructure. It works with misalignment monitors and stops further model output within 35 seconds of an intervention decision. The inference-layer integration fails closed if Control Room goes down, and frontier research workloads already have that protection.

Gemini 4 Agents Discover Formally Verified Quantum Programs

Google researchers built Gemini 4 agent teams that synthesize quantum programs against formal specifications and verify correctness and cost in Lean. The paper reports improvements over prior baselines of up to 290x for quantum arithmetic and 11x, 8x and 2.5x for simulations relevant to fusion, photochemistry and drug metabolism. Version two was submitted November 9.
