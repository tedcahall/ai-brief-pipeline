📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, cutting prices 20% to $8/$40 per million tokens and taking first place on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 65, though Artificial Analysis measured its max-effort cost per task at about 2.6 times Opus 5.5 and SemiAnalysis called it a marginal improvement.

Meta answered at the low end with Muse Ember at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens, Google added two TPU systems for confidential inference and long context, and Baidu said its ERNIE Evolve research agent made the in-development ERNIE-5.5-Flash about six times faster on Kunlunxin chips.

On safety, OpenAI detailed Control Room, which can halt any research model workload within 35 seconds of a decision to intervene, while Elon Musk said xAI has finished pre-training Grok 4.8 and plans a soft rollout to Bot users this week.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, Cutting Prices 20%

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, the same underlying model, with Fable carrying cyber and biology safeguards for general availability. Anthropic says both generate output up to 30% faster than the 5.1 versions at $8/$40 per million input/output tokens, down from $10/$50, and that 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours. Artificial Analysis ranks Fable 5.2 first on its Intelligence Index at 65.

Meta Launches Muse Ember, Its Smallest Model for Agent Workloads

Meta introduced Muse Ember, its smallest and most efficient model, built for repetitive agent work such as tool calls, coding subtasks, routing and long-document parsing. It costs $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens on the Meta Model API and has a 1M-token context window. Meta says it matches Claude Haiku 5.5's best Artificial Analysis score at about a third of the cost.

OpenAI Builds Control Room to Halt Research Model Workloads Within 35 Seconds

OpenAI described Control Room, a central service that tracks every research inference workload, including agentic sampling for training and evaluation, and stops model output within 35 seconds of a decision to intervene. Inference engines check each request against the service, which works with OpenAI's misalignment monitors and is built to stop output if it fails. OpenAI requires every agentic research workload to have an approved safety case.

Google Adds TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max, Four TPU Systems in One Year

Google expanded its eighth-generation TPU line with TPU 8tx, built for confidential inference with hardware-level protection of data and model weights while in use, and TPU 8i Max, which doubles compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth over TPU 8i for massive context windows. Google says this is the first time it has introduced four TPU systems in one year and that TPUs now have nearly 100 customers.

Baidu's ERNIE Evolve Automates AI Research, Speeds Up ERNIE-5.5-Flash

Baidu unveiled ERNIE Evolve, an automated research system that chooses research directions, edits code and runs experiments, with each candidate scored by a fixed evaluator hidden from all agents. Baidu says it beat the strongest public results it compared on NanoChat AutoResearch and NanoGPT Speedrun, and raised the in-development ERNIE-5.5-Flash's generation speed on Kunlunxin P800 chips to 623.6% of baseline. These are Baidu's own reported results.
