Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 for demanding technical work, while Meta introduced Muse Ember for lower-cost, high-volume agent tasks. 🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2

Anthropic launched Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, the same underlying model with different cybersecurity and biology safeguards. Standard pricing is $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens; Anthropic reports up to 30% faster output than version 5.1. Fable is broadly available, while advanced Mythos access requires verification. Independent testing also highlights higher maximum-effort task costs despite lower token prices.

Meta Launches Muse Ember for Low-Cost Agent Work

Meta introduced Muse Ember on its API and Muse Code at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens. The model supports a million-token context and text, image, audio and video inputs. Artificial Analysis reports an Intelligence Index score of 36 and about $0.14 per task at maximum effort, while noting weaker knowledge accuracy and mixed coding results.

Baidu Introduces ERNIE Evolve for Automated AI Research

Baidu introduced ERNIE Evolve, a research system that chooses experiments, edits code in isolated environments and retains changes only after evaluation and review. The company reports a 33.7% throughput gain on deterministic inference for its in-development ERNIE-5.5-Flash, preserving bitwise consistency. These are Baidu-reported engineering results, not independent replication or a release announcement for the underlying Flash model.

Google Adds Confidential TPU 8tx and Dual-Chip TPU 8i Max Systems

Google expanded its eighth-generation TPU portfolio with two systems built around existing chip designs. TPU 8tx pairs training silicon with AMD EPYC hosts for confidential inference; TPU 8i Max combines two TPU 8i chips in one package. Google says the latter doubles compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth relative to TPU 8i, targeting large-context agent workloads rather than announcing an entirely new silicon generation.

OpenAI Details Control Room to Halt Research Model Output

OpenAI disclosed Control Room, a centralized authorization system deployed across its research infrastructure. The company says it can halt model output within 35 seconds after an instruction to stop a job, using checks in the inference layer. Fail-closed behavior is active for frontier research workloads, with remaining research jobs still rolling out. This describes a safeguard, not proof that all agent actions stop instantly.
