🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Introduces Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2

Anthropic introduced Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 for demanding technical work. The company says the models improve coding, reasoning and vision. Its launch announcement describes the pair as collaborators on ambitious projects; the performance claims should be read as Anthropic’s own assessment, not independent verification.

Meta Introduces Muse Ember for Efficient Agent Work

Meta introduced Muse Ember, a small model designed for recurring agent workloads. The company says it can sequence tools, run tests and make targeted code fixes, with a one-million-token context window and native support for text, images, audio and video. These are Meta’s reported capabilities.

OpenAI Details Control Room for Stopping Research Workloads

OpenAI describes Control Room, a centralized service that tracks research inference jobs and can stop further model output when safety or security concerns arise. The company says output stops within 35 seconds after a stop decision and that frontier research workloads fail closed if the service becomes unavailable.

Google Cloud Adds Specialized TPUs for Confidential Inference and Long Context

Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian announced two specialized TPU systems: 8tx for secure and confidential inference and 8i Max for large context windows. The announcement extends the company’s TPU lineup alongside systems intended for training and real-time reasoning. The described applications are Google’s stated design goals.

Researchers Use AI Agents to Discover Verified Quantum Programs

In a preprint, researchers describe Gemini 4 agent teams generating quantum programs checked in the Lean theorem prover. They report cost improvements against prior baselines, including 290-fold for quantum arithmetic and smaller gains in selected scientific simulations. The results are research claims about formal programs, not demonstrated practical quantum-computer speedups.
