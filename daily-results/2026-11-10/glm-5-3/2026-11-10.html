🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2

Anthropic's new models for demanding technical work score above Claude Opus 5.5 on most evaluated benchmarks, including FrontierCode 2.0 at 49.1 percent and SWE v2 at 69.9 percent, with outputs up to 30 percent faster than the 5.1 versions at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. Anthropic reports the agents solved two open problems in black-hole physics, formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours with 1,000 agents, and designed three working electronic devices, with Mythos available through cyber and bio verification programs.

Meta Launches Muse Ember, Its Smallest and Most Efficient Model

Meta Superintelligence Labs introduced Muse Ember for the repeated work of agents, including tool calls, coding subtasks and long document parsing, with a 1M-token context window and native text, image, audio and video input. Meta says it matches Claude Haiku 5.5's best Artificial Analysis Index score of 36 at about a third of the cost per task, beats every GPT-6 Luna setting it tested, and is available now at $0.10 per million input tokens.

Google Adds TPU 8tx and 8i Max for Confidential and Long-Context AI

Google expanded its eighth-generation TPU portfolio with TPU 8tx, which keeps data and model weights protected in hardware during active use for regulated customers, and TPU 8i Max, which pairs two 8i chips in one package for double the compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth aimed at massive context windows. It is the first year Google has introduced four TPU systems, and TPUs now serve nearly 100 customers.

OpenAI's Control Room Can Stop Research Workloads in 35 Seconds

OpenAI deployed Control Room, a central system that tracks every research inference workload, including agentic sampling for training and evaluation, and denies further model output within 35 seconds of a stop decision. The system is enforced at the inference layer and fails closed, denying all requests if Control Room itself goes down or is tampered with; ChatGPT and the public API are not covered.

ByteDance's Seedream 5.5 Keeps Edits Stable Over 100 Rounds

ByteDance Seed released Seedream 5.5, an image generation and editing model that keeps unedited regions pixel-identical across up to 100 rounds of editing and natively outputs transparent and semi-transparent backgrounds. ByteDance says experts preferred it over GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst and Nano Banana 2.1 on text rendering and prompt realism, with the rollout beginning on Dreamina, CapCut and BytePlus, first in China.
