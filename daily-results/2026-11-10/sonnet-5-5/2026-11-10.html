🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 With Lower Prices and Faster Output

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, which it describes as the same underlying model, with Fable carrying cybersecurity and biology safeguards so it can be offered to all users. Anthropic says outputs are up to 30% faster than 5.1 at $8 per million input and $40 per million output tokens. It reports 49.1% on FrontierCode 2.0 against 45.5% for Opus 5.5, and adds an optional fast mode.

Meta Releases Muse Ember, a Small Agent Model Priced at $0.10 per Million Input Tokens

Meta introduced Muse Ember, its smallest and most efficient model, aimed at repeated agent work such as tool calls, coding subtasks and long document parsing. It has a 1 million token context window and accepts text, image, audio and video. Meta lists $0.10 per million input and $0.50 per million output tokens, and says that on Artificial Analysis it matches Claude Haiku 5.5's best score at about a third of the cost.

OpenAI Details Control Room, a System That Halts Research Model Output Within 35 Seconds

OpenAI said Control Room tracks model workloads across its research infrastructure and halts output within 35 seconds of a decision to stop or pause a job. Inference engines check with the service before fulfilling each request and refuse if the job is stopped or the service is down. OpenAI says it covers research workloads, not its external serving stack, and attributes the 35-second lag to a caching layer it is working to eliminate.

Google Adds TPU 8tx for Confidential Inference and TPU 8i Max to Its Eighth-Generation Lineup

Google announced two more eighth-generation TPU systems, bringing its total to four in one year. TPU 8tx targets confidential inference, keeping data and model weights protected in use, and Google says it offers up to 2x lower latency than TPU 8t. TPU 8i Max combines two TPU 8i chips for double the compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth. Google says TPUs now have nearly 100 customers.

Meituan Releases LongCat-2.5 Weights, a 1.6 Trillion Parameter Multimodal Model, Under MIT License

Meituan published LongCat-2.5 weights on Hugging Face on November 9 under the MIT license, one week after introducing the model, VentureBeat reports. Meituan describes a natively multimodal model with 1.6 trillion total and about 48 billion active parameters, though the repository metadata shows 1.8T. It reports 88.4 on Terminal Bench v2.1, up from 70.8 for its predecessor; these benchmark figures are vendor-reported.
