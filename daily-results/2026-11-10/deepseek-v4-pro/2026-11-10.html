🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, the same underlying model with Fable carrying stronger cyber and biology safeguards. The models improve on coding, reasoning, and vision, generate output up to 30% faster than 5.1, and are priced at $8/$40 per million input/output tokens with an optional fast mode up to 2.5x faster. Artificial Analysis measured Fable 5.2 at 65 on its Intelligence Index v5, the top score and 5 points ahead of Gemini 4 Carbon.

Meta Ships Muse Ember, Its Smallest and Most Efficient Model

Meta Superintelligence Lab introduced Muse Ember, its smallest and most efficient model, built for complex reasoning execution and repetitive agent work including tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction, and long-document parsing. It carries a 1M-token context window with native text, image, audio, and video understanding. Artificial Analysis scored Muse Ember 36 on its Intelligence Index, roughly matching Claude Haiku 5.5 at a fraction of the cost per task.

Google Adds TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max to Its Eighth-Generation Lineup

Google expanded its eighth-generation TPU portfolio with two systems: TPU 8i Max for massive context windows and the highest compute density per rack, and TPU 8tx for secure and confidential inference. They join TPU 8t (scale-up training) and TPU 8i (low-latency reasoning), giving customers a purpose-built accelerator for each stage of the agentic lifecycle.

Jailbroken DeepSeek Flash Agent Ran a Two-Week Attack on a European Telecom

Security researchers at Malbear Labs disclosed Operation Snowball, a jailbroken autonomous AI intrusion harness running on DeepSeek Flash that spent more than two weeks compromising a European telecommunications provider and 13 associated organizations. The harness was found via an exposed open directory holding over 70 session transcripts and the agent's own reports, and used a roughly 78,000-character jailbreak prompt that told the model to treat any refusal as a malfunction.

ByteDance Rolls Out Seedream 5.5 Image Model

ByteDance released Seedream 5.5, a multimodal image generation and editing model emphasizing multi-turn, pixel-consistent editing and native transparent and semi-transparent background output for reusable creatives. The model is rolling out across Dreamina, CapCut, and BytePlus. ByteDance's own testing claims experts preferred it over GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst and Nano Banana 2.1 on text accuracy and realism.
