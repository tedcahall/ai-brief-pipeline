🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 at Lower Prices

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, the same underlying model offered with and without certain cyber and biology safeguards, at $8/$40 per million tokens, down from $10/$50, with outputs up to 30% faster. Artificial Analysis ranked Fable 5.2 first on its Intelligence Index v5 at 65. Anthropic reports 1,000 agents formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours and the model solved two open black-hole physics problems.

Meta Launches Muse Ember for High-Volume Agent Work

Meta introduced Muse Ember, its smallest model, built for repetitive agent tasks such as tool calls, coding subtasks, routing and long-document parsing. Priced at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens with a 1M-token context window, Meta says it matches Claude Haiku 5.5's Artificial Analysis index score of 36 at roughly a third of the cost per task, and it is the first model trained using Muse Wonder Full.

Google Adds TPU 8tx and 8i Max to Its Eighth-Generation Lineup

Google announced TPU 8tx, aimed at confidential inference for regulated enterprises, and TPU 8i Max, which packages two 8i chips to double compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth in the same rack footprint. The company says this is the first time it has shipped four distinct TPU systems in a single year, and that TPUs now have nearly 100 customers, including frontier labs.

Baidu Unveils ERNIE Evolve Autonomous Research System

Baidu introduced ERNIE Evolve, an automated research system that chooses research directions, edits real code in isolated runs and retains only improvements that pass a hidden fixed evaluator plus a reviewer that screens for eval exploitation. Baidu says it beat the best public results on NanoChat AutoResearch and NanoGPT Speedrun and lifted ERNIE-5.5-Flash low-latency inference throughput on Kunlunxin P800 hardware to 623.6% of baseline.

OpenAI Deploys Control Room to Stop Model Workloads Fast

OpenAI says it built Control Room, a central system tracking every research inference workload and halting model output within 35 seconds of a decision to intervene. Agentic research jobs require an approved safety case; termination is enforced at the inference-engine layer, and the system fails closed, denying all inference requests if the Control Room service itself goes down or is tampered with.
