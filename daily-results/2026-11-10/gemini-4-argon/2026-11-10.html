🌐 TOP STORIES

Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 With Lower Token Pricing

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, reporting up to 30% faster generation than version 5.1 and cutting standard API prices to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. Anthropic says a 1,000-agent Fable 5.2 team formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours, while Artificial Analysis ranked Fable 5.2 first on Intelligence Index v5 at 65.

Meta Launches Muse Ember Efficient Multimodal Model for High-Volume Agent Work

Meta introduced Muse Ember, its smallest multimodal model built for high-volume agent tasks with a 1-million-token context window and up to 512,000 output tokens at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens. Independent Artificial Analysis testing scored Muse Ember at 36 on its Intelligence Index, matching Claude Haiku 5.5 at roughly one-third the cost per task.

Google Cloud Expands Eighth-Generation TPU Portfolio With TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max

Google Cloud unveiled two eighth-generation accelerator systems, TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max, marking four TPU systems launched in a single year. TPU 8tx pairs TPU 8t silicon with 5th Generation AMD EPYC hosts for hardware-encrypted confidential inference at up to 2x lower latency than TPU 8t, while dual-chip TPU 8i Max doubles compute, SRAM, and HBM bandwidth for large-context agent workloads.

OpenAI Deploys Control Room to Halt Research Model Workloads Within 35 Seconds

OpenAI detailed Control Room, a centralized fail-closed authorization service deployed across its internal research infrastructure to track and halt model workloads when safety or security concerns arise. Connected to reinforcement learning, evaluation, and agentic sampling engines alongside misalignment monitors, Control Room revokes job permissions to stop token generation within 35 seconds and blocks output automatically if the control service goes offline.

Meituan Releases Open Weights for 1.6-Trillion-Parameter LongCat-2.5 Under MIT License

Meituan published the 1.8-terabyte open-weight checkpoint for LongCat-2.5 on Hugging Face under an MIT license, featuring 1.6 trillion total parameters with roughly 48 billion active per token and a 1-million-token context window, VentureBeat reports. In Meituan's evaluations, the multimodal agent model scored 88.4 on Terminal Bench v2.1 and 64.6 on DeepSWE v1.1, though it trailed newer frontier models on Terminal-Bench 4.0.
