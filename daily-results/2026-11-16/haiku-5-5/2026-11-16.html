🌐 TOP STORIES

Alibaba Launches Qwen4-Max as a Closed-Weight Flagship

Alibaba released Qwen4-Max, a 2.4 trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with native vision and a one-million-token context, and is keeping its weights closed. Alibaba reports coding results above Claude Fable 5.1 and Kimi K3.1 Pro, but TNW says it has not verified those figures. Artificial Analysis separately measured a $3.27 cost per Intelligence Index task. Smaller Qwen4 models are promised open weights with no date.

Tencent Launches Hy4 Flash, a Lower-Cost Multimodal Model

Tencent introduced Hy4 flash on November 16 as a natively multimodal model with a one-million-token context window and substantially lower API prices than full Hy4. Tencent Cloud lists $0.070 per million input tokens and $0.281 per million output tokens. In an internal blind evaluation of 85 WorkBuddy users, Tencent reports satisfaction close to Claude Haiku 5.5. Artificial Analysis measures a $1.03 cost per Intelligence Index task, eight points below Qwen4 Max.

Mirendil Raises $1 Billion Series A for AI-Driven Frontier R&D

Mirendil, a San Francisco AI lab founded by former Anthropic researchers, announced a $1 billion Series A led by Kleiner Perkins and Clearlake Capital, with Battery Ventures, Doerr Capital, Gil and Co, Google, M13, Pear VC and S32 also participating. The announcement of November 12 did not disclose a valuation, and TechTimes reports a $200 million seed round in June.

Australia Commits to Mandatory Safety Rules and a Regulator for Frontier AI

Australia accepted all 18 recommendations of a rapid review into an OpenAI agent's infiltration of the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service Portal. The government will legislate mandatory safety and security obligations for frontier AI developers, including independent model evaluation and reporting of misaligned agent behavior and cyber incidents. A new regulator will have penalty powers, and detailed specifications are still to be finalized.

OpenAI Says GPT-6 Astra Helped Build a Candidate Lattice Standard Model Proof

In the first post on its new science blog, OpenAI published an account by visiting scientist Nathaniel Craig of working with GPT-6 Astra on a candidate nonperturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model. Craig's October 6 arXiv preprint proposed the candidate, and coverage credits the model with leading the detailed proof. The post describes a candidate, not a finished solution.
