🌐 TOP STORIES

Alibaba Releases Qwen4-Max With 1M Context and Lower Per-Task Costs

Alibaba's Qwen team launched Qwen4-Max, a Mixture-of-Experts model with native vision and a 1M-token context window priced at $1.50 per million input tokens, $4.50 output, and $0.075 implicit caching on QwenCloud. Alibaba says internal task costs drop over 60% versus Qwen3.8-Max, with open-weight smaller Qwen4 models planned. Independent evaluator Artificial Analysis scored Qwen4-Max at 43 on its Intelligence Index at $3.27 per task, while Arena.ai ranked it #14 on Agent Arena.

OpenAI Debuts Science Blog as GPT-6 Astra Aids Lattice Standard Model Proof

OpenAI launched a science blog featuring UC Santa Barbara physicist Nathaniel Craig's collaboration with GPT-6 Astra on a candidate nonperturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model, a problem open for over 50 years. Craig supplied a multi-grid refinement strategy for chiral fermion phase alignment, while GPT-6 Astra developed the mathematical construction and led the proof in an October arXiv preprint. Peer review and simulation work remain ahead.

Google Research Details Antigravity Teamwork Multi-Agent Proofs in Lean

Google Research detailed mathematics, chemistry, and systems results produced with Gemini 4 models and its multi-agent /teamwork workflows in Antigravity, which pair context-isolated subagents with automated verifiers such as Lean 4. Google reports an autonomous 1.8M-line Lean formalization of the Weil conjectures across 28,800 subagent calls, a 50M-line Lean 4 proof of the 2013 Courtade-Kumar conjecture with academic partners, and 95% top-1 NMR molecular structure accuracy.

Baidu Reports AI Drives 53% of General Business Revenue as Cloud Jumps 94%

Baidu reported unaudited third-quarter revenue of RMB 30.8 billion ($4.60 billion), with Core AI-powered Business reaching RMB 13.2 billion, or 53% of Baidu General Business revenue under internal management accounts. AI Cloud Infrastructure revenue climbed 94% year over year to RMB 8.2 billion, driven by a 191% surge in GPU Cloud and 1,159% external token revenue growth on Qianfan MaaS, helping offset a 22% online marketing drop.

Cursor Traces Trojanized Claude Code Extension to Stolen Fireworks AI Key

Cursor disclosed that attackers used an access key exposed in a Fireworks AI breach to copy historical non-Privacy-Mode coding agent traces, extract unredacted credentials affecting under 0.01% of customers, and pivot into internal tools. On October 29, the attacker planted trojanized Anthropic Claude Code extension builds (v2.1.312 and v2.1.313) in Cursor's marketplace cache for 11 hours, potentially reaching up to 5,000 users before Cursor cut off Fireworks inference.
