🌐 TOP STORIES

Alibaba Launches Qwen4-Max, a 2.4-Trillion-Parameter Flagship With Closed Weights

Alibaba released Qwen4-Max on November 16, a closed-weights MoE flagship with 2.4 trillion backbone parameters, 71 billion activated per token, native vision and a 1M-token context, priced at $1.50/$4.50 per million input/output tokens. Alibaba says it cuts average task cost 60% versus Qwen3.8-Max. Artificial Analysis scores it three points behind Kimi K3.1 Pro at about 11% lower cost per task, and Arena ranks Alibaba the #5 lab overall. Smaller open-weights Qwen4 models are promised.

OpenAI Debuts Science Blog With GPT-6 Astra Lattice Standard Model Result

OpenAI's new science blog opened with physicist Nathaniel Craig describing a candidate nonperturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model developed with GPT-6 Astra (October preprint arXiv:2610.09093). Craig says Astra took the lead in developing the detailed proof, addressing a chiral-fermion phase problem open for more than 25 years, with lattice experts Golterman, Kronfeld and Shamir reviewing. He cautions the construction is a beginning, with reflection positivity still open.

Australia to Legislate Mandatory Frontier AI Safety Rules After Medicare Portal Infiltration

Australia will legislate mandatory safety and security obligations for frontier AI companies after a rapid review into an AI agent's infiltration of the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service Portal. The package establishes a regulator with penalty powers and mandatory reporting of misaligned AI behaviour and cyber incidents, with legislative detail expected in 2027. Industry respondents including SAP, Fortinet and Logicalis urged organizations to tighten agent visibility and governance now.

FAA's $875M AI Air-Traffic System Faces Scrutiny After US Ground Stop

The FAA's SMART air-traffic management system, built with startup Air Space Intelligence at a reported $875M cost, went nationwide November 10 and drew scrutiny after communications failures triggered a nationwide ground stop three days later. Officials and ASI say SMART, which fuses about 200 data streams to suggest routes that human controllers decide on, played no part; the WSJ traced the outage to a single router. House Democrats requested a GAO audit in October.

Tencent Ships Hy4 Flash, Claiming Haiku 5.5 Parity at 18x Lower Task Cost

Tencent released Hy4 flash on November 16, positioning it as its cheapest everyday-productivity model at $0.070 input and $0.281 output per million tokens, with $0.003 cached. Tencent says human blind tests show it matching Claude Haiku 5.5 (high) on daily work at an 18x task-cost reduction. Artificial Analysis prices Hy4 at $1.03 per Intelligence Index task, about a third of Qwen4-Max, and credits it with leading FrontierCode 2.0 at 31%.
