Alibaba Launches Qwen4-Max With 1M-Token Context and Lower Prices

Alibaba launched Qwen4-Max with a 2.4-trillion-parameter MoE backbone, native vision and a one-million-token context window. Trump Administration Plans AI National Champions for Export Push

The Trump administration plans to designate more than two dozen US AI companies as national champions, including Nvidia, Amazon, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic, according to the Wall Street Journal. The program would bundle chips, models and other products for overseas buyers. The announcement could come within a week, although timing could change.

Tencent Launches Low-Cost Hy4 Flash for Everyday Productivity

Tencent launched Hy4 flash with multimodal inputs and a one-million-token context window. List prices are $0.070 per million input tokens, $0.281 per million output tokens and $0.003 for cached input. Tencent said it approached Claude Haiku 5.5 satisfaction in an 85-user blind test and cost one-twentieth of full Hy4 per WorkIndex task; these are vendor results.

OpenAI Details GPT-6 Astra's Role in Standard Model Research

Physicist Nathaniel Craig described how GPT-6 Astra helped turn his strategy into a candidate nonperturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model and took the lead on a detailed proof. Outside lattice theorists reviewed the work. Craig stresses that the candidate requires further scientific scrutiny and remains an answer in principle, not yet a practical simulation method.
