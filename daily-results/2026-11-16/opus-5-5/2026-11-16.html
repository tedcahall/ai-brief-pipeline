🌐 TOP STORIES

Alibaba Launches Qwen4-Max as Its Closed-Weight Flagship

Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen4-Max, a mixture-of-experts model with native vision and a 1M-token context window, via the QwenCloud API at $1.50/$4.50 per million input/output tokens. Alibaba says it beats Qwen3.8-Max with up to about 50% fewer tokens. Artificial Analysis scored it 43, 3 points behind Kimi K3.1 Pro. Weights stay closed; smaller Qwen4 models will get open weights.

OpenAI Says GPT-6 Astra Led Proof in Lattice Standard Model Work

In the first post on OpenAI's new science blog, physicist Nathaniel Craig describes an October preprint proposing a candidate nonperturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model. Craig writes that GPT-6 Astra turned his strategy into a construction and took the lead in developing the detailed proof, addressing the long-standing difficulty of putting chiral theories on a lattice.

Tencent Releases Hy4 flash, Its First Low-Cost Flash-Tier Model

Tencent Hunyuan released Hy4 flash, which it says matches Claude Haiku 5.5 (high) on daily work in human blind tests at an 18x lower task cost. API pricing is $0.070 input, $0.281 output and $0.003 cached per million tokens. BigGo Finance reports 224B total parameters with 12B active and a 1M-token context, trailing the flagship Hy4 on complex tasks.

Bessent Calls for Outside CEO and Dramatic Shakeup at Nvidia

New developments: Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Sunday that Nvidia's board should look outside the company for its next CEO and questioned whether the chipmaker pursued illicit AI chip sales to boost earnings. CNBC reports he also questioned Nvidia's financial disclosures. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO after Jensen Huang's resignation last Thursday.

Life Insurers' AI Debt Holdings Reach $85.2 Billion, Moody's Says

The New York Times reports that insurers are increasingly financing the AI buildout. US life insurers held $85.2 billion in AI debt as of June 30, up 24% in six months, and Moody's projects up to $185 billion by the end of next year. One example: Athene bought $2.4 billion of debt from a vehicle that buys chips and leases them to xAI.
