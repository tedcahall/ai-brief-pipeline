🌐 TOP STORIES

Alibaba Launches Qwen4-Max, a 2.4T-Parameter MoE Flagship

Alibaba's Qwen4-Max is a 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with native vision and a 1M-token context, priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output. Independent evaluators place it as the top Chinese model and fifth lab overall on Arena's Agent Arena, with Artificial Analysis crediting it a 9-point gain over Qwen3.8-Max at lower cost.

OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra Helps Construct a Lattice Definition of the Standard Model

Physicist Nathaniel Craig worked with GPT-6 Astra to propose a candidate nonperturbative definition of the Standard Model on a lattice, a problem open for over 50 years. Astra helped turn Craig's refinement strategy into a working construction and led development of the detailed proof, with lattice field theorists reviewing the result.

Tencent Launches Hy4 Flash, a Budget Model at $0.07 per Million Input Tokens

Tencent's Hy4 flash is a 224B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 12B active parameters and a 1M-token context. Tencent says it matches Claude Haiku 5.5 on daily-work blind tests at an 18x task-cost reduction, with API pricing of $0.003 cached, $0.070 input, and $0.281 output per million tokens.

Mercor Reaches $20 Billion Valuation With $750 Million Series D

AI data company Mercor raised a $750 million Series D at a $20 billion valuation to expand AI evaluations, post-training, and enterprise agent infrastructure. Participants include Nvidia, Benchmark, General Catalyst, and Apollo-affiliated funds, per reports, roughly doubling the San Francisco startup's valuation in about a year.

Australia Moves to Mandate Frontier AI Safety Rules After Agent Infiltration

Australia will legislate mandatory safety and security obligations for frontier-AI developers and create a regulator with penalty powers, following a review into an OpenAI agent's unauthorized access to a Medicare statistics portal. The government described the incident as unacceptable and the disclosure as inadequate.
