🌐 TOP STORIES

Alibaba launches Qwen4-Max with a million-token context

Alibaba released Qwen4-Max, a proprietary mixture-of-experts model that accepts text, images and video and supports a one-million-token context window. The company says its internal Work360 average task cost fell from $4.43 to $1.67 versus Qwen3.8-Max. Its benchmark comparisons are vendor-reported, and performance varies across tests.

Tencent introduces lower-cost Hy4 flash

Tencent introduced Hy4 flash for everyday work, with multimodal input and a one-million-token context window. Tencent’s announcement lists API pricing of $0.070 per million input tokens and $0.281 per million output tokens. The company’s comparison with Claude Haiku is based on its own human blind tests, not a universal performance guarantee.

Physicist describes using GPT-6 Astra on a lattice proposal

Physicist Nathaniel Craig describes working with GPT-6 Astra on a candidate lattice definition of the Standard Model. Craig says he supplied the strategy and the model helped develop the construction and detailed proof. The underlying proposal appeared in an October preprint; this is an account of a candidate, not confirmation that the problem is definitively solved.

India securities regulator plans AI disclosure and oversight guidelines

India’s securities regulator plans guidelines for AI use in capital markets, including disclosure, accountability and human oversight of critical decisions, its chairman said according to PTI. No issuance timeline was specified. The regulator already holds supervised firms responsible for their AI tools and previously consulted on broader model governance proposals.

Samsung affiliates expand their AI data center push

Samsung affiliates are expanding their role in AI data center infrastructure, from construction and financing to cooling, The Korea Herald reports. A potential acquisition of US cooling company Modine remains at the talks stage, according to prior Bloomberg reporting cited by the paper; Samsung declined to comment. No completed deal has been announced in that report.
