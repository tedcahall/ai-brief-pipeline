🌐 TOP STORIES

Alibaba Launches Qwen4-Max With One-Million-Token Context

New developments: Alibaba launched Qwen4-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with native vision and a one-million-token context window. The company says it uses up to about 50 percent fewer tokens than Qwen3.8-Max, while Artificial Analysis independently measured a nine-point Intelligence Index gain and lower cost per task. Alibaba's first-party prices are $1.50 per million input tokens, $4.50 per million output tokens, and $0.075 per million cached tokens. Weights were not released at launch, although Alibaba said smaller Qwen4 models would follow.

Tencent Launches Hy4 Flash for Low-Cost Agentic Work

New developments: Tencent launched Hy4 flash, a multimodal model with a one-million-token context window aimed at high-volume everyday productivity. Tencent says blind human tests matched Claude Haiku 5.5 on daily work at 18 times lower task cost. API pricing is $0.003 per million cached tokens, $0.070 per million input tokens, and $0.281 per million output tokens. Artificial Analysis separately reported that Hy4 cost about $1.03 per Intelligence Index task, though its index score trailed Qwen4 Max by eight points.

GPT-6 Astra Assists a Candidate Lattice Definition of the Standard Model

OpenAI published the first post on its science blog describing physicist Nathaniel Craig's work with GPT-6 Astra on a candidate nonperturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model. Craig says Astra turned a refinement strategy into a construction and led development of the detailed proof, while outside lattice theorists reviewed the work. The post stresses that the result remains a candidate requiring scientific scrutiny, more practical simulation work, and unresolved questions about reflection positivity and the continuum limit.

Apple Starts a 200,000-Person Study of Health AI Use

Apple launched the Apple Health AI Usage Study with OpenEvidence, enrolling up to 200,000 US adults to evaluate trust, comprehension, response quality and everyday actions taken with conversational health AI. Participants can ask health questions, view linked peer-reviewed citations, and optionally share selected HealthKit data. Apple says study data will not train AI models and that the app includes guardrails that direct concerning questions toward professional care. The app is not intended to diagnose or treat conditions.

OpenAI Starts a Controlled Rollout of ChatGPT Wallet

OpenAI began rolling out ChatGPT Wallet through its dots assistant for US ChatGPT Pro subscribers with eligible cards. Users approve each purchase with a security check, and the payment network supplies a token rather than exposing the underlying card number to the business or agent. OpenAI built the service with American Express, Mastercard and Visa and published a commerce playbook for businesses adapting payment, risk and web-access systems to agent-initiated transactions.
