🌐 TOP STORIES

Alibaba Launches Qwen4-Max With Native Vision and Lower API Prices

Alibaba released Qwen4-Max with native vision, a one-million-token context window and API access through QwenCloud. Input and output cost $1.50 and $4.50 per million tokens, with cached input at $0.075. Artificial Analysis reports a nine-point Intelligence Index gain over Qwen3.8 Max. Smaller open-weight Qwen4 models are promised, not yet released in this announcement.

Tencent Introduces Hy4 Flash With Low-Cost Productivity Pricing

Tencent announced Hy4 Flash at $0.070 per million input tokens, $0.281 per million output tokens and $0.003 for cached input. The company says human blind tests show daily-work performance matching Claude Haiku 5.5 on high settings at an eighteenfold reduction in task cost. Those comparative results are Tencent’s claims, not independent measurements.

OpenAI Publishes Astra-Assisted Standard Model Lattice Research Account

Physicist Nathaniel Craig describes using GPT-6 Astra to develop a candidate nonperturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model, building on his October preprint. The new account details model-assisted construction and proof work alongside human expert review. Craig stresses that scientific scrutiny remains necessary, practical simulations need further work, and reflection positivity at finite lattice spacing remains an open question.

MMFlash Targets Faster Vision-Language Model Decoding

The MMFlash preprint proposes training speculative drafters to emphasize visually dependent tokens they predict poorly, plus sparse attention for long videos. The authors report lossless decoding speedups up to 3.58 times across their multimodal tests, with primary evaluations on Qwen3.5 models. These are research-team benchmark results, not independently replicated production performance, and vary by model and workload.

SEBI Plans AI Disclosure and Human-Oversight Guidelines

India’s securities regulator plans detailed AI-use guidelines covering disclosures, accountability and human oversight of critical decisions, chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey said, according to PTI. MediaNama reports that no issuance timeline was specified. The planned rules follow an earlier consultation; they should not be confused with already enacted requirements or a newly published final rulebook.
