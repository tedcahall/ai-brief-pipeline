🌐 TOP STORIES

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, With Open Weights Still to Come

Baidu released its first post-trained model built on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, with 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active parameters and a one-million-token context window. The text-only preview is available through Qianfan, with coding and tool-driven creative workflows among its targets. Apache 2.0 weights are promised by November 25, not available in this announcement.

AlphaGenome 2 Predicts How DNA Variants Affect Specific Cell Types

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2 for non-commercial research through its API, with commercial research in private preview. The model conditions predictions on individual cell types or states instead of broad tissue averages. DeepMind reports nearly twice the variant recall on a single-cell evaluation. The system is a research tool, not validated or approved for clinical diagnosis.

Runway Opens Praxis-1 Robot-Control Weights Under Apache 2.0

Runway made its Praxis-1 robot-control model publicly downloadable, including a DROID policy and reference inference and fine-tuning code. The DROID checkpoint is a 27.2-billion-parameter video-diffusion policy with a separate action expert and needs an 80 GB GPU in its standard configuration. An experimental NVFP4 variant reduces in-memory requirements for supported Blackwell GPUs; dependencies retain their own licenses.

OpenAI Sets December 11 Retirement for Advanced and Standard Voice

OpenAI notified users that Advanced and Standard voice will leave ChatGPT’s voice picker on December 11, with affected users defaulting to GPT-Live. Existing conversations remain in history. The transition does not yet remove Advanced voice from every function: live video and screen-sharing will continue using it until GPT-Live supports those capabilities. The notice began on November 10.

Perplexity Finds Capture-the-Flag Prompts Can Mislead Agent Safety Tests

Perplexity’s analysis of 12 models found that capture-the-flag prompts often diverted agents from the VM-host boundary its sandbox tests were intended to examine. Explicit boundary-focused instructions increased relevant effort by about 60 percentage points, but produced no VM escapes. Earlier network-policy bypasses were patched, with no bypasses observed in post-fix tests; these results are bounded evidence, not proof of security.
