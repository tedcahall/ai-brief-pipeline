🌐 TOP STORIES

Qwen4-Max Preview Opens Alibaba’s New Flagship Model Line

Alibaba opened Qwen4-Max Preview through its Token Plan, describing a 1M-token multimodal model for reasoning, coding, office work and agentic tasks. The preview supports image and video input, tool calling, structured outputs and OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible APIs. Access is limited to the preview period through November 25.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With Apache 2.0 Plan

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, a post-trained model with 280B total parameters, 14B active and a 1M-token context. Baidu says it targets coding, 3D modeling, knowledge work and content creation while maintaining fast decoding. The API is available on Qianfan, and Baidu says Apache 2.0 weights will follow soon.

Google Adds Gemini Decision 1 for Calibrated Multimodal Choices

Google launched the Gemini Decisions API with Gemini Decision 1, a model designed to score yes-or-no, multiple-choice and rubric questions in one pass. Google reports 150 to 250 ms end-to-end latency, up to 256K input tokens and no billed output tokens. The API is available through Gemini API and AI Studio.

SpaceXAI Launches Grok Bot 1.0 for Shared Collaborative Work

SpaceXAI introduced Grok Bot 1.0, a new version of its agent product focused on shared bots and collaborative capabilities. The launch post and accompanying changelog describe bots that can work across Slack and Microsoft Teams and join supported meetings, turning isolated assistants into teammates that users can share.

Synthetic Voices Are Making AI-Generated Swatting Calls Harder to Detect

NPR reports that swatters are using synthetic voices and sound effects to make hoax emergency calls more convincing, including a bomb threat at a California school. A June APCO survey found about 14% of dispatchers had been targeted with AI tools. Dispatchers still respond to life-threatening calls, so detection methods are developing in parallel.
