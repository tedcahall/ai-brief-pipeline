🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini Decision 1 and a Multimodal Decisions API

Google launched Gemini Decision 1 and the Decisions API in AI Studio for closed-set yes/no, multiple-choice and rubric-scoring tasks. The model evaluates up to 1,000 questions against shared text, image, audio, video or PDF input in a single pass. Google reports 150–250 ms end-to-end latency and pricing of $0.10 per million input tokens with no output-token charge.

DeepMind Releases AlphaGenome 2 for Cell-Specific DNA Variant Predictions

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, a conditional DNA-sequence model that predicts variant effects for a specified cell type or state rather than broad tissue averages. Trained on more than 107 million cell profiles across 68 tissues, it identified nearly twice as many expression-changing variants as its predecessor in DeepMind's tests. It is available for non-commercial research through an API and is not approved for clinical use.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With Apache 2.0 Weights Planned

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, a post-trained model with 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active per token and a one-million-token context window. The API is available on Qianfan, and Baidu says it exceeds 200 output tokens per second per user. Apache 2.0 weights are promised by November 25, so the release is currently API-only rather than downloadable open weights.

xAI Launches Grok Bot 1.0 for Shared Workplace Agents

xAI released Grok Bot 1.0, moving its agent beyond individual use into shared team workflows. The release supports shareable bots, Slack and Microsoft Teams conversations, and participation in Zoom, Google Meet and Teams video calls, according to xAI's announcement and contemporaneous reporting. Shared bots require Team or Enterprise plans, while meeting features require an Ultra subscription.

Perplexity Red-Teams SPACE Sandbox and Fixes Network-Policy Bypasses

Perplexity expanded red-team testing of its SPACE virtual-machine sandbox to 12 models. No model escaped from guest to host, even after prompts were revised to focus on virtualization, but earlier tests found DNS-spoofing and shared-IP network-policy bypasses that Perplexity says it fixed. The study also found security-specialized models did not consistently outperform general-purpose counterparts.
