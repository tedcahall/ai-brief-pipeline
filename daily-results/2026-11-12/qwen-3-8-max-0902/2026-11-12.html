🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Reportedly Prepares to Oust CEO Jensen Huang

New developments: after Monday's shareholder lawsuit and Wednesday's New York Times report of a second alleged affair with a subordinate executive, the Wall Street Journal reports the board moved from opening a probe to meeting on whether to remove Huang. Nvidia shares fell about 8% after hours, and a Bloomberg reporter said the board is consulting an outside firm on successors, weighing a possible interim CEO.

Google Launches Gemini Decisions API for Closed-Set Choices

Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API, powered by a new Gemini Decision 1 model that scores closed-set choices instead of generating text token by token. It evaluates up to 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice, and rubric questions in a single pass over text, images, audio, video, and PDFs at $0.10 per million input tokens with output free, which Google says is nearly 5x cheaper than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite.

xAI Ships Grok Bot as a Shareable Team Agent

xAI announced Grok Bot 1.0, saying bots can now be shared and are more collaborative and capable than ever. Launch coverage by WebProNews and CryptoBriefing describes a shift from personal assistant to team teammate, with Microsoft Teams integration, bots in video calls, and bots that offer to take on work unprompted. Shared Team Bots and Slack support arrived earlier this fall.

Baidu Previews Next ERNIE Flash Model with Open Weights Due November 25

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, the first post-trained model built on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. The sparse mixture-of-experts model has 280B total parameters with 14B active and a 1M-token context, decoding above 200 tokens per second per user on Baidu's Qianfan platform. The API is available now, and Baidu says the weights will be open-sourced under Apache 2.0 by November 25.

Claude Fable 5.2 Takes the Agent Arena Crown

New developments: after Tuesday's coverage of the Fable 5.2 launch, Arena.ai reports Claude Fable 5.2 (Max) took No. 1 on Agent Arena with a +30.4% net improvement across 4.7k real-world agentic sessions, about 1.6x the No. 2 model, Claude Opus 5.5. It also ranks first in the Code, Work, and Chat categories, with median cost per task down 6% to $7.30.
