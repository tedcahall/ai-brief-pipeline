📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Google launched Gemini Decision 1 and a Decisions API that scores up to 1,000 closed-set questions in one pass across text, images, audio, video and PDFs, at $0.10 per million input tokens with no output-token charge. 🌐 TOP STORIES

Google launches Gemini Decision 1 for closed-set choices

Google on November 11 introduced Gemini Decision 1 and the Gemini Decisions API in Google AI Studio. It scores up to 1,000 yes/no, choice or rubric questions on one shared input across text, images, audio, video and PDFs. Google prices input at $0.10 per million tokens and bills no output tokens. It says the model leads its own suite. Artificial Analysis scored it 66, seven points behind two models it named.

DeepMind releases AlphaGenome 2 for cell-type DNA prediction

Google DeepMind on November 11 released AlphaGenome 2, a DNA model that predicts how a sequence and its variants affect one cell type or state at a time. DeepMind says training used more than 100 million cell profiles across 68 tissues and found nearly twice as many expression-changing variants as its prior model. Non-commercial access is open through the AlphaGenome API. Commercial research is in private preview. Those figures are DeepMind's.

xAI introduces shareable Grok Bot 1.0 teammates

The Grok Bot account on November 11 introduced Grok Bot 1.0 as shareable teammates for Slack, Microsoft Teams and meetings. SpaceXSI posted the same launch at 18:30 UTC, saying bots can be shared and are more collaborative and capable than ever. Neither post states a price or a model change. Secondary dates for an earlier beta disagree, so this brief treats November 11 as the introduction those accounts posted.

Alibaba opens a limited Qwen4-Max preview on Token Plan

Alibaba Cloud documents Qwen4-Max-Preview for coding, office work, frontend design and agent tasks, with text, image and video input. The page lists a 1 million-token context and a 393,216-token output budget shared with reasoning. Access is Token Plan only, from 10:00 November 11 to 24:00 November 25, 2026, China Standard Time, after which Alibaba says the preview will be retired. No parameter count or benchmark table is on the page. Flagship is Alibaba's word.

Baidu releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview and plans open weights

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, the first post-trained model on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, with 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active and a 1 million-token context. The page says the API is on Qianfan and that Apache 2.0 weights will be open-sourced soon. The ERNIE account said that date is November 25 and cited more than 200 tokens per second on Qianfan. Game and 3D examples on the page are Baidu's own demonstrations.
