Nvidia's Board Weighs Ousting Jensen Huang After Second Affair Report

Shares fell more than 9% after hours.

Google Launches Gemini Decision 1 for Fast, Calibrated Closed-Set Decisions

Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API, powered by a new Gemini Decision 1 model that scores candidate options in a single forward pass instead of generating text. It answers up to 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice or rubric questions across text, images, audio, video and PDFs in 150-250 ms at $0.10 per million input tokens, with no output billing. Google says it leads its benchmark suite with the lowest calibration error.

Google DeepMind Releases AlphaGenome 2 for Cell-Type-Specific DNA Predictions

AlphaGenome 2 predicts how DNA variants change gene expression in specific human cell types and states rather than broad tissue averages. Trained on more than 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues, it identifies nearly twice as many expression-changing variants as DeepMind's previous model, the lab says. It is available for non-commercial research through the AlphaGenome API and in private preview for commercial research.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, Open Weights Due by November 25

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, the first post-trained model on its ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, with 280B total parameters, 14B active and a 1M-token context. The API is live on Baidu's Qianfan platform, and Baidu says the weights will be open-sourced under Apache 2.0 by November 25. Arena reports it trending toward the No. 2 open model in Code Arena WebDev at 1668 points.

SpaceX's AI Unit Moves Grok Bot 1.0 Out of Beta With Proactive Team Agents

Grok Bot 1.0 turns the beta AI teammates into shareable Team Bots that work in Slack, Microsoft Teams and video calls, learn from everyone they work with, and offer to take on unfinished work without being asked. The company also cites faster, more reliable browser use and document and presentation building. Team Bots require Team or Enterprise plans; meetings come with Team, Enterprise and Ultra.
