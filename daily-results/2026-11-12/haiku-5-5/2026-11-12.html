🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini Decisions API, Built on Gemini Decision 1

Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API in Google AI Studio, powered by Gemini Decision 1 (gemini-decision-1). The endpoint scores yes/no, multiple-choice and rubric questions, up to 1,000 per request, across text, images, audio, video and PDFs, returning probabilities and confidence scores. Google reports $0.10 per million input tokens with zero output tokens billed and the lowest calibration error on its benchmarks; these figures are company-reported.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With Open Weights Pending

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, the first post-trained model built on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. It has 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active and a 1-million-token context, and its API is live on the Qianfan platform. Baidu says the weights will be open-sourced under Apache 2.0 soon, and its X post gives November 25. Capability claims are company-reported.

Google DeepMind Unveils AlphaGenome 2 for Cell-Type DNA Variant Prediction

DeepMind announced AlphaGenome 2, a model that predicts how DNA variants change gene activity in specific human cell types rather than broad tissue averages. The company says it was trained on more than 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues and finds nearly twice as many expression-altering variants as its prior state-of-the-art model. It is available for non-commercial research through the AlphaGenome API, with private preview for commercial research.

Perplexity Finds Standard CTF Prompt Distorted Red-Team Tests of Its Agent Sandbox

Perplexity published red-team research on SPACE, the VM sandbox behind its Computer agents, testing 12 models on escaping the VM to take a host credential and bypassing the network policy to reach a blocked URL. The company found the standard Capture the Flag setup steered agents away from the security boundary under test, and that security-specialized models did not reliably do better. Per the company, post-fix testing found no bypasses.

Anthropic Opens IPO Roadshow for Class A Offering at $70 to $78 per Share

New developments: Anthropic launched its IPO roadshow on November 10 for a proposed offering of 825,000,000 Class A shares, 801,351,354 of them from Anthropic, at an expected $70.00 to $78.00 per share on Nasdaq as ANT. The amended S-1 describes a $26.0 billion concurrent private placement contingent on closing, and the registration statement is not yet effective. The release does not state gross proceeds; the terms imply about $56 billion to $63 billion before fees.
