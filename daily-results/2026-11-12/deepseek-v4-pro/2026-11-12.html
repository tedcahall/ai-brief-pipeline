🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini Decisions API With Decision 1 Model

Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API, powered by its new Gemini Decision 1 model, for closed-set yes/no, multiple-choice and rubric-scoring decisions across text, images, audio, video and PDFs in a single pass, evaluating up to 1,000 questions. Google reports the model leads benchmark evaluations with the lowest calibration error and prices it at $0.10 per million input tokens with no output-token charge, roughly five times cheaper than Flash-Lite.

DeepMind Releases AlphaGenome 2 to Predict Cell-Type DNA Variant Effects

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, a DNA-sequence model that predicts how DNA and its variants affect individual human cell types and states, trained on more than 100 million cell profiles across 68 tissues. The model nearly doubles detection of disease-linked causal variants on single-cell immune data versus its predecessor and is available now for non-commercial research through the AlphaGenome API and Colab tutorials.

Baidu Ships ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With Apache 2.0 Weights Due by Nov 25

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, a post-trained model built on its ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base with 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active per token, a one-million-token context and more than 200 tokens per second per user on Baidu Qianfan. The model is free on OpenRouter until November 30, and Baidu says the weights will be open-sourced under Apache 2.0 by November 25.

Perplexity's SPACE Red-Team Finds CTF Prompts Skew Sandbox Security Tests

Perplexity published Escaping SPACE: Part II, finding that standard Capture-the-Flag prompts caused nine of 12 models to spend at most 4% of effort probing the VM-host boundary in its open-source Firecracker-based SPACE sandbox, while security-tuned GPT-5.6 Cyber missed a DNS-spoofing flaw its base model found. No agent escaped, and Perplexity says it patched the network bypasses with no post-fix bypasses.

xAI Launches Grok Bot 1.0 With Team Collaboration and Shareable Bots

xAI launched Grok Bot 1.0, moving its AI-teammate product out of beta and adding shareable Bots plus collaboration across Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Zoom and Google Meet video calls. Bots run on their own computers in parallel around the clock and are included with Premium, Super, Ultra, Teams, and Enterprise subscriptions.
