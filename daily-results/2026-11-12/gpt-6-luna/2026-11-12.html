🌐 TOP STORIES

Google DeepMind Introduces AlphaGenome 2 for Cell-Specific Genetic Predictions

Google DeepMind says AlphaGenome 2 predicts how DNA variants affect specific human cell types and states, rather than averaging across broad tissues. Trained on more than 107 million cell profiles from 68 tissues, it identifies nearly twice as many expression-linked variants as its predecessor on the company’s reported evaluation. The API is available for noncommercial research; commercial access is in private preview.

Perplexity Finds Its Sandbox Red-Team Prompts Can Miss the Security Boundary

Perplexity reports testing 12 models on two tasks against SPACE, its virtual-machine sandbox: stealing a host credential through an escape and reaching a blocked URL by bypassing network policy. The company says a conventional capture-the-flag framing sometimes diverted agents from the intended boundary, and security-specialized models did not consistently perform better. This is a company-run evaluation, not evidence of a confirmed external breach.

New York Opens RAISE Act Registration Ahead of January 2027 Requirements

New York opened a registration portal for frontier AI developers covered by its RAISE Act, with registration due January 1, 2027. TechCrunch reports that firms above $500 million in annual revenue must publish safety frameworks, report critical incidents within 72 hours, and issue public transparency reports. The law also establishes a state office to oversee covered labs, with civil penalties of up to $1 million for an initial violation and $3 million for later violations.

Japan Weighs Binding Safety Orders and Penalties for AI Businesses

Japan is considering requiring AI-related businesses to take safety measures and allowing binding government orders after incidents involving advanced AI, with penalties for noncompliance. Kyodo, citing a source familiar with the matter, says a closed-door working group discussed the proposal and will examine which developers and service providers should be covered. The group aims to outline possible legal revisions by year-end; this remains a proposal, not enacted law.

ElevenLabs and NVIDIA Expand Nemotron-Powered Voice-Agent Partnership

ElevenLabs and NVIDIA announced an expanded partnership on November 11. NVIDIA’s Nemotron 3.5 Super VL is now offered in ElevenAgents, while ElevenLabs says it plans to add 15 megawatts of compute using NVIDIA systems and is deploying a sovereign voice agent for Poland’s national e-Health platform. Benchmark and scale claims in the announcement are company-reported; the integration and planned infrastructure are the concrete disclosed steps.
