🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Prepares to Oust Jensen Huang, WSJ Reports

New developments: the Wall Street Journal reported Wednesday that Nvidia's board is preparing to remove co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang, with directors meeting as soon as Wednesday, days after the board opened an independent probe into his admitted affair. CBS News reports the administration is aware of the plan and that shares fell more than 8% after hours, extending a market-value decline of more than $450 billion since Nov. 8.

DeepMind Releases AlphaGenome 2 to Predict DNA Variant Effects in Specific Cell Types

Google DeepMind announced AlphaGenome 2 on Nov. 11, a redesigned DNA sequence model that predicts how variants affect gene activity in one tissue, cell type or cell state at a time rather than across bulk tissue averages. DeepMind says it was trained on more than 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues and finds nearly twice as many expression-changing variants as its predecessor. It is available for non-commercial research via the AlphaGenome API.

Google Launches Gemini Decisions API With Gemini Decision 1 for Closed-Set Choices

Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API in Google AI Studio on Nov. 11, powered by a new Gemini Decision 1 model that scores candidate options in a single forward pass instead of generating text. One multimodal input of up to 256K tokens can carry up to 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice or rubric questions and returns calibrated probabilities in 150 to 250 ms. Google prices it at $0.10 per million input tokens with no output tokens billed.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, Promises Apache 2.0 Weights by Nov. 25

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview on Nov. 11, the first post-trained model built on its ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, with 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active and a 1 million-token context. Baidu says it exceeds 200 tokens per second per user on Qianfan and targets coding, 3D modeling and agentic knowledge work. The API is live now, and Baidu says the weights will be open-sourced under Apache 2.0 by Nov. 25.

Runway Open-Sources Praxis-1, a 27B World Action Model for Robot Control

Runway released Praxis-1 on Nov. 11, a 27-billion-parameter world action model that turns video pretraining into control signals for real robots, with open weights and code under Apache 2.0, including commercial use. Runway reports a 46.2% success rate on NVIDIA's RoboLab-120 leaderboard, which it says leads listed open robot foundation models; those figures are the company's own. vLLM-Omni added day-zero support and NVIDIA published an NVFP4 checkpoint for the RTX 5090.
