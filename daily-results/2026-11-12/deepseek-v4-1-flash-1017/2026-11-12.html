🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini Decision 1 and the Gemini Decisions API

Google introduced Gemini Decision 1 and a Decisions API in Google AI Studio that scores one multimodal input of up to 256K tokens against as many as 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice or rubric questions in a single pass. Google reports 150 to 250 milliseconds end to end and prices input at $0.10 per million tokens with no output charge. Artificial Analysis scored it 66 on its Decision Index, behind leaders at 73.

Anthropic Sets IPO Price Range at $70 to $78, Implying a $1.4 Trillion Valuation

New developments: Anthropic's updated S-1 prices 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78, a $57.75 billion to $64.35 billion raise implying up to $1.404 trillion fully diluted. The filing discloses a private $26 billion sale of non-voting Class N stock at the IPO price to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC, plus run-rate revenue of $81 billion at the end of October. Trading is expected near November 19 under ticker ANT.

DeepMind's AlphaGenome 2 Predicts Variant Effects Cell by Cell

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, a DNA sequence model that predicts how variants affect gene activity in specific cell types and states instead of tissue averages. It was trained on more than 100 million cell profiles across 68 tissues and identifies nearly twice as many expression-changing variants as the previous model. DeepMind says it is available for non-commercial research through the AlphaGenome API and in private preview for commercial use.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With Open Weights Due by November 25

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, the first post-trained model on its ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, with 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active per token and a 1 million token context. Baidu says it serves above 200 tokens per second on Qianfan and plans Apache 2.0 open weights by November 25. The company claims strength in coding, 3D modeling and long-horizon agent work, and Arena's Code Arena ranks it near the top open models.

Alibaba Opens a Qwen4-Max Preview Limited to Its Token Plan

Alibaba opened a Qwen4-Max preview to Token Plan subscribers through the end of November, also reachable inside QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen app and QwenStudio. The model accepts text, images and video with a 1 million token context and a 393,216 token output cap shared with reasoning. Alibaba has published no benchmarks, parameter count, model card, per-token price or weights, and has not dated the full Qwen4 release.
