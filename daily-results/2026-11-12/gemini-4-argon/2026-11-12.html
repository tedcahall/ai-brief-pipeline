🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini Decisions API and Gemini Decision 1 for Single-Pass Multimodal Scoring

Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API in Google AI Studio, powered by its new Gemini Decision 1 model. Instead of decoding text token by token, the endpoint evaluates up to 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice, and rubric-scoring questions in a single forward pass across up to 256,000 tokens of text, images, audio, video, and PDFs. Google reports 150 to 250 millisecond end-to-end latency at $0.10 per million input tokens with zero output tokens billed.

Google DeepMind Releases AlphaGenome 2 to Predict Cell-Specific DNA Variant Effects

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, a DNA sequence model trained on more than 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues to predict how genetic variants affect specific human cell types and states. DeepMind reports the model identifies nearly twice as many gene-expression-altering variants as its predecessor and adapts to unseen cell contexts using small laboratory samples without retraining. AlphaGenome 2 is available via API for non-commercial research and in private commercial preview.

Baidu Releases 280B-Parameter ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With Open Weights Due by November 25

Baidu launched ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, the first post-trained model built on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. The Mixture-of-Experts architecture pairs 280 billion total parameters with 14 billion active parameters, hybrid sparse attention, and a 1-million-token context window. Baidu says the model generates over 200 tokens per second on its Qianfan platform across long-horizon coding, 3D CAD scripting, and game development, with Apache 2.0 open weights scheduled for release by November 25.

xAI Launches Grok Bot 1.0 With Shared Multiplayer Teammates for Slack, Teams and Video Calls

xAI released Grok Bot 1.0, upgrading its beta agent platform into shareable Team Bots that collaborate across Slack, Microsoft Teams, and video calls. According to xAI, the bots maintain shared organizational context across team members, join live meeting chats to surface relevant research, and proactively monitor projects to draft materials or follow up on unfinished tasks. The update also improves browser reliability and lowers compute overhead for document and presentation generation.

Perplexity Red-Teams SPACE Agent Sandbox Across 12 Models, Exposing CTF Evaluation Blind Spots

Perplexity published Part II of its security evaluation of SPACE, the virtual-machine sandbox isolating Perplexity Computer agents. Testing 12 frontier models on host-escape and network-bypass tasks, researchers found standard Capture the Flag prompts caused 9 of 12 models to spend at most 4% of their effort probing the VM-host virtualization layer. Explicitly naming the boundary increased targeted effort by roughly 60 percentage points, while all VM escape attempts and post-patch network bypasses failed.
