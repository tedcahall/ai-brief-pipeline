🌐 TOP STORIES

DeepMind's AlphaGenome 2 Predicts DNA Variant Effects in Individual Cell Types

Google DeepMind announced AlphaGenome 2 on November 11, a next-generation DNA sequence model that predicts how DNA sequences and their variations impact specific human cell types rather than whole tissues. Trained on more than 100 million cell profiles across 68 tissues, it adapts to new lab data without retraining and is available for non-commercial research via API and Colab.

Google Launches Gemini Decisions API With the Gemini Decision 1 Model

Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API on November 11, powered by the new Gemini Decision 1 model, which scores up to 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice and rubric questions across text, images, audio, video and PDFs in a single pass instead of generating text token by token. Google reports $0.10 per million input tokens with output tokens billed at zero.

Alibaba Opens a Qwen4-Max Preview Ahead of the Qwen4 Family

Alibaba's Qwen team announced on November 11 that Qwen4 is coming and put the flagship Qwen4-Max into preview on Alibaba's Token Plan subscription, with early access through QwenWork, Qoder, the Qwen app and QwenStudio. Reports from the rollout describe a 1M-token context preview with no published benchmarks, parameter count or weights yet.

Baidu's ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Moves Toward Open Apache 2.0 Weights

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview on November 11, a 280B-parameter model with 14B active parameters and a 1M-token context, with improvements claimed in coding, agentic tasks, knowledge work and deep research. Baidu says the weights will be open-sourced under Apache 2.0 soon; Arena.ai's Code Arena shows it trending near the top of open models.

Perplexity's "Escaping SPACE: Part II" Red-Teams 12 Models in Its Agent Sandbox

Perplexity published follow-up research on November 11 from red-teaming SPACE, the Firecracker microVM sandbox behind Perplexity Computer, giving 12 models root inside the guest VM with two goals: escape to the host and bypass network policy. No model achieved a VM-to-host escape; the report also finds standard Capture the Flag task design steers agents away from the boundary under test and that cybersecurity-specialized models did not outperform general ones.
