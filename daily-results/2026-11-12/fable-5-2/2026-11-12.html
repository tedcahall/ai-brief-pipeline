🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Prepares to Oust CEO Jensen Huang as Shares Slide

New developments: Nvidia's board is preparing to remove CEO Jensen Huang, The Wall Street Journal first reported, and sources told CBS News the Trump administration is aware of the plan. Shares fell more than 8 percent after hours, extending a market value loss of more than $450 billion since the board's probe was announced on November 8. The Next Web, citing Reuters, reported that directors met late Wednesday without announcing a decision.

Google Launches Gemini Decisions API With Gemini Decision 1 Model

Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API, an endpoint for closed-set decisions powered by the new Gemini Decision 1 model. It scores up to 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice and rubric questions against one multimodal input in a single forward pass, with 150 to 250 millisecond latency at $0.10 per million input tokens and no billed output tokens. Artificial Analysis scored it 66 on its Decision Index, seven points behind the leaders.

DeepMind Releases AlphaGenome 2 to Predict DNA Variant Effects by Cell Type

Google DeepMind announced AlphaGenome 2, a DNA sequence model that predicts how variants affect specific human cell types and states rather than broad tissue averages. Trained on more than 100 million individual cell profiles across 68 tissues, it identifies nearly twice as many expression-changing variants as its predecessor, DeepMind says. It is available for non-commercial research through the AlphaGenome API and in private preview for commercial research.

Alibaba Opens Two-Week Qwen4-Max-Preview on Its Token Plan

Alibaba opened Qwen4-Max-Preview, the first model of its Qwen4 generation, to Token Plan subscribers from November 11 through November 25, after which the preview model will be retired. Documentation lists text, image and video input, a one-million-token context window and up to 393,216 output tokens shared between thinking and the answer. TechTimes notes Alibaba has published no benchmarks, model card or per-token price.

SpaceXSI Launches Grok Bot 1.0 With Shared Team Bots for Slack and Teams

SpaceXSI, the SpaceX AI unit behind Grok, launched Grok Bot 1.0, an update to the AI teammates it introduced in beta. Bots can now be shared across a team and brought into Slack, Microsoft Teams and video calls, can offer to take on unfinished work proactively, and are described as faster and more reliable at browser tasks. The company says bots respect individual permissions and ask before sensitive actions.
