🌐 TOP STORIES

Baidu Introduces ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, With Open Weights Promised Later

Baidu introduced the first post-trained model built on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. The company reports 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active parameters, a one-million-token context and more than 200 tokens per second per user on Qianfan. Weights are promised by November 25, not already available; the speed figure is Baidu’s claim.

Runway Opens Praxis-1 Robot-Control Model Under Apache 2.0

Runway says Praxis-1 is now available to everyone with open weights under Apache 2.0, translating video pretraining into control for real robots. The accompanying vLLM-Omni integration accepts camera observations, robot state and task instructions to generate action chunks. Its maintainers report offline inference and serving support qualified on one H100 80GB GPU; that is not proof of general robotic reliability.

AlphaGenome 2 Predicts DNA-Variant Effects in Specific Cell Types

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2 for non-commercial research through its API, with commercial research in private preview. Trained on more than 100 million cell profiles, it conditions DNA predictions on particular cell types and states. DeepMind reports improved variant recall over its earlier model. These are research predictions, not a clinically validated diagnostic tool.

Perplexity Finds Capture-the-Flag Prompts Can Mislead Agent Security Tests

Perplexity’s expanded SPACE study evaluated 12 models and found that standard capture-the-flag prompts often diverted effort from the VM-host boundary. More focused prompts increased virtualization-layer effort by roughly 60 percentage points, but no run achieved a VM-to-host escape. Network-policy bypasses were a separate finding; Perplexity says it fixed those flaws and observed no bypasses in post-fix testing.

ElevenLabs Adds NVIDIA Nemotron and Expands Sovereign Voice-Agent Infrastructure

ElevenLabs and NVIDIA expanded their collaboration, adding Nemotron 3.5 Super VL to ElevenAgents and planning 15 MW of additional Vera Rubin capacity. The companies also describe an on-premises voice agent for Poland’s national appointment system, using confidential computing to keep citizen data within government infrastructure. Proposed robotics and on-device applications remain exploratory rather than announced deployments.
