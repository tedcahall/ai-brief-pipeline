🌐 TOP STORIES

Google Launches Gemini Decision 1 and Decisions API for Single-Pass Multimodal Choices

Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API in Google AI Studio, powered by Gemini Decision 1, to evaluate up to 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice or rubric questions in one pass across text, images, audio, video and PDFs up to 256K tokens. Priced at $0.10 per million input tokens with zero output charges, it scored 66 on Artificial Analysis's Decision Index v2.1, leading reasoning subsets while trailing overall leaders Drex Max v1.1 and GLiDE v1.2 at 73.

Baidu Releases 280B-Parameter ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With Open Weights Due by Nov. 25

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, its first post-trained model on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, combining a Multi-Router Latent Mixture-of-Experts architecture of 280 billion total and 14 billion active parameters with a 1-million-token context window. Baidu reports over 200 tokens per second per user on Qianfan, offers free OpenRouter access through Nov. 30, and plans Apache 2.0 open weights by Nov. 25 as it trends second among open models on Code Arena WebDev.

Google DeepMind Releases AlphaGenome 2 to Predict Single-Cell DNA Variant Effects

Google DeepMind unveiled AlphaGenome 2, a conditional DNA sequence model trained on over 107 million cell profiles across 68 tissues to predict how genetic variants alter gene expression, chromatin accessibility and DNA methylation inside specific cell types. On the TenK10K immune dataset, DeepMind reports 30 percent causal-variant recall versus 16 percent for fine-tuned AlphaGenome 1, and the model adapts to unseen cell states without retraining via an API for non-commercial research.

Runway Open-Sources 27B Praxis-1 World Action Model for Robotics Under Apache 2.0

Runway published open weights, inference code and fine-tuning code under the Apache 2.0 license for Praxis-1, a 27.2-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts video-diffusion policy that translates video pretraining into 15 Hz robot control. The Hugging Face release includes base, single-arm DROID and bimanual YAM checkpoints plus an RTX 5090 NVFP4 build that shrinks runtime memory from 54.4 GB to 17.5 GB, alongside day-zero vLLM-Omni serving support.

Nvidia Board Meets on Ousting CEO Jensen Huang After Second Reported Affair

New developments: Nvidia's board convened late Wednesday to decide whether to remove CEO Jensen Huang, CNBC confirmed after The Wall Street Journal reported directors were preparing to force him out and The New York Times reported a romantic relationship with Nvidia health care vice president Kimberly Powell. The escalation beyond Sunday's probe into an earlier affair with an alleged Chinese spy sent Nvidia shares down over 9 percent after hours.
