🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO

Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO and board director, effective immediately, less than two weeks after he resigned. The company said senior U.S. government input left the board confident his return poses no national security risk. Colette Kress continues as CFO, an independent committee investigation continues, and Fortune reported the shares were up about 34% at midday.

Z.ai Launches GLM-5.4, Its First Loop Transformer

Z.ai introduced GLM-5.4, which loops 28 layers of its GLM-5 base model for two iterations at 750B scale to deepen reasoning without adding parameters. The company says it extends coding to video, games, 3D shaders and CAD, adds vision input and supports 1M-token context. It is live for GLM Coding Plan users; API access follows a safety evaluation, and weights will be released later.

OpenAI Cyber Pipeline Yields 948 Linux Root Exploits and Patches

OpenAI said a five-stage agent pipeline built on an upcoming cyber model, which it called GPT-6 Cyber on X, dynamically reproduced 1,724 Linux kernel vulnerabilities and produced 948 local privilege escalation exploits, with candidate patches for maintainer review. The mid-October scan cost about $1.21 million in API credits. Separately, OpenAI and Cisco released Shirudo, an open-source eBPF runtime for shielding unpatched systems.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Weights Under Apache 2.0

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, the first post-trained model built on its ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, as open weights on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0. The mixture-of-experts model has 280B total and 14B active parameters with a 1M-token context, and Baidu highlights coding, 3D modeling and knowledge work. The company says post-training is ongoing, with API access on its Qianfan platform.

Sierra Raises $1.75 Billion at a $30 Billion Valuation

Customer-experience agent company Sierra, co-founded by Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor, raised a $1.75 billion Series F led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed, doubling its valuation to $30 billion within six months, TechCrunch reported. Sierra says it serves almost half of the Fortune 50 and doubled run-rate revenue to $400 million in five months. It plans to expand its agent platform and sales team.
