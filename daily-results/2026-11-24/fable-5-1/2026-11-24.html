🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as President and CEO

Nvidia said its board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as President, CEO and board director, effective immediately, after discussions that satisfied directors about his candor and ability to lead. The board said input from senior levels of the US government left it confident the reinstatement poses no national security risk. An independent committee's investigation into a reported foreign intelligence effort targeting Huang will proceed, and Nvidia is creating a Chief Compliance Officer role.

Z.ai Launches GLM-5.4, Its First Loop Transformer Model

Z.ai introduced GLM-5.4, describing it as the start of "Generalized Coding," in which code becomes the foundation for producing video, games, 3D shaders and CAD models, and adding vision input. Its Loop Transformer reuses 28 layers of the GLM-5 base model across two iterations for deeper reasoning without adding parameters; Z.ai claims intelligence comparable to 2T-3T-parameter models. It is available to GLM Coding Plan subscribers; API access follows a safety evaluation, with weights later.

OpenAI and Cisco Open-Source Shirudo to Shield Running Linux Systems

OpenAI and Cisco released Shirudo, an open-source Linux runtime that uses eBPF to load cryptographically signed "shields" that block vulnerable code paths without restarting a service, giving defenders time to test and roll out patches. In a controlled test, a Codex-generated shield blocked a Java4Shell exploit attempt against a running Elasticsearch service. Shirudo is licensed under BSD 2-Clause, uses TPM-backed attestation records, and OpenAI invited developers and operators to contribute.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Weights Under Apache 2.0

Baidu's ERNIE team published ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0, a mixture-of-experts model with 280B total parameters, 14B active per token and a 1M-token context, built on the earlier ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. Baidu describes it as an intermediate post-training checkpoint aimed at coding, game development, 3D modeling, knowledge work and video creation, says post-training is ongoing, and offers the API on its Qianfan platform. Capability claims are Baidu's own.

DeepMind's Gemini 4 Agent Teams Cut Quantum Simulation Costs Up to 11.8x

Google DeepMind and Google Quantum AI used teams of Gemini 4 agents, with every result proved in Lean, to optimize quantum circuits for scientific simulation. The agents cut the computing cost of an inertial confinement fusion calculation by 11.8x and reduced required error-corrected qubits from 5,650 to 4,338, with 8x and 2.5x gains on a photochemistry molecule and the Cytochrome P450 enzyme. DeepMind open-sourced the Lean library and verified solutions on GitHub.
