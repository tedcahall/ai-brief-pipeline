🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO and Board Director

Nvidia's board voted unanimously on November 23 to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO, and director after receiving input from senior U.S. government officials that his return poses no national security risk. Colette Kress returns to CFO after serving as interim CEO. The board's independent investigation continues alongside a new National Security and Export Control Committee and a search for a Chief Compliance Officer.

Z.ai Launches GLM-5.4 Built on Loop Transformer Architecture

Z.ai introduced GLM-5.4, its first flagship model built on a Loop Transformer architecture that runs 28 layers of its 750B-scale GLM-5 base model across two iterations per pass. Using gated progressive updates, a hybrid KV cache, and sparse attention index reuse, GLM-5.4 supports a 1M-token context and 128K output tokens. The model targets generalized coding across software, video, games, and 3D modeling.

CrowdStrike Agrees to Acquire Zscaler in $43.9 Billion All-Stock AI Security Deal

CrowdStrike agreed on November 23 to acquire network security provider Zscaler in an all-stock transaction valued at $43.9 billion, giving Zscaler shareholders 1.135 CrowdStrike shares per share. Both boards unanimously approved the deal, which is expected to close in the second half of CrowdStrike's fiscal 2028. Management aims to unify endpoint and network security into a single control plane for agentic AI applications.

OpenAI and Cisco Open-Source Shirudo Runtime to Shield Linux Systems

OpenAI and Cisco launched Shirudo, a BSD 2-Clause open-source Linux runtime that uses eBPF programs to deploy cryptographically signed, TPM-attested temporary shields on running workloads without restarts. Designed to protect services while permanent patches are prepared, Shirudo was demonstrated blocking a Java4Shell exploit against a live Elasticsearch instance using a shield generated by OpenAI's Codex.

UN Chief Calls for Multilateral AI Safety Framework Within a Year

Speaking at the close of the Bridgetown Special Summit on AI Safety in Barbados on November 23, UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres urged Member States to begin negotiations on a multilateral AI Risk Management Framework. Guterres called for shared testing, transparency standards, independent model oversight, and a Global Fund for AI Capacity-Building so developing nations can evaluate AI safety locally.
