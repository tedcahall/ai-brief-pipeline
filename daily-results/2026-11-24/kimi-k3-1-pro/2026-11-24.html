🌐 TOP STORIES

Z.ai Launches GLM-5.4, Its First Flagship Loop Transformer

Z.ai introduced GLM-5.4 with weight-shared layers that run for multiple iterations, deepening computation without increasing parameter count. The release targets coding for video, games, 3D shaders and CAD, and adds vision capabilities. Z.ai said general API access will follow safety evaluation and that weights will be released responsibly, without committing to a date.

Nvidia Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as President and CEO

Nvidia’s board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director, effective immediately. The company said the board received input from senior US government officials and concluded his return posed no national security risk. An independent committee’s investigation will continue, while Nvidia creates a compliance committee and recruits a chief compliance officer.

OpenAI and Cisco Open-Source Shirudo for Live Linux Defense

OpenAI and Cisco released Shirudo, a BSD-licensed runtime that lets agents develop targeted eBPF shields for running Linux systems while permanent patches are prepared. Operators approve signed shields before deployment. In a controlled test, a Codex-generated shield blocked a Java4Shell exploit against Elasticsearch without restarting the service, demonstrating temporary protection without downtime.

DeepMind Uses Gemini 4 Agent Teams to Design Lean-Verified Quantum Circuits

New developments: Google DeepMind used independent Gemini 4 agent teams and Lean proofs to improve quantum algorithms, moving beyond its earlier quantum-optics result. Reported computing-cost reductions reached 11.8 times for a fusion calculation and eight times for a light-sensitive molecule. DeepMind open-sourced its Lean library, problem specifications and verified solutions.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Weights Under Apache 2.0

Baidu published ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights under Apache 2.0 in FP4, FP8 and BF16 variants. The intermediate post-training checkpoint has 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active per token and a one-million-token context. Its model card cautions that post-training continues and some capabilities may remain below the eventual official release.
