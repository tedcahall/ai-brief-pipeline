🌐 TOP STORIES

Z.ai Introduces GLM-5.4, Its First Loop Transformer

Z.ai introduced a flagship model that reuses 28 layers of GLM-5 across two passes to deepen reasoning without adding parameters. The model accepts images and video, and Z.ai is pitching it for work beyond conventional software, including 3D shaders and CAD. Coding Plan users have access; Z.ai says the general model API will open after a security assessment.

Baidu Releases Open Weights for ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview

Baidu published Apache 2.0 weights for the first post-trained checkpoint built on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. The mixture-of-experts model has 280 billion total parameters, uses 14 billion per token and supports a one-million-token context. Baidu is highlighting coding and 3D modeling demos, but says post-training is still underway and the checkpoint is a preview, not the final release.

Nvidia Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO

New developments: Nvidia’s board voted unanimously to restore Jensen Huang as president, CEO and a director, effective immediately, ending CFO Colette Kress’s stint as interim CEO. Chair Harvey Jones stays in place. The board said its separate inquiry into reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort will continue, and announced a new committee to oversee national security and export controls.

DeepMind Uses Gemini Agents to Find Cheaper Quantum Circuits

New developments: In work distinct from its recent quantum optics proof, Google DeepMind and Google Quantum AI used teams of Gemini agents and the Lean theorem prover to design and check more efficient quantum circuits. They report an 11.8-fold reduction in estimated computing cost for one fusion-related calculation. The circuits have not run at that scale on quantum hardware, and the researchers released their Lean library and verified solutions.

UN Chief Calls for Global AI Risk Talks at Barbados Summit

At the Bridgetown Special Summit on AI Safety, UN Secretary-General António Guterres called for member states to begin talks on a shared framework for managing AI risk. He proposed common testing practices, transparency standards and independent evaluation of advanced models, with support for countries that lack testing capacity. The summit did not adopt binding rules.
