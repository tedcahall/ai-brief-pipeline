🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO and Director; Shares Jump 31%

Nvidia's board voted unanimously on November 23 to reinstate Jensen Huang immediately as president, CEO, and director, about two weeks after he resigned amid reports of extramarital affairs including with an alleged Chinese spy. The board cited senior U.S. government input that his return poses no national security risk, while keeping its independent investigation active and returning interim CEO Colette Kress solely to CFO. CNBC reported Nvidia shares closed up 31%.

Z.ai Launches 750B GLM-5.4 Flagship on a Loop Transformer Architecture

Chinese AI lab Z.ai launched GLM-5.4, a roughly 750B-parameter flagship that runs 28 layers of its GLM-5 base model twice per token using gated updates and sparse-attention reuse to add computational depth without extra weights. The model adds vision and a 1M-token context window for coding, 3D shaders, games, and CAD. It is live in Z.ai Chat and ZCode, with API access, benchmark scores, and open weights still pending.

Baidu Releases Open-Weight ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Under Apache 2.0

Baidu released Apache 2.0 open weights on Hugging Face and ModelScope alongside Qianfan API access for ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, a Mixture-of-Experts text model with 280B total and 14B active parameters, a 1M-token context window, FP4/FP8/BF16 checkpoints, and a DSpark speculative-decoding head. Baidu notes post-training is ongoing and reports self-evaluated scores of 46.5 on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and 61.9 on AutomationBench across coding, Blender and CAD modeling, and multi-file knowledge tasks.

SpaceX AI Unit Reportedly Earns $3.9 Billion Monthly Renting GPUs to Rivals

SpaceX's AI business generates over half of SpaceX's revenue by leasing GPU capacity to competitors, with Anthropic paying roughly $3.1 billion monthly under contracts up to $120.5 billion through 2029 and Google paying about $800 million, according to The New York Times as cited by MLQ.ai. Elon Musk reportedly told engineers SpaceX trails in AI, prioritized revenue per GPU over benchmarks, and disbanded the 60-person Grok Imagine team.

Sierra Raises $1.75 Billion Series F at a $30 Billion Valuation

Enterprise customer-experience AI startup Sierra, co-founded by Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor, announced it is raising $1.75 billion in Series F funding at a $30 billion valuation, doubling its $15 billion valuation from May, TechCrunch reported. Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners co-led the round alongside Benchmark, Sequoia, GV, and Tiger Global. Sierra charges per completed outcome rather than token usage and reports surpassing $400 million in annual recurring revenue.
