🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO

Nvidia said its board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, chief executive and a director, effective immediately. Chair Harvey C. Jones said discussions left the board satisfied with Huang's candor and, after input from senior U.S. officials, confident the return poses no national security risk. Colette Kress remains CFO after serving as interim CEO. The independent investigation into reported foreign-intelligence targeting continues, and Huang said he supports it.

Z.ai Introduces GLM-5.4 Loop Transformer

Z.ai said GLM-5.4 is its first flagship built on a loop transformer, reusing 28 layers of the GLM-5 base for two iterations at the 750 billion parameter scale, with a 1 million token context. The company says this matches models of 2 trillion to 3 trillion parameters, a vendor claim rather than an independent result. Coding-plan access is open. The API waits on a security assessment, and weights are not yet released.

Baidu Publishes Open Weights for ERNIE Flash Preview

Baidu's ERNIE account said before the cutoff that ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights are available under Apache 2.0, with 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active and a 1 million token context, and that post-training is still underway. A post-cutoff read of the linked Hugging Face card also says the preview weights are under Apache 2.0. The linked release page still said open-sourcing would come soon.

OpenAI and Cisco Launch Shirudo Linux Runtime

OpenAI and Cisco launched Shirudo, an open-source Linux runtime that uses eBPF programs to shield vulnerable code paths without restarting a service. They said a Codex-generated shield blocked a Java4Shell attempt against a running Elasticsearch process in a controlled test, and that operators approve shields before they are enforced. The project is BSD-licensed, with its BPF programs dual-licensed under BSD and GPL. The exploit block is a vendor-described test, not an independent field study.

Microsoft Details Rocket Training Infrastructure

Microsoft AI described Rocket, its in-house reinforcement-learning system for training runs that last weeks to months on thousands of GPUs. The company said Rocket is now post-training MAI Base 2, a three-trillion-parameter base model, without distillation from third-party models. Microsoft said the design targets staleness, numerical mismatch and hardware failures that compound on long climbs from a base model. Those details come from Microsoft's own technical post.
