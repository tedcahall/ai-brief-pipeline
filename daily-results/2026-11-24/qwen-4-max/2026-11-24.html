🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO After Unanimous Vote

Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and board director, effective immediately, saying it was satisfied with his candor and that input from senior US government levels indicated no national security risk. Shares rose about 31% Monday, adding nearly $1 trillion in market value (Fortune, BBC). The independent-director investigation continues, and Nvidia formed a National Security and Export Control Committee.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Weights Under Apache 2.0

Baidu released weights for ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview under Apache 2.0, a mixture-of-experts model with 280B total parameters, 14B active and a 1M-token context. Baidu describes it as an intermediate post-training checkpoint, with post-training ongoing, and says it is strong at coding, 3D modeling, knowledge work and content creation. The API is available on Qianfan and weights are posted on Hugging Face.

Z.ai Introduces GLM-5.4, Its First Loop Transformer Coding Model

Z.ai introduced GLM-5.4, its first flagship built on a Loop Transformer that re-executes a shared block of layers for deeper reasoning without adding parameters. Z.ai claims intelligence comparable to 2T-3T parameter models and says it validated the architecture across 1B, 4B, 30B and 750B base models. API access opens after safety evaluation and weights will be released responsibly; the model targets coding, video, games, 3D shaders and CAD.

OpenAI and Cisco Launch Shirudo, an Open-Source Runtime for Agent-Driven Linux Defense

OpenAI and Cisco launched Shirudo, an open-source Linux runtime that lets agents deploy eBPF shields to block exploits while services keep running. In a controlled test documented by Isovalent, a Codex-generated shield blocked a Java4Shell exploit attempt against a running Elasticsearch service without a restart. Shields are cryptographically signed and TPM-backed records track protections. OpenAI announced the project November 23.

Sierra Raises $1.75 Billion Series F at $30 Billion Valuation

Sierra, the customer-experience AI company co-founded by Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor, raised a $1.75 billion Series F led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed at a $30 billion valuation, double its level less than six months earlier. Sierra says it serves almost half of Fortune 50 companies and reported run-rate revenue of $400 million last month. TechCrunch reported the round November 23.
