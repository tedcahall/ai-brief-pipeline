🌐 TOP STORIES

NVIDIA Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as President, CEO, and Board Director

NVIDIA announced on November 23 that its board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO, and board director effective immediately, citing input from senior U.S. government officials that his return poses no national security risk. Colette Kress returns to her CFO role, Harvey Jones remains board chair, an independent-director investigation continues, and NVIDIA formed a National Security and Export Control Committee while launching a Chief Compliance Officer search.

Z.ai Launches GLM-5.4, Its First Flagship Loop Transformer Model

Z.ai released GLM-5.4 on November 23, adapting its roughly 750B-parameter GLM-5 base into a Loop Transformer that runs 28 layers across two iterations using per-token gates, hybrid sliding-window KV caching, and sparse attention index reuse. Available on GLM Coding Plan, ZCode, and Z.ai Chat with vision and a 1M-token context window, Z.ai claims intelligence comparable to 2T-3T parameter models without yet publishing benchmark scores; API access and open weights await safety evaluation.

Baidu Releases Open-Weight ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview With 1M Context

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview on November 24, a 280B-total, 14B-active mixture-of-experts model with a 1M-token context window built on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. Open weights are available on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0 in FP4, FP8, and BF16 alongside Qianfan API access, with post-training still ongoing. Baidu showcased the text-only model on long-horizon coding, Unreal Engine and Unity game development, procedural Blender and CAD 3D modeling, and knowledge work.

OpenAI's GPT-6 Cyber Pipeline Validates 948 Linux Kernel Root Vulnerabilities

OpenAI disclosed on November 23 that a five-stage vulnerability research pipeline powered by its upcoming cyber model, described as GPT-6 Cyber, audited 5,034 Linux kernel targets covering roughly 7% of the codebase. In controlled runs, analyst and triage agents surfaced 2,308 candidate findings, dynamically reproduced 1,724 vulnerabilities in KASAN virtual machines, and generated 948 local privilege escalation exploits plus candidate patches for human review and upstream maintainer reporting.

Sierra Raises $1.75 Billion Series F at $30 Billion Valuation

Enterprise customer-experience AI startup Sierra, co-founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor, announced on November 23 a $1.75 billion Series F round that doubles its valuation to $30 billion within six months. Led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners with existing backers including Benchmark, Sequoia, GV, and Tiger Global, Sierra serves nearly half of Fortune 50 companies and recently doubled run-rate revenue to $400 million in five months.
