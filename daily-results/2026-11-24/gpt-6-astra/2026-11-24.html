📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Z.ai announced GLM-5.4 with a loop-transformer design, while Baidu and Ant Ling made new model weights available. Availability differs: GLM-5.4’s API remains subject to safety evaluation, and Baidu labels its release an unfinished post-training preview.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Z.ai Announces GLM-5.4 With a Loop Transformer

Z.ai announced GLM-5.4, which reuses layers of its GLM-5 base model for deeper reasoning and adds vision for visual coding. The company highlights video, games, 3D shaders and CAD rather than conventional coding alone. Its announcement says API access will follow safety evaluation and weights will be released responsibly; neither should be treated as already available.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Weights

Baidu says ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights are available under Apache 2.0. The model has 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active per token and a one-million-token context. Its model card identifies it as an intermediate post-training checkpoint, not a finished release. Coding, 3D and knowledge-work results are vendor evaluations, with post-training still underway.

Ant Ling Releases Multimodal Ling-3.2-air

Ant Ling released Ling-3.2-air, an open-weight multimodal model with 124 billion total parameters and 5.5 billion active. Its model card reports AutomationBench-AA improving from 15.7% to 61% versus its predecessor. The same disclosure shows a legal-task all-pass rate falling from 8.3% to 0.8% after a material-hallucination check, an important qualification to the reported gains.

Anthropic Opens Zero-Retention Enterprise Safeguards Beta

Anthropic opened Enterprise Frontier Safeguards in public beta for eligible businesses after development with more than 100 customers. Automated monitoring checks for misuse across requests while inputs and outputs remain in customer-controlled storage. Anthropic says neither it nor cloud providers retain copies. The service supports Claude Platform, AWS and Google Cloud; Microsoft Foundry support is still forthcoming.

UN Calls for a Multilateral AI Risk Framework

At the Bridgetown AI safety summit, UN Secretary-General António Guterres called for member states to begin discussions on a multilateral AI Risk Management Framework. Proposed elements include shared testing, transparency, safeguards and independent model oversight. This is a call for negotiations, not an adopted global law. The UN also emphasized countries’ unequal capacity to evaluate AI safely in local contexts.
