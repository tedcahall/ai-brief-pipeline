🌐 TOP STORIES

Z.ai Introduces GLM-5.4 With a Loop Transformer for Generalized Coding

Z.ai introduced GLM-5.4, which runs 28 layers of its GLM-5 base twice to deepen computation without adding parameters. The company targets coding-driven video, games, shaders and CAD, with added vision capabilities. Its announcement says API access will follow safety evaluation and weights will follow safety hardening; the introduction is not an unrestricted API or open-weights release.

Baidu Releases ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Weights Under Apache 2.0

Baidu says ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights are now available under Apache 2.0. The model card describes a text-only mixture-of-experts model with 280 billion total parameters, 14 billion active per token and a one-million-token context. This is an intermediate checkpoint with post-training still underway, not a finished release; its published performance results are vendor evaluations.

NVIDIA Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; Investigation Continues

NVIDIA’s board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and a director, effective immediately. Harvey Jones remains chair, and interim CEO Colette Kress continues as CFO. The company says its independent investigation will continue and it will cooperate with government inquiries. It also announced a search for a chief compliance officer; reinstatement does not mean those investigations have ended.

OpenAI and Cisco Launch Shirudo for Live Linux Exploit Shields

OpenAI and Cisco launched Shirudo, an open-source runtime for deploying temporary eBPF protections while teams prepare permanent patches. In a controlled test, a Codex-generated shield blocked a Java4Shell exploit against a running Elasticsearch service without a restart. Operators approve deployments, and shields are signed and verified. The demonstration supports a specific defense, not a claim of general autonomous protection.

DeepMind’s Gemini Agent Teams Find Lean-Verified Quantum Circuit Savings

Google DeepMind reports that teams of Gemini 4 agents designed cheaper quantum circuits and verified their correctness in Lean. One fusion-related circuit reduced a combined qubit-and-operation cost metric by 11.8 times; other gains covered chemistry and arithmetic. The library and solutions are open source. These are resource estimates for future quantum hardware, not measured speedups on deployed quantum computers.
