🌐 TOP STORIES

Z.ai Launches GLM-5.4 Flagship Coding Model Built on a Loop Transformer

Chinese AI developer Z.ai introduced GLM-5.4, a roughly 750-billion-parameter model and its first flagship built on a Loop Transformer architecture. According to Z.ai, the model executes 28 layers of the GLM-5 base network twice per token to add reasoning depth without increasing parameter count, targeting visual coding, video, 3D shaders, and CAD modeling across a one-million-token context window. GLM-5.4 is live in Z.ai Chat and ZCode, with API access and weights planned.

Microsoft AI Details Rocket Reinforcement Learning Framework for MAI Base 2

Microsoft AI published technical details on Rocket, its in-house reinforcement learning framework built to post-train its three-trillion-parameter MAI Base 2 foundation model without distilling from third-party models. Microsoft says Rocket supports agentic training runs spanning weeks to months across thousands of GPUs by enforcing bit-exact numerical consistency between sampler and learner kernels, managing rollout staleness with fast weight transfers, and recovering dynamically from hardware failures.

Nvidia Board Unanimously Reinstates Jensen Huang as President and CEO

Nvidia announced that its board of directors voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, chief executive officer, and board director, effective immediately. Board chair Harvey C. Jones said the board consulted senior levels of the United States government and concluded Huang's return poses no national security risk. Interim chief executive Colette Kress continues as chief financial officer.

Enterprise Agent Startup Sierra Raises $1.75 Billion Series F at $30 Billion Valuation

Customer-experience AI agent company Sierra, co-founded by Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor, raised $1.75 billion in a Series F round co-led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners, with participation from Benchmark, Sequoia, GV, and Tiger Global. The financing doubles Sierra's valuation from $15 billion in May 2026 to $30 billion. Sierra reported serving nearly half of the Fortune 50 and recently stated its outcome-based pricing model helped double run-rate revenue to $400 million.

OpenAI and Cisco Release Shirudo Open-Source eBPF Runtime for Live Linux Defense

OpenAI and Cisco launched Shirudo, an open-source Linux security runtime built on Isovalent's Tetragon platform that lets security teams deploy AI-generated eBPF shields to block exploits without restarting running services. In a controlled test reported by the companies, a Codex-generated shield blocked a Java4Shell exploit against a live Elasticsearch instance while preserving normal traffic. Shirudo uses cryptographic signatures, the Linux eBPF verifier, and Trusted Platform Module attestation to audit deployed protections.
