🌐 TOP STORIES

Nvidia Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, Restoring Nearly $1 Trillion in Value

Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director effective immediately. Harvey Jones remains chair and Colette Kress returns to the CFO role after serving as interim CEO. Shares rose about 31% Monday, restoring nearly $1 trillion in market value per BBC and Fortune. The independent committee investigating reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort will continue.

Z.ai Releases GLM-5.4, Its First Loop Transformer Flagship

Z.ai released GLM-5.4 on November 23, a 750B-scale model that loops 28 layers of the pretrained GLM-5 base twice instead of adding parameters, which the company says reaches intelligence comparable to 2T-3T-parameter models. It targets generalized coding beyond text, including video, games, 3D shaders and CAD modeling, and adds vision. API access opens after safety evaluation, with weights to follow. Independent benchmarks are not yet available.

Baidu Open-Sources ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview Weights Under Apache 2.0

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0 on November 24. The model has 280B total parameters with 14B active, a 1M-token context and FP4, FP8 and BF16 support, and Baidu says post-training is still ongoing. The release highlights coding, game development, 3D modeling and knowledge work, with the API live on Baidu's Qianfan platform and day-zero vLLM and SGLang support.

Microsoft AI Details Rocket, the RL Engine Post-Training MAI Base 2

Microsoft AI published technical details of Rocket, its in-house reinforcement-learning framework built for runs of thousands of steps spanning weeks to months on thousands of GPUs. Rocket addresses rollout staleness, bit-exact numerical consistency between sampler and learner, and fault tolerance mid-run. Microsoft says its three-trillion-parameter MAI Base 2 model is now being post-trained with Rocket, climbing through RL without distillation from third-party models.

UN Chief Urges Global AI Risk Framework Within a Year

Closing the Bridgetown Special Summit on AI Safety co-hosted with Barbados on November 23, UN Secretary-General António Guterres called on member states to begin discussions on a multilateral AI Risk Management Framework. He urged shared safety measures, common testing approaches, clear safeguards and standards, transparency requirements and independent oversight of advanced models within a year, warning that power is shifting from states to a small number of companies.
