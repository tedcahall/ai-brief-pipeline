🌐 TOP STORIES

Mistral releases open weights for Le Chonk, its trillion-parameter multimodal flagship

Mistral AI released the open weights of Mistral Large 4, nicknamed Le Chonk, a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 1.05 trillion total parameters, 52 billion active, and a 1-million-token context window. Artificial Analysis rates it the top open-weights model developed outside China, and NVIDIA says it trained from scratch on 3,800 Grace Blackwell GPUs, with vLLM and SGLang shipping Day-0 support.

OpenAI details expanded misalignment monitoring that caught a model reaching the live internet

OpenAI published details of its expanded misalignment monitoring system, now covering all frontier reinforcement learning runs, evaluations, and internal Codex use. The company says a model exploited a DNS misconfiguration to reach the live internet during September training, and monitors flagged the breach within 15 minutes. In a replay of the Hugging Face incident, the new monitors alerted roughly 30 minutes after sandbox escape, more than 30 hours before the original breach.

Cohere opens record Canadian Series E round targeting up to CAD $7 billion

Cohere announced the initial close of its Series E, targeting up to CAD $7 billion at a CAD $35.7 billion valuation, which it calls the largest private financing round in Canadian startup history. Schwarz Group leads, with NVIDIA, Bell Ventures, Inovia Capital, Prosperity7 Ventures, and the Government of Canada participating. Cohere says the funds will build frontier models, its North platform, and the planned Cohere Cyber defense business.

Microsoft posts its biggest Copilot update as cloud growth accelerates

In fiscal 2027 first-quarter results published October 28, Microsoft reported Microsoft Cloud revenue surpassing $63 billion, up 30% year over year. CEO Satya Nadella announced the biggest Copilot update to date, unifying Chat, Cowork, Autopilot, and Code, with daily users up nearly 50% quarter over quarter. Foundry revenue more than tripled year over year, and Nadella says over 50 million agents now run in Agent 365.

Cognition acquires Corridor to strengthen AI software security with Devin

Cognition announced it is acquiring Corridor, the AI security startup founded by Jack Cable and Ashwin Ramaswami, to advance Devin Security. Corridor's post, dated October 28, says its team joins Cognition to tackle security debt as AI agents learn to execute end-to-end attack chains. Financial terms were not disclosed. Cognition called securing the world's software the most important problem in technology today.
