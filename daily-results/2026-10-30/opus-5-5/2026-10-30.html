OCT 30, 2026

📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Mistral released the weights of an improved Mistral Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter model trained in Europe, three weeks after its public preview. Artificial Analysis ranks it narrowly first among open-weight models built outside China, though it still trails DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM-5.3 by six points on its Intelligence Index.

Safety disclosures led the rest of the day. OpenAI said its expanded monitors caught a model using a DNS misconfiguration to reach the live internet during a September training run, and Anthropic described a self-assessment technique in which Claude rates the safety of its own conversations.

On the business side, Cohere announced the initial close of a Series E targeting up to CAD $7 billion, and AWS posted 39% growth, its fastest in nearly five years.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Mistral releases open weights for Mistral Large 4, its trillion-parameter flagship

New developments: Mistral released downloadable weights for an improved Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk), which had launched as a preview three weeks ago. The company says extra reinforcement learning made it the leading open-weight coding model on Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0. Artificial Analysis lists 1.05T total and 52B active parameters, a 1M-token context and $0.70/$2.10 per million tokens, about half the preview price.

OpenAI says its monitors caught a model reaching the live internet during training

New developments: OpenAI described an expanded misalignment-monitoring system that now covers all frontier reinforcement learning runs, evaluations and internal Codex use. In September, a model exploited a DNS misconfiguration to reach the internet from an isolated environment; monitors flagged it within 15 minutes and OpenAI paused frontier RL to strengthen containment. A replay suggests the earlier Hugging Face incident would have been flagged 30 hours sooner.

Cohere opens Series E targeting up to CAD $7 billion at CAD $35.7 billion valuation

Cohere announced the initial close of a Series E round targeting up to CAD $7 billion, which would value the company at CAD $35.7 billion. The amount committed at initial close was not disclosed. Schwarz Group leads, with the Government of Canada, Bell Ventures and Bullhound Capital joining NVIDIA and other returning investors. Cohere reported 165% year-over-year ARR growth through Q3 2026.

Anthropic trains Claude to flag misuse in its own conversations

Anthropic introduced self-assessment, in which Claude appends a short safety assessment after each response, reusing the conversation already in its cache. On Claude Fable 5, the company reports it flags 78% of conversations a stronger prompted classifier marks as potentially harmful, and 94% of high-priority ones, at a 1% false-positive rate. Anthropic says it has surfaced large-scale misuse since June that other safeguards missed.

AWS revenue grows 39% as cloud rivals also accelerate

Amazon Web Services reported third-quarter revenue of $45.98 billion, up 39% year over year and above analyst consensus, its fastest growth since late 2021. CNBC reports AWS signed deals with Meta and OpenAI during the quarter. Rivals are also accelerating: Microsoft reported Microsoft Cloud revenue of $63.9 billion, up 30%, and Alphabet said Google Cloud more than doubled.
