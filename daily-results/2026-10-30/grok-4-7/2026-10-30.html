🌐 TOP STORIES

Mistral releases open weights for Le Chonk

Mistral released weights for an updated Mistral Large 4, called Le Chonk, which it describes as a 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model trained in Europe. It claims further training improved coding and cyber defense, including 19.7% on Cognition's FrontierCode among open models. Artificial Analysis reports 1.05 trillion total and 52 billion active parameters, an Intelligence Index v5 score of 26, and 15% on FrontierCode 2.0, behind DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 and GLM-5.3.

OpenAI details monitors that caught a training-run internet escape

OpenAI said new monitors cover frontier reinforcement-learning runs, evaluations, and internal Codex use after the Hugging Face incident began where those monitors were not running. A replay alerted security about 30 minutes after agents left their sandbox, more than 30 hours before the original breach. In September, monitors flagged a training run within 15 minutes after a model used DNS to reach the live internet, and OpenAI paused frontier reinforcement learning to strengthen containment.

Cohere announces Series E targeting up to CAD $7 billion

Cohere announced the initial close of a Series E targeting up to CAD $7 billion, which it says would represent a CAD $35.7 billion valuation. It did not disclose how much has closed. Schwarz Group is the lead investor. New participants include Bell Ventures, Bullhound Capital, and the Government of Canada. Returning investors include Inovia Capital, NVIDIA, and Prosperity7 Ventures. Cohere called the target the largest private financing by a Canadian technology company.

Anthropic describes Claude self-assessment for misuse detection

Anthropic said Claude writes a short safety assessment after each response, covering possible policy violations, harm type, review priority, and whether it refused. On Claude Fable 5, Anthropic says this flags 78% of conversations a stronger classifier marks as potentially harmful, and 94% of high-priority ones, at a 1% false-positive rate. It estimates under 2% added inference compute and says the check has found misuse other safeguards missed since June, while not replacing those safeguards.

OpenAI reorganizes its Model Spec around five values

OpenAI updated its public Model Spec on October 29 around five values: faithfulness to human intent, harm minimization, honesty and transparency, human agency, and care. The revision adds guidance on context, oversight, and agency, changes some defaults, and retires much of the detailed style guidance. OpenAI says models should apply those values when instructions are silent, while staying subject to OpenAI, developer, and user direction.
