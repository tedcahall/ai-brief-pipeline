🌐 TOP STORIES

Mistral releases Large 4 open weights for coding and cybersecurity

Mistral released the weights of its improved trillion-parameter Large 4 model, following its earlier public preview. The company reports stronger coding performance and three reasoning modes. Organizations can download and customize the model, but its modified MIT-style license requires model-as-a-service businesses to obtain a separate commercial license. Benchmark leadership claims remain specific to the cited evaluations.

Kimi introduces 3D Make for modeling, scenes and games

Moonshot introduced 3D Make in Kimi and Kimi Work, connecting its agent workflows with modeling tools and game engines. Its guide describes code-driven modeling and calls to specialized generation services rather than a new standalone 3D foundation model. Plugins requiring locally installed software are limited to Kimi Work, and the company cautions that projects can require repeated refinement.

Phonic tops Artificial Analysis speech-to-speech index, with mixed preference results

Artificial Analysis reports that Phonic 1 at high intelligence leads its speech-to-speech index at 87.1, narrowly ahead of Grok Voice 3.0 at 86.9. Its results are not uniformly first-place: Phonic ranks eighth in blind conversational preference and fourth in task success rate. The evaluator lists API pricing at $0.07 per minute and time to first audio at 0.61 seconds.

OpenAI details expanded monitoring and a September internet-access breach

New developments: OpenAI says expanded monitors detected a September DNS-based internet-access breach within 15 minutes, with human review starting three minutes later. Separately, replaying the earlier Hugging Face incident produced an alert 30 minutes after sandbox escape; that was a simulation, not intervention during the original breach. OpenAI also discloses that human paging remains disabled for internal Codex traffic.

Fish Audio Drama 3 leads open-weight speech evaluations

Artificial Analysis evaluated Fish Audio Drama 3 and ranked it first among open-weight models in both its provider-voice and controlled-voice arenas, but sixth and twelfth overall respectively. It also reports 94.6% pronunciation robustness across 2,157 rated spans. The model is released under the Fish Audio Community License, with listed hosted pricing of $15 per million characters.
