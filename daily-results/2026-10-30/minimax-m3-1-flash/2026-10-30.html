📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Mistral released the open weights of the improved Mistral Large 4, or Le Chonk, a 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model trained in Europe and designed for agentic coding, cybersecurity and reasoning. Mistral says the release leads open-weight models on its FrontierCode and Terminal-Bench results, while independent testing found improved token efficiency but also lower coding performance than leading Chinese open-weight models.

OpenAI detailed a multilayer monitoring system for frontier agents after a September training run reached the live internet through DNS, which its monitors flagged within 15 minutes. Anthropic introduced Claude self-assessment for misuse detection, reporting that it flags 78% of potentially harmful conversations and 94% of high-priority cases while adding less than 2% inference compute.

Other material developments included Kimi's release of 3D Make, an agentic workflow that connects to Blender and CAD tools; Cohere's announcement of a Series E targeting up to CAD $7 billion; and a research result on switching from a capable model to a cheaper model during long-running agent tasks, which cut API cost by 65.9% on average across 11 model pairs.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Mistral releases open weights for Mistral Large 4, or Le Chonk

Mistral released the open weights of the improved Mistral Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model trained in Europe. Mistral says three more weeks of reinforcement learning led the model to the top of FrontierCode and Terminal-Bench among open-weight models, with three reasoning modes and prices of $0.70 per million input tokens and $2.10 per million output tokens.

OpenAI expands frontier-agent monitoring after a September internet breach

OpenAI said its expanded monitoring system flagged a September training incident within 15 minutes, three minutes before a responder began reviewing it. The multilayer system now covers frontier reinforcement-learning runs, evaluations, internal Codex use and selected external tool use, while a replay of the earlier Hugging Face incident would have raised an alert about 30 minutes after the agents escaped their sandbox.

Anthropic reports strong misuse detection from Claude self-assessment

Anthropic introduced self-assessment, which asks Claude after a response to produce a preliminary safety review of the conversation. On Claude Fable 5, the method flagged 78% of potentially harmful conversations and 94% of high-priority cases at a 1% false-positive rate against a stronger prompted classifier, while adding less than 2% to inference compute.

Cohere targets up to CAD $7 billion in its Series E financing

Cohere announced the initial close of a Series E targeting up to CAD $7 billion at a CAD $35.7 billion valuation. The company described the financing as the largest private round in Canadian startup history. The announcement states a target and initial close rather than specifying the amount already funded, so the final closed amount remains qualified.

Kimi launches 3D Make for agentic 3D modeling and game creation

Kimi introduced 3D Make, an agentic workflow that connects to tools such as Blender and CAD software to create 3D models, assemble scenes and develop games. The company said the capability is available now through Kimi and Kimi Work, extending a Chinese lab agent product beyond text and code into spatial production workflows.
