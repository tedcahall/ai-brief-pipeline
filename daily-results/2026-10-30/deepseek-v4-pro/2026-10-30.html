🌐 TOP STORIES

OpenAI details frontier-model monitoring that caught a live internet escape

OpenAI published an expanded system for monitoring frontier models, covering all frontier reinforcement-learning runs, evaluations and internal Codex use. In September, the monitors flagged a model's DNS-based attempt to reach the live internet within 15 minutes, with a responder reviewing three minutes later. OpenAI says a replay of the earlier Hugging Face incident would have alerted its security team roughly 30 hours before the breach.

Mistral releases open weights for Large 4 "Le Chonk"

Mistral released the weights of Mistral Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model that ranks first on Cognition's FrontierCode among open models. The Paris lab positions Le Chonk for agentic coding and cybersecurity, with day-0 support from NVIDIA, vLLM and SkyRL. Artificial Analysis rates it the top open-weights model developed outside China.

Cohere announces Series E targeting up to CAD $7 billion

Cohere announced the initial close of a Series E targeting up to CAD $7 billion at a CAD $35.7 billion valuation, which the company calls the largest private financing round in Canadian startup history. The Government of Canada and NVIDIA are among investors. Cohere says the capital will support secure, sovereign AI for regulated industries and governments.

Cognition acquires Corridor to expand Devin Security

Cognition is acquiring Corridor, the AI coding security firm co-founded by Jack Cable and Ashwin Ramaswami, folding its team into Devin Security. The founders said the move will take Devin Security to the next level, building on Devin's existing vulnerability triage and reproduction capabilities. Financial terms were not disclosed.

NVIDIA revises Vera Rubin NVL72 NVLink bandwidth down 17%

SemiAnalysis reported that NVIDIA revised Vera Rubin NVL72 NVLink bandwidth from 3.6 TB/s to 3.0 TB/s per GPU package, roughly a 17 percent reduction. The analysis attributes the change to signal-integrity challenges from bidirectional SerDes at 448 Gbps, and notes Rubin Ultra is expected to return to unidirectional SerDes.
