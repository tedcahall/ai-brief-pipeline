📊 AI INDUSTRY PULSE

Mistral released updated weights for its Large 4 model, while Phonic introduced a speech-to-speech system designed to use tools during calls. Anthropic described an offline Claude self-assessment layer that flags potential misuse but does not replace real-time safeguards.

Google expanded file editing inside Gemini, and procurement specialists urged clearer standards for federal use of AI in bid evaluations. Moonshot also announced a Kimi workflow that connects to 3D creation tools.

🌐 TOP STORIES

Mistral releases improved Large 4 model weights

Mistral released the weights of an updated Large 4, its roughly trillion-parameter model previously available in preview. The model accepts text and images and produces text or code, with 52 billion parameters active per task. Mistral says additional training improved coding and cyber-defense performance; benchmark claims depend on the tests and configurations used.

Phonic introduces a speech-to-speech model for voice agents

Phonic introduced Phonic-1, a speech-to-speech model designed for agents that use tools during calls. The company says the model can begin using tools while a caller is still speaking. Artificial Analysis placed its high setting first on a composite speech index, though eighth on its blind preference arena. The model is available through Phonic.

Anthropic describes Claude self-assessment for detecting misuse

Anthropic says Claude can assess conversations after responding and flag potential misuse for offline review. In the company’s evaluation, Claude Fable 5 flagged 78% of conversations identified by a stronger prompted classifier, at a 1% false-positive rate. Anthropic says the approach is not reliable enough to stop a dedicated attacker and complements other safeguards.

Gemini adds in-chat document and PDF editing

Google says Gemini users can generate, preview and edit documents, presentations, spreadsheets and code within chats. PDF preview and editing work on web and mobile, while the other listed formats are supported on the web. Google says the capabilities are available across the languages in which the Gemini app is offered.

Experts urge federal procurement rules for AI-assisted evaluations

Industry and legal experts urged the Federal Acquisition Regulatory Council to address how agencies use AI in procurement, Federal News Network reported. A court required the Army to include AI-generated proposal evaluations in a contract dispute’s record, while finding the challenger had not shown that AI made the overall evaluation arbitrary. The proposals are recommendations, not adopted rules.
