## Scoring and interpretation

Headline 5 + stories 5 × 9 + tweets 10 × 5 = 100. Headline column is points; Stories/Tweets are matches. Equal scores share ranks; speed does not break ties. This measures selection agreement, not factual accuracy, editorial quality or model capability.
Every model contributes to its own reference consensus. All ties at the fifth-story and tenth-tweet vote cutoffs receive credit. Rows use vote count, mean selected position and stable ID to order ties. * marks scoring eligibility.
Eligible pools: 8 events at >=4 votes; 10 posts at >=7 votes. Denominator: 16 editions (all sixteen canonical models completed terminal runs this date: four complete_local and twelve review_required).

## Scope, timing and status

All sixteen HTML editions and terminal worker records are present. Twelve runs are review_required; no independent visual certification of the newsletters is claimed here. Worker status is not a factual-quality score.
All initial X and web evidence copies match the frozen shared-inputs-auto manifest (47 entries). Web evidence is the authorized recapture (09:45:00-09:45:04 UTC); X is the saved morning capture. Identical evidence does not mean identical complete input: runtime instructions, model sessions and follow-up research differ.
Elapsed times are worker-reported and include tools/research/validation, not just inference. Where run.json stored free-text timing (GLM, Grok, MiniMax, Haiku), the worker terminal summary seconds were used. Scheduler lane durations differ and are not used here.
