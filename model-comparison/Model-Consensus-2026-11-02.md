## Editorial differences and checks worth making

- Flash uniquely selects the Anthropic filing and GPT-6.1 Sol release. Low consensus credit for these selections is not evidence they are wrong or unimportant. They warrant source/date checks before drawing quality conclusions.
- DeepSeek accounts range from broad proactive chat monitoring (Pro/Qwen) to a narrower authorized single-repository research prototype (Sol/Opus). These may describe different layers of the same design; check implementation scope before treating the broader claim as delivered functionality.
- GLM describes NVIDIA results as corroborated by secondary coverage, while Terra, Grok, Kimi and Qwen explicitly frame them as submission claims. Repetition by another publisher does not establish independent benchmark replication.
- Budget language varies: Luna and Opus explicitly say proposed spending; several other editions say allocates or pledges. Check legislative status before implying enacted expenditure.
- MiniMax selects a NAVER investment article whose URL is dated October 30. Its freshness rationale should be checked against the edition cutoff; an automated pass does not itself resolve timeliness.
- The mathematical essay is attributed to guest author Frank Calegari by Astra/MiniMax, not to host Terence Tao.
- No primary-source fact adjudication was performed here. These are concrete differences in saved wording and selection, not findings that one model is factually correct.
