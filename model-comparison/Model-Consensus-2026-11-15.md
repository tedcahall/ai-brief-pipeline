## Scoring and interpretation

Headline 5 + stories 5 × 9 + 10 tweets × 5 = 100. Headline column is points; Stories/Tweets are matches. Equal scores share ranks; speed does not break ties. This measures selection agreement, not factual accuracy, editorial quality or model capability.
Every model contributes to its own reference consensus. All ties at the fifth-story and tenth-tweet vote cutoffs receive credit. Rows use vote count, mean selected position and stable ID to order ties. * marks scoring eligibility.
Eligible pools: 6 events at >=7 votes; 11 posts at >=9 votes. Denominator: 19 editions (all nineteen canonical models completed terminal runs this date: four complete_local and fifteen review_required).

## Scope, timing and status

All nineteen HTML editions and terminal worker records are present. Fifteen runs are review_required (worker-side visual inspection unavailable); no independent visual certification of the newsletters is claimed here. Worker status is not a factual-quality score.
All initial X and web evidence copies match the frozen shared-inputs-auto manifest (50 entries). Web evidence is the authorized recapture (09:45 UTC); X is the saved morning capture. Identical evidence does not mean identical complete input: runtime instructions, model sessions and follow-up research differ.
Elapsed times are worker-reported and include tools/research/validation, not just inference. Eighteen run.json files record numeric elapsed_seconds this date; glm-5-3 records a worker elapsed string (296 s) parsed for the timing table. Scheduler lane durations differ and are not used here.
