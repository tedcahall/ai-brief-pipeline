## Scoring and interpretation

Headline 5 + stories 5 × 9 + 10 tweets × 5 = 100. Headline column is points; Stories/Tweets are matches. Equal scores share ranks; speed does not break ties. This measures selection agreement, not factual accuracy, editorial quality or model capability.
Every model contributes to its own reference consensus. All ties at the fifth-story and tenth-tweet vote cutoffs receive credit. Rows use vote count, mean selected position and stable ID to order ties. * marks scoring eligibility.
Eligible pools: 5 events at >=10 votes; 11 posts at >=7 votes. Denominator: 20 editions (all twenty canonical cohort models completed terminal runs this date: four complete_local and sixteen review_required).

## Scope, timing and status

All twenty HTML editions and terminal worker records are present. Sixteen runs are review_required (worker-side visual inspection unavailable); no independent visual certification of the newsletters is claimed here. Worker status is not a factual-quality score.
All initial X and web evidence copies match the frozen shared-inputs-auto manifest (51 entries). Web evidence is the authorized 09:45 UTC recapture; X is the saved morning capture (cutoff 10:00:53 UTC). Identical evidence does not mean identical complete input: runtime instructions, model sessions and follow-up research differ.
Elapsed times are worker-reported and include tools/research/validation, not just inference. Seventeen run.json files record numeric elapsed_seconds; glm-5-3 records a human string (419 s); haiku-5-5 is computed from its worker start/end timestamps; gpt-6-1-terra has only a lower-bound measurement (216 s) because its first action was not timestamped. Scheduler lane durations differ and are not used here.
qwen-4-max had one earlier coordinator-recorded failed attempt (Alibaba provider rejected the input as failing data inspection; 290 s scheduler time, session 4975de30, no HTML produced) before its successful run; the failed attempt is not part of this consensus.
