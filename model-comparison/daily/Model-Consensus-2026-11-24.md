## Scoring and interpretation

Headline 5 + stories 5 × 9 + 10 tweets × 5 = 100. Headline column is points; Stories/Tweets are matches. Equal scores share ranks; speed does not break ties. This measures selection agreement, not factual accuracy, editorial quality or model capability.
Every model contributes to its own reference consensus. All ties at the fifth-story and tenth-tweet vote cutoffs receive credit. Rows use vote count, mean selected position and stable ID to order ties. * marks scoring eligibility.
Eligible pools: 6 events at >=8 votes; 10 posts at >=12 votes. Denominator: 23 editions (all twenty-three canonical cohort models completed terminal runs this date: 6 complete_local and 17 review_required).

## Scope, timing and status

All twenty-three HTML editions and terminal worker records are present. 17 runs are review_required (worker-side visual inspection unavailable); no independent visual certification of the newsletters is claimed here. Worker status is not a factual-quality score.
All initial X and web evidence copies match the frozen shared-inputs-auto manifest (54 entries). Web evidence is the authorized 09:45 UTC recapture; X is the saved morning capture. Identical evidence does not mean identical complete input: runtime instructions, model sessions and follow-up research differ.
Elapsed times are worker-reported counters from run.json and include tools/research/validation, not just inference. gpt-6-luna records elapsed_seconds_from_first_captured_timestamp (exact from the first captured timestamp, with the earlier initial template read uncaptured); haiku-5-5 records elapsed_seconds alongside an elapsed_mm_ss display; opus-5-5, deepseek-v4-pro and sonnet-5-5 record both numeric elapsed_seconds and a free-text display; argon, carbon, qwen-4-max, neon, fable-5-1, fable-5-2, kimi, minimax, grok, deepseek-flash and hydrogen record numeric elapsed_seconds with display variants. Scheduler lane durations differ and are not used here.
