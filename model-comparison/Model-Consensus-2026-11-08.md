## Scoring and interpretation

Headline 5 + stories 5 × 9 + tweets 10 × 5 = 100. Headline column is points; Stories/Tweets are matches. Equal scores share ranks; speed does not break ties. This measures selection agreement, not factual accuracy, editorial quality or model capability.
Every model contributes to its own reference consensus. All ties at the fifth-story and tenth-tweet vote cutoffs receive credit. Rows use vote count, mean selected position and stable ID to order ties. * marks scoring eligibility.
Eligible pools: 5 events at >=5 votes; 10 posts at >=6 votes. Denominator: 12 editions (partial-batch warning: all twelve scheduled models completed terminal runs this date, seven review_required and five complete_local).
